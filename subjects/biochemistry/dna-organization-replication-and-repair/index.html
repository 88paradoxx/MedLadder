Sample MCQ #1

A 42 yr old patient with symptoms of premature aging and is diagnosed as Werner syndrome. The most likely mechanism?

AShort telomere with damaged DNA and loss of helicase
BIncreased length of telomere
CIncreased advanced glycation end products
DDecreased lipid peoxidation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Short telomere with damaged DNA and loss of helicase

Werner syndrome is characterized by the dramatic, rapid appearance of features associated with normal aging. Individuals with this disorder typically grow and develop normally until they reach puberty Werner syndrome is characterised by the defective DNA Helicase Enzym, which causes short telomere and damage DNA

Sample MCQ #3

Radiant damage can lead to following types of DNA damages which leading to cancer, except:

AFormation of pyrimidine dimer
BFormation of purine dimer
CElimination of bases
DCross linking of DNA strands
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Formation of purine dimer

Some Types of DNA Damage Caused by Radiation Option A: Formation Pyrimidine Dimer (Thymine dimer) - condensation of adjacent thymine Option B: Formation of purine dimer not seen commonly. Option C: Formation of apurinic or apyrimidinic sites by elimination of corresponding bases, Hydrolysis of N glycosidic bond which connect N base with deoxyribose sugar Option D: Formation of strand (chain) breaks or cross-linking of DNA strands Chain break - Disintegration of backbone element, can be single strand or double strand Cross linkage - Between bases in same or opposite strands, also between DNA and protein molecules (eg, histones) Reference: Harper's illustrated biochemistry 32nd edition CH 56 PN 683
