Sample MCQ #2

How many ATPs are produced by the oxidation of one molecule NADPH in oxidative phosphorylation?

AZero
B1.5
C2.5
D3
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Zero

Option A: NADPH is never used to produce ATP in oxidative phosphorylation, instead it is used for reductive biosynthesis of many compounds. Option B: The oxidation of one molecule FADH2 in oxidative phosphorylation produces 1.5 ATPs. Option C: The oxidation of one molecule NADH in oxidative phosphorylation produces 2.5 ATPs. Option D: It was believed that the oxidation of one molecule NADH in oxidative phosphorylation produces 3 ATPs earlier, but recently it is corrected to 2.5. Reference: Harper's illustrated biochemistry 32nd edition CH 11 PN 108

Sample MCQ #5

In TCA Cycle, conversion of pyruvate to acetyl coenzyme A and carbon dioxide:

AInvolves the participation of lipoic acid.
BIs activated when pyruvate decarboxylase of the pyruvate dehydrogenase complex (PDHC) is phosphorylated by PDH kinase in the presence of ATP.
COccurs in the cytosol.
DRequires the coenzyme biotin.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Involves the participation of lipoic acid.

The PDH Complex contains five coenzymes that act as carriers or oxidants for the intermediates of the reactions. E1 requires thiamine pyrophosphate (TPP), E2 requires lipoic acid and CoA, and E3 requires FAD and NAD+. [Note: TPP, lipoic acid, and FAD are tightly bound to the enzymes and function as coenzymes&ndash;prosthetic groups][Note: Lipoic acid linked to a lysine residue in E2 functions as a &ldquo;swinging arm&rdquo; that allows interaction with E1 and E3.] The PDHC catalyzes an irreversible reaction that is inhibited when the decarboxylase component (E1) is phosphorylated. The PDHC is located in the mitochondrial matrix. Biotin is utilized by carboxylases, not decarboxylases. Reference: Lippincott SAE, page no. 207-208 Reference: Harper's 32 nd edition, chapter - 17

Sample MCQ #6

Vitamin deficiency causing neonatal seizures

AThiamine
BPyridoxine
CRiboflavin
DPantothenic acid
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Pyridoxine

Option B: The main clinical manifestation of pyridoxine-dependent epilepsy is generalized seizures, which usually occur in the first days of life and are unresponsive to conventional anticonvulsant therapies. Some mothers of affected fetuses report abnormal intrauterine fluttering movements. The seizures are usually tonic-clonic in nature but can be almost any type. Other manifestations such as dystonia, respiratory distress, and abdominal distention with vomiting, hepatomegaly, hypoglycemia and hypothermia may be present. Learning problems and speech delay are common sequelae. Late-onset forms of the condition (as late as 5 years of age) have been reported. Consequently, a trial with vitamin B6 is recommended in any infant with intractable convulsions Option A, Option C & Option D: Thiamine, Riboflavin & Pantothenic acid deficiency not reported to cause neonatal seizures.
