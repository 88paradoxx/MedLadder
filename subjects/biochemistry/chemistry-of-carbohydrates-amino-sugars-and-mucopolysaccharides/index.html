Sample MCQ #3

All of the following is true about Epimer except

AWhen sugars are different from one another, in configuration with regard to a single carbon atom, along with the reference carbon atom, they are called epimers
BMannose is epimer of Glucose at 2nd Carbon
CGalactose is epimer of glucose at 4th Carbon
DMannose and Galactose are not epimers of glucose
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Mannose and Galactose are not epimers of glucose

A) When sugars are different from one another, in configuration with regard to a single carbon atom, along with the reference carbon atom, they are called epimers : This statement is true and defines the concept of epimers. B) Mannose is epimer of Glucose at 2nd Carbon : This statement is true. Mannose and glucose differ in their configuration at the second carbon atom. C) Galactose is epimer of glucose at 4th Carbon : This statement is also true. Galactose and glucose differ in their configuration at the fourth carbon atom. So, the only statement that is not true is option D , which states that Mannose and Galactose are not epimers. In fact, they are epimers of glucose, but at different carbon atoms (Mannose at the second carbon and Galactose at the fourth carbon). Reference: Harper's 32 nd edition, Page 154
