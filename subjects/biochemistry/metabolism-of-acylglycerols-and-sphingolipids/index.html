Sample MCQ #1

Thermogenin protein is mainly found in:

AGolgi complex
BMitochondria
CEndoplasmic reticulum
DNucleus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Mitochondria

Explanation: Thermogenin , also known as Uncoupling Protein 1 (UCP1) , is located in the inner mitochondrial membrane of brown adipose tissue (BAT) . (Option A,C and D ruled out) Its primary function is non-shivering thermogenesis . UCP1 acts as a proton channel, allowing protons to leak back into the mitochondrial matrix without passing through ATP synthase. This uncouples oxidative phosphorylation from ATP synthesis, dissipating the proton gradient energy as heat to maintain body temperature, especially in newborns and hibernating mammals. Key Takeaways: Thermogenin (UCP1) is an uncoupling protein found in the mitochondria of brown adipose tissue. It generates heat by uncoupling electron transport from ATP production.

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following molecules is present in Gangliosides:

APhosphate
BSulfate
CSerine
DSialic Acid
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Sialic Acid

Ganglioside Sphingosine + Fatty acid + Oligosaccharide that contain one, two, three or four molecules of sialic acid. Gangliosides are complex glycosphingolipids derived from glucosylceramide that contain one or more molecules of a sialic acid. Neuraminic acid is the principal sialic acid found in human tissues. Gangliosides are present in nervous tissues in high concentration. They function in cell-cell recognition and communication as a receptor for hormone. Reference: Harper's 32nd edition, page 218

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is a non-phosphorylated lipid?

ACephalin
BLecithin
CGlycosphingolipids
DCardiolipin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Glycosphingolipids

Glycosphingolipids are non phosphorylated lipid. GLYCOLIPIDS (GLYCOSPHINGOLIPIDS) ARE IMPORTANT IN NERVE TISSUES & IN THE CELL MEMBRANE. All other phospholipids are derivatives of phosphatidic acid, in which the phosphate is esterified with one OH group of glycerol and the other two OH groups are esterified to two long chain fatty acids (glycerophospholipids). Phosphatidic acid is important as an intermediate in the synthesis of triacylglycerols as well as phosphoglycerols but is not found in any great quantity in tissues. Reference: Harper's 32nd edition, page 215,218

Sample MCQ #4

A short stature male presented with enlarged and weak heart muscle, weakness of skeletal muscle, recurrent infection and diagnosed with Barth syndrome. Which of the following lipid component is decreased in this disorder?

ALecithin
BCeramide
CCardiolipin
DPhosphatidyl serine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Cardiolipin

Phosphatidic acid is a precursor of phosphatidylglycerol, which in turn gives rise to cardiolipin. This phospholipid is found only in mitochondria and is essential for the mitochondrial function. Decreased cardiolipin levels or alterations in its structure or metabolism cause mitochondrial dysfunction in aging and in pathological conditions including heart failure, hypothyroidism, and Barth syndrome (cardioskeletal myopathy). Reference: Harper's 32nd edition, page 217

BCardiolipin

✓ Correct Answer: A. Sphingomyelin

Sphingomyelin, a sphingosine-based phospholipid, is a major structural lipid in the membranes of nerve tissue and. Is the only Sphingophospholipid. Sample MCQ #8

Gangliosides are complex glycosphingolipids present abundantly in neuronal cell membranes and play essential roles as cell-surface receptors. Specific pathogens and toxins use these gangliosides to gain entry into host cells. Gangliosides can bind with which of the following for that purpose?

ACholera toxin
BTetanus toxin
CRotavirus
DInfluenza virus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Rotavirus

Explanation: Rotavirus binds to GM1 and GM3 gangliosides on intestinal epithelial cells for attachment and entry into the cells. Causes gastroenteritis , leading to vomiting, diarrhoea, and fever , primarily in young children.
