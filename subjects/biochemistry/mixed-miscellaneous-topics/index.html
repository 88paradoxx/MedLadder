Sample MCQ #1

Calories from TPN: 100mg dextrose. 30mg amino acids, 40 mg lipids

A840
B530
C680
D880
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 880

Carbohydrates & protein (amino acid) provide energy of 4 Kcal/gm, whereas fats are a concentrated source of energy providing 9 Kcal/gm. So total calorie calculation = (100 x 4) + (30 x 4) + (40 x 9) = 880 So Option D is correct.

Sample MCQ #2

N-acetyl-cysteine replenishes:

AGlutathione
BGlycine
CGlutamate
DGABA
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Glutathione

The active part of both Glutathione and N-Acetyl Cysteine is the Sulfhydryl group of Cysteine. So N-Acetyl Cysteine replenishes Glutathione. Reference: Harper's 32 nd edition, page 23

AProduced from arginine

✓ Correct Answer: D. Acts through c AMP

Nitric Oxide acts through cGMP Formed from Arginine iNOS, eNOS, nNOS are three isoforms of Nitric Oxide Synthase Nitric Oxide Uncharged molecule has an unpaired electron, so it is highly reactive, free radical. Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following is not a part of the classic triad to diagnose primary hemochromatosis?

AMicronodular cirrhosis with marked brown pigmentation.
BDiabetes mellitus
CSkin pigmentation
DGlomerulopathy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Glomerulopathy

Explanation: The classic triad for diagnosing primary hemochromatosis consists of: Micronodular cirrhosis with marked brown pigmentation Diabetes mellitus Skin pigmentation Glomerulopathy is not part of the classic triad for diagnosing primary hemochromatosis. While kidney involvement can occur in advanced hemochromatosis, it is not considered a hallmark feature for diagnosis.

Sample MCQ #8

If the RNA sequence is CAAGGU, what would be the corresponding sequence of the DNA template strand?

ACAAGGT
BGTTCCA
CACCTTG
DTGGAAC
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. ACCTTG

Explanation: Given RNA sequence: CAAGGU Relationship between RNA and DNA: RNA is transcribed from the DNA template strand. The DNA template strand is complementary to the RNA sequence. Base pairing rules: Cytosine (C) pairs with Guanine (G) Adenine (A) pairs with Thymine (T) Uracil (U) in RNA pairs with Adenine (A) in DNA Step-by-step conversion: RNA: CAAGGU Complementary DNA template strand (3' &rarr; 5'): C pairs with G A pairs with T A pairs with T G pairs with C G pairs with C U pairs with A Resulting template strand: GTTCCA Since DNA sequences are usually written in the 5' &rarr; 3' direction, reverse the above sequence: 5' &rarr; 3' template strand: ACCTTG
