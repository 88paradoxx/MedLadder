Sample MCQ #7

Which amino acid provides nitrogen for conjugation with sodium benzoate in the treatment of urea cycle disorders?

AGlutamine
BGlutamate
CGlycine
DArginine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Glycine

Explanation: Sodium benzoate acts as a nitrogen scavenger in patients with urea cycle disorders. It conjugates with glycine to form hippuric acid , which is then excreted in urine, effectively removing nitrogen from the body. This helps reduce hyperammonemia in affected patients. Explanation of other options: Glutamine (option A): Major nitrogen carrier in the body but not directly conjugated with benzoate. Glutamate (option B): Participates in amino group transfers but not used in sodium benzoate therapy. Arginine (option D): Used in some urea cycle therapies to stimulate residual urea cycle activity, but does not donate nitrogen to benzoate. Reference: https://www.uptodate.com/contents/urea-cycle-disorders-management https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK547972/
