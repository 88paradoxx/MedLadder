Sample MCQ #1

Catabolism of H2O2 is carried out by:

APeroxisomes
BMitochondria
CEndoplasmic reticulum
DLysosomes
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Peroxisomes

Peroxisomes (or) microbodies are concerned with three vital functions in a cell namely: Generation and degradation of hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) Oxidation of very long chain fatty acids (ag, C20 C22) Detoxification of harmful chemicals Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.38

Sample MCQ #2

How many atoms of copper are incorporated in Erythrocuprein?

AOne
BTwo
CThree
DFour
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. One

Explanation: Erythrocuprein, also known as ceruloplasmin, is a copper-carrying protein in the blood. Each molecule of ceruloplasmin contains one atom of copper that is essential for its enzymatic activity, which includes oxidising iron and functioning in iron metabolism.

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following sentence is true regarding chemical modification of LDL (Low density lipoprotein) by free radicals?

AEasily recognized by liver LDL receptor
BEasily cleared by liver
CIncreases risk of atherosclerosis
DNot taken up by macrophage scavenger receptors
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Increases risk of atherosclerosis

Chemical modification of the proteins or lipids in plasma low-density lipoprotein (LDL) leads to abnormal LDL. Option A & Option B: Such modified LDL is not recognized by the liver LDL receptors, and so is not cleared by the liver. Option C: Such modified LDL is highly atherogenic. Option D: The modified LDL is taken up by macrophage scavenger receptors. Lipid-engorged macrophages infiltrate under blood vessel endothelium (especially when there is already some damage to the endothelium), and are killed by the high content of unesterified cholesterol they have accumulated. This occurs in the development of atherosclerotic plaques. Reference: Harper's illustrated biochemistry 32nd edition CH 45 PN 542

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following molecule is present in glutathione peroxidase as prosthetic group?

AIron
BHeme
CSelenium
DZinc
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Selenium

In erythrocytes and other tissues, the enzyme glutathione peroxidase, containing selenium as a prosthetic group, catalyzes the destruction of H2O2 and lipid hydroperoxides through the conversion of reduced glutathione to its oxidized form, protecting membrane lipids and haemoglobin against oxidation by peroxides. So, Option C is correct. Reference: Harper's illustrated biochemistry 32nd edition CH 12 PN 114

Sample MCQ #6

The co-factor for glutathione peroxidase is:

AManganese
BSelenium
CCopper
DMagnesium
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Selenium

Explanation: Glutathione peroxidase (GPx) is a key antioxidant enzyme that reduces hydrogen peroxide and organic hydroperoxides to water and alcohols, respectively, using glutathione as a reducing agent. Selenium is an essential co-factor for this enzyme, as it is incorporated into its active site as the amino acid selenocysteine , which is critical for its catalytic activity. Explanation of other options: Manganese (Option A): Manganese is a co-factor for enzymes like manganese superoxide dismutase and arginase . Copper (Option C): Copper is a co-factor for copper-zinc superoxide dismutase and cytochrome c oxidase. Magnesium (Option D): Magnesium is a co-factor for many ATP-dependent enzymes like kinases and polymerases. Key Takeaways: Selenium is the crucial co-factor for the antioxidant enzyme glutathione peroxidase. A deficiency can impair the body's ability to neutralize peroxides.

Sample MCQ #7

Which mineral serves as a cofactor for the enzyme glutathione peroxidase?

AZinc
BMagnesium
CSelenium
DCopper
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Selenium

Explanation: The enzyme glutathione peroxidase is an important antioxidant enzyme that helps protect cells from oxidative damage by reducing hydrogen peroxide and organic hydroperoxides. Zinc is a cofactor for many enzymes and plays a crucial role in cellular metabolism, immune function, and protein synthesis. It is also involved in the activity of several antioxidant enzymes, such as superoxide dismutase (SOD), which helps neutralize free radicals. However, it is not the primary cofactor for glutathione peroxidase.

Sample MCQ #8

In humans, ascorbic acid cannot be synthesized because of ?

ADeficiency of G6PD
BDeficiency of xylulose kinase
CDeficiency of L-gulonolactone oxidase
DDeficiency of phosphoglucomutase
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Deficiency of L-gulonolactone oxidase

Ans. is 'c' i.e., Deficiency of L-gulonolactone oxidase In man, other primates and guinea pigs, ascorbic acid can be synthesized due to absence of L-glucanolactone oxidase, an enzyme used in lower animals to synthesise ascorbic acid as a byproduct of uronic acid pathway (glucuronic acid cycle).
