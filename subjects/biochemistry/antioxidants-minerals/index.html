Sample MCQ #1

Catabolism of H2O2 is carried out by:

APeroxisomes
BMitochondria
CEndoplasmic reticulum
DLysosomes
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Peroxisomes

Peroxisomes (or) microbodies are concerned with three vital functions in a cell namely: Generation and degradation of hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) Oxidation of very long chain fatty acids (ag, C20 C22) Detoxification of harmful chemicals Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.38

Sample MCQ #2

How many atoms of copper are incorporated in Erythrocuprein?

AOne
BTwo
CThree
DFour
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. One

Explanation: Erythrocuprein, also known as ceruloplasmin, is a copper-carrying protein in the blood. Each molecule of ceruloplasmin contains one atom of copper that is essential for its enzymatic activity, which includes oxidising iron and functioning in iron metabolism.

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following sentence is true regarding chemical modification of LDL (Low density lipoprotein) by free radicals?

AEasily recognized by liver LDL receptor
BEasily cleared by liver
CIncreases risk of atherosclerosis
DNot taken up by macrophage scavenger receptors
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Increases risk of atherosclerosis

Chemical modification of the proteins or lipids in plasma low-density lipoprotein (LDL) leads to abnormal LDL. Option A & Option B: Such modified LDL is not recognized by the liver LDL receptors, and so is not cleared by the liver. Option C: Such modified LDL is highly atherogenic. Option D: The modified LDL is taken up by macrophage scavenger receptors. Lipid-engorged macrophages infiltrate under blood vessel endothelium (especially when there is already some damage to the endothelium), and are killed by the high content of unesterified cholesterol they have accumulated. This occurs in the development of atherosclerotic plaques. Reference: Harper's illustrated biochemistry 32nd edition CH 45 PN 542
