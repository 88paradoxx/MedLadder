Sample MCQ #1

What is true regarding medium chain fatty acids ?

ADon't require pancreatic lipase
BNot deposited in adipose tissue
CDiffuse directly into portal circulation
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Ans. is 'd' i.e.,All of the above [Ref Vasudevan 6" p. 160] Metabolism of short chain fatty acids (SCFAs) and medium chain fatty acids (MCFAs) is drastically different from long chain fatty acids (LCFAs). SCFAs and MCFAs do not require pancreatic lipase and bile salts for digestion, but required for LCFAs digestion. SCFAs and MCFAs are directly absorbed from intestine into portal circulation, whereas LCFAs are taken by lymphatics, after incorporation into chylomicrons. SCFAs and MCFAs are oxidized by peripheral cells and are not used for storage. LCFAs are esterified with glycerol to form triacylglycerol, storage form of lipid

Sample MCQ #2

In which of the following locations can cardiolipin be found?

AOuter mitochondrial membrane
BInner mitochondrial membrane
CCytosol
DMitochondrial matrix
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Inner mitochondrial membrane

Explanation: Cardiolipin is a unique phospholipid present predominantly in the inner mitochondrial membrane . Functions: Maintains the structural integrity of mitochondria. Stabilizes and optimizes the activity of the electron transport chain (ETC) complexes and their supercomplexes. Structure & Synthesis: Has a distinctive dimeric structure with four fatty acid chains. Synthesized within mitochondria from phosphatidylglycerol and CDP-diacylglycerol (CDP-DAG) . Explanation of other options: Outer mitochondrial membrane (A): Mostly contains phosphatidylcholine & phosphatidylethanolamine, not cardiolipin. Cytosol (C): Cardiolipin is membrane-bound and absent in cytosol. Mitochondrial matrix (D): Not a membrane lipid, hence not present here.

Sample MCQ #4

The human body is incapable of producing double bonds between which carbon atoms?

ABetween carbons 6-7
BBetween carbons 9-10
CBetween carbons 5-6
DBetween carbons 10-11
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Between carbons 10-11

In humans, the enzyme desaturase introduces double bonds to synthesize fatty acids and the human body has desaturases 9,6,5 and 4. The desaturase 9 introduces a double bond at carbons 9-10 and introducing double bonds beyond the 10 th carbon atom is not possible de novo. This is the reason why polyunsaturated acids &omega;-6 linoleic acid and &omega;-3 linolenic acid are essential fatty acids. Reference: LIPPINCOTT illustrated reviews 6 th edition page 188

Sample MCQ #5

Which of the following is a lipotropic factor : ?

ASphingomyelin
BHistidine
CMethionine
DBilirubin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Methionine

Ans. is 'c' i.e., Methionine [Ref Essentials of biochemistry/p. 761] Lipotropic factors are substances which prevent accumulation of fat (TGs) in liver. Primary lipotropic factors choline, betaine, methionine, lecithine, inositol. Other factors with some lipotropic action :- Vitamin-13,2, folic acid, casein, glycine, essential fatty acids, selenium, vitamin E and serine.

Sample MCQ #8

All are true about Cervonic acid except?

AFound in cerebral cortex and sperms
BIs synthesised from linoleic acid
CDeficiency causes Retinitis pigmentosa
DBreast milk is a source
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Is synthesised from linoleic acid

Cervonic acid, also known as DHA, is an &omega;-3 fatty acid found in breast milk, fish oils and algal oil. Highest concentrations are in the retina, cerebral cortex and sperms. It is formed from &alpha;-Linolenic acid and NOT linoleic acid. It's deficiency is implicated in retinitis pigmentosa. Reference: LIPPINCOTT illustrated reviews 6 th edition page 363
