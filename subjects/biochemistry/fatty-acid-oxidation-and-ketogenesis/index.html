Sample MCQ #1

The most common storage form of lipids is?

AFatty acids
BLipoproteins
CNeutral fats
DPolar fats
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Neutral fats

Triacylglycerol (TG), which is a neutral fat, is the most common storage form of lipids. They are esters of trihydric alcohol glycerol and fatty acids. Reference: Harper's illustrated biochemistry 32nd edition page 493

APalmitic acid
BStearic acid
COleic acid
DOmega-3 fatty acid

✓ Correct Answer: D. Omega-3 fatty acid

Significance of &omega;3 Fatty Acid Decrease the risk of Cardiovascular Disease Appear to replace arachidonic acid in platelet membranes Lower the production of thromboxane and tendency of the platelet aggregation Decrease Serum Triglycerides Important for Infant Development Lower the risk of various mental illness (Depression, ADHD) Lower the risk of chronic degenerative diseases such as Cancer, Rheumatoid Arthritis, and Alzheimers Disease . Sample MCQ #3

Fatty Acid present in human milk

ACervonic Acid
BEicosa pentanoic Acid
CArachidonic Acid
D&alpha; Linolenic Acid
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Cervonic Acid

Docosahexaenoic acid/Cervonic acid (DHA; &omega;3, 22:6), which is synthesized to a limited extent from &alpha;-linolenic acid or obtained directly from fish oils, is present in high concentrations in retina, cerebral cortex, testis, and sperm. DHA is particularly needed for development of the brain and retina and is supplied via the placenta and milk. Patients with retinitis pigmentosa are reported to have low blood levels of DHA. Reference: Harper's 32nd edition, page 239

ACarboxylation
BOxidation
CDecarboxylation
DAmination

✓ Correct Answer: C. Decarboxylation

Amino acid is converted to keto acid by Deamination or transamination. Amino acid is converted to biological amines by Decarboxylation. Examples. Histidine to histamine Tyrosine to tyramine. Tryptophan to tryptamine Serine to ethanolamine Reference: Harper's 32 nd edition, page 320

Rothera's test used for detection of:

AProteins
BGlucose
CFatty Acid
DKetone Bodies

✓ Correct Answer: D. Ketone Bodies

Test for Ketone Bodies Gerhardt's Ferric Chloride Test: Detect Acetoacetate Rothera's Nitroprusside Test: Detect Acetoacetate and Acetone None of the above tests detect Beta hydroxybutyrate, since it lacks the keto group towards which these tests are directed. Sample MCQ #6

True regarding peroxisomal disorders?

ALorenzo oil causes neonatal adrenoleukodystrophy
BZellweger syndrome is also called cerebrohepatorenal syndrome
CRefsum's disease is a common disorder caused by phytanic acid accumulation
DZellweger syndrome is characterised by absence of peroxisomes in the brain only
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Zellweger syndrome is also called cerebrohepatorenal syndrome

Lorenzo oil is used for the treatment of Adrenoleukodystrophy. Zellweger Syndrome, also called cerebrohepatorenal syndrome, occurs in individuals with a rare inherited absence of peroxisomes in all tissues. They accumulate C26-C38 polyenoic acids (VLCFA) in brain tissue and also exhibit a generalized loss of peroxisomal functions. The disease causes severe neurologic symptoms, and most patients die in the first year of life. Refsum disease is a rare neurologic disorder caused by a metabolic defect (absence of enzyme phytanoyl CoA dioxygenase) that results in the accumulation of phytanic acid. Phytanic Acid is found in dairy products and ruminant fat and meat. Reference: Harper's illustrated biochemistry 32 nd edition page 532

Sample MCQ #7

Enzyme responsible for complete oxidation of glucose to CO2 and water is present in:

ACytosol
BMitochondria
CLysosomes
DEndoplasmic reticulum
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Mitochondria

Under aerobic conditions, pyruvate is taken up into mitochondria, and after oxidative decarboxylation to acetyl-CoA is oxidized to CO2 by the citric acid cycle Under anaerobic conditions, pyruvate is reduced by the NADH to lactate, catalyzed by lactate Reference: Harper's 32 nd edition, Page no.172
