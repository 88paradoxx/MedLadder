Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following enzymes participates in the glycolytic pathway?

AGlucose-6-phosphatase
BPhosphofructokinase-1
CPyruvate carboxylase
DGlycerol kinase
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Phosphofructokinase-1

Explanation: Phosphofructokinase-1 (PFK-1) is a key enzyme of glycolysis that catalyses the conversion of fructose-6-phosphate to fructose-1,6-bisphosphate using ATP and is the main rate-limiting and regulatory step of the pathway. it is inhibited by ATP and citrate and activated by AMP and fructose-2,6-bisphosphate.

Sample MCQ #3

Selenocysteine is associated with ?

ACarbonic anhydrase
BCatalase
CDeiodinase
DTransferase
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Deiodinase

Ans. C. Deiodinase Selenocysteine is considered as 21 standard amino acid. It is present at the active site of some enzymes that catalyze redox reactions, e.g. thioredoxin reductase, glutathione peroxidase, and the deiodinase (converts thyroxin to triiodothyronine). Biosynthesis of selenocysteine requires cysteine, serine, ATP and a specific t-RNA. Serine provides the carbon skeleton of selenocysteine. Selenocysteine has a structure similar to cysteine, but containing the trace element selenium in place of sulfur atom of cysteine.
