Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following amino acid is purely ketogenic?

AValine
BIsoleucine
CAlanine
DLeucine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Leucine

Based on metabolism, amino acids can be classified into - Ketogenic - These amino acids yield only acetyl CoA on oxidation which cannot be used for gluconeogenesis. Ex: Lysine and leucine Ketogenic and glucogenic - Yield both acetyl CoA and intermediates that can be used for gluconeogenesis. Ex: Phenylalanine, tyrosine, tryptophan, and isoleucine Glucogenic - These amino acids yield only pyruvate, or four- and five-carbon intermediates of the citric acid cycle, which can be used for gluconeogenesis. Ex: All the remaining amino acids Reference: Harper's 32nd edition, page 146

In winter, water vapours and pollutants comes to lie in the lowermost layer of atmosphere by -

AAcid rain
BGreenhouse effect
CTemperature inversion
DNone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Temperature inversion

Ans. is `c' i.e., Temperature inversion A temperature inversion is a thin layer of the atmosphere where the normal decrease in temperature with height swithees to the temperature increasing with height. An inversion can lead to pollution such as smog being trapped close to the grand (lower layers of atmosphere). Temperature inversion may occur during the passage of a cold front or result from the invasion of sea air by a cooler onshore breeze. o Overnight radiative cooling of surface air often results in a nocturnal temperature inversion that is dissipated after sunrise by the warming of air near the ground.

Sample MCQ #5

Oxidative deamination occurs in ?

ACytoplasm of all cells
BMitochondria of all cells
CCytoplasm of hepatocytes
DMitochondria of hepatocytes
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Mitochondria of hepatocytes

Ans. is 'd' i.e., Mitochondria of Hepatocytes [Ref : Harper 29'/e p. 274] Deamination means removal of amino group of amino acid in the form of ammonia. Thus, an amino acid is converted to a keto acid. Deamination coupled with oxidation is called oxidative deamination. It occurs in the mitochondria. Oxidative deamination occurs primarily in the liver (major organ) and kidney.

Sample MCQ #6

Sulfur of cysteine are not used/utilized in the body for the following process/product:

AHelp in the conversion of cyanide to thiocyanate
BThiosulfate formation
CIntroduction of sulfur in methionine
DDisulfide bond formation between two adjacent peptide
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Introduction of sulfur in methionine

Methionine is an essential amino acid, so it cannot be synthesized from Cysteine. Sulfur of cysteine is donated by sulfur of methionine. This is called trans-sulfuration reaction. PLP is the coenzyme of transsulfuration. The reaction is catalyzed by Cystathionine beta Synthase and Cystathionase enzyme. Reference: Harper's 32 nd edition, page 284,285

Sample MCQ #8

Which is elevated in pyridoxal phosphate deficiency?

AFIGLU
BXanthurenic acid
CMethylmalonic acid
D5-HIAA
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Xanthurenic acid

Urinary metabolite in Vitamin B 6 deficiency: Xanthurenic acid Urinary Metabolite in Folic Acid Deficiency: Formimino Glutamic acid, Homocystine Urinary metabolite in Vitamin B 12 deficiency: Homocystine, Methyl Malonic Acid 5-HIAA is elevated in carcinoid syndrome Tryptophan metabolism - Tryptophan is degraded to amphibolic intermediates via the kynurenine-anthranilate pathway. Tryptophan 2,3-dioxygenase opens the indole ring, incorporates molecular oxygen, and forms N-formylkynurenine. Hydrolytic removal of the formyl group of N-formylkynurenine, catalyzed by kynurenine formylase, produces kynurenine. Since kynureninase requires pyridoxal phosphate , excretion of xanthurenate in response to a tryptophan load is diagnostic of vitamin B6 deficiency. Reference: Harper's 32nd edition, page 306,308
