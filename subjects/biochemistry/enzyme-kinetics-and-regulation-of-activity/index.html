Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is an example of an allosteric enzyme?

AHexokinase
BPhosphofructokinase
CChymotrypsin
DTrypsin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Phosphofructokinase

Explanation: Phosphofructokinase-1 (PFK-1) is a classical allosteric enzyme and a key regulatory step of glycolysis, whose activity is modulated by allosteric effectors like ATP and citrate (inhibitors) and AMP, ADP, and fructose-2,6-bisphosphate (activators). These regulators bind to sites other than the active site (allosteric sites), causing conformational changes and leading to a sigmoidal reaction curve , unlike Michelis-Menten enzymes. Hexokinase shows feedback inhibition by glucose-6-phosphate but is not a classical allosteric enzyme. Chymotrypsin and trypsin are digestive proteases regulated by zymogen activation (proteolytic cleavage) and not by allosteric modulation.
