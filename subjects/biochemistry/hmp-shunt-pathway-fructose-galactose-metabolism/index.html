Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following sentence is best suitable for Glucose?

AIt is ketose and usually exists as a furanose ring in a solution.
BA C-4 epimer of galactose
CIt is produced from dietary starch by action of alpha-amaylase
DIt utilize in biological systems only in L-Isomeric form.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. A C-4 epimer of galactose

Glucose is aldose sugar that typically exists as a pyranose ring in a solution. Fructose is a ketose with a furanose ring in a solution. Glucose and Galactose differ in only configuration around carbon 4, they are C-4 epimers that are interconvertible by action of an epimerase. Alpha-amaylase does not produce monosaccharides. Carbohydrates exist in D-isomeric form that is found in biological system while amino acid found in L-Isomeric form. Reference: Harper's 32 nd edition, page no.154

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following are enantiomers:

AD-Galactose and L-Glucose
BD- Galactose and I-Glucose
CD-Mannose and L-Mannose
DD-Mannose and I-Mannose
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. D-Mannose and L-Mannose

D and L Isomerism [Enantiomers] Difference in the orientation of H and OH group around Penultimate Carbon atom results in two mirror images called D and L isomers (Enantiomers). The penultimate carbon atom is called Reference Carbon atom. The penultimate carbon atom in Glucose and Fructose is C-5. Most of the naturally occurring Monosaccharides are D isomers [Unlike Amino Acids, which are L isomers.] Examples of Enantiomers D Glucose and L Glucose D Fructose and L Fructose D Mannose and L Mannose D Glyceraldehyde and L Reference: Harper's 32 nd edition, Page no. 153
