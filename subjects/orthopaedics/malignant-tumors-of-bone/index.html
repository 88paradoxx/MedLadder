Sample MCQ #1

Commonest site of occurrence of chondrosarcoma is

APelvis
BFemur
CRibs
DProximal tibia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Pelvis

Chondrosarcoma A chondrosarcoma is a malignant tumor derived from cartilage cells and it tends to maintain its cartilaginous character throughout its evolution. Highest incidence is in the fourth and fifth decades and men are affected more often than women. Most commonly involved bones are pelvis(most common), femur(2 nd most common), humerus, ribs and shoulder girdles. It may be at metaphysis or diaphysis. This tumor is slow growing but can metastasise to distant site The usual presentation is dull ache or gradually enlarging mass. Radiological features Expansile, radiolucent lesion in the medullary cavity(concentric) Endosteal scalloping. Punctate comma shaped or popcorn calcification. Treatment Surgery is the treatment of choice Reference: Campbell, 12 th edition, Page no. 914.

Sample MCQ #3

A 45 year old male presented with an expansile lesion in the distal femoral metaphysis. The lesion shows endosteal scalloping and punctate calcification. Diagnosis?

AChondroblastoma
BChondrosarcoma
COsteosarcoma
DMetastasis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Chondrosarcoma

The diagnosis is chondrosarcoma. The points in favor are The age of the patient is 45 years. The common age for chondrosarcoma is between 40 to 60 years. The site of origin is the metaphysis. The x ray finding of endosteal scalloping and punctate calcification is seen in chondrosarcoma. Osteosarcoma and Chondroblastoma are commonly seen in the 2nd decade of life Metastasis does not produce endosteal scalloping and punctate calcification Reference: Miler's 8th Edition of Review of Orthopaedics, page no. 1885

Sample MCQ #4

Increased LDH levels are a bad prognostic factor in which tumor?

AOsteosarcoma
BOsteoid osteoma
CGiant cell tumor
DEwing's tumor
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Ewing's tumor

Poor prognostic factors for Ewing's sarcoma Distant metastases Advanced and extensive disease with fulminant course. Large tumor size(> 8cm) Proximal location Older age(> 17 years) Male gender Genetic aberrant P-53 expression Relapse Early(within Poor response to chemotherapy Elevated ESR, WBC, LDH value indicates more extensive disease and poor prognosis. Ewing sarcoma is the most chemosensitive and most radiosensitive malignant bone tumor. Ewing sarcoma(> osteosarcoma) is the most common tumor showing bone to bone metastasis, hence mandating a bone scan to detect the metastasis. Reference: Maheshwari Essential Ortho, 5 th edition, Page no. 243.

Sample MCQ #5

What is the most common primary malignancy of the skeleton?

AMultiple myeloma
BChondrosarcoma
COsteochondroma
DOsteosarcoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Multiple myeloma

Metastasis is the most common malignancy of the skeleton, but multiple myeloma is the most common primary malignancy of the skeleton. Osteochondroma is the most common benign tumor of the skeleton. Reference: Maheshwari Essential Ortho, 5 th edition, Page no. 247.

Sample MCQ #6

Which is the most common radiation induced tumour?

AOsteosarcoma
BEwing's sarcoma
CMultiple myeloma
DChondrosarcoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Osteosarcoma

The most common radiation induced tumour is osteosarcoma. Such tumors are called secondary osteosarcoma. They have a very poor prognosis. The most common radiation induced benign tumour is osteochondroma. Reference: J. Maheshwari's 5th Edition of Essential Orthopaedics page no. 242

Which of the following is bone forming malignant tumour?

DGiant cell tumor

✓ Correct Answer: B. Osteosarcoma

Bone-forming tumours are a sub-set of bone tumours that are characterised by their propensity to form excess osteoid. They can be further subdivided into benign and malignant tumours. Benign tumours Osteoid osteoma Osteoblastoma Bone island(enostosis) Osteopoikilosis Osteoma Malignant tumours Malignant bone-forming tumours are all osteosarcomas. Osteosarcoma(conventional) Telangiectatic osteosarcoma Low-grade central osteosarcom Reference: Adam's Outline of orthopedics, 14th edition, Pg: 113.

Sample MCQ #8

Most common site of Admantinoma is

AMandible near symphisis manti
BMandible near the molar tooth
CDiaphysis of tibia
DHard palate
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Diaphysis of tibia

Adamantinoma is a low grade malignant tumor most commonly affecting the tibia. It is a diaphyseal tumor. Tibia (85% cases) followed by fibula is the most common site for adamantinoma of long bones. It occurs between 20 to 40 years of age. The swelling is associated with only minimal pain and it increases in size gradually. Typically, it appears as a honeycomb-like, loculated lesion on X-ray. Metastasis is not common. Treatment is by resection. Reference: Maheshwari Essential Ortho, 5 th edition, Page no. 237.
