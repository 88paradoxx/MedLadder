Sample MCQ #1

Chronic fluorosis is caused by drinking water with fluoride content of

A1-3 ppm
B3-5 ppm
C5-10 ppm
D> 10 ppm
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. > 10 ppm

Chronic fluorosis is seen in localities where the water supply contains > 10 ppm fluoride (Davidson). Fluorosis It is characterized by excessive deposition of calcium in bone & soft tissues due to increased intake of fluorides through drinking water. Clinical features: Dental fluorosis&mdash; earliest sign of fluorosis (mottling of enamel) &ndash; MC involve the incisors of upper jaw. Skeletal fluorosis&mdash; pain in back & joints, stiff spine, paranesthesia, spastic paraparesis & anemia. Radiological findings: Spine: Calcification of posterior longitudinal ligament. Pelvis: Calcification of ischio pubic & sacroiliac ligaments Forearm & leg: calcification of interosseous membrane. Treatment: Defluridation of water (Nalgonda technique) Reference: Maheswari, 55th ed, p. 314

Sample MCQ #2

Triple deformity of knee includes all EXCEPT:

APosterior subluxation of tibia
BMedial rotation of tibia
CLateral rotation of tibia
DFlexion
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Medial rotation of tibia

Tuberculosis of the knee is classical cause of triple deformity. It includes: Flexion Lateral(external) rotation of tibia Posterior subluxation of tibia Better treatment results are seen with ATT and arthrodesis This condition can also be seen in Rheumatoid arthritis. Reference: Maheshwari Essential Orthopedics, 5 th edition, Page no. 201.
