Sample MCQ #3

A 50 year old male on walker support presents with back pain and weakness of both lower limbs for 1 month. MRI reveals TB spine with abscess compressing the spinal cord. Next line of action is?

AEmergency surgery and drainage of the abscess
BElective surgery with anterolateral decompression
CElective surgery with ALD + fixation of spine
DStart ATT and monitor
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Start ATT and monitor

In, India we follow the middle path regime for treatment of TB paraplegia. All patients with TB paraplegia are first started on ATT and advised bed rest and given a spine brace. These patients are the monitored for improvement. Patients who improve on ATT are continued with it. Those who are not improving are treated with surgery. Indications of surgery in middle path regime are Bowel and bladder involvement Worsening of paraplegia while on ATT Appearance of a new neurological deficit while on treatment No improvement in paraplegia even after 3-4 weeks of ATT In this question, the patient does not have any bowel or bladder symptoms, so he is started on ATT and monitored. Reference: J. Maheshwari's 5th Edition of Essential Orthopaedics page no. 193

Sample MCQ #4

Anterolateral tibial bowing is associated with which of the following lower extremity conditions in children?

ACalcaneovalgus foot deformity
BCongenital pseudoarthrosis of the tibia
CFibular hemimelia
DCongenital talipes equinovarus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Congenital pseudoarthrosis of the tibia

Congenital pseudarthrosis of the tibia: A birth defect in the lower third of the tibia in children, whereby a fracture in this region fails to unite. "It is a congenital condition characterized by bowing of tibia (mostly anterolateral bowing). Neurofibromatosis type 1 is found in 50&ndash;55 % of patients with congenital pseudoarthrosis (while 10% of patients with neurofibromatosis have pseudoarthrosis). It is equally distributed in boys and girls and is almost always unilateral. There is little tendency for the lesion to regress, and the bone eventually fractures through the weakened area. Treatment: Before a fracture occurs, a total contact orthosis is prescribed as prophylaxis. But once a fracture occurs. surgery is indicated. Reference- Maheshwari 5 th /ep. PG NO 225
