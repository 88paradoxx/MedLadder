Sample MCQ #2

In comparison of two patient. Patient-1 is 34yr old female with pathological fracture of shaft of femur due to metastasis of breast cancer and patient-2 is 64year old female with pathological fracture of shaft femur due to osteoporosis. Which of the following is true?

APatient -1 will have delayed union compared to patient-2
BPatient -2 will have delayed union compared to patient-1
CBoth fractures will unite in the same time
DCan't predict the outcome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Patient -1 will have delayed union compared to patient-2

Osteoporotic pathological fracture has good prognosis because it follows natural process of bone healing but with some delay. But pathological fracture of malignant lesion has very poor potential for healing and usually ends up in nonunion. Sample MCQ #3

A lady with osteoporosis is on prolonged bisphosphonates. She develops pain in her right hip. Next investigation is?

ASerum calcium
BBiopsy
CX ray
DDEXA scan
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. X ray

In this case, we have to get an x-ray to rule out an atypical fracture. One of the complications of prolonged bisphosphonate therapy is the development of atypical fractures. Atypical fractures occurs after drug usage of more than 5-7 year It occurs due to the defective remodeling of bones, resulting from osteoclast suppression by bisphosphonates. These fractures are commonly seen in the sub-trochanteric region of the femur. These are called atypical fractures because they involve the lateral cortex and not the medial cortex of bone as is typically seen. Reference: Koval's Handbook of fracture's 5th edition, page no. 375

When treating osteoporosis with alendronate, what is the most common side effect?

ADIARRHEA
BEPIGASTRIC DISTRESS
CDRY MUCUS MEMBRANE
DINSOMNIA

✓ Correct Answer: B. EPIGASTRIC DISTRESS

Alendronate is generally given orally in a dose of 70 mg/week. Patient is advised to take 6&ndash;8 oz of water and not to lie down for at least half an hour as these can cause gastritis and esophagitis. Reference: Manish Kumar Varshney, 1 st edition, Page no. 84.

Sample MCQ #8

What bone disease would result from a loss of osteoprotegrin(OPG) protein?

AOsteopetrosis
BOsteoporosis
COsteomalacia
DMultiple heriditary exostosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Osteoporosis

Osteoprotegerin(OPG) is a member of the TNF receptor family but it is secreted and acts like a cytokine. OPG has bone protective properties by inhibiting osteoclast development and activation. Osteoprotegerin is a decoy receptor which binds and thereby opposes RANK ligand. RANK ligand is the primary agent that activates osteoclasts and causes bone resorption. Hence, the loss of OPG will result in excessive bone resorption by osteoclasts causing osteoporosis. Reference: Manish kumar varshney, 1 st edition, Page no. 67.
