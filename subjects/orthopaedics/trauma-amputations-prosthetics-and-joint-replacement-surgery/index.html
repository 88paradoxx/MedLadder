Sample MCQ #1

A 50-year-old woman has pins and needles sensation that worsens at night and is relieved when she shakes her hand. She also has episodic left knee pain throughout the day. She takes medications for constipation. Her BMI is 35 kg/m2. Her pulse is 57/min and blood pressure is 120/75 mm Hg. Sensation is decreased to light touch in the thumb and index finger. DTR = 1+ and there is mild edema in the legs. Which of the following treatments is most likely to benefit the patient?

APrednisone
BIbuprofen
CSurgery
DL-thyroxine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. L-thyroxine

This obese patient has neuropathic pain along the distribution of the median nerve that is consistent with carpal tunnel syndrome (CTS). The patient presents with classic signs of hypothyroidism: constipation, fatigue, bradycardia, hyporeflexia, and lower limb edema (which is usually myxedematous and non-pitting, but pitting edema can also occur due to excessive albumin in extracellular spaces). While the precise etiopathology is uncertain, it is assumed that the excess deposition of glycosaminoglycans, hyaluronic acid, and mucopolysaccharides in hypothyroidism causes myxedema. These characteristic depositions can subsequently cause compression of the carpal tunnel. With thyroid hormone replacement and return to a euthyroid state, symptoms should improve within weeks to months. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 699

Sample MCQ #2

A professional tennis player presents with shoulder pains especially whilst serving the ball. He has limited passive and active shoulder abduction to less than 60&deg;. His temperature is 36.5&deg;C and he has a normal white cell count. There is tenderness around the anterior portion of the shoulder joint. Which diagnosis is likely?

AGlenohumeral joint osteoarthritis
BBursitis
CTennis elbow
DSupraspinatus tendonitis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Supraspinatus tendonitis

Pain during abduction with limitation of movement is suggestive of supraspinatous tendonitis. Palpation or compression around the greater tubercle of the humerus is particularly tender. Glenohumeral joint OA is very rare. Though Rotator cuff tendinitis is often accompanied by a sub acromial bursitis, the more likely answer here is supraspinatus tendinitis, considering the recurrent overhead abduction as in a tennis player. Tennis elbow causes pain the elbow. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st Ed, Ch: 375, Pg: 2878

Sample MCQ #3

A 35-year-old female presents to the emergency room after a motor vehicle collision where her leg was pinned under the car for over 30 minutes. On arrival at the casualty, her BP was 80 systolic. Which of the following is the most accurate way to diagnose compartment syndrome in this patient?

AParaesthesia in her foot
BDiastolic blood pressure minus intra-compartmental pressure is less than 30 mmHg
CDiastolic blood pressure minus intra-compartmental pressure is greater than 30 mmHg
DIntra-compartmental pressure measurement of 25 mmHg
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Diastolic blood pressure minus intra-compartmental pressure is less than 30 mmHg

The classical definition of compartment syndrome is an intracompartmental pressure of 30mm of Hg or above. However, this is not true for hypotensive patients. The best way to diagnose compartment syndrome in a hypotensive patient is to calculate the delta P value. Diastolic blood pressure minus intracompartmental pressure is called delta p, If delta p is less than 30 mm hg patient requires immediate fasciotomy for compartment syndrome Reference- Maheshwari ortho 5th edition, page number 47

Sample MCQ #4

A 3 year old boy is brought with the history that he had a fight with his older sister, who pulled his hand forcefully to get her favorite toy from him. After this, the boy started crying with pain on the elbow. O/E the boy is keeping his forearm pronated. X ray did not show any fractures. The next step is

AMRI to rule out occult fractures
BManipulate the elbow into supination and flexion
CApply crepe bandage and repeat x ray after 1 week
DApply a plaster cast and repeat x ray after 1 week
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Manipulate the elbow into supination and flexion

This is a case of pulled elbow also called the nursemaid&rsquo;s elbow. It is a subluxation of radial head caused due to an axial pull distal to the elbow. Seen in children Clinical features Minimal inflammation Pronated forearm X ray - normal Treatment - flexion and supination of the forearm will reduce the radial head and correct the subluxation. Reference: J. Maheshwari's 5th Edition of Essential Orthopaedics page no. 105

Sample MCQ #5

The anterior approach to total hip arthroplasty requires dissection between which of the following muscle planes?

ASartorius and gluteus maximus
BGluteus minimus and rectus femoris
CRectus femoris and sartorius
DTensor fascia lata and sartorius
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Tensor fascia lata and sartorius

DISCUSSION: The anterior approach to the hip joint involves identifying the plane between the tensor fascia lata and the sartorius muscles. In the anterolateral approach to hip, we use the plane between TFL and Gluteus medius. Reference: Berry, D. J. (2001). PRIMARY TOTAL HIP ARTHROPLASTY. In M. W. Chapman (Ed.), Chapman's Orthopaedic Surgery (3rd ed., pp. 2749&ndash;2759). Lippincott Williams & Wilkins

Sample MCQ #6

In Anterolateral approach for hip surgery, intermuscular plane is made between tensor fascia lata and which other muscle?

AGluteus Maximus
BGluteus Minimus
CGluteus Medius
DGemellus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Gluteus Medius

The plane of dissection in anterolateral approach to hip is between TFL and gluteus medius Incision: Curvilinear incision centered over the greater trochanter running posteriorly along the shaft of the femur with hip in 30&ndash;40&deg; of flexion starting 2&ndash;3 cm behind the ASIS. Soft tissue dissection: Plane is developed between the TFL and the gluteus medius (protects the inferior branch of the superior gluteal nerve). TFL is then retracted medial and anterior, and the gluteus medius retracted posterior and lateral exposing gluteus minimus posteriorly and rectus anteriorly. Reference: Manish kumar varshney 1st edition page no 1094.

Sample MCQ #7

A healthy 72-year-old woman is seen 14 days after cemented total knee arthroplasty. She reports increasing pain and swelling for the last 4 days accompanied by 4 days of wound drainage. Examination reveals that she is afebrile, and has erythema and moderate serosanguinous drainage from the wound. The knee is moderately swollen. Aspiration of the knee reveals no organisms on Gram stain. Culture results are expected back in 48 hours. Optimal management should consist of?

AInitiation of a first-generation cephalosporin while awaiting culture results.
BInitiation of broad-spectrum antibiotics while awaiting culture results.
CUltrasound to evaluate for fluid collection around the knee.
DSurgical debridement of the knee before culture results are available.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Surgical debridement of the knee before culture results are available.

DISCUSSION: Increased pain, swelling, erythema, and drainage 2 weeks removed from the primary arthroplasty are all signs of a probable infection. Erythrocyte sedimentation rate and C-reactive protein may not be helpful as they are elevated postoperatively even in the absence of infection. Even in the absence of infection, persistent wound drainage is an indication for surgical debridement to prevent subsequent infection. When a postoperative infection is easily recognized by clinical examination, there is no need to wait for a positive culture before proceeding with debridement. Reference: Burke, D. W., & O'Flynn, H. (2001). PRIMARY TOTAL KNEE ARTHROPLASTY. In M. W. Chapman (Ed.), Chapman's Orthopaedic Surgery (3rd ed., pp. 2869&ndash;2896). Lippincott Williams & Wilkins.

Sample MCQ #8

A 16 year old child, who is a known case of hemophilia presented with painful swelling of right knee after a trivial fall. What should not be done?

AX ray of the right Knee
BFactor 8 assay
CIce application
DAspiration to relieve pain
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Aspiration to relieve pain

In most instances of hemarthrosis, the first line of management is aspiration of joint, which will relieve the pressure within the joint and provide pain relief to the patient. However, this is contraindicated in hemophilia, as even if we aspirate a joint and remove all the blood, it will re-accumulate within few hours. This is because, the deficiency of clotting factor 8 will not allow clot formation and there will be continued bleeding into the joint. Reference: Miler's 8th Edition of Review of Orthopaedics, page no. 699
