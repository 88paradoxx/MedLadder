✓ Correct Answer: A. Pseudomonas

Answer- A. Pseudomonas staphylococcus aureus was the most common organism causing infection in open fractures. More recent studies have shown that gram negative organisms such as pseudomonas aeruginosa and E.coli are becoming more common

Sample MCQ #3

Tension band wiring is indicated in fracture of which of the following ?

AFracture humerus
BOlecranon
CFracture tibia
DFracture spine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Olecranon

Tension band wiring is indicated in the treatment of two types of olecranon fracture. First type is a clean break with separation of the fragments, and second type is comminuted fracture of the olecranon with displced fragments. A crack in the olecranon without displacement is treated by immobilising the elbow in an above elbow plaster slab in 30 degrees of flexion.

Sample MCQ #4

An athlete who has been training constantly for 6 months for the upcoming national selections has pain over posteromedial aspect of right leg. He has point tenderness on the posteromedial aspect of mid tibia and the pain is aggravated by activity. What is the treatment plan?

AIntramedullary nailing
BORIF with distal tibia plate
CCast immobilization
DNo treatment is required.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Cast immobilization

From the history, patient has stress fracture. Stress fractures are generally managed conservatively with cast immobilization. Exception is stress fracture of neck of femur, which might require surgical fixation. Reference: Rockwood and green adult fracture 8&#7511;&#688; edition, page-668

All of the following is used for giving skeletal traction except:

ARush pin
BSteinmann's pin
CKirschner's wire
DBohler's stirrup

Rush pin is used for pediatric diaphyseal fracture fixation. All other devices are used for giving traction. Reference- Rockwood and Green&rsquo;s adult fracture 8&#7511;&#688; edition, page-214

Sample MCQ #6

Open fracture in a child of 2 &frac12; years is managed by?

AExternal fixator
BDebridement
COpen reduction and internal fixator
DIntramedullary nailing
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Debridement

In pediatric patients generally managed conservatively so option a, c, d are not true. And for open fracture debridement is more important. Reference- Rockwood and green's pediatric fracture 8&#7511;&#688; edition, page-124

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following statement is false

AAll fractures heal with callus formation
BAll intra articular fracture requires anatomical reduction
CRelative stability involves restoring length, rotation and alignment
DFracture healing requires combined activity of osteoblast and osteoclast cells
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. All fractures heal with callus formation

Fracture healing is of two types. Direct and indirect bone healing. Direct bone healing occurs without callus formation. Indirect healing occurs with callus formation. Reference- Rockwood and green&rsquo;s adult fracture trauma 8&#7511;&#688; edition, page-198

Sample MCQ #8

10 days old open grade-3A fracture shaft of tibia with pus discharge should be treated with?

AOpen reduction with internal fixation
BClosed reduction and intramedullary reamed nail
CDebridement followed by external fixator
DCast immobilization
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Debridement followed by external fixator

Open fracture with improper soft tissue condition is an indication for external fixation. Infection is considered a contraindication for any kind of implant fixation Reference- AO trauma basic trauma manual- Principle of open fracture management and its complication
