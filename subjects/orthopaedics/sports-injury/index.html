Sample Sports Injury MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

All are causes of recurrent dislocation of shoulder except

ABankart's lesion
BBony Bankart
CHill Sach' lesion
DRotator cuff tear
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Rotator cuff tear

Rotator cuff tear is not a cause of recurrent dislocation of shoulder. The Common injuries causing recurrent dislocation of the shoulder are Bankart lesion Bony Bankart lesion Hill Sach's lesion Reference: J. Maheshwari's 5th Edition of Essential Orthopaedics page no. 90

Sample MCQ #2

A young cricket player comes to you with knee pain and inability to extend it fully. He gives a history of twisting injury to his right knee when he collided another player while fielding. His MRI shows a double PCL sign. What is the appropriate management?

AArthroscopic repair
BApplication of above knee cast for 6 weeks
CIcing, rest, compression and elevation for 2 weeks and then isometric and knee bending exercises
DIcing, rest, compression and elevation for 2 weeks and then gradual return to sports
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Arthroscopic repair

Double PCL sign on MRI is classically seen in bucket handle tear of a meniscus. The inability to fully extend the knee suggest a locked knee, again pointing towards a bucket handle meniscal tear. The best treatment of acute bucket handle meniscal tear in young athletes is arthroscopic repair. However if the meniscus injury is in the white- white zone, then a partial meniscectomy is done as the meniscus will not heal even if we do a repair because of the poor blood supply at the white -white zone. Reference - Manish Kumar Varshney 1 st edition page no 894.

Sample MCQ #3

Patient with history of knee injury 3 months back presents with knee pain. O/E knee was unstable with knee in flexion but stable in extension. What structure is injured?

AACL - anteromedial fibers
BACL - posterolateral fibers
CPCL
DMedial meniscus - posterior horn
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. ACL - anteromedial fibers

ACL is made up of 2 bundles Anteromedial - which becomes tight in flexion Posterolateral - which becomes tight in extension In this patient, the knee is unstable in flexion but stable in extension. This means that the anteromedial fibres of the ACL are torn but the posteromedial fibers are intact. Reference: Miler's 8th Edition of Review of Orthopaedics, page no. 728

Sample MCQ #4

A 23 year old female slipped from stairs, causing her right foot to go into excessive inversion. She has pain and swelling around the ankle. Which structure is most likely injured?

ADeltoid ligament
BAnterior tibiofibular ligament
CAnterior talofibular ligament
DSyndesmosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Anterior talofibular ligament

The most common ligament injured in inversion injuries of the ankle is the ATFL (anterior talofibular ligament) In fact, it is the most commonly injured ligament in the body. It is said that, every human will get an ATLF strain at least once in their lifetime. Reference: Koval's Handbook of fracture's 5th edition, page no. 452

Sample MCQ #5

Patient presents to the OPD with pain and feeling of buckling of right knee. He gives history of injury during playing hockey 3 months back. On examination, the knee was unstable anteriorly in extension but was stable in 90 degrees of flexion. Probably injury involves:

AACL anteromedial fiber
BACL posterolateral fiber
CPCL
DAnterior portion of medial meniscus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. ACL posterolateral fiber

The ACL ligament is the primary restraint to anterior tibial displacement, accounting for approximately of the resistance to the anterior drawer test when the knee is at of flexion and neutral rotation. ACL has 2 bundles &ndash;anteomedial and posterolateral. The anteromedial band is tight in flexion, providing the primary restraint, whereas the posterolateral bulky portion of this ligament is tight in extension. The posterolateral bundle also provides the principal resistance against hyperextension. Therefore in ACL tears Complete ACL tear will produce instability in both flexion and extension. Anteromedial bundle tear will produce instability in flexion Posterolateral bundle tear will produce instability in extension. Reference - Manish Kumar Varshney 1 st edition page no 879.

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following is not a feature of ACL injury?

APop sound in the knee during the time of injury
BSwelling of the knee immediately after the injury
CFeeling of knee buckling while attempting to walk
DDifficulty climbing up stairs
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Difficulty climbing up stairs

In patients with ACL tears, there is difficulty in climbing down stairs and not in climbing up stairs. This is because while coming down stairs, the tibia will be pulled anteriorly by gravity and the tibia will translate anteriorly as there is no restraint from the ACL. This will result in a feeling of giving way and instability. Features of Acute ACL tear are Pop sound at the time of injury Immediate effusion of knee Pain Unable to weight bear Features of Chronic ACL tear are Feeling of instability Difficulty going down stairs Occasional pain Reference: Miler's 8th Edition of Review of Orthopaedics, page no. 733, 741

Sample MCQ #7

Inversion injury to ankle caused damage to which of the following structures?

ATibiofibular ligament
BDeltoid ligament
CCalcanonavicular ligament
DTalofibular ligament
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Talofibular ligament

Inversion injury to ankle involves damage to talofibular ligament (the lateral complex). INVERSION INJURY An inversion force with the foot in plantar flexion results in the sprain of the anterior talofibular ligament of the ankle. It is the most common ligament injury occurring in the body. Reference - Maheshwari Essential Orthopaedics, 4th ed., 135

Sample MCQ #8

In dancers, peroneal muscle weakness has been shown to be the cause of which of the following?

AAnkle sprain
BFibula fracture
CAcute cuboid subluxation
DAchilles rupture
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Ankle sprain

The most common acute skeletal injury in the dancer is the inversion sprain of the ankle and thought to be due to relative peroneal muscle weakness. Sprains may occur in any ligament in the foot or ankle, but most common ones involve the lateral ligament complex (anterior talofibular, calcaneal-fibular, and posterior talofibular ligaments); the anterior tibiofibular, the lateral talocalcaneal ligament; and, occasionally the medial (deltoid) ligament. Regardless of the method of treatment, adequate physical therapy and proper rehabilitation are necessary to restore normal use following injury. Restoration of full peroneal strength is essential to minimize risk of recurrent sprains. Reference - Manish Kumar Varshney 1 st edition page no 1262.
