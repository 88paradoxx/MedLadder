Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following pairs of structures are antagonist to each other around the ankle joint?

APeroneus longus and peroneus brevis
BPosterior tibialis and anterior tibialis
CPosterior tibialis and peroneus brevis
DAnterior tibialis and peroneus brevis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Posterior tibialis and peroneus brevis

PL&TA are antagonist to each other as PL plantar flexes and TA dorsiflexes ankle joint. PB & PT are antagonist to each other as PB everts the foot and PT inverts the ankle joint. Reference - Manish Kumar Varshney 1 st edition page no 1242

Sample MCQ #2

A 35-year-old man presents to the emergency department with pain and swelling of the right knee for the past three days. Also, for the past two days, he has felt feverish. The patient is able to ambulate, but walking exacerbates the pain in his knee. Temperature is 39.3&deg;C (102.7&deg;F). On physical examination, the right knee is red, warm to touch, and tender. A large effusion is noted. Which of the following diagnostic studies of the knee is the most appropriate initial step?

AArthrocentesis
BArthroscopy
CBone scan
DCT scan
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Arthrocentesis

The clinical presentation described, is characteristic of septic arthritis of knee joint. The best method for confirming the diagnosis is to aspirate the knee joint to look for pus. The process of joint aspiration is also called arthrocentesis. Option B- Arthroscopy, is incorrect because this study is not the initial step in diagnosis. Option C- Bone scan, is incorrect because this study is appropriate to nonspecifically localize areas of inflammation but cannot be used to distinguish infectious from sterile processes. Option D- CT scan, and Option (E), MRI, are incorrect because these studies are more sensitive for diagnosing osteomyelitis and periarticular abscesses. Reference- Maheshwari Ortho 5th edition, page no 170.

Sample MCQ #3

Which deformity of tibia is seen in the triple deformity of the knee?

AExtension, Posterior subluxation & external rotation
BFlexion, posterior subluxation & external rotation
CFlexion, posterior subluxation & internal rotation
DExtension, Anterior subluxation & internal rotation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Flexion, posterior subluxation & external rotation

Triple deformity of knee consists of posterior subluxation of tibia, external rotation of tibia and flexion. The mnemonic &lsquo;P ER F&rsquo; can be used to remember this. Triple deformity is classically seen in late stages of TB of knee joint. Rarely rheumatoid arthritis can also produce similar deformity. Reference: Apley&rsquo;s System of Orthopaedics and Fractures, 8th Edition, Page Number-42.
