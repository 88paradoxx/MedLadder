Sample MCQ #1

Inferior thyroid artery supplies which structures 1. Esophagus 2. Thyroid 3. Parathyroid 4. Thymus

A1 and 3
B1,2 and 3
C1,3,4
DAll
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 1,2 and 3

The inferior thyroid artery (ITA) is considered the principal blood supplier of the thyroid gland . The ITA gives rise to the ascending cervical artery, the inferior laryngeal artery, and also gives pharyngeal, tracheal , and esophageal ITA also supplies the four parathyroid glands . Blood supply of esophagus: Cricopharyngeal muscle (inlet of esophagus) is supplied by superior thyroid artery Cervical esophagus: predominantly by inferior thyroid artery Thoracic esophagus: aorta, both right and left bronchial artery, along with descending branch from inferior thyroid artery, intercostal artery and ascending branch from inferior phrenic artery Abdominal esophagus: left gastric artery and inferior phrenic artery Blood supply to the thymus comes from the internal thoracic artery .

Sample MCQ #2

Gold standard investigation for a patient having signs of multiple injuries

AWhole body CT
BWhole body MRI
CCT angio
DDSA
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Whole body CT

Explanation: Whole-Body CT (WBCT) in Severe Blunt Trauma WBCT (head-to-pelvis with IV contrast) is the gold standard for evaluating the severely injured adult with blunt trauma. Selective (region-specific) imaging has no role in these patients due to the high likelihood of multisystem injury and the need for comprehensive assessment. WBCT is time-critical and should be performed as early as possible during resuscitation to guide timely interventions. If a patient requires immediate trauma laparotomy without prior WBCT , Apply a pelvic binder and do not remove it until a pelvic fracture is excluded. Obtain an immediate pelvic X-ray , either in the emergency department or on arrival to the operating theatre. Key Takeaways: Whole-body CT is the preferred investigation when there are signs of multiple injuries or deranged physiology.

Sample MCQ #3

All of the following statements are incorrect regarding evaluation of patient with chest injury except

ACT chest is almost universally performed during the initial evaluation of patient with risk of thoracic injury
BDeviation of trachea at sternal notch suggest intrathoracic pressure on same side of deviation
CThoracic MDCTA is useful for evaluation of pericardium
DThoracic MDCTA has become the standard approach to evaluation of chest injury
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Thoracic MDCTA has become the standard approach to evaluation of chest injury

Thoracic MDCTA has become the standard approach to evaluation of the chest and provides superior visualization of the chest wall, vasculature, pleural spaces, and lung parenchyma. Option A : Chest radiography is almost universally performed during the initial assessment on patients at risk for thoracic injuries. Option B : Deviation of the trachea at the sternal notch may reveal intrathoracic tension on the side opposite the trachea. Option C: Thoracic MDCTA is unreliable for evaluation of pericardium, given cardiac motion degradation. Reference : Sabiston textbook of Surgery, chapter: 17, Edition: 21st, Pg: 405

Sample MCQ #4

Cardiac injuries causing cardiovascular collapse requires urgent thoracotomy. Which kind of thoracotomy requires in this case?

ALeft anterolateral thoracotomy
BLeft posterolateral thoracotomy
CRight anterolateral thoracotomy
DRight posterolateral thoracotomy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Left anterolateral thoracotomy

For cardiac injuries that cause cardiovascular collapse, a left anterolateral thoracotomy is performed in the emergency department and may be extended to a contralateral (&ldquo;clamshell&rdquo;) incision if needed. When time permits, most cardiac injuries are best approached through a median sternotomy. Reference : Sabiston textbook of Surgery, chapter: 17, Edition: 21st, Pg: 409
