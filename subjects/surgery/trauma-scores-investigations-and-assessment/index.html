Sample MCQ #1

All of the following are component of revised trauma score except

AGCS
BPulse rate
CRespiratory rate
DSystolic BP
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Pulse rate

The Revised Trauma Score is another well-studied physiologic scoring system that characterizes the condition of the injured patient by incorporating - Glasgow Coma Scale Systolic blood pressure Respiratory rate. These scores have been of value for research purposes and have been successfully used to make triage decisions. Reference : Sabiston textbook of Surgery, chapter: 17, Edition: 21 st , Pg: 389

Sample MCQ #2

Which one of the following is the first priority if you are performing a primary survey on a patient?

AEnsuring adequate circulatory volume
BEnsuring patent airway
CEnsuring adequate cardiac output
DEnsuring patient is oriented to time place and person
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Ensuring patent airway

Ensuring a patent airway is the first priority in the primary survey. This is essential, because efforts to restore cardiovascular integrity will be futile unless the oxygen content of the blood is adequate. Simultaneously, all patients with blunt trauma require cervical spine immobilization until injury is excluded. This is typically accomplished by applying a hard collar or placing sandbags on both sides of the head with the patient's forehead taped across the bags to the backboard. Soft collars do not effectively immobilize the cervical spine. For penetrating neck wounds , however, cervical collars are not believed useful because they provide no benefit, but may interfere with assessment and treatment Reference : Schwartz Principles of surgery, chapter: 7, Edition: 11th, Pg: 184

Sample MCQ #3

Injury severity score to categorise an injury as a severe injury is?

ALess than 9
BLess than 3
CBetween 9 and 16
DMore than 25
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. More than 25

Baker and colleagues presented the ISS, calculated by summing the squares of the AIS severity codes for the three most severely injured body regions. The ISS ranges from 1 to 75 , with severity groupings being defined as - Minor injury (ISS less than 9) Moderate injury (ISS between 9 and 16) Serious injury (ISS between 16 and 25) Severe injury (ISS more than 25). Reference : Sabiston textbook of Surgery, chapter: 17, Edition: 21st, Pg: 389

Sample MCQ #4

Identify the fourth stage from the philosophy of 'damage control orthopedic' or the 'damage control surgery'

AResuscitation
BDecontamination
CFracture splintage
DHemorrhage control
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Decontamination

Knowledge acquired at the molecular level related to the immune-inflammatory response to injury and the concepts of the phenomena of the first hit (the impact exerted on the homeostatic mechanisms as a result of the original trauma sustained) and the second hit (the additional physiological stress induced by surgical procedures) led to the acceptance and implementation of the so call 'damage control orthopaedics' (DCO) philosophy, which is called damage control surgery (DCS ) in more generalized settings. The stages of DCO are: Resuscitation Hemorrhage control Decompression Decontamination Fracture splintage Reference : Bailey & Love's short practice of surgery, chapter: 26, Edition: 28 th , Pg: 350-351

Sample MCQ #5

A patient of RTA was brought to the hospital with a left flank bruise associated with hematuria. Patient was hemodynamically stable. Which of the following investigations should be done for hematuria?

ACECT
BDPL
CRGU
DMCU
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. CECT

Renal trauma Ominous sign is : hematuria and flank bruise Investigation: Stable patients: IOC- CECT Unstable patient: IOC: IVP( on table IVP is done) IVP( intravenous pyelogram is done to all patients undergoing surgery for renal trauma to look for renal perfusion RTA having left flank bruise associated with hematuria suggest renal trauma. Conservative management with close surveillance is sufficient in the majority of cases of isolated renal trauma. Lower grade injuries, sometimes including grade IV, can be managed conservatively with bed rest, antibiotics in case of penetrating injuries and serial hemoglobin estimation. Re-imaging is usually done after 2-4 days in cases of falling hemoglobin levels, fever or expanding fank mass. Persistent urinary leak can be managed with an internal DJ stent or PCN.

Sample MCQ #6

All of the following are indications for radiological assessment in renal injury except

APenetrating injury
BGross hematuria
CShock with microscopic hematuria
DAll are indication
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All are indication

The indications for radiological assessment are: Penetrating injuries. Gross haematuria. Shock in combination with microscopic haematuria. Children: in whom the kidneys are much lower and less well protected. Reference: Love & Bailey short practice of surgery, chapter: 76, Edition: 27th, Pg: 1413-1414

Sample MCQ #7

RTA with multiple fractures initial treatment would be -

AManagement of shock
BSplinting of limbs
CAirway management
DCervical spine protection
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Airway management

Answer- C. Airway management Management of trauma begins with primary survey. The ATLS (Advanced Trauma Life Support) defines primary survey as assessment of the `A,B,C' i.e., Airway with cervical spine protection, Breathing and circulation.
