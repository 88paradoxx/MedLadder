Best gas used for creating pneumoperitonium at laparoscopy is :

AN2
B02
CCO2
DN20 Goniometer is used

✓ Correct Answer: C. CO2

CO2 CO, is the gas used to create pneumoperitoneum during laparoscopy. Other option is - N20 : But it is expensive, less soluble in blood and supports combustion. Sample MCQ #2

Which among the following is a false statement for physical structure of suture material?

AMonofilament sutures are smooth
BMonofilament sutures have poor knotting ability
CMultifilament sutures have good knotting ability
DMultifilament sutures have less bacterial contamination
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Multifilament sutures have less bacterial contamination

Physical structure . Suture material may be monofilament or multifilament. Monofilament suture material is smooth and tends to slide through tissues easily without any sawing action, but is more difficult to knot effectively. Such material can be easily damaged by gripping it with needle holder or forceps and this can lead to fracture of the suture material. Multifilament or braided sutures are much easier to knot, but have a surface area of several thousand times that of monofilament sutures and thus have a capillary action and interstices where bacteria may lodge and be responsible for persistent infection or sinuses. In order to overcome some of these problems, certain materials are produced as a braided suture, which is coated with silicone in order to make it smooth. Reference : Love & Bailey short practice of surgery, chapter: 7, Edition: 27th, Pg: 90

Sample MCQ #5

What should be the minimum length of the suture material left after cutting to avoid unravelling?

A1-2 mm
B3-4 mm
C4-5 mm
D5-6 mm
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 1-2 mm

When the suture is cut after knotting, the ends should be left about 1-2 mm long to prevent unraveling. This is particularly important when using monofilament material. Reference : Love & Bailey short practice of surgery, chapter: 7, Edition: 27th, Pg: 96

Sample MCQ #6

The conversion factor to find out the outer diameter of a cannula in millimeters, expressed in French size is

A0.66
B0.33
C0.03
D3.3
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 0.33

The French scale begins at zero, and each increment of one French unit represents an increase of 1/3 (0.33) millimeter in outer diameter (3): i.e., French size &times; 0.33 = outside diameter (mm). Thus, a catheter that is 5 French units in size will have an outer diameter of 5 &times; 0.33 = 1.65 mm Reference: E Book of Baheti's Understanding Anesthetist Equipments and Procedures a Pratical Approach First Edition Chapter 33 Page No 339 -334.
