✓ Correct Answer: A. Doppler flow USG

Answer is A (Doppler flow USG): 'Stenosis at the origin of the internal carotid Artery can be identified and quantified reliably by ultrasonography that combines B mode ultrasound image with a Doppler ultrasound assessment of flow velocity.'

Sample MCQ #2

Which nerve gives rise to the recurrent laryngeal nerve?

APhrenic nerve
BVagus nerve
CHypoglossal nerve
DExternal laryngeal nerve
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Vagus nerve

Explanation: The recurrent laryngeal nerve (RLN) is a branch of the vagus nerve . The nerve recurs around the arch of the aorta on the left and the subclavian artery on the right. The clinical significance of this is that on the left, the nerve has a longer course to reach the tracheoesophageal groove and therefore runs in a medial plane. On the right, the course is shorter, and the nerve runs more obliquely to reach the tracheoesophageal groove. Approximately 2% of nerves on the right are non-recurrent and will enter the larynx from above.

Pre-operative prophylaxis for pheochromocytoma

AAlpha blockade after beta blocker
BBeta blockade after alpha blocker
CAlpha blocker
DBeta blocker
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Beta blockade after alpha blocker

Answer- B. Beta blockade after alpha blocker Beta blockers such as propanalol at doses of 10 to 40 mg every 6 to 8 hours are often needed in patients who have persistant tachycardia and arrhythmias. n-blockers should only be added after adequate a-blockade and rehydration. a-adrenergic agonists may be needed in immediate post operative period to prevent post operative hypotension and cardiovascular collapse.
