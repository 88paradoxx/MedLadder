Sample MCQ #1

All of the following are correct regarding meningitis except

ARaised ICP should be excluded before LP
BCSF should be sent for microscopy and culture
CTreatment is broad spectrum antibiotics including anaerobic coverage
DAll are correct
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All are correct

Meningitis is a feared complication of neurosurgery and head injury. CT scan allows exclusion of raised intracranial pressure and should be done prior to lumbar puncture. CSF should be sent for microscopy and culture, along with protein and glucose level assay. Treatment is with broad spectrum antibiotics including anaerobic coverage. Empirical therapy is started with 3rd generation cephalosporin + Vancomycin &plusmn; Ampicillin . Reference : Bailey & Love's short practice of surgery 28th Edition, Chapter 48, Cranial neurosurgery, Pg: 706

Sample MCQ #2

Reperfusion injury occurs when blood flow is restored after a period of ischemia. During this phase, accumulated metabolites and ions re-enter circulation, creating systemic disturbances. According to surgical principles described in Bailey and Love, which substance is chiefly responsible for the clinically significant component of reperfusion injury?

APotassium
BSodium
CUrea
DCalcium
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Potassium

Explanation: During prolonged ischemia, cells lose membrane integrity and release potassium and acid into surrounding tissues. Once reperfusion occurs, this accumulated potassium rapidly enters the systemic circulation. This sudden potassium surge can cause myocardial depression, dangerous cardiac arrhythmias, vasodilation, worsening hypotension and can contribute significantly to multi organ injury. During ischemia: Cells lose ATP and membrane pumps begin to fail Potassium leaks out of cells into the interstitial space Acidity increases due to anaerobic metabolism Inflammatory pathways are activated locally During reperfusion: The accumulated potassium and acids enter systemic circulation Inflammatory mediators and cell fragments activate leukocytes These changes may contribute to acute lung injury, acute kidney injury and multi organ failure

Sample MCQ #3

All of the following statements are correct regarding GCS score except:

AIt reflects level of consciousness
BScore ranges from 3 to 15
CGCS is composed of a measure of eye opening, verbal response, and motor function
DVerbal response is reflective of outcomes after TBI
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Verbal response is reflective of outcomes after TBI

Explanation: The Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS) reflects the level of consciousness. Scores range from 3 to 15 and assess: Eye opening Verbal response Motor function The motor score alone, not the verbal response, is most reflective of outcomes after traumatic brain injury (TBI). Head Injury Severity & GCS Severity classification of head injury is based on the post-resuscitation Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS) GCS, especially the motor score, is the best predictor of neurological outcome. Alcohol and drugs may complicate assessment. GCS-Based Classification: Minor head injury: GCS 15 with no loss of consciousness (LOC) . Mild head injury: GCS 14 or 15 with LOC . Moderate head injury: GCS 9-13 . Severe head injury: GCS 3-8 . Key Takeaways: Motor component of GCS is the best predictor of TBI outcomes.
