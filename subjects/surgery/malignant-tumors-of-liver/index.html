Sample MCQ #1

Which among the following is not a common site for hematogenous spread in phyllodes tumour?

ALungs
BMediastinum
CAbdominal viscera
DBrain
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Brain

Metastases from malignant phyllodes tumors occur via hematogenous spread. Common sites of metastasis include lung, bone, abdominal viscera, and mediastinum . Reference : Sabiston textbook of Surgery, chapter: 35, Edition: 21 st , Pg: 832

Sample MCQ #2

Which among the following is the causative agent for malignant thyroid lymphoma?

ARiedel's thyroiditis
BAcute suppurative thyroiditis
CAutoimmune thyroiditis
DSubacute thyroiditis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Autoimmune thyroiditis

Aetiology of malignant thyroid tumours The great majority of thyroid cancers have no known aetiological factor. The most important identifiable aetiological factor in differentiated thyroid carcinoma (particularly papillary) is irradiation of the thyroid under 5 years of age. Short latency aggressive papillary cancer is associated with the ret/PTC3 oncogene and later developing, possibly less aggressive, cancers with RET/PTC1. The incidence of follicular carcinoma is high in endemic goitrous areas, possibly due to TSH stimulation. Malignant lymphomas sometimes develop in autoimmune thyroiditis, and the lymphocytic infiltration in the autoimmune process may be an aetiological factor. Reference : Bailey & Love's short practice of surgery, chapter: 55, Edition: 28th, Pg: 867

Sample MCQ #3

What is the most common primary malignant tumor in adults?

AAnaplastic astrocytoma
BGlioblastoma
CMedulloblastoma
DMeningioma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Glioblastoma

The most common primary malignant tumor in adults is Glioblastoma . The most common primary malignant tumor in children is Medulloblastoma. The most common primary CNS tumor in adults is Astrocytoma. The most common primary CNS tumor in children is Pilocytic astrocytoma. Reference : Bailey & Love's short practice of surgery 28th Edition, Chapter 48, Cranial neurosurgery, Pg: 715

Sample MCQ #4

Which among the following is the most common type of malignant anal cancer?

AAdenocarcinoma
BMelanoma
CEpidermoid carcinoma
DSarcoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Epidermoid carcinoma

Anal malignancy is rare and accounts for less than 2% of all large bowel cancers. The crude incidence rate is 0.65 per 100 000. Those arising below the dentate line are usually squamous, whereas those above are variously termed basaloid, cloacogenic or transitional. Collectively they are known as epidermoid carcinomas , and account for >70% of anal malignancies; management and prognosis is similar for this group. Adenocarcinomas are the next commonest. Other tumours include melanoma, lymphoma, and sarcoma. Reference : Bailey & Love's Short Practice of Surgery, 28th Edition, Page Number-1443.
