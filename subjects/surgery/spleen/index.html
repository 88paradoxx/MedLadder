Sample Spleen MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following should not be given before a splenectomy patient?

ATyphoid
BH.influenzae type B
CMeningococcal
DPneumococcus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Typhoid

Postsplenectomy septicaemia may result from S. pneumoniae, Neisseria meningitidis, Haemophilus influenzae or Escherichia coli. Published guidelines emphasize that most infections after splenectomy could be avoided through measures that include offering patients appropriate and timely immunization, antibiotic prophylaxis, education and prompt treatment of infection Immunization in post - splenectomised pt. includes Hib + meningococcal + pneumococcal vaccines.

Sample MCQ #2

AImmunosuppression
BElderly age
CSickle cell anaemia
DLymphoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Elderly age

Reference : Sabinston Textbook of Surgery 21 st Edition, SECTION X ABDOMEN, Chapter 57, The Spleen, Pg: 1579.

Sample MCQ #3

Which among the following organisms doesn't cause infection after splenectomy?

AStreptococcus pneumoniae
BHaemophilus influenza
CNeisseria meningitides
DStaphylococcus aureus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Staphylococcus aureus

Splenic filtration may be particularly important for removal of microorganisms for which the host does not have a specific antibody. The immune functions of the spleen become evident after splenectomy, when patients are noted to be at risk for specific infections related to encapsulated bacteria Streptococcus pneumoniae, Haemophilus influenza, and Neisseria meningitidis. Asplenia and hyposplenism could be a result of surgical absence of spleen or lack of functioning of an anatomically present spleen. The most serious sequela is overwhelming postsplenectomy infection (OPSI). Reference : Bailey & Love's short practice of surgery 28 th Edition, Chapter 70, The Spleen, Pg: 1231.
