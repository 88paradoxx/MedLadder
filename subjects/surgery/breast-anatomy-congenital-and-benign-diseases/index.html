Sample Breast - Anatomy, Congenital and Benign Diseases MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following statement about breast tissue is false?

AFibrous stroma is absent beneath the areola
BAlveolar epithelium is columnar in the resting phase
C15-20 lactiferous ducts open in the nipple
DMyoepitheliocytes facilitates the passage of the milk along the ducts
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Alveolar epithelium is columnar in the resting phase

Alveolar epithelium is cuboidal in the resting phase, and columnar during lactation. The fibrous stroma forms septa (suspensory ligaments of Cooper) which anchor the skin and gland to the pectoral fascia. It forms the major part of the gland but is absent beneath the areola and nipple. Reference: Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Upper Limb Page number - 749

Sample MCQ #2

In lymphatic drainage of the breast, the major portion (about 75%) enters eventually into which group of nodes?

ADeltopectoral
BCentral Axillary
CLateral Axillary
DParasternal
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Central Axillary

About 75% of the lymph draining the breast goes to the axillary lymph nodes, via the pectoral lymph nodes. All of this lymph from the pectoral lymph nodes must drain to the central lymph nodes as well. This is why it is so important to examine all these groups of axillary lymph nodes when performing a breast exam. Most of the rest of the lymph drainage from the breast goes to the parasternal nodes, although a small amount goes to the opposite breast and a small amount drains to the abdominal wall. Reference: Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Upper Limb Page number - 737

Sample MCQ #3

Which among the following flaps is not used in breast reconstruction surgery?

ALattisimus dorsi flap
BPerforator abdominal flap
CTransverse rectus abdominis flap
DPectoralis major flap
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Pectoralis major flap

Autologous tissue reconstruction in breast surgery is done using the following muscle flaps- Latissimus dorsi flap - based on the thoracodorsal pedicle Muscle-preserving perforator abdominal flaps - Ex: Deep inferior epigastric perforator flap Transverse rectus abdominis flap - based on superior or inferior epigastric artery or both. Reference : Love & Bailey short practice of surgery, chapter: 53, Edition: 27th, Pg: 879
