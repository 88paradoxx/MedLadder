Sample Benign Lesions of Thyroid MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A 27-year-old female presents with a thyroid swelling. Which of the following symptoms is unlikely to occur due to compression of adjacent structures?

AHoarseness of voice due to recurrent laryngeal nerve compression
BDysphagia due to oesophagus compression
CSuperior vena cava syndrome
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. None of the above

Explanation: Thyroid enlargement can compress adjacent structures, producing multiple clinical manifestations. Hoarseness of voice due to recurrent laryngeal nerve compression (Option A): The recurrent laryngeal nerve runs posteromedial to the thyroid. Compression may cause hoarseness , vocal cord paresis, or paralysis. Dysphagia due to esophagus compression (Option B): The esophagus lies medial and posterior to the thyroid. Compression can lead to difficulty swallowing or dysphagia. Superior vena cava syndrome (Option C): Although the SVC is located in the superior mediastinum and is rarely affected, large substernal goiters or massive thyroid enlargements can rarely cause SVC compression, leading to facial and upper limb edema, dyspnea, and facial plethora.

Sample MCQ #2

In a 35-years old pregnant female with hyperthyroidism, which antithyroid drug should be given in the first trimester?

AMethimazole
BCarbimazole
CPropylthiouracil
DLugol's iodine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Propylthiouracil

Antithyroid drugs Those in common use are carbimazole and propylthiouracil : these are ANTI-TPO drugs. Antithyroid drugs are used to restore the patient to a euthyroid state and to maintain this for a prolonged period in the hope that a permanent remission will occur, i.e. that production of TSH-RAb will diminish or cease. Propylthiouracil has no teratogenicity effect. Antithyroid drugs cannot cure a toxic nodule. The overactive thyroid tissue is autonomous and recurrence of the hyperthyroidism is certain when the drug is discontinued. Anti-thyroids in pregnancy: 1 st trimester: propylthiouracil is used (carbimazole not used because it's teratogenic and may cause aplasia cutis) 2 nd trimester onwards: carbimazole (propylthiouracil not used throughout pregnancy due to risk of hepatitis)

Sample MCQ #3

Which among the following radioactive substance is not used in the thyroid scintigraphy technique?

ATechnicium 99
BRadiolabelled 123 iodine
CRadiolabelled 131 iodine
DRadiolabelled 139 iodine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Radiolabelled 139 iodine

Thyroid scintigraphy is performed using either technetium-99m pertechnetate or a radiolabelled iodine nuclide (typically 123I or 131I). Depending on the specific study, the tracer can be administered intravenously, orally, or via inhalation, and images are obtained anywhere from 30 minutes (for intravenous [IV] injection) to 24 hours (for oral administration) afterward. The thyroid normally displays symmetric uptake bilaterally. Nodular disease can be classified into whether there is increased focal uptake (the so-called "hot" nodule) or not (the "cold" nodule). Classically, "hot" nodules in the setting of hyperthyroidism are thought to be benign toxic nodules with extremely low risk of malignancy, whereas "cold" nodules carry a 15% risk of malignancy. Reference : Sabiston textbook of Surgery, chapter: 37, Edition: 21 st , Pg: 882
