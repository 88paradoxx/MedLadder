Sample MCQ #1

A 22-year-old woman with a history of migraine is prescribed a progestin-only pill. What is the main contraceptive mechanism of action of this drug?

ADecreases tubal motility
BInhibits ovulation
CCreates an unfavorable endometrium for implantation
DThickens cervical mucus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Thickens cervical mucus

Progestin-only pills are useful for women who are unable to take regular oral contraceptives that contain estrogen due to their side-effects or to the exacerbation of existing conditions like migraine. Daily intake thickens the cervical mucus thus preventing the entry of sperm. The dose of progesterone is not sufficient to inhibit ovulation or reduce luteinising hormone (LH) secretion. Although it decreases tubal motility, this is more related to the occurrence of ectopic pregnancy. It may make the endometrium out-of-phase but not unfavorable for implantation. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 3034

Sample MCQ #3

All of the following are localized variety of Basal cell carcinoma except?

ANodular
BNodulocystic
CMultifocal
DNaevoid
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Multifocal

BCC can be divided into localised (nodular; nodulocystic; cystic; pigmented and naevoid) and generalised (superficial: multifocal and superficial spreading; or infiltrative: morphoeic, ice pick and cicatrizing). Nodular and nodulocystic variants account for 90% of BCC. Typical naevi can be sporadic or familial (familial atypical multiple mole-melanoma [FAMMM] syndrome). Possession of more than five lesions confers a relative risk of melanoma six times greater than usual; within FAMMM syndrome, they confer a 100% risk of malignant melanoma and patients with FAMMM syndrome should be screened for melanoma 6-monthly, lifelong. Reference: Bailey & Love's short practice of surgery 28th Edition, Chapter 45 Pathophysiology of the skin and subcutaneous tissues, Pg: 651-652
