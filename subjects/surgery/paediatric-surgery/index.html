Sample MCQ #1

A 16-year-old boy was brought to the emergency ward with wheeze and an extensive rash after eating at a Chinese restaurant. On examination, he had extensive wheezes in his chest, stridor, as well as urticaria covering his upper and lower limbs. His BP is 82/50 mmHg. What is the most likely diagnosis?

AC1 Esterase Deficiency
BIdiopathic Urticaria
CBronchitis
DAllergy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Allergy

The scenario is consistent with a food allergy, e.g. nuts, leading to an anaphylactic reaction. The patient should be treated with hydrocortisone (IV or IM) as well as antihistaminic drugs. Hereditary angioedema is an autosomal dominant condition caused by C1 esterase deficiency which leads to recurrent attacks of angioedema Bronchitis presents with a productive cough, fever and dyspnoea. It is usually caused by a viral infection. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal medicine, 21st Edition, Page Number-2728.

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following defines sepsis according to SEPSIS-3 criteria?

ASuspected or documented infection & increase in any two of the SOFA points
BSuspected or documented infection plus vasopressor therapy needed to maintain MAP>65 mmHg and serum lactate >2.0mmol/L despite adequate fluid restriction
CSuspected or documented infection plus two SIRS criteria
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Suspected or documented infection & increase in any two of the SOFA points

Sepsis is defined by SEPSIS-3 criteria as suspected or documented infection and an acute increase in >2 sepsis-related organ failure assessment (SOFA) points. Documented or suspected infection + Need for vasopressors to maintain MAP more than or equal to 65mmHg and elevated lactate (>2 mmol/L ) defines the septic shock according to SEPSIS -3 criteria.SIRS criteria were included in earlier criteria (SEPSIS I & II). Reference: Harrison's principles of internal medicine 20th Ed chap 297, pg.2045

Sample MCQ #3

All electrolyte abnormalities are seen in immediate postoperative period except:

ANegative nitrogen balance
BHypokalemia
CGlucose intolerance
DHyponatremia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Hyponatremia

Option A : Due to increased catabolism in response to surgical stress there will be Negative nitrogen balance occuring. Option B : There is frequently shifting of potassium into cells as well as losses occur in postoperative period that leads to hypokalemia. Option C : Following surgical stress there will be increased glucose production but decreased glucose utilization at tissue level due to insulin resistance, that leads to glucose intolerance. Option D : Following surgery there will be shifting of fluid into extracellular space seen that leads to Hypernatremia. Reference: Bailey & Love's short practice of surgery, chapter: 1, Edition: 28 th
