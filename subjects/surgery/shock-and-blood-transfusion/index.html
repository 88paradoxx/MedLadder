Sample MCQ #4

All of the following statements are true except:

AIn patient with active bleeding due to major trauma first step should be controlling Bleeding rather than starting high fluid therapy.
BIn patient with Bowel obstruction first step should be to resuscitate a patient
CIn patient with active bleeding due to major trauma first step always be fluid therapy.
DAirway and breathing must be ensured in every patient presenting with shock.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. In patient with active bleeding due to major trauma first step always be fluid therapy.

In patients who are actively bleeding (major trauma, aortic aneurysm rupture, gastrointestinal hemorrhage), it is counterproductive to institute high-volume fluid therapy without controlling the site of hemorrhage. Increasing blood pressure merely increases bleeding from the site while fluid therapy cools the patient and dilutes available coagulation factors. Thus operative hemorrhage control should not be delayed and resuscitation should proceed in parallel with surgery. Conversely, a patient with bowel obstruction and hypovolemic shock must be adequately resuscitated before undergoing surgery otherwise the additional surgical injury and hypovolemia induced during the procedure will exacerbate the inflammatory activation and increase the incidence and severity of end-organ insult. Reference: Bailey & Love's short practice of surgery, chapter: 2, Edition: 28th

Sample MCQ #5

In case of blood loss, ideal replacement fluid is?

ABlood products
BRinger lactate
C0.9% saline
D20% Albumin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Blood products

In case of blood loss, blood products are the ideal replacement fluids as crystalloids and colloids have no oxygen carrying capacity and also don't contain coagulation factors, RBCs etc. Reference: Bailey & Love's short practice of surgery, chapter: 2, Edition: 28th, Pg: 21

Sample MCQ #7

When evaluating a patient in shock, a fluid challenge is often given to assess responsiveness to volume expansion. This involves administering a small but adequate bolus of fluid and observing the hemodynamic response. What is the commonly recommended bolus volume to assess fluid responsiveness?

A150 ml
B100 ml
C250 ml
D200 ml
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 250 ml

Explanation: A 250 ml fluid bolus is the standard amount used to assess fluid responsiveness in shock. This volume is large enough to produce measurable hemodynamic changes, yet small enough to avoid fluid overload, especially in critically ill or borderline patients. It is used in both emergency and critical care settings. Fluid challenge principles: Quickly determine whether the patient is "fluid responsive" Typically uses 250 ml crystalloid bolus Response assessed by: Blood pressure improvement Heart rate reduction Improvement in perfusion (urine output, mental status) Ultrasound assessment of IVC or stroke volume variation A positive response suggests hypovolemia, while lack of response indicates need for vasopressors or search for other causes of shock.
