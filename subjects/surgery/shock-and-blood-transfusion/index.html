Sample Shock and Blood Transfusion MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A child was brought to the ER with a lesion on the forearm as seen. As a surgery resident, you decide to excise the lesion in emergency ot. Which of the following investigations will test for the entire coagulation?

AaPTT
BPT
CCT
DThromboelastography
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Thromboelastography

Thromboelastography can be used for guiding intra operative transfusion. But it cant be employed in other disorders of bleeding and thrombosis. It has some role in venom induced coagulopathy

Sample MCQ #2

What would be maintenance fluid rate in case of shock in a man with 65 kgs of weight?

A55 ml/hour
B120 ml/hour
C75 ml/hour
D105 ml/hour
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 105 ml/hour

For patient weighting more than 40 kgs simple rule to calculate maintenance water is = weight in kgs +40 So, in our patient maintenance fluid would be 65 + 40=105 ml/hour. Reference: Sabiston textbook of Surgery, chapter: 4, Edition: 21 st

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is not the complication of massive transfusion?

AHypocalcemia
BHypomagnesemia
CHypokalemia
DHyperkalemia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Hypomagnesemia

Complications from massive transfusion Complications from massive transfusion include: coagulopathy; hypocalcaemia; hyperkalaemia; hypokalaemia; hypothermia.
