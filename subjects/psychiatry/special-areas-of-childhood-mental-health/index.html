Sample MCQ #1

A 25 year old female is presented to OPD with complaints of agitation, muttering to herself and sleep disturbances. She claims that her husband has stolen the baby from her womb. She has delivered a healthy child three weeks back, however has not taking care of the baby. What is the most probable diagnosis?

APostpartum Blues
BPostpartum Depression
CPostpartum Psychosis
DMania
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Postpartum Psychosis

Nihilistic delusion is associated postpartum and duration is around 3 weeks. Diagnosis is Postpartum psychosis. Reference: Kaplan and Saddocks Synopsis of Psychiaitry, 11th Edition, Chapter 8, Page No.: 357

Sample MCQ #2

Which one of the following is NOT a poor prognostic factor in OCD?

AMale gender
BPoor insight
CEarly onset
DFamily history of OCD
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Family history of OCD

It is observed that about 20&ndash;30% of those with OCD show a significant improvement, 40&ndash;50% have a moderate improvement, while the rest have a chronic or worsening course. Poor prognostic factors include yielding to compulsions, a childhood onset , male , bizarre content of compulsions, need for hospitalization , a coexisting major depressive disorder , the presence of overvalued ideas , and the presence of personality disorder (especially schizotypal). (Option A, B & C) A good prognosis is indicated by good premorbid adjustment, the presence of a precipitating event, and episodic nature. The content or theme of obsessions and family history do not relate to the prognosis . Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 10, page no. 420

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is/are leading cause of death among 15-24 years old?

AUnintentional injury
BHomicide
CCancer
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Unintentional injury and homicide were the leading causes of death among residents ages 15-24, each accounting for 34.2% and 32.9%, respectively, of all deaths. These were followed by suicide (9.9%) and cancer (5.8%). Accidents (unintentional injuries), homicide, suicide, cancer, and heart disease make up the five leading causes of death for teenagers Reference : https://cchealth.org/health-data/hospital-council/2010/pdf/11_lcd_by_age.pdf
