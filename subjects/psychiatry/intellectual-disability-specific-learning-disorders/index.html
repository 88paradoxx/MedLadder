Sample MCQ #1

Fetal alcohol syndrome is a known preventable cause of intellectual disability. Which of the following is true about alcohol use in pregnant women?

AAny amount of alcohol use is not recommended during pregnancy
BUp to 3 standard drinks per day in pregnancy is not causative for fetal alcohol syndrome
CMore than 7 standard drinks per week in pregnancy can lead to fetal alcohol syndrome
DOccasional use of alcohol during pregnancy is not harmful
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Any amount of alcohol use is not recommended during pregnancy

The National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism defines at-risk alcohol use for healthy nonpregnant women as > 3 drinks per occasion or > 7 drinks per week and any amount of drinking for women who are pregnant or at risk of pregnancy. Reference: Alcohol Use in Pregnancy (nih.gov)

Sample MCQ #2

Deciding to take an antacid before indulging in a spicy meal to avoid gastric distress is an example of which form of learning?

AClassical conditioning
BPositive reinforcement
CNegative reinforcement
DPunishment
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Negative reinforcement

Negative reinforcement refers to giving a stimulus or event , which when its cessation or termination is contingent on a response, increases the likelihood that the response will occur again . Avoidance learning is seen in negative reinforcement , where to avoid an adverse event, the stimuli is avoided. For example, to avoid gastric distress, antacid is taken before eating a spicy meal. Reference : Morgan and King's Introduction to Psychology, 7th edition, Chapter 4 Principles of Learning, Page number 149.

Sample MCQ #3

A 25-year-old male, who once got trapped in an elevator, has now started avoiding all small enclosed spaces like other elevators, closed rooms, bathrooms, etc. for the fear that something embarrassing might happen to him there. The principle of Pavlovian conditioning which explains this is known as:

AStimulus discrimination
BStimulus generalisation
CExtinction
DAversion
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Stimulus generalisation

A person or animal tends to generalize the conditioned response to other stimuli that are somewhat similar to the original conditioned stimuli. The greater the similarity , the greater the generalisation among conditioned stimuli. Hence, in the above case scenario, where the person is phobic to all small enclosed spaces, which are like his original conditioned stimuli of being trapped in an elevator , is suggestive of stimulus generalisation. Reference : Morgan and King's Introduction to Psychology, 7th edition, Chapter 4 Principles of Learning, Page number 146.

Sample MCQ #4

Students are often encouraged to perform better by giving medals and trophies for their achievement in academics or other extracurricular activities. This form of learning which modulates behavior is known as:

AClassical conditioning
BObservational learning
CPositive reinforcement
DCognitive learning
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Positive reinforcement

Positive reinforcer is a stimulus or event , which, when it follows a response, increases the likelihood that the response will be made again. For example, receiving medals or trophies, praise from parents, etc. can act as positive reinforcers to enhance performance of students. Reference : Morgan and King's Introduction to Psychology, 7th edition, Chapter 4 Principles of Learning, Page number 149.

Sample MCQ #5

A 7-year-old boy with severe intellectual disability was evaluated when he transferred to a new private school. Observed in his classroom, when he was prompted to engage in tasks that required that he take his hands out of his pockets, he began hitting his head with his hands. If his hands were held by the teacher, he hit his head with his knees. He was adept in contorting himself, so that he could hit or kick himself in almost any position, even while walking. Such behaviour is least likely with:

ALesch Nyhan syndrome
BSmith Magenis syndrome
CPrader Willi Syndrome
DTurner syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Turner syndrome

Genetic syndromes associated with self-injurious behaviour are Lesch Nyhan syndrome, Rett syndrome, Prader-Willi syndrome, Smith-Magenis syndrome and Oculocerebral syndrome of Lowe. Reference: Self-injurious behaviour as part of genetic syndromes - PubMed(nih.gov)
