Sample MCQ #1

A 29-year-old male CT3 psychiatric trainee was put down badly by his consultant in a ward round. He couldn't say anything to the consultant when the consultant shouted at him. When he returned home, he became verbally abusive and aggressive towards his wife. What is the most likely phenomenon?

ADisplacement
BIdentification
CProjection
DReaction formation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Displacement

Anna Freud described 10 defence mechanisms in her work. Reaction formation projection and regression are three of them. In displacement, emotions or wishes are transferred from the original object or person to a more suitable substitute. ( Option A is CORRECT ) Reaction formation is seen in patients with obsessive&ndash;compulsive disorder . ( Option D ) Here the attitude is opposed to the oppressed wish and constitutes the opposite reaction Projection : unacceptable thoughts, emotions and feelings are projected on to some other person ( Option C ) Identification : a person takes the attributes of others to him or herself ( Option B ) Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, eleventh edition, chapter 4, page no. 161

Sample MCQ #2

Regarding the treatment of obsessive-compulsive disorder, which of the following is most correct?

ACognitive and behavioural strategies are more effective than serotonergic antidepressants
BGroup therapy is less effective than individual therapy
CMore frequent therapy sessions are associated with improved outcomes
DTreatment of any kind is more successful if compulsions are present
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Treatment of any kind is more successful if compulsions are present

Treatment is more effective if compulsions are present . Cognitive and behavioural therapies have similar effectiveness to SSRIs. (Option A) Likewise, group and individual therapies have similar success rates. (Option B) Patients with more bizarre obsessional thoughts are less likely to resist carrying out compulsive acts. The frequency of psychotherapy sessions has not been shown to influence outcome although duration of each session and the therapy overall is associated with better outcomes. (Option C) Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 10, page no. 423
