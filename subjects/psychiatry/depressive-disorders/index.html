Sample Depressive Disorders MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

What percentage of new mothers is believed to develop postpartum depression?

A<1%
B10-15%
C25-30%
D35-40%
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 10-15%

Postpartum depression is relatively common, occurring in about 10% to 15% of new mothers. Symptoms are indistinguishable from those characteristics of nonpsychotic major depression and usually develop insidiously over the 6 months following delivery. Some women , however, experience an acute onset of symptoms immediately after delivery and occasionally depression starts during pregnancy. Ambivalence toward the child and doubts about the mother's own parenting abilities are common, but the rate of suicide is low. Reference : Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh Edition, Chapter 27th, Psychiatry and Reproductive Medicine, Page no. 839.

Sample MCQ #2

What is the feature of atypical depression?

AThe individual shows psychotic symptoms that align with depressive themes
BThere is a marked decrease in sleep and a high level of energy
CMood brightens in response to positive events, with symptoms like weight gain and hypersomnia
DThe presence of severe agitation and distinct despair
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Mood brightens in response to positive events, with symptoms like weight gain and hypersomnia

Explanation: Atypical depression is defined by mood reactivity (mood improves with positive events) along with features like increased appetite/weight gain, hypersomnia, leaden paralysis, and rejection sensitivity.

Sample MCQ #3

All of the following symptoms are a common overlap in grief and Major Depressive Disorder, except:

ASleep impairment
BWeight loss
CWorthlessness
DIrritability
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Worthlessness

Symptoms like sadness, sleep impairment, appetite impairment and weight loss can be similar symptoms in both grief and MDD. (Option A, B & D) However, symptoms like worthlessness, hopelessness and suicidal ideation are infrequent in grief and if present, may be only in context of the bereaved person. Self-esteem (self-worth) is usually preserved in grief. Reference: Kaplan and Sadock&rsquo;s Synopsis of Psychiatry, Chapter 34 End of life issues, Page number 1356.

ALithium
BBuspirone
CHaloperidol
DFluoxetine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Fluoxetine

SSRI is the first line management for depression Reference: Kaplan and Saddocks Synopsis of Psychiaitry, 11th Edition, Clinical Practice Guideline for Depression, Chapter 8, Page No.: 375

Sample MCQ #5

What happens to the time between depressive episodes as a patient experience more episodes?

AIt increases
BIt decreases
CIt remains constant
DIt becomes unpredictable
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. It decreases

Explanation: As a patient experiences more depressive episodes, the time between episodes generally decreases and episodes become more frequent over time.

Sample MCQ #6

A young boy feels that dirt has hung onto him whenever he passes through the dirty street. He knows that there is actually no such thing after he has cleaned once but he is not satisfied and compelled to think so. Although he knows that this is completely irrational but he can't control his thoughts. It gives him anxiety and depressive mood. Which of the following is wrong about his condition?

AEgo dystonic
BSerotonin imbalance
CExposure therapy and response prevention can be supportive
DNo risk of depression
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. No risk of depression

This is a case of OCD. OCD patients can easily slip into depression as it is ego dystonic. Reference: Kaplan and Saddocks Synopsis of Psychiaitry, 11th Edition, Chapter 10, Page No.: 418

He complains that he doesn't get satisfied after washing his hands for one time. Although he knows that this is completely irrational but he can't control his thoughts. It gives him anxiety and depressive mood. Which of the following is wrong about his condition?

AEgo dystonic
BJudgement power is normal
CInsight is present
DReality testing is absent

✓ Correct Answer: D. Reality testing is absent

Clinical scenario depicts OCD. It's a neurotic condition and patient is intact with the reality. Reference: Kaplan and Saddocks Synopsis of Psychiaitry, 11th Edition, Chapter 10, Page No.: 421

Sample MCQ #8

All of the following delusional themes are mood congruent in a patient of Major Depressive Disorder, except:

ADelusion of guilt
BDelusion of poverty
CDelusion of nihilism
DDelusion of grandeur
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Delusion of grandeur

In depression , delusions of guilt, poverty, nihilism, reference are commonly seen to be mood-congruent However, grandiose delusions in which a person believes himself to be of supreme importance is commonly seen as a feature of mania and not depression Reference: Fish's Clinical Psychopathology, third edition, Chapter 4 Disorders of Thought and Speech, Page no. 51
