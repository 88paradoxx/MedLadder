ASigmund Freud
BCarl Jung
CAlfred Adler
DEric Erikson

✓ Correct Answer: C. Alfred Adler

Alfred Adler&rsquo;s individual psychology gives the concept of inferiority complex. And he also gave the concept of importance of birth order in personality structure . Reference : Morgan and King&rsquo;s Introduction to Psychology, 7 th edition, Chapter 14 Personality, Page number 572.

Sample MCQ #2

A 30 year male is over enthusiastic and sensation seeking. He loves to explore new places and dishes of different cities. He makes big list of trying bungee jumping and sky diving and planning to organize camping in a deep forest. His personality trait is considered as:

ALow in conscientiousness
BHigh in openness to experience
CExtrovert
DHigh in neuroticism

✓ Correct Answer: B. High in openness to experience

Openness to experience (High means adventurous) Conscientiousness Extraversion Agreeableness Neuroticism Reference: Kaplan and Saddocks Synopsis of Psychiaitry, 11th Edition, Chapter 22, Page No.: 761

Sample MCQ #3

Mr Mark, who was flying on spicejet. Suddenly he got up and started giving instruction to air hostesses to do pole dance. He instructed everyone in a melodramatic manner to clap for these ladies. He started behaving like a boss. One old man shouted on him and asked him to shut up and sit down. Mr Mark felt insulted and became very anxious. Which type of personality Mr Mark has?

ABorderline
BAnti-social
CNarcissistic
DSchizotypal
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Narcissistic

Excessive sense of self-importance and fragile emotion at the same time: Narcissistic PD. Reference: Kaplan and Saddocks Synopsis of Psychiaitry, 11th Edition, Chapter 22, Page No.: 753

Sample MCQ #4

An 18-year-old girl is brought by her parents with history of frequent episodes of irritability, suicidal threats and excessive emotionality on trivial issues. On asking the patient, she denies of having any symptoms and blames her parents for not raising her properly. She reports of feeling chronically empty from inside. All of the following defence mechanisms explain her symptoms except:

AActing out
BSomatisation
CDenial
DProjection
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Somatisation

The above case scenario depicts Cluster B personality disorder , Borderline Personality Disorder . In this disorder, defence mechanisms like denial, acting out, projection are frequently used. Reference : Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11th edition, Chapter 22 Personality Disorders, Page number 750

Sample MCQ #6

All of the following tools can be used to assess personality except:

ARorschach test
BTAT test
CMMSE
DMMPI
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. MMSE

Rorschach and TAT test &rarr; projective personality tests Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Inventory &rarr; pencil-and-paper method of assessing personality . Mini-Mental Status Examination (MMSE) &rarr; used to assess cognition and not personality . Reference : Morgan and King's Introduction to Psychology, 7th edition, Chapter 13 Psychological Assessment and testing, Page number 546.

Sample MCQ #7

A 20-year-old male presents with long-standing pattern of suspiciousness and mistrust on persons in general. In this case scenario, Paranoid personality disorder can be differentiated from Schizophrenia by:

AAbsence of hallucinations or formal thought disorder
BSocial withdrawal
CRestricted affect and diminished emotions
DUsing projection as primary defence mechanism
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Absence of hallucinations or formal thought disorder

Unlike in paranoid schizophrenia , patients with paranoid personality disorder do not have hallucinations or any formal thought disorder or disorganized speech However, in both Paranoid personality disorder and Schizophrenia, persons may appear socially withdrawn , lack friends and confidants , are unemotional and do not express emotions (restricted affect) In both, paranoid ideations are common , that is they interpret the world as threatening and believe intentions of others as malevolent , exploiting or deceiving In such paranoid ideations, defence mechanism of projection is often used in which unacknowledged feelings of self are attributed and projected onto others Reference: Kaplan and Sadock&rsquo;s Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 22, Personality Disorders, Page 744

Sample MCQ #8

Which of the following terms describe personality changes occurring after traumatic brain injury?

APseudo-anxious
BPseudo-psychopathic
CPseudo-psychotic
DPseudo-manic
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Pseudo-psychopathic

Two subsets of personality changes seen after traumatic brain injury: Pseudo-depressed personality: characterised by apathy and blunted affect Pseudo-psychopathic personality: characterised by disinhibition, egocentricity, and sexual inappropriateness. Reference: Kaplan and Sadock&rsquo;s Comprehensive Textbook of Psychiatry, 10 th edition, Chapter 2.5 Neuropsychiatric consequences of Traumatic Brain injury, Page number 1400.
