Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is a technique used in treatment of tic disorders?

AHabit reversal
BEye sensitisation
CSystemic desensitisation
DHypnosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Habit reversal

Habit reversal technique is used in management of tic disorders. It involves several components aimed at increasing tic awareness, developing competing responses to tics and building and sustaining motivation and compliance. Reference: Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11 th edition, Chapter 31.8c, Tourette's disorder, Page number 1202.

Sample MCQ #2

A 35-year-old married woman with a history of depressive disorder presents in the outpatient department complaining of loss of libido. Which antidepressant medication would be the most suitable to prescribe to address this side effect?

AFluoxetine
BPhenelzine
CMirtazapine
DVenlafaxine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Mirtazapine

Mirtazapine has a lower incidence of sexual side effects than most other antidepressants at 24%. SSRIs may indirectly decrease libido through a direct inhibition of sexual arousal and orgasm.(Option A) Tricyclic antidepressants may decrease libido indirectly through sedation. Venlafaxine can inhibit arousal and orgasm.(Option D) Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 29th, Page no. 993.

Sample MCQ #3

You get a call from the duty obstetrician about a 32-year-old woman who was 15 week's pregnant and her bipolar affective disorder was stabilized on lithium. Which of the following statements about use of lithium in pregnancy is correct?

ALithium is not excreted in breast milk.
BLithium use does not affect the fetus in the second trimester.
CSerum lithium levels increase in the third trimester of pregnancy.
DSerum lithium levels increase in the immediate postpartum period.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Serum lithium levels increase in the immediate postpartum period.

Lithium is excreted in breast milk , so it should be explained clearly to the mother about the effect of lithium in newborn babies.( OPTION A IS FALSE ) Lithium can affect the baby during the second and third trimesters. It can have toxic effects if serum lithium levels are very high and can also cause cardiac dysfunction, diabetes insipidus and hypothyroidism in the fetus.( OPTION B IS FALSE ) Lithium levels fall during the third trimester of pregnancy.( OPTION C IS FALSE) However, immediately after delivery, the serum concentration starts to rise , so lithium levels should be closely monitored.( CORRECT OPTION D) Lithium use is usually associated with Ebstein's anomaly of the tricuspid valve. Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 29th, Page no. 987.

Sample MCQ #6

A 45-year-old female, following a quarrel with her husband and children, presented with sudden loss of voice over for few hours. Though she would try to speak out hard, only whispering sounds would be heard. In this case, hysterical aphonia can be ruled out from other organic causes of aphonia by:

ANormal cough sound
BAbnormal structure of larynx
CNormal movement of vocal cords
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Normal cough sound

Hysterical aphonia ( Conversion disorder symptom ) is presented with sudden loss of voice which is usually precipitated by an acute psychological stressor In hysterical aphonia, though the patient can make only whispering sounds, however, on asking the patient to cough or laugh , he/ she can produce normal cough sound or laughter sound The structure of larynx is found to be the normal The movement of vocal cords may slightly be diminished Reference: Kaplan and Sadock&rsquo;s Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 13.4, Functional Neurological Symptom Disorder (Conversion Disorder), Page 476

Sample MCQ #7

A 3-year-old girl child had normal development until 18 month's age, after which regression of achieved milestones, deceleration of head circumference, hand wringing movements and impairment in intellectual functions and communication skills was observed. Which is the mode of inheritance of genetic abnormality in this girl?

AAutosomal dominant
BAutosomal recessive
CX linked dominant
DX linked recessive
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. X linked dominant

Rett syndrome, now diagnosed in the DSM-5 as a form of autism spectrum disorder, is believed to be caused by a dominant X-linked gene. It is degenerative and affects only females. Head circumference deceleration, deterioration in communications skills, motor behaviour and social functioning starts at about 1 year of age. Symptoms include ataxia, facial grimacing, teeth-grinding and loss of speech. Stereotypical hand movements, including hand-wringing, are typical. Reference: Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11 th edition, Chapter 31.3, Intellectual disability, Page number 1124.
