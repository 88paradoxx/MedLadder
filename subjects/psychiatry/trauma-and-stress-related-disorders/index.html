Sample Trauma and Stress-Related Disorders MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Which among these symptoms must be present to fulfil the criteria for PTSD according to DSM-V?

AIntrusion
BAvoidance
CHyperarousal
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

The 5th edition of DSM-V criteria for PTSD specify that the following symptoms must have lasted >1 month: Intrusion Avoidance Alterations of mood and cognition Hyperarousal Reference: Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11th Edition, Page Number-349.

Sample MCQ #2

A 40-year-old man was recently rescued by the fire department from his building which caught fire. He presented with flashbacks, repeated distressing memories and disturbed dreams of the event for about 20 days after the traumatic incident. Which of the following characteristics differentiate acute stress disorder from PTSD in his case?

ATime duration
BSeverity of stressor
CNumber of symptoms
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Time duration

According to DSM-5, acute stress disorder (ASD) and PTSD are differentiated by time duration If symptoms are present up to one month of exposure to traumatic event, it fulfils the criteria for ASD However, if symptoms occur more than a month, the criteria of PTSD is fulfilled In this question, the person developed symptoms of flashbacks, distressing memories, disturbed dreams for 20 days after experiencing the fire accident Hence, the diagnosis of ASD would be more appropriate Reference : Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11th edition, Chapter 11, Trauma and stressor related disorders, Page 439-441

Sample MCQ #3

In the context of Acute Stress Disorder, which of the following symptoms is not typically included?

AFlashbacks
BAutonomic symptoms of panic
CEmotional blunting
DDisorientation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Emotional blunting

Explanation: Emotional blunting is more commonly associated with PTSD, not typically listed as a symptom of Acute Stress Disorder. Acute Stress Disorder (ASD) Diagnostic Criteria (DSM-5) Symptoms occur 3 days to following exposure to trauma. Symptoms arise transiently in response to stress and usually resolve quickly. Required Symptoms: Presence of symptoms from intrusion, negative mood/cognition, dissociation, avoidance, or arousal categories. Nine symptoms needed from these categories. Symptoms Include: Cognitive "daze," narrowed attention, disorientation. Autonomic panic symptoms. May include partial or complete amnesia related to the event. Exclusions: Not due to substance use or other psychiatric disorders.

Sample MCQ #4

Which pharmacologic treatment is recommended for nightmares associated with PTSD?

ASertraline
BPrazosin
CFluoxetine
DAlprazolam
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Prazosin

Explanation: Prazosin is noted for its effectiveness in treating nightmares related to PTSD. Pharmacotherapy: Effective Medications: Sertraline and paroxetine are the most supported. Venlafaxine (SNRI), risperidone (atypical antipsychotic), and topiramate (anticonvulsant). Prazosin (for nightmares), while benzodiazepines lack supportive evidence and are often discouraged. Low-dose trazodone and the orexin antagonist suvorexant are used for insomnia

Sample MCQ #5

All of the following conditions are included in 'depressive disorders' section of DSM-5 except:

ADisruptive mood dysregulation disorder
BPremenstrual dysphoric disorder
CAcute stress disorder
DDysthymia

✓ Correct Answer: C. Acute stress disorder

Acute stress disorder is classified under trauma-and stressor related disorders in DSM-5. While other conditions like Disruptive mood dysregulation disorder, premenstrual dysphoric disorder, and dysthymia are classified under depressive disorder spectrum. Reference: Diagnostic and Statistical manual, 5 th edition.

Sample MCQ #7

In a patient of PTSD, a psychotherapeutic technique is practiced in which the patient focuses on the lateral movement of the clinician's finger while maintaining a mental image of the trauma experience. The general belief is that symptoms can be relieved as patients work through the traumatic event while in a state of deep relaxation. What is this technique called?

ASystematic Desensitization
BGuided imagery
CEye movement and desensitization reprocessing
DImplosive therapy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Eye movement and desensitization reprocessing

A variant of trauma-focused CBT for PTSD is called eye movement desensitization and reprocessing (EMDR) , in which an exposure and cognitive reprocessing interventions are paired with directed eye movements In this, the patient focuses on the lateral movement of the clinician's finger while maintaining a mental image of the trauma experience The general belief is that symptoms can be relieved as patients work through the traumatic event while in a state of deep relaxation Reference : Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11th edition, Chapter 11, Trauma and stressor related disorders, Page 445

Sample MCQ #8

Out of a 20-year-old girl having recently suffered sexual assault, a 25-year-old female fired from her job for her incompetence, a 30-year-old male whose father recently underwent a surgical procedure, and a 17-year-old boy having recently witnessed a car accident that killed his mother, which one of them are more prone to develop PTSD in terms of the stressor they went through?

AThose who faced stalking and job loss
BThose who suffered sexual assault and witnessed car accident
CThose who suffered sexual assault and witnessed surgical procedure
DThose who suffered job loss and witnessed surgical procedure
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Those who suffered sexual assault and witnessed car accident

PTSD occurs following exposure to a violent, accidental, traumatic event The stressor should be severe enough so as to endanger one&rsquo;s or close one&rsquo;s life or should be sufficiently overwhelming to affect almost everyone , e.g., sexual assault, directly experiencing or witnessing an accidental death, etc. Stressors such as job loss or a regular surgical procedure are not severe enough to cause PTSD Reference : Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11th edition, Chapter 11, Trauma and stressor related disorders, Page 437
