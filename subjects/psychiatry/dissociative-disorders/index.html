Sample Dissociative Disorders MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A 45-year-old divorced male bus driver was referred for psychiatric consultation for complaints of memory loss for the previous 12 years, behaving and responding to the environment as if it were 12 years previously, e.g., he didn't recognize his 8-year-old son, and denied recollection of current events like name of current president. On mental status examination, the patient displayed intact intellectual function and brain imaging was normal. The patient's mother described a family history of brutal beatings and physical discipline subjected towards the patient. Pick the correct diagnosis.

ADissociative amnesia
BPost-traumatic stress disorder
CMalingering
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Dissociative amnesia

The above case scenario is suggestive of dissociative amnesia. The main feature of dissociative amnesia is an inability to recall important autobiographical information , usually of a traumatic or stressful nature, that is inconsistent with ordinary forgetting. Dissociative amnesia can occur in post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) , however other symptoms should be present as well to fulfil the criteria for the same. (Option B) In malingering, there is usually a secondary gain. (Option C) Reference: Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11th edition, Chapter 12, Dissociative disorders, Page number 452.

Sample MCQ #2

All of the following are non-pathological forms of amnesia, except:

AChildhood amnesia
BDissociative amnesia
CHypnotic amnesia
DDream associated amnesia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Dissociative amnesia

Ordinary forgetfulness, infantile and childhood amnesia, amnesia for sleep and dreaming and hypnotic amnesia are non-pathological forms of amnesia. (Option A, C & D) Dissociative amnesia is pathological. Reference: Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11th edition, Chapter 12, Dissociative disorders, Page number 452.

Sample MCQ #3

Psychotherapeutic technique proven to be effective in dissociative identity disorder?

AHypnosis
BDialectical behavior therapy
CMindfulness based therapy
DGroup therapy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Hypnosis

Treatment option out of these, in dissociative disorders is hypnosis. Hypnosis can be useful for accessing specific alter personality states and their sequestered affects and memories. Hypnosis is also used to create relaxed mental states in which negative life events can be examined without overwhelming anxiety. Reference: Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11th edition, Chapter 12, Dissociative disorders, Page number 461.

Sample MCQ #4

A 21-year-old lady presented with history of episodes where she would behave as if possessed by some spirit. She would display extraordinary strength and become aggressive towards her family members, speak in foreign language and display bizarre behavior. She would always claim amnesia for the event. Pick the correct diagnosis for her condition.

ADissociative trance
BPossessive trance
CDissociative identity disorder
DDissociative amnesia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Possessive trance

Possession trance , involves single or episodic alternations in the state of consciousness , characterized by the exchange of the person's customary identity with a new identity usually attributed to a spirit, divine power, deity, or another person. In this possessed state, the individual exhibits stereotypical and culturally determined behaviors or experiences being controlled by the possessing entity. There must be partial or full amnesia of the event. The trance or possession state must not be a normally accepted part of a cultural or religious practice and must cause significant distress or functional impairment in one or more of the usual domains. Reference: Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11th edition, Chapter 12, Dissociative disorders, Page number 463.
