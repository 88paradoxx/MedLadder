Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following medications has the highest propensity to cause QT prolongation on ECG?

ARisperidone
BQuetiapine
CHaloperidol
DOlanzapine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Quetiapine

ECG abnormalities occur in approximately 25% of all patients on antipsychotics. The most commonly reported changes are the prolonged QT interval(suggestive of repolarization disturbances), depressed ST segments and abnormal T waves. Thioridazine, pimozide, quetiapine, sertindole and droperidol prolong QTC interval to a higher extent than other antipsychotics . This predisposes to a fatal form of arrhythmia called torsade de pointes (polymorphic ventricular arrhythmia) leading to sudden death . Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 29th, Page no. 1027.

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following is thought to best describe the receptor binding of aripiprazole?

ADopamine antagonist
BDopamine partial agonist
CGlutamate antagonist
DDopamine Serotonin antagonist
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Dopamine partial agonist

Aripiprazole is thought to differ from most other antipsychotics in that it is a partial agonist at dopamine D2 receptors rather than an antagonist. It is also thought to have 5HT2A antagonist and 5HT1A partial agonist. Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 29th, Page no. 1028.

Sample MCQ #5

Which of the following antipsychotic agents is least likely to prolong the QTc interval on ECG recording at therapeutic doses?

AAripiprazole
BChlorpromazine
CHaloperidol
DQuetiapine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Aripiprazole

Aripiprazole has no effect on the QTc interval. Chlorpromazine and Quetiapine are associated with a moderate effect on QTc .(Option B & D) Pimozide, Haloperidol and sertindole are drugs for which extensive QTc prolongation has been noted. (Option C) Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 29th, Page no. 1028.

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following antipsychotics is least likely to cause sedation?

AAripiprazole
BChlorpromazine
CClozapine
DPromazine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Aripiprazole

In comparison with other antipsychotics, aripiprazole has a very low relative incidence of sedation. All of the other antipsychotics like Chlorpromazine, Clozapine and Promazine are associated with high relative incidences of sedation .(Option B, C & D) Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 29th, Page no. 1028.

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following is a formal thought disorder?

ADelusions of persecution
BTangentiality
CThought broadcasting
DThought insertion
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Tangentiality

A formal thought disorder is considered to be a schizophrenic language disorder It characterised by disordered speech, and results from disorganised thoughts. It is often a symptom of schizophrenia, mania, severe depression with psychosis and other psychotic disorders. Tangentiality is a formal thought disorder in which there is tangential association of ideas leading to disruption in the smooth continuity of speech. Thought insertion, thought broadcasting, thought withdrawal and thought block are Schneider&rsquo;s first rank symptoms . (Option C & D) Delusion of persecution is a fixed belief in which patients believe that they are being followed, persecuted and spied on, and that the perceived persecutor will cause them harm. (Option A) Reference: PGNIP2, Cijo Alex, 2 nd Edition, Chapter 3, page no. 27

Sample MCQ #8

What is the recommended duration of antipsychotic maintenance treatment after a first episode of psychosis?

A3 months
B6 months
C1 year
DIndefinitely
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 1 year

Explanation: For patients after their first psychotic episode, antipsychotic maintenance therapy is recommended for at least one year to reduce relapse risk, longer or indefinite treatment may be needed after more episodes.
