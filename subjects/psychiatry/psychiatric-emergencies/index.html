Sample MCQ #1

A 27-year-old male CT2 psychiatric trainee was put down by his consultant in a ward round and the consultant shouted at him. After work, he went to the gym and exercised for half an hour more than usual. What is the most likely phenomenon?

AReaction formation
BRepression
CSublimation
DPassive Aggression
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Sublimation

This is the transformation of our instinctual energy to a more socially acceptable one . So, in this case, rather than shouting back at the consultant, the trainee went to the gym and transformed his anger and frustration into exercising . This is a mature defence mechanism. Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, eleventh edition, chapter 4, page no. 162

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following drugs is least likely to cause serotonin syndrome?

AVenlafaxine
BEcstasy(MDMA)
CFluoxetine
DOlanzapine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Olanzapine

Olanzapine is not associated with serotonin syndrome . Other drugs like Venlafaxine, Ecstasy and Fluoxetine have all been implicated in serotonin syndrome.(Option A, B & C) Ecstasy has been associated with serotonin syndrome in combination with venlafaxine. Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 29th, Page no. 1020.

Sample MCQ #3

24-year-old Miss D had come to the Psychiatric OPD with the c/o Unresponsive episodes characterized by clonic movements and weeping spells lasting for 1 hour followed by an interpersonal conflict with her boyfriend. Supportive counselling was provided and she was prescribed with few medications. Two months later, she began to develop galactorrhoea with irregular menstrual cycle. Which of the following medications is more likely to cause it?

AEscitalopram
BLevosulpiride
CClonazepam
DSertraline
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Levosulpiride

Levosulpiride is an atypical antipsychotic that blocks D2 receptors commonly prescribed in Conversion Disorder Considering the clinical presentation of the patient, all of the above medications can be provided to the patient but Levosulpiride is more prone to cause hyperprolactinemia leading to side effects like amenorrhoea, galactorrhoea, anorgasmia Escitalopram and Sertraline are SSRIs (Selective Serotonin Reuptake inhibitors) which may also lead to Hyperprolactinaemia but in much lesser propensity while Clonazepam is a Benzodiazepine lacking such effect Reference: Kaplan and Sadock&rsquo;s Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 29 Psychopharmacological Treatment, page no. 1014

Sample MCQ #4

27-year-old patient presents to the Psychiatric OPD with the chief complains of low mood with weeping spells, disinterest in her routine activities, early morning awakening, reduced appetite and suicidal ideation since last 15 days followed by the death of her husband. Patient is 4 months pregnant and unwilling to get admitted. Which of the following anti-depressant medication should be avoided in this patient?

ASertraline
BFluoxetine
CParoxetine
DEscitalopram
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Paroxetine

Amongst all the antidepressants mentioned, Paroxetine is Category D drug. Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors(SSRIs) are prone to cause teratogenicity in the form of - Heart defects like ASD, VSD in the first trimester Pulmonary Hypertension in the third trimester Should be cautiously used, avoiding Paroxetine altogether. Reference : Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 29th, Psychopharmacological treatment, Page no. 1016.

Sample MCQ #5

A 45-year-old man in the medical ward on linezolid antibiotics developed confusion, muscle rigidity and hyperthermia shortly after he was placed on citalopram for low mood. What is the most likely diagnosis?

ASerotonin syndrome
BDrug overdose
CAlcohol withdrawal state
DMetabolic disturbance
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Serotonin syndrome

Serotonin syndrome is a rare but serious condition characterised by excess serotonergic activity. Symptoms include confusion, muscle rigidity, hyperreflexia and GI upset . Other symptoms include hyperthermia and fluctuation of blood pressure. Linezolid is an antibiotic used in treatment of gram-positive bacteria. It is a weak monoamine oxidase inhibitor and interaction with selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor has been documented. Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 29th, Page no. 1020.

Sample MCQ #6

There is high risk of development of which psychiatric illness along with OCD?

APhobia
BPanic disorder
CDepression
DHypochondriasis

✓ Correct Answer: C. Depression

OCD is ego dystonic. There is high risk of development of depression. And both should be treated simultaneously. Reference: Kaplan and Saddocks Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11th Edition, Chapter 10, Page No.: 418

Sample MCQ #7

This syndrome presents with affected individuals being cortically blind but unaware of it and hence deny they have a problem. Patients start falling over furniture as they can't see. However, they believe they can still see and describe their environments in detail but are wrong in their description. Identify this syndrome.

AWallenberg syndrome
BGerstmann syndrome
CBalint syndrome
DAnton syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Anton syndrome

Anton's syndrome (Anton-Babinski syndrome): Occurs following damage to the occipital lobe. Affected individuals are cortically blind but are unaware of this and deny they have a problem (Anosognosia). It often presents as the patient starts falling over furniture as they can't see. Affected individuals believe they can still see and describe their environments in detail but are wrong in their description (confabulation). Reference: Kaplan and Sadock&rsquo;s Synopsis of Psychiatric, Chapter 1.2 Functional Neuroanatomy, Page number 6.

Sample MCQ #8

Which statement accurately reflects the psychiatric considerations in patients with epilepsy?

APsychosis is more commonly observed during the ictal phase of seizures than during the interictal phase.
BMood disorders, such as depression, are rarely seen in patients with epilepsy
CIncreased religiosity and personality changes are often noted in patients with temporal lobe epilepsy
DMost patients with epilepsy do not require any psychological support
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Increased religiosity and personality changes are often noted in patients with temporal lobe epilepsy

Explanation: Personality disorders, mood disorders, and psychotic symptoms may be more common in patients with epilepsy, particularly those with temporal lobe epilepsy. Psychotic symptoms are generally more prevalent during the interictal period rather than the ictal phase. Ictal symptoms are typically characterised by disorganised behaviour rather than psychosis (Option A) . Mood disorders, including depression and anxiety, are more common in patients with epilepsy than in the general population. This can significantly affect the patient's quality of life (Option B) . Many patients with epilepsy benefit from psychological support and counselling to address the psychosocial implications of their diagnosis, including coping with chronic illness and managing stigma (Option D) .
