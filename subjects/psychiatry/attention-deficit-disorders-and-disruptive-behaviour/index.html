Sample MCQ #1

A child with untreated ADHD is most likely to develop which of the following disorders during adolescence?

ABinge eating
BSelective mutism
CSeparation from parents leading to depression
DConduct disorder
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Conduct disorder

Explanation: Untreated Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) in childhood is associated with an increased risk of developing Conduct Disorder during adolescence. Children with ADHD often display impulsivity, aggression, and difficulty following rules, which may escalate into more severe behavioural problems, including violating the rights of others and engaging in antisocial behaviour . Explanation for other options: Binge eating (Option A) is not typically associated with untreated ADHD, although some individuals with ADHD may engage in emotional eating. Selective mutism (Option B) is more commonly associated with anxiety disorders and is not directly linked to ADHD. Separation from parent leading to depression (Option C) is not a common progression from ADHD, though emotional difficulties can arise, especially in children with comorbid conditions

Sample MCQ #2

A 50-year-old homeless male veteran is brought to the emergency department by the police for disruptive behaviour. On mental status examination, he has an elevated affect, but also has psychomotor agitation and paranoia; he says he "feels fantastic" but is wary of answering any questions, quickly becoming irritated. On physical examination, the patient exhibits a moderately elevated blood pressure and pulse rate. He is most likely intoxicated with which of the following substances?

AAlcohol
BBarbiturates
CBenzodiazepines
DCocaine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Cocaine

Cocaine(or other stimulants such as amphetamine) intoxication can present with Euphoria Irritability Anxiety Psychotic symptoms such as paranoia Elevated vital signs Conversely, intoxication with alcohol, barbiturates, benzodiazepines and opiates generally causes depression, somnolence and depressed vital signs(OPTIONS A, B, & C). Reference: Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 20.9, Stimulant-related disorders, Page no. 675.

Sample MCQ #3

In a delirious patient, disturbances in attention increase towards the evening time due to sensory deprivation. This is known as:

AClouding of consciousness
BTwilight state
CSundowning
DMoonlighting
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Sundowning

In delirious state, the patient becomes more disturbed, disoriented, hyper vigilant towards the evening after sunset due to sensory deprivation. This is known as sun downing. Reference: Kaplan and Sadock&rsquo;s Comprehensive Textbook of Psychiatry, 10 th edition, Chapter 10.2 Delirium, Page number 3062.

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following is true regarding the clinical presentation of OCD?

AAcute onset
BEarly presentation to clinic
CLong duration of untreated illness
DChronic deteriorating course
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Long duration of untreated illness

Patients with obsessions usually harbour their difficulties for a long time (often 5 to10 years) before they present to a doctor . (Option A & B) The ego-dystonic nature of obsessions results in anxiety and reduces help seeking and sharing of their secret illness. OCD has its origin in adolescence or childhood . But most patients do not seek help until they are in their twenties or thirties . Often the onset of depression brings OCD to clinical attention. Reference: Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 10, page no. 423

Sample MCQ #5

All of the following Disruptive and Impulse control disorders are seen in adults except?

AIntermittent Explosive Disorder
BOppositional defiant disorder
CKleptomania
DPyromania
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Oppositional defiant disorder

Intermittent Explosive disorder, Kleptomania and Pyromania are classified as Disruptive and Impulse Control disorders seen in adulthood. Whereas Oppositional defiant disorder and Conduct disorder are seen in childhoodor adolescence . Reference: Kaplan and Sadock&rsquo;s Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11 th edition, Chapter 19 Disruptive, Impulse control, and Conduct disorders, Page number 608.

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following is the most critical criterion for diagnosing gender dysphoria in children, according to the DSM-5?

AStrong preference for clothing typical of another gender
BInsistence on being another gender or desire to be another gender
CDislike of one's sexual anatomy
DDesire for primary or secondary sexual characteristics of the desired gender
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Insistence on being another gender or desire to be another gender

Explanation: The DSM-5 emphasizes the child's self-perception and the desire or insistence that one is another gender as the most critical criterion for gender dysphoria in children. Other behaviours (like preference for opposite-gender clothing or playmates) are supportive but not primary diagnostic criteria. Gender Dysphoria In Children: Key Diagnostic Criterion: The most critical factor for diagnosing gender dysphoria in children is desire or insistence on being another gender. Behavioural Characteristics: Preference for clothing, toys, and games typically associated with the opposite gender. Desire to take on roles of the opposite gender during play. Rejection of same-gender stereotypes and preference for other-gender playmates. Dislike of their own sexual anatomy or a desire to have the sexual characteristics of the desired gender.

Sample MCQ #7

All of the following are sub-types of Oppositional Defiant disorder except:

AAngry-Irritable
BVindictive
CCrying
DArgumentative
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Crying

DSM-5 has divided oppositional defiant disorder into three types: Angry/Irritable Mood Argumentative/ Defiant Behaviour Vindictiveness Crying is not a sub-type of Oppositional Defiant Disorder. Reference : Kaplan and Sadock&rsquo;s Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11 th edition, Chapter 31.12 Oppositional Defiant Disorder, Page number 1244.

Sample MCQ #8

All of the following themes are usually present in children with conduct disorder except:

ALack of remorse/ guilt for their actions
BLack of empathy for others
CLack of social emotions
DLack of proper communication skills
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Lack of proper communication skills

Individuals with conduct disorder can exhibit a persistent interpersonal and emotional pattern that can be characterized by at least two of the following: Lack of remorse or guilt Callous lack of empathy Unconcerned about performance Shallow or deficient affect. Communication skills, however, are not lacking in those with conduct disorder. Reference : Kaplan and Sadock&rsquo;s Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11 th edition, Chapter 31.12 Conduct disorder, Page number 1247.
