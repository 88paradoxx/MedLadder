Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following is an adverse effect of an antidepressant drug, k/as a selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor?

ASexual dysfunction
BDry mouth
CBlurring of vision
DConstipation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Sexual dysfunction

Sexual dysfunction is a well-documented adverse effect of SSRIs. These drugs can cause various sexual problems, such as decreased libido, delayed ejaculation or difficulty achieving orgasm. This adverse effect can significantly impact a patient's quality of life. Other options: B.Dry mouth - Dry mouth is a common side effect of many medications, including some antidepressants, but it is not specific to SSRIs. C.Blurring of vision - Blurred vision is not a commonly reported side effect of SSRIs. D.Constipation - Constipation is also not a common side effect of SSRIs. Key Takeaway: Sexual dysfunction is a common and well-documented adverse effect of SSRIs , which can impact adherence to treatment.
