Sample MCQ #1

DNA finger printing cannot be taken from ?

ASaliva
BTooth
CBuccal mucosa
DBlood
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Tooth

Ans. is 'b' i.e., Tooth [Ref Parikh 6th le p. 7.2-7.15] DNA finger-printing/ DNA profiling Is a technique employed by forensic scientists to assist in the identification of individuals by their respective DNA profile it is a fool proof method to conclusively fix the paternity/ maternity. The most desirable method of collecting a reference sample is the use of a buccal swab. When this is not available (e.g. because a court order may be needed and not obtainable) other methods may need to be used to collect a sample of blood, saliva, semen, or other appropriate fluid or tissue from personal items (e.g. toothbrush, razor, etc.) or from stored samples (e.g. banked sperm or biopsy tissue)

Sample MCQ #2

A vegetable vendor with no prior criminal record went missing and was discovered dead after a week with advanced decomposition. What methods would be used to identify the body?

AGeneral recognition
BDNA profiling
CDental records
DFingerprints
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. DNA profiling

Explanation: DNA profiling is a highly reliable method for identifying a body, even when it is in an advanced state of decomposition. Explanation for other options: General Recognition (Option A): Not reliable due to significant alteration of features from decomposition. Dental Records (Option C): May not be available for a vegetable vendor, making this method less practical. Fingerprints (Option D): More dependable if the deceased has a criminal record; otherwise, fingerprints may not be useful. Reference: https://www.fortunejournals.com/ijabpt/pdf/63045-II-Mahesh%20Singh-forensic%20dentistry[1].pdf

Sample MCQ #3

Identical twins have similarities in all the following except:

ABlood group
BDNA fingerprinting
CFingerprint pattern
DIris colour
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Fingerprint pattern

Quetelet's rule: Every nature-made object shows infinite variations of forms, and in the world&rsquo;s, crime records no two identical fingerprint patterns have been reported. Hence, it is unique to every individual. Fingerprint pattern differs even in identical twins. Blood group, DNA fingerprinting and iris color are same in identical twins Reference: Textbook of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology, Anil Aggrawal, Page No. 104

Sample MCQ #4

In tattoos, which of the following colours is least likely to fade over time?

ARed
BGreen
CBlack
DPurple
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Black

Black areas in a tattoo are the least likely to fade over years. This is because the black pigment (carbon particles) are more resistant than the other pigments to transportation by the lymphatic system. Thus, they tend to persist in the skin. Colors such as blue, green, or red are scavenged by tissue cells and leached into the lymphatic system after a number of years or decades. Light colors such as white, pink, and blue fade faster. Reference : Knight's Forensic Pathology, 4th edition, Page N0. 98

Sample MCQ #7

Identical twins will not have same

AFingerprint pattern
BDNA fingerprint pattern
CHLA system
DBlood group
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Fingerprint pattern

Identical twins have the same DNA fingerprint pattern, HLA system and Blood group. But the fingerprints pattern is different in identical twins, that's why it's the best method of identification. Reference: The Essentials of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology, KS Narayan Reddy, 35th edition, Page No. 62
