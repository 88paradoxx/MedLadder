Sample MCQ #1

A woman found comatose in her house is life-flighted to the nearest regional medical center. Blood gases reveal a normal PaO2 but a lower than normal arterial O2 saturation. Which of the following conditions is most consistent with the findings?

AAnemia
BCarbon monoxide poisoning
CLow V/Q ratio
DHypoventilation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Carbon monoxide poisoning

Hemoglobin has 210 x greater affinity for carbon monoxide than for oxygen. Thus, in carbon monoxide poisoning, the amount of dissolved oxygen, as reflected by the PaO2 may be normal, but the saturation of hemoglobin with oxygen will be lower than expected for a given PaO2. V/Q mismatch with low V/Q units, hypoventilation, and right-to-left shunting are all causes of hypoxemia (decreased PaO2). In anemia, hemoglobin concentration is reduced, but the saturation of hemoglobin O2 is normal. Reference: Ganong, pp 683-691. Levitzky, pp 180-183.

Sample MCQ #2

What is the cause of death in smoke inhalation?

AHistotoxic hypoxia
BIschemic hypoxia
CAnaemic hypoxia
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Anaemic hypoxia

Victims of smoke inhalation are affected by carbon monoxide poisoning . In carbon monoxide poisoning, the cause of death is anaemic hypoxia Carbon monoxide has a higher affinity to haemoglobin compared to oxygen and hence the amount of haemoglobin available for oxygen transport is markedly reduced. It displaces oxygen from its combination with haemoglobin and forms a relatively stable compound known as carboxyhemoglobin . It thus reduces the oxygen content of the blood, and hence, that of the tissues. It acts as a chemical asphyxiant and produces death due to anaemic anoxia. References: Textbook of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology: Principles & Practice 5th edition Page no: 113; The Essentials of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology, KS Narayan Reddy, 35th Edition, Page No. 101

Sample MCQ #3

Lee Jones test is used to detect:

ACarbolic acid
BArsenic
CCyanide
DLead
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Cyanide

Lee Jones test is used to detect cyanide. Lee Jones test: To 5 ml of stomach contents add few crystals of ferrous sulphate and 5 drops of 2% sodium hydroxide. Boil and cool and add 10 drops of 10% hydrochloric acid. Greenish-blue colour indicates cyanide, and purplish colour salicylates. Reference: The Essentials of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology, KS Narayan Reddy, 35th edition, Page No. 480
