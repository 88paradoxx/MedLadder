Sample MCQ #1

A chronic alcoholic assaulted his neighbour. He had quit drinking 4 days back but now has delirium tremens. He is:

AFully responsible for his act
BFamily is responsible for his act
CPartially responsible for his act
DNot responsible for his act
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Not responsible for his act

Medico-legal Aspects of delirium tremens: It is a medical emergency and should be treated on an inpatient basis. When a person is in delirium tremens commits any illegal act, he is not held responsible by the reason that he is considered to be mentally unsound during this state (Sec. 22 BNS). 22 BNS: Act of a person of unsound mind. - Nothing is an offence which is done by a person who, at the time of doing it, by reason of unsoundness of mind, is incapable of knowing the nature of the act, or that he is doing what is either wrong or contrary to law. Reference: Review of Forensic medicine and toxicology, Gautam Biswas, 5 th edition. Page No. 606

Sample MCQ #2

An alcoholic patient is brought for medical examination for alleged assault of his neighbour. On examination, abnormal behaviour with irrelevant talk is noted. He has history of abstaining from alcohol for past 4 days and is diagnosed to be a case of delirium tremens. What is true according to the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhitha?

AHe is criminally responsible for his act
BHe is partially responsible for his act
CHave diminished responsible for his act
DNot responsible for his act under section 22 BNS
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Not responsible for his act under section 22 BNS

Sec. 22 BNS: Act of a person of unsound mind.-Nothing is an offence which is done by a person who, at the time of doing it, by reason of unsoundness of mind, is incapable of knowing the nature of the act, or that he is doing what is either wrong or contrary to law. Delirium tremens is a psychiatric condition arising as a result of abstinence from alcohol. In case of delirium tremens, he doesn't know that what is he doing. So, he is not criminally responsible for his act under section 22 BNS. Reference: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhitha, 2023
