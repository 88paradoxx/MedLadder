Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following pelvic structures is not developed from urogenital sinus?

ARectum
BUrinary bladder
CVestibular glands
DDistal 1/5th of vagina
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Rectum

The cloaca (endodermal) is divided by formation of the urorectal septum by the seventh week (7 th ) and is separated to create the rectum and the urogenital sinus. Urogenital sinus has 3 parts The cephalad or vesicle portion &rarr; urinary bladder The middle or pelvic portion &rarr; female urethra The caudal or phallic part &rarr; distal 1/3 of vagina (sinovaginal bulb) and the greater vestibular (Bartholin), urethral, and paraurethral (Skene) glands Reference : William's Obstetrics 25th Edition, chapter 7, page no. 121

Sample MCQ #3

Which one of the following congenital malformations of the fetus can be diagnosed in the first trimester by ultrasound?

AAnencephaly
BInencephaly
CMicrocephaly
DHoloprosencephaly
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Anencephaly

As discussed in chapter on fetal malformation, anencephaly can be diagnosed as early as 8-10 weeks/first trimester in experienced hands. Microcephaly: - USG in Obs and Gynae Callen 4/e, p 298, 299 It has decreased head size. In these patients both brain mass and total cell number are reduced. There is a disproportion in size between the skull and face. It is difficult to recognize this anomaly by ultrasound - A head circumference below 2 SD from the mean is used as a diagnostic criteria in midtrimester scan. Holoprosencephaly is the presence of a single centrally placed cerebral ventricle and may be associated with midline abnormalities of the face. With the use of a vaginal probe it can be recognised in the beginning of midtrimester. Reference: Williams Obs. 21/e, p 1120; USG in Obs. and Gynae by Callen 4/e, p 284, Fernando Arias 3/e, p 64, 66, 69

Sample MCQ #6

A 19-year-old girl with painless ulcer in labia majora with everted margins:

ATreponema pallidum
BChlamydia
CGonorrhea
DHerpes genital ulcer disease
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Treponema pallidum

The M/c causes of genital ulcers in young, sexually active women are: Herpes simplex virus (HSV) Treponema pallidum (syphilis) Haemophilus ducreyi (chancroid). In this case, a painless ulcer with an everted margin leaves no doubt that the cause of the ulcer is syphilis (treponema pallidum). reference: J B Sharma, Textbook of Gynecology 1 st edition p359

Sample MCQ #8

True statement about female genital tuberculosis:

AGenital tract involvement results from lymphatic spread
BPremenstrual histopathological examination is diagnostic
CPolymerase chain reaction (PCR) techniques have got higher sensitivity in detection
DReproductive outcome following antituberculous chemotherapy is satisfactory
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Polymerase chain reaction (PCR) techniques have got higher sensitivity in detection

Genital tuberculosis is usually secondary to primary infection (lungs, bones, lymph nodes). It spreads by haematogenous route (not lymphatic route) leading to endosalpingitis. Caseous granulomatous lesions with giant cells on pathological examination are suggestive of TB but are not diagnostic as it can be seen in fungal infection and sarcoidosis. PCR is more sensitive (85&ndash;95%) than microscopy and bacteriological culture. This method can detect fewer than 10 organisms in clinical specimens compared to 10,000 necessary for smear positivity. Reproductive outcome even after treatment is poor. Pregnancy rate is about 20%, live birth rate is only 7%. The risk of miscarriage and ectopic pregnancy are high. Reference: Dutta Gynae 6th/ed p 140
