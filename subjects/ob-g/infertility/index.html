Sample MCQ #1

A 44-year-old female complains of lower abdominal pain and vaginal discharge. On examination, there is cervicitis along with a mucopurulent cervical discharge. The Gram smear of the discharge shows presence of abundant pus cells, but no bacteria. The best approach to isolate the possible causative agent would be:

ACulture on chocolate agar supplemented with hemin
BCulture on McCoy cells
CCulture on a bilayer human blood agar
DCulture on Vero cell lines
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Culture on McCoy cells

Patient is complaining of abdominal pain and vaginal discharge. On examination, cervicitis and mucopurulent discharge is seen - which indicates she is having PID. The presence of pus cells in the absence of an organism indicates chlamydial infection (most common STD today). It is an intracellular organism that grows only on McCoy or HeLa cell cultures. It cannot be grown on other media and hence, often goes unnoticed, later leading to infertility. Culture in McCoy cells is 100% specific for chlamydia but is inexpensive, technically difficult and takes 3-7 days to obtain the result. Reference: Harrion 18th/ed p 1426.

Sample MCQ #2

In the perspective of the busy life schedule in the modern society, the accepted minimum period of sexual cohabitation resulting in no offspring for a couple to be declared infertile is:

AOne year
BOne and a half - year
CTwo years
DThree years
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. One year

If a couple fails to achieve pregnancy after one year of &ldquo;unprotected&rdquo; and regular intercourse, it is an indication to investigate the couple for infertility. Infertility: Primary When no previous pregnancies have occurred. Secondary When a prior pregnancy although not necessary although not necessarily a live birth, has occurred Reference : Shaw 15 th /ed, p 200 Reference : Novak 13 th /ed, p 973

Sample MCQ #3

Most commonly used ovulation induction drug is

ALetrozole
BClomiphene citrate
CGonadotropins
DRaloxifene
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Clomiphene citrate

M/C used drug for ovulation Induction: clomiphene citrate Clomiphene Citrate: It is a racemic mixture of enclomiphene and zuclomiphene. Enclomiphene is a more potent isomer responsible for its ovulation-inducing action. Dose = 50&ndash;250 mg. However, the US FDA-approved maximum dose for clomiphene citrate is 100 mg Clomiphene blocks &ldquo;Estrogen&rdquo; receptors &rarr; increase FSH from pituitary &rarr;growth of follicles Thus it can be used only in patients with intact hypothalamic &ndash;pituitary ovarian axis With clomiphene success rate for ovulation is 80% and success rate for pregnancy is 40% Reference: JB Sharma textbook of Gynecology e1 p218

Sample MCQ #4

The following are the contraindications of tubal reconstructive surgery except:

ALength of tube <4 cms
BPatients over 30 years of age
CPelvic tuberculosis
DReversal done after 5 years of sterilization
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Patients over 30 years of age

Tubal reconstruction surgery can be done for a number of reasons including for reversal of sterilisation procedure. The most favourable outcome is seen when it is done for reversal of sterilisation procedures. Factors for Poor Outcome Following Tuboplasty Dense pelvic adhesions. Loss of fimbriae. Bilateral hydrosalpinx > 3 cm. Length of the reconstructed tube Reversal done after 5 years of sterilization operation. Presence of other factors for infertility Reference: Dutta Gynae 6 th /ed p 248.

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis in a 28-year-old obese woman presenting with Oligomenorrhea, infertility and hirsutism?

APolycystic ovaries
BEndometriosis
CPelvic inflammatory disease
DTurner&#39;s syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Polycystic ovaries

A young woman presenting in the third decade with obesity, oligomenorrhea, infertility and hirsutism leaves no doubt for the diagnosis of PCOS. Ovulatory dysfunction such as oligomenorrhea or hyperandrogenism. Clinical or biochemical evidence of hyperandrogenism. USG criteria Since this female has oligomenorrhea and hirsutism, 2 of the criteria are being fulfilled. Reference: Shaw 15th/ed pg no: 371

Sample MCQ #7

In hysterosalpingography, fallopian tubes are seen beaded in appearance with clubbing of the fimbrial end and ampulla. What is likely cause :

ATuberculosis
BCandidiasis
CChlamydia
DGonococcus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Tuberculosis

HSG showing 'Bead-like fallopian tube and clubbing of ampulla are suggestive of genital tuberculosis' HSG findings in case of tuberculosis: Rigid non-peristaltic pipe like tube called as lead pipe appearance. Beading of tube and variation in filling density. Elongated and distended tube with everted fimbriae and patent abdominal ostium called 'tobacco pouch' appearance. Calcification of tube, Bilateral cornual block. Vascular or lymphatic intravasation of dye. Tubal diverticula Uterine cavity - irregular outline, honeycomb appearance or presence of uterine synechiae (Asherman&rsquo;s syndrome). In a proven case of genital tuberculosis, HSG is contraindicated as it may spread the infection. So, HSG should not be performed until pelvic tuberculosis is excluded by endometrial study. Reference : Dutta Gynae 5th/ed p 137-38
