Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following statements is true about the fetal fibronectin (fFN) test?

AIn this test fFN is measured from maternal serum
BPositive test in first trimester increases risk of abortion
CNegative test means delivery will not occur in next 7 days
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Negative test means delivery will not occur in next 7 days

Fibronectin is a glycoprotein that binds the fetal membranes to the decidua. Normally it is found in the cervicovaginal discharge before 22 weeks and again after 37 weeks of pregnancy. Presence of fibronectin in the cervicovaginal discharge between 24 weeks and 34 weeks is a predictor of preterm labor. When the test is negative it reassures that delivery will not occur within the next 7 days . Reference : D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 22

Sample MCQ #3

Pain in early labor is limited to dermatomes:

AT10 - L1
BS1 - S3
CL4 - L5
DL2 - L3
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. T10 - L1

In the early stages of labour pain is mainly uterine in origin because of painful uterine contraction "The pain of uterine contractions is distributed along the cutaneous nerve distribution of T 10 to L 1 In later stages pain is due to dilatation of the cervix. "The pain of cervical dilatation and stretching is referred to the back through sacral plexus." Reference : Dutta Obs 7/e, pg 117

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following statements is not true regarding obstructive uterine rupture?

AOccurs in cases of obstructed labor
BUsually involves fundal area first
CMargins of rupture are ragged and necrosed
DFHS is absent in all cases
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Usually involves fundal area first

Obstructive rupture-This is the end result of an obstructed labor. The rupture involves the anterior lower segment and usually extends through one lateral side of the uterus to the upper segment. The margins are ragged and necrosed. Nonobstructive rupture-Grand multipara are usually affected and rupture usually occurs in early labor. Weakening of the walls due to repeated previous births as mentioned earlier may be the responsible factor. The rupture usually involves the fundal area and is complete. Reference: D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 29

Sample MCQ #6

Which layer of myometrium is described as 'living ligature' of uterus

AOuter layer
BMiddle layer
CInner layer
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Middle layer

3 layers of uterus Endometrium Myometrium Perimetrium/serosal Layers of myometrium Outer longitudinal Middle interlacing Inner circular The middle layer is the thickest of the three and consists of bundles of muscle separated by connective tissue. There is a tendency for the muscle bundles to interlace, and as the blood vessels which supply the uterus are distributed in the connective tissues, the caliber of the vessels is in part controlled by the contraction of the muscle cells. The purpose of this layer is therefore in part hemostatic. This layer is described as living ligatures of the uterus , and is responsible for control of bleeding in the third stage of labor. Reference: Shaw&rsquo;s textbook of gynecology, 16th edition, chapter 1

Sample MCQ #8

A 22-year-old primigravida patient came to hospital at 35 weeks of gestation complaining of abdominal pain. She denied having any vaginal discharge. She had regular uterine contraction and her cervical length was 2 cm in TVS. Obstetrician performed fFN test which was positive. What would be the most probable diagnosis for such a patient?

AIUGR
BIUD
CPreterm labor
DPROM
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Preterm labor

American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (2020e) defines preterm labor to be regular contractions accompanied by a change in cervical dilation, effacement, or both or to be regular contractions and cervical dilation of at least 2 cm at initial presentation. fFN: Detected in cervicovaginal secretions in women who have normal pregnancies with intact membranes at term, fFN appears to reflect stromal remodeling of the cervix before labor . Reference : Williams obstetrics 26 th edition, chapter 45
