Sample MCQ #1

Head is at what station when it is engaged?

A0
B1
C-1
D2
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 0

Engagement of head rules out CPD at the level of pelvic inlet. If the head is engaged, it means the head is at &lsquo;O&rsquo; station. Engagement can be: When sagittal suture of the head of fetus lies in the transverse diameter of pelvic inlet (midway between the pubic symphysis and the sacral promontory) It occurs in 25% cases It occurs commonly in nulliparous women. Causes of nonengagement of head in a nullipara at term: Malpresentations / Occipito Posterior position/deflexed head Cephalopelvic disproportion Placenta previa / Tumors in the lower segment Tumours of the fetal neck/Cord around the neck / Hydrocephalus Polyhydramnios Distended bladder and rectum. Reference : Dutta Obs. 7/e, pg 81, 82, 352

Sample MCQ #2

Most common cause of retained placenta is

AAtonic uterus
BAdherent placenta (PAS)
CPresence of constriction ring
DPoor maternal pushing efforts
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Atonic uterus

Causes behind retained placenta: Placenta completely separated but retained is due to poor voluntary expulsive efforts. The commonest cause of retention of non-separated placenta is atonic uterus. Morbidly adherent placenta&mdash;partial or rarely, complete. Placenta incarcerated following partial or complete separation due to constriction ring (hourglass contraction), premature attempts to deliver the placenta before it is separated. Reference: D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 28

Granulosa cells produces estrogen with the help of the enzyme:

AAlkaline phosphatase
BAromatase
CAcid phosphatase
DGlucuronidase

✓ Correct Answer: B. Aromatase

Estrogen produced by theca cells is converted to androgens in granulosa cells with the help of enzyme aromatase. Sample MCQ #4

Management of retained unseparated placenta is:

AMethotrexate
BManual removal of placenta
CControlled cord traction
DHysterotomy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Manual removal of placenta

Management of retained placenta: Placenta is separated and retained: expell the placenta out by controlled cord traction. Unseparated retained placenta: Manual removal of placenta is to be done under general anaesthesia. Reference: D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 28

Sample MCQ #5

In non-immune hydrops which of the following is NOT seen:

ASkin oedema
BAscites
CLarge placenta
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. None of the above

"Hydrops is characterized by excess fluid in two or more body areas such as thorax, abdomen or skin. It is often associated with hydramnios and a hydropic thickened placenta". It is characterised by: Increased skin thickness (> 5 mm) / skin oedema (first sign seen on USG). Placental enlargement Pleural effusion Ascites The fetus is in Buddha position with a halo around the head. Reference : Williams 23/e, pg 626

AhCG

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following statements is true regarding inversion of the uterus?

AInversion can be induced but in most cases it is spontaneous
BAlways occur with placenta in situ
CAcute inversion can go in chronic one if left untreated
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Acute inversion can go in chronic one if left untreated

(A) Inversion can be induced but in most cases it is spontaneous: This statement is misleading; while inversion can occur spontaneously, but more commonly it is induced-Most common cause is mismanagement of third stage of labour. (B) Always occurs with placenta in situ: This is not true; inversion can occur with or without the placenta attached. (C) Acute inversion can indeed progress to chronic inversion if not treated, making this the correct statement. Reference: D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 28

Sample MCQ #8

A 30-year-old patient delivered in the labour room after spontaneous labour. Delivery was uneventful, patient vitally stable, uterus well contracted but after delivery placenta didn't expelled spontaneously. Diagnosis of retained placenta was made and she was taken in OT for manual removal of placenta under GA. During the procedure the plane of cleavage couldn't be made out between placenta and uterine wall. What is the most likely diagnosis?

AEndometritis
BPlacenta accreta spectrum
CHourglass contraction
DSuccenturiate lobe
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Placenta accreta spectrum

During attempted manual removal if no cleavage between the placenta and the uterine wall is made out, diagnosis of a total placenta accreta is certain. Endometritis is a remote complication of retained placenta. Reference: D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 28
