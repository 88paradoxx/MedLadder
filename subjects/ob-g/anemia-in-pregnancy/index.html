Sample MCQ #1

The lab for your pregnant patient indicates microcytic anemia. Optimal treatment of normocytic or mild microcytic anemia associated with pregnancy uses which of the following?

AA high-fiber diet
BFerrous sulfate tablets
CFolic acid supplements
DHydroxocobalamin injections
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Ferrous sulfate tablets

Iron deficiency microcytic anemia is the anemia that is most commonly associated with In this condition, oral iron supplementation is indicated. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 834-37

Sample MCQ #2

All of the following are fetal complications of prolonged pregnancy except:

AFetal ascites
BFetal distress
COligohydramnios
DAnencephaly
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Fetal ascites

Fetal complications during pregnancy&mdash;There is diminished placental function, oligohydramnios and meconium stained liquor. These lead to fetal hypoxia and fetal distress. Reference: D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 22

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is a non-surgical method for replacement of uterine inversion?

AO'sullivan's method
BHuntington procedure
CHaultain method
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. O'sullivan's method

Hydrostatic(O&rsquo;Sullivan&rsquo;s) method: The inverted uterus is replaced into the vagina. Warm sterile fluid (up to 5 liters) is gradually instilled into the vagina through a douche nozzle. The vaginal orifice is blocked by the operator's palms supplemented by labial apposition around the palm by an assistant. Alternatively, a silicon cup (vacuum extraction cup) is placed into the vagina. The douche can be placed at a height of about 3 feet above the uterus. The water distends the vagina and the consequent increased intravaginal pressure leads to replacement of the uterus. Reference: D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 28

Sample MCQ #4

Neonatal complications of prolonged pregnancy include:

AHyperglycaemia
BAnaemia
CChemical pneumonitis
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Chemical pneumonitis

Neonatal complications Chemical pneumonitis, atelectasis and pulmonary hypertension are due to meconium aspiration; Hypoxia (low Apgar scores) and respiratory failure; Hypoglycemia and polycythemia and Increased NICU admissions. Reference: D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 22

Sample MCQ #5

30 years old lady with failed medical treatment of uterine polyp, the surgical treatment of uterine polyp can be:

ARemoval by twisting
BRemoval by morcellement
CHysteroscopy
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Endometrial polyp -- Hysteroscopy and resection (best) Removal by ovum forcep Incase of recurrence and, if family completed&mdash; uterine curettage hysterectomy Cervical polyp -- Twisting of the pedicle Big fibroid in vagina -- Removal of polyp by morcellation If associated with inversion enucleate Ref. Dutta Gynae 6th/ed p 287, 288

Sample MCQ #6

In pregnancy, which type of anemia is not common in India?

AVitamin B12 anemia
BFolic acid anemia
CIron + folic acid anemia
DIron deficiency anemia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Vitamin B12 anemia

In countries like India, anemia due to iron and folic acid deficiency commonly occur during pregnancy. Only Vitamin B 12 deficiency as a cause of anemia is rare. 'Megaloblastic anemia caused by lack of vitamin B 12 , that is cyanocobalamin, during pregnancy is exceedingly rare' - Williams Obs. 22/e, p 1147, 23/e, p 1082 Reference: Dutta Obs. 7/e, p 268; Williams Obs. 22/e, p 1147, 23/e, p 1082; Fernando Arias 3/e, p 469

Sample MCQ #7

Dose of folic acid per day for treating megaloblastic anemia in pregnancy:

A400 Micrograms
B4 mg
C1 mg
D2 mg
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 1 mg

DOSE OF FOLIC ACID IN PREGNANCY: PER DAY: To prevent neural tube defect: 400 micro grams In pregnant females with previous h/o ntd: 4mg To treat megaloblastic anaemia: 1mg In patients with sickle cell disease: 4mg Reference: High Risk Pregnancy, Arias 4/e, p 237

Sample MCQ #8

Fourteen weeks pregnancy with third degree prolapse. Best management will be:

ASling surgery
BFoot end elevation
CRing Pessary
DNo treatment
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Ring Pessary

Other indications of Ring pessary: In puerperium &ndash; to facilitate involution. Patient unfit or unwilling for surgery. Women who have undergone at least one previous attempt at surgical intervention without relief. Diagnostic &ndash; It may be placed diagnostically to identify which women are at risk for urinary incontinence after prolapse correction surgery. Note: Pessaries are of two varieties : Supportive pessary eg&ndash;Ring pessary Space occupying pessary eg&ndash;Gellhorn pessary Reference: Dutta Obs 6th/ed p 312-3; Shaw 15th/ed p 337-8; Williams Gynae 1st/ed p 545-7
