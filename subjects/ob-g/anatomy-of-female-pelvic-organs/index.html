Sample MCQ #4

Which is/are a feature of testicular feminization syndrome:

AXX pattern
BCommonly reared as male
CWell-formed female internal genitalia
DHigh testosterone levels
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. High testosterone levels

In Testicular Feminization Syndrome Genotype &ndash; 46XY Gonads &ndash; Testis (intrabdominal) Phenotype &ndash; Female Internal Genitalia &ndash; Is of males (except a short vagina may be present) They are reared as female. Lab investigation : Testosterone levels may be normal/high/low depending on the degree of androgen resistance and the contribution of estradiol to feedback inhibition of the hypothalamus pituitary axis. Laboratory evaluation demonstrates elevated LH levels, normal or slightly elevated testosterone levels, and a 46 XY Karyotype. Reference: Dutta Gynae 6th/ed p 443

Sample MCQ #5

A 12 years old female is admitted as a patient of dys- germinoma of the right ovary 4x5 cm in size with an intact capsule. Best treatment will be:

AOvarian cystectomy
BOophorectomy on the involved side
CBilateral oophorectomy
DHysterectomy with bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Oophorectomy on the involved side

Though Dysgerminoma is the most radiosensitive tumour known, treatment of choice is oophorectomy of involved side followed by cisplatin based chemotherapy to preserve fertility, as dysgerminoma is seen in young women with average age incidence being 20 years. BEP: Bleomycin, Etoposide, Cisplatin Reference : Devita 7 th /ed pg 1391; Reference : Williams Gynae 1 st /ed pg 741

Sample MCQ #6

Asymptomatic carriage of gonococcal infection in female is commonly seen in:

AEndocervix
BVagina
CUrethra
DFornix
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Endocervix

For identification of organisms in PID the materials are collected from the following available sources: Discharge from urethra or Bartholin's gland Cervical canal Collected pus from the fallopian tubes during laparoscopy or laparotomy. The material collected is subjected to Gram's stain and culture (aerobic/anaerobic). The findings of Gram-negative diplococci is very much suggestive of gonococcal infection. There are 2 important points which we should remember about gonorrhoea infection. Squamous epithelium is resistant to gonococcal infection Gonorrhoea is an STD (i.e. it is transmitted by ascending infection along with sperms) Now, since squamous epithelium is resistant to gonococcal infection, in vagina and ectocervix gonococcal infection cannot occur (as both are lined by squamous epithelium). Reference: Dutta Gynae 5th/ed p 143.

Sample MCQ #7

All of the following statements hold true for melanoma of vulva except:

AIt is the 2nd M/C vulvar cancer
BM/C site is labia minora
CMay arise from junctional nevus
DHas a poor prognosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. M/C site is labia minora

Melanoma is the second most common vulvar cancer. The common sites are the clitoris and labia majora. It may arise from a junctional nevus. Radical vulvectomy and bilateral regional lymphadenectomy (en-block) is the preferred treatment. Pelvic lymphadenectomy does not alter the prognosis. Radiation therapy, adjuvant chemotherapy, or immunotherapy are ineffective. Overall prognosis is poor. Reference: Dutta Gynae 6 th /ed pg 337

Sample MCQ #8

The nerve roots blocked in pudendal nerve block is:

AL1,2,3
BL2,3
CS2,3,4
DS4
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. S2,3,4

Pudendal nerve block typically involves blocking the nerve roots S2, S3, and S4, which are responsible for innervating the perineum and pelvic floor. Reference : Dutta Obs 7/e, pg 518
