Sample MCQ #1

Type of cerclage done in a non-pregnant patient with cervical incompetence?

ALash and lash cerclage
BBenson and Durfee
CWurms cerclage
DLaparoscopic cerclage
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Laparoscopic cerclage

The purpose of the Lash and Lash operation is to correct the defect in the cervical wall and restore competence to the cervix, thereby allowing gestation to proceed to term. Lash and Lash operation is a pervaginal procedure done in non pregnant females Benson Durfee is an abdominal cerclage procedure that can be done oin bothe pregnant as well as non pregnant females. Wurms cerclage is an emergency cerclage procedure done during pregnancy Still, Laparoscopic cerclage is the most preferred operative procedure in non pregnant females.

Sample MCQ #2

Comprehensive emergency obstetric care does not include:

AManual removal of placenta
BHysterectomy
CBlood transfusion
DCesarean section
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Hysterectomy

Setting standards of emergency obstetrics and newborn care: Basic emergency obstetric and newborn care provided in health centres, large or small include the facilities for: Administration of antibiotics, oxytocics and anticonvulsants. Manual removal of the placenta. Removal of retained products following miscarriage or abortion. Assisted vaginal delivery preferably with vacuum extractor. Comprehensive emergency obstetric and newborn care, typically delivered in district hospital, includes all basic functions above, plus cesarean section, safe blood transfusion and care to sick and low birth weight newborns including resuscitation. It is recommended that for every 5,00,000 people there should be 4 facilities offering comprehensive essential obstetric care. Reference: KDT 6/e, p 251,484,488,Dutta Obs 7/e, p 288

Sample MCQ #3

A cross-sectional analysis across obstetrics and gynecology wards in several Indian hospitals reported an MMR of 4.21 per 1,000 live births. Based on WHO guidelines, how is maternal mortality defined?

ADeath after delivery
BDeath from full-term pregnancy within 24 hr of delivery
CDeath during pregnancy and within 1 year after delivery
DDeath during pregnancy and within 6 weeks of delivery
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Death during pregnancy and within 6 weeks of delivery

Explanation: Maternal mortality refers to the death of a woman during pregnancy or within 6 weeks (42 days) after its termination. WHO Definition: Maternal mortality is defined as the annual number of deaths of women from causes related to or worsened by pregnancy or its management (excluding accidental or incidental causes), occurring during pregnancy or within 42 days of its end, regardless of the pregnancy's duration or location. Late Maternal Death: This refers to the death of a woman from direct or indirect obstetric causes occurring more than 42 days but less than one year after the end of pregnancy. Options A, B, and C are incorrect. Reference: https://www.who.int/data/gho/indicator-metadata-registry/imr-details/4622 https://sciencedirect.com/topics/medicine-and-dentistry/maternal-mortality
