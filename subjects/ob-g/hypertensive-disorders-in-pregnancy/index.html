Sample MCQ #1

A female of 36 weeks gestation presents with hypertension, blurring of vision and headache. Her blood pressure reading was 180/120 mm Hg and 174/110 mm Hg after 20 minutes. How will you manage the patient?

AAdmit the patient and observe
BAdmit the patient, start antihypertensives and continue pregnancy till term.
CAdmit the patient, start antihypertensives, MgSO4 and terminate the pregnancy
DAdmit oral antihypertensives and follow up in out-patient department
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Admit the patient, start antihypertensives, MgSO4 and terminate the pregnancy

In the question, patient is presenting with Headache, Blurring of vision, B/P = 180/120 mm of Hg (later 174/110 mm of Hg) i.e. she is a case of severe pregnancy induced hypertension. First step in the management of this case would be to prevent seizures i.e. give MgSO4. Her B/P should be controlled with antihypertensive and since pregnancy is >34 weeks, therefore terminate pregnancy (which is the definitive management). Reference : Fernando Arias Practical Guide to High Risk Pregnancy and Delivery 3/e, pg 417, 420, 424

Sample MCQ #2

Preconceptional intake of which of the following results in decrease in incidence of neural tube defect?

AVitamin A
BFolate
CVitamin E
DVitamin C
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Folate

"Folic acid has been shown to effectively reduce the risk of neural tube defects (NTDs). A daily 4 mg dose is recommended for patients who have had a previous pregnancy affected by neural tube defects. It should be started at least 1 month (ideally 3 months) prior to pregnancy and continued through the first 6-12 weeks of pregnancy." -COGDT 10/e, p 197 Remember: Therapeutic dose of folic acid (to be given in females with a previous history of baby with NTD) - 4 mg. Prophylactic dose of folic acid = 0.4 mg i.e., 400 mg Duration: It should be started 1 month before conception and continued till 3 months of pregnancy. Note: Women with insulin dependent diabetes mellitus and those with seizure disorders treated by valproic acid and carbamazepine are also at greater risk for NTD's and should be given therapeutic dose of folic acid i.e 4 mg. Reference: Dutta Obs. 7/e, p 409; COGDT 10/e, p 197

Sample MCQ #3

A second gravida patient with a history of pre eclampsia comes to OPD at 5 months of amenorrhea. Which test would you like to perform for prediction of pre eclampsia in this pregnancy?

AUmbilical artery doppler
BUterine artery doppler
CMCA doppler
DRoll over test
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Uterine artery doppler

Doppler ultrasound Presence of diastolic notch between 22-24 weeks by Doppler velocimetry in the uterine artery predicts the development of preeclampsia. It is probably the best available test. Reference : Arias 5 th edition pg no 192
