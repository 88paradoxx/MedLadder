Sample MCQ #1

True about bacterial vaginosis:

AIt does not increase the risk of HIV
BTreatment of male partners reduces recurrence.
CPregnant women should be routinely screened for bacterial vaginosis.
DAtopobium vagina is associated with recurrence.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Atopobium vagina is associated with recurrence.

Associated with increased risk of acquiring HIV, C. trachomatis, and N. gonorrhoeae. Treatment of male partners with metronidazole does not prevent recurrence of bacterial vaginosis. Recurrence: Metronidazole gel twice weekly for >3 months Recommendation is against routine screening of pregnant women for bacterial vaginosis. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 136, Pg: 1083

Sample MCQ #2

A 46-year-old female suffering from severe menorrhagia underwent hysterectomy 6 years back. She wishes to take hormone replacement therapy. I/V/O hot flushes and atrophic vaginitis.The treatment of choice is:

AProgesterone
BEstrogen and progesterone
CEstrogen
DNone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Estrogen

Both estrogen alone or estrogen + progesterone can be used for treating menopausal symptoms. The choice depends on whether the uterus is present or not. "For women with a uterus, a progestin should be combined with an estrogen, to lower the risk of endometrial cancer." All women who have intact uterus should receive combined estrogen - progesterone therapy. Reference: Williams Gynae 1st/ed pp 495, 499-500

Sample MCQ #3

A young lady presents to your office with a complaint of copious vaginal discharge, but there is no cervical discharge on per speculum examination. Which of the following should be given for the management?

AMetronidazole and fluconazole
BMetronidazole and azithromycin
CMetronidazole and doxycycline
DFluconazole only
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Metronidazole and fluconazole

Syndromic management of sexually transmitted infections (WHO 1991) Principle: Treatment of STDs should be initiated at the patient's first visit to a clinic. At the same time the couple is counseled about the importance of condom use and prevention of STD transmission. Syndromic managements are based on epidemiological studies all over the world. Syndromic diagnosis and laboratory assisted diagnosis have been found similar in terms of accuracy. Method: Management is done by criteria for syndromic diagnosis of PID. These include detection of vaginal discharge abdominal pain, cervical motion tenderness, bilateral adnexal providers are trained up to follow a standardized protocol (flow chart) to treat such a patient. This is particularly suitable in a health care setting of developing countries. Reference: Dutta Gynae 6 th /ed p 176; Internet search-www. naconlinr.org/upload/publication/ste

Sample MCQ #5

Amsel's criteria of bacterial vaginosis includes all except

AWhite, milky, nonviscous, adherent vaginal discharge
BVaginal pH less than 4.5
CAmine odour with 10% KOH
DPresence of clue cells
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Vaginal pH less than 4.5

Bacterial vaginosis occurs due to alteration of vaginal flora; lactobacilli decrease whereas coccobacilli and Gardnerella increase. Amsel&rsquo;s criteria includes: White milky, non viscous, adherent vaginal discharge Vaginal pH more than 4.5 Positive whiff test: amine odour with 10% KOH Presence of clue cells Increase in number of gardnerella vaginalis and reduced number of lactobacilli Treatment: tab. Metronidazole 2 g single dose or tinidazole or clindamycin 300 mg BD for 7 days Reference: J B Sharma, Textbook of Gynecology 1 st edition p333

Sample MCQ #8

Most common gynecological problem in prepubertal girls is

AAbdominal pain
BVulvovaginitis
CVaginal spotting
DVaginal dryness
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Vulvovaginitis

Vulvovaginitis occurs due to lack of estrogen. Most commonly seen in children. Causes Foreign body Worm infestation Tuberculosis Infections such as candida Symptoms are intense pruritus and offensive vaginal discharge. Estrogen cream and treatment of infections are done for its management. Reference: J B Sharma, Textbook of Gynecology 1 st edition p332
