Sample MCQ #2

Obstetric shock Index (OSI) is calculated by:

AHeart rate divided by Mean arterial pressure
BHeart rate divided by Diastolic blood pressure
CHeart rate divided by Systolic blood pressure
DHeart rate divided by Pulmonary arterial pressure
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Heart rate divided by Systolic blood pressure

Shock index (SI) refers to heart rate (HR) divided by systolic blood pressure (SBP). The normal value is 0.5&ndash;0.7. For obstetric shock index (OSI) normal value in pregnancy is 0.7&ndash;0.8. Increase in the pulse rate and a fall in systolic blood pressure that is seen with significant haemorrhage, results in an increase in the shock index. If it increases above 0.9&ndash;1.1, intensive resuscitation may be required. If OSI > 1 indicates massive blood obstetric haemorrhage that required blood transfusion. Reference:Arias practical guide to high risk pregnancy and delivery 4 th edition, chapter 23
