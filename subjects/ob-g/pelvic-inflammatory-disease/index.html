Sample MCQ #1

A 16-year-old boy is being treated with desmopressin for diabetes insipidus. His plasma glucose level (fasting) is 108 mg/dl, sodium 139 mmol/l (137-144), potassium 4.5 mmol/l (3.5-4.9) and calcium 10.9 mg/dl. He still has complaints of polyuria, polydipsia and nocturia. What could be the most probable cause?

ADiabetes mellitus
BNephrogenic diabetes insipidus
CCentral diabetes insipidus
DSIADH
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Nephrogenic diabetes insipidus

This boy most probably has nephrogenic diabetes insipidus due to hypercalcemia. In this condition, renal tubules are resistant to antidiuretic hormone (ADH), which is due to a receptor defect of vasopressin-2 receptor or a post-receptor defect in an ADH-sensitive water channel called aquaporin-2. Central DI (ADH deficiency) show good response to desmopressin (V2 agonist) administration. The findings do not suggest diabetes mellitus, which is also associated with these symptoms. In patients with the syndrome of inappropriate secretion of ADH (SIADH), low sodium levels are the hallmark. Metabolic causes of Nephrogenic diabetes insipidus: Chronic Hypokalemia Chronic Hypercalcemia Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 2920

Sample MCQ #3

The preferred site for obtaining cultures in a patient with acute PID during laparotomy is,

AEndocervix
BEndometrium
CPouch of Douglas
DFallopian tubes
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Fallopian tubes

For identification of organisms in PID the materials are collected from the following available sources: Discharge from urethra or Bartholin&rsquo;s gland Cervical canal Collected pus from the fallopian tubes during laparoscopy or laparotomy. The material collected is subjected to Gram&rsquo;s stain and culture (aerobic/anaerobic). The findings of Gram-negative diplococci is suggestive of gonococcal infection. Reference: Dutta Gynae 6th/ed pg 130

Sample MCQ #5

Epidermal inclusion cysts contains:

AFragments of stratified epithelium
BDegenerated squamous cells
CVaginal debris
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

After birth-related epithelial trauma and healing, fragments of stratified epithelium occasionally is embedded beneath the vaginal surface. Similar to its native tissue, this buried epithelium continues to shed degenerated cells . As a result, epidermal inclusion cysts are formed, which are filled with debris . Reference: williams obstetrics 26th edition chapter 2

Sample MCQ #6

All are the sequelae of PID except

AEctopic pregnancy
BChronic pelvic pain
CMultiple pregnancy
DDyspareunia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Multiple pregnancy

A): Ectopic pregnancy - This is correct. PID can lead to scarring and damage to the fallopian tubes, increasing the risk of ectopic pregnancy. (B): Chronic pelvic pain - This is correct. PID can cause inflammation and scarring in the pelvic organs, leading to persistent pain. (C): Multiple pregnancy - This is incorrect. Multiple pregnancy is not a recognized sequelae of PID. (D): Dyspareunia - This is correct. Dyspareunia, or painful sexual intercourse, can result from the pelvic inflammation and scarring associated with PID Reference: J B Sharma, Textbook of Gynecology 1st edition p353
