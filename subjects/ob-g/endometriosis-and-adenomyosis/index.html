Sample MCQ #1

A 19-year-old woman gives a history of sharp pain in the lower abdomen for 2 days every month approximately 2 weeks before the menses. The most probable etiology for her pain is:

AEndometriosis
BDysmenorrhea
CPelvic tuberculosis
DMittelschmerz
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Mittelschmerz

A female giving a history of sharp pain in lower abdomen, every month, 2 weeks before the menstruation suggests mittelschmerz as the diagnosis. Mittelschmerz is synonymous to painful ovulation. Pain is associated with rupture of ovarian follicle at the time of ovulation Ref. Dutta Gynae 4th/ed p 172, 5th/ed p 178

AGNRH analogue
BDanazole
CLH
DHysterectomy

Sample MCQ #4

True regarding adenomyosis is:

AMost common in nullipara
BCharacterised by growth of endometrium outside uterus
CPresents with menorrhagia, dysmenorrhea, and an enlarged uterus
DMore common in young women
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Presents with menorrhagia, dysmenorrhea, and an enlarged uterus

(B) Adenomyosis is a condition where there is ingrowth of endometrium (both gland + stroma) directly into the myometrium. Earlier it was called Endometriosis interna It is more common in Multiparous females(i.e.option a is correct). (A) Adenomyosis is more common in women who have had children, so it is not most common in nulliparous women. (C) Adenomyosis commonly presents with symptoms such as menorrhagia (heavy menstrual bleeding), dysmenorrhea (painful periods), and an enlarged uterus. 60 (D) Adenomyosis is more common in women who are in their 40-50years , so it is not typically associated with younger women. Reference: Shaw 15th/ed pp 474-5; Novak 15th/ed pp 484-5.

Sample MCQ #5

Scar endometriosis can occur following:

AClassical Caesarean Section
BHysterotomy
CEpisiotomy
DAll of the above

✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Endometriosis sometimes occurs in abdominal wall scars following operations on uterus or tubes and is known as scar endometriosis. Operations most likely to be followed by scar endometriosis : Hysterotomy Classical cesarean section Myomectomy Ventrofixation Following operations for section of fallopian tube Following operations for removal of pelvic endometriosis Episiotomy Ref. Sample MCQ #7

All are true regarding endometriosis, except:

AHormone dependent condition
BCan involve lung, pleura
CContains clear fluid
DOvary is the most common site
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Contains clear fluid

Endometriosis is occurence of functioning endometrial tissue (glands + stroma) outside the uterine cavity. Whatever the initial genesis of endometriosis, its further development depends mainly on estrogen (Option &ldquo;a&rdquo;). It can occur anywhere in the body, Most common site being ovary (Option &ldquo;d&rdquo;). Can also involve lungs and pleura (Option &ldquo;b&rdquo;). In endometriosis the ovary contains tarry dark brown fluid (due to presence of blood pigments like hemosiderin) and cul de sac has yellow brown fluid. Clear fluid is not seen anywhere, So, Option &ldquo;c&rdquo; is incorrect. Reference: Shaw 15th/ed p 466.

All are used in treatment of endometriosis except:

AMedroxyprogesterone acetate
BTibolone
COCP
DDanazol
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Tibolone

Drugs used in the management of endometriosis Mnemonic - Proctor and Gamble Always Offer Good Deals Proctor &ndash;Progesterone -First line management &rarr; Injectable progestin-DMPA and &rarr; LNG-IUS &rarr; Oral progestins- continuous administration Gamble &ndash;GnRH analogue Always &ndash;Antiprogesterone-mifepristone Offer &ndash;OCP Good &ndash;Gestrinone Deals &ndash;Danazol Superficial peritoneal and ovarian implants may respond well to medical management but large endometriomas and extra pelvic endometriosis are unlikely to respond and will ultimately require surgical excision. Reference: Shaw 15th/ed pp 472-3; Novak 14th/ed pp 1164-9.
