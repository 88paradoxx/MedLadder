Sample MCQ #1

A 42-year-old man presents with headaches, excessive sweating, galactorrhoea, polyuria and polydipsia. Visual field examination reveals bitemporal hemianopia. His blood pressure is 160/110 mmHg. Blood tests: fasting glucose 150 mg/dl, prolactin 1200 mU/l (<360).What is the most probable diagnosis?

AProlactinoma
BDiabetes mellitus
CAcromegaly
DCushing's disease
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Prolactinoma

Prolactinoma: (>200) Clinical features: Amenorrhoea Galactorrhoea Infertility Compressive symptoms: B/L TEMPORAL HEMIANOPIA. DOC: CABERGOLINE - FOR ALL CASES BROMOCRIPTINE- IF PREGNANCY IS DESIRED. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 2912

Sample MCQ #2

A 50-year-old male patient presents with polyuria and polydipsia of 2 months duration. He has been on a chronic medication for last 15 years. The urine is hypoosmolar. His blood sugars are normal. Which could be the drug?

APhenytoin.
BLithium
CAmlodipine.
DMetoprolol.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Lithium

Polyuria, polydipsia with large volume of hypoosmolar urine suggest the diagnosis of Diabetes insipidus. Among the following, Lithium therapy causes nephrogenic diabetes. Other drugs causing Diabetes Insipidus: Lithium, Demeclocycline, Methoxyflurane, Amphotericin B, Aminoglycosides, Cisplatin, Rifampin, Foscarnet. Lithium causes DI by entering the Collecting Duct via the ENaC, where it down regulates the Aquaporin channels. Lithium also causes Chronic Tubulointerstitial Disease. Amiloride can be used to prevent and treat Lithium induced Diabetes Insipidus. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, Ch: 316, Pg: 2363.

Sample MCQ #3

As per IAS DP the proposed single step testing with a 75 gram oral glucose for gestational diabetes, the fasting glucose cut off is

A90
B92
C95
D100
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 92

It is recommended that an FPG( Fasting plasma glucose) value in early pregnancy &ge;5.1 mmol/l (92 mg/dl) should also be classified as Gestational Diabetes Mellitus. International Association of Diabetes and Pregnancy Study Groups Consensus Panel; Metzger BE, Gabbe SG, Persson B, Buchanan TA, Catalano PA, Damm P, Dyer AR, Leiva Ad, Hod M, Kitzmiller JL, Lowe LP, McIntyre HD, Oats JJ, Omori Y, Schmidt MI. Sample MCQ #4

A 29-year-old woman came to the diabetes clinic with her two children. This is her third pregnancy and although not normally diabetic, she was diagnosed with gestational diabetes mellitus. Recent fasting plasma glucose levels have been in the range of 80-90 mg/dl and she is taking BD mixed insulins. Later that evening she collapses while shopping and is brought to the Emergency Department with blood glucose of 56 mg/dl. Which of the following represents the most appropriate management in this patient?

AReduce her dose of BD mixed insulin by 50%
BReduce her dose of BD mixed insulin by 20%
CCheck a random cortisol to rule out Addison's disease
DChange her to a basal bolus regime
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Change her to a basal bolus regime

This lady's fasting plasma glucose levels are appropriate, but taking a BD mixed insulin regime is probably not appropriate to her lifestyle where her mealtimes and portion sizes may be slightly irregular given the fact that she has two other children to manage. A basal bolus regime would give her more flexibility in varying insulin dose. The coexistence of Addison's is unlikely and increasing the size of her meals would only promote more excess weight gain and worsening insulin resistance. Reference: ADA Standards of Diabetes Care - 2023

Sample MCQ #5

Which of the following blood glucose readings is diagnostic of gestational diabetes according to IADPSG criteria?

AFasting blood glucose > 126 mg/dl
BFasting blood glucose > 92 mg/dl
C2-hour OGTT value > 140 mg/dl
D2-hour OGTT value > 180 mg/dl
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Fasting blood glucose > 92 mg/dl

The screening for Diabetes mellitus should be performed in women at the first prenatal visit First trimester screening is performed for high risk women according to ADA while ACOG advocates universal screening for all women The diagnosis of Gestational Diabetes mellitus is made if either one of the following is satisfied: Fasting blood glucose > 92 mg/dl 1 hr post OGTT > 180 mg/dl 2 hr post OGTT > 153 mg/dl If fasting blood glucose is more than 126 mg/dl or 2-hour post glucose load value is > 200 mg/dl, the diagnosis of overt diabetes mellitus is made Reference: ADA Standards of Medical Care in Diabetes 2023

Sample MCQ #6

A 54 year old dark skinned man presents with lethargy, polyuria, polydipsia and stiffness of the hands. He has evidence of an arthropathy affecting the 2nd & 3rd metacarpophalangeal joints of both hands. Xray confirms evidence of degenerative disease at these sites. Which of the following the likely diagnosis?

ABehcet's syndrome
BHaemochromatosis
CAmyloidosis
DReiter's syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Haemochromatosis

Clues pointing towards hemochromatosis: Dark skinned-> bronzing of skin Polyuria, polydipsia-> Diabetes mellitus Destructive arthropathy in typical location The characteristic feature of haemochromatotic arthropathy is involvement of the second and third metacarpals. The arthropathy can lead to extensive joint destruction. These patients may have episodes of acute, inflammatory pseudogout from such deposition. Arthropathy and hypogonadism do not respond to Iron lowering therapy. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Ch: 374, Pg: 2871

Sample MCQ #7

In a nondiabetic high risk pregnancy, the ideal time for non-stress test monitoring is:

A48 hours
B72 hours
C96 hours
D24 hours
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 72 hours

Intervals between NST testing The interval between tests is arbitrarily set at 7 days. According to ACOG, more frequent testing is advocated for women with post term pregnancy, type I diabetes mellitus, IUGR or gestational hypertension. In these circumstances some investigators recommend twice weekly (i.e. after 72 hours) with additional testing performed for maternal or fetal deterioration regardless of the time elapsed since the last test. Generally daily NST is recommended with severe preeclampsia remote from term. Reference: Williams Obs. 23/e, p 339
