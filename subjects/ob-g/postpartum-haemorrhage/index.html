Sample MCQ #3

A G2P1 had an uneventful vaginal delivery of a 3.4 kg baby girl. Placenta was completely expulsed soon after delivery. After delivery of placenta there was excessive vaginal bleeding uterus was hard and contracted. Most likely cause of bleeding would be:

AAtonic PPH
BRetained placental tissue
CCervical tear
DPelvic hematoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Cervical tear

Cervical tear is the commonest cause of traumatic postpartum haemorrhage. Clinical feature: Excessive vaginal bleeding immediately following delivery in presence of a hard and contracted uterus. Repair should be done under general anesthesia, in lithotomy position with a good light. Reference: D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 29

Sample MCQ #4

In case of severe PPH blood loss is more than:

A500 ml
B1 L
C1.5 L
D2 L
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 2 L

The average blood loss is vaginal delivery: 500 ml, caesarean delivery: 1000 ml and caesarean hysterectomy:1500 ml. Depending upon the amount of blood loss, PPH can be Minor ( Major (> 1L) or Severe (> 2L). Reference:D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 28

Sample MCQ #5

Pathogenicity of PPH presented by four T's include all of the following except:

AAtonic uterus
BTissue reaction
CDefect in coagulation
DInjury to birth canal
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Tissue reaction

Four basic pathologies are expressed as the four Ts&rsquo; (RCOG): Tone (atonicity), Tissue (retained bits, blood clots), Trauma (genital tract injury) and Thrombin (coagulopathy). Reference:D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 28

Sample MCQ #7

All of the following conditions can lead to PPH except:

ARetained placental tissue
BPlacenta previa
CEctopic pregnancy
DVaginal tear

✓ Correct Answer: C. Ectopic pregnancy

Haemorrhage in early pregnancy: abortion, ectopic pregnancy, hydatidiform mole and implantation bleeding. PPH: bleeding after 3 rd stage of labor (after delivery) Reference:D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of obstetrics 8 th edition, chapter 16

Sample MCQ #8

Likely size of uterus at 8 weeks postpartum is:

A100 gm
B500 gm
C700 gm
D900 gm
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 100 gm

Weight of uterus : Immediately after delivery - 1000 gm At the end of 1 week - 500 gm At the end of 2 weeks - 300 gm At the end of 4 weeks it weighs - 100 gm (Pre-pregnant state). Also Know : Immediately following delivery, the fundus is just below the umbilicus (13.5 cm above the symphysis pubis/ 20 weeks gestational age size.) Involution of the uterus After 24 hours of delivery, height of uterus decreases by 1.25 cm/day. Uterus is a pelvic to organ. by the end of 2 weeks. Uterus returns almost to its normal size (pre pregnant size) by the end of 8 weeks. The process by which the postpartum uterus returns to its pre pregnant state is called Involution . Involution is achieved by decrease in the size of muscle fibres (and not in the number). Reference : Dutta Obs. 7/e, pg 145; Williams Obs. 24/e, pg 669
