Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following are screening views in mammography?

ACC
BMLO
CBoth of the above
DOblique
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Both of the above

Mammography is primarily a screening and not a diagnostic tool. The mediolateral oblique (MLO) and craniocaudal (CC) views are standard screening views, whereas additional views described used mainly for diagnostic evaluation of lesions. Reference: Primer of diagnostic Imaging, sixth edition Page number - 561
