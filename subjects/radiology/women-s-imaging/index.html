Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following are screening views in mammography?

ACC
BMLO
CBoth of the above
DOblique
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Both of the above

Mammography is primarily a screening and not a diagnostic tool. The mediolateral oblique (MLO) and craniocaudal (CC) views are standard screening views, whereas additional views described used mainly for diagnostic evaluation of lesions. Reference: Primer of diagnostic Imaging, sixth edition Page number - 561

Sample MCQ #4

Doppler ultrasound in first trimester is used for?

AFetal monitoring
BFetal anemia evaluation
CDetection of aneuploidy
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Detection of aneuploidy

In the first trimester, the focus of Doppler US is (a) detection of aneuploidy and an increased risk for congenital heart disease with evaluation of the ductus venosus waveform, and (b) screening for women more likely to develop preeclampsia by evaluation of the uterine artery waveform. Reference: A Radiologist&rsquo;s Guide to the Performance and Interpretation of Obstetric Doppler US Anne M. Kennedy and Paula J. Woodward RadioGraphics 2019 39:3, page number 893-910

Sample MCQ #5

What is the normal limit of endometrium in postmenopausal women?

A5 mm
B10 mm
C20 mm
D1 mm
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 5 mm

A double-layer thickness of less than 5 mm without focal thickening excludes significant disease and is consistent with atrophy. Homogeneous, smooth endometrium measuring 5 mm or less are considered within the normal range with or without hormonal replacement therapy Reference: Imaging the Endometrium: Disease and Normal Variants Kenneth M. Nalaboff, John S. Pellerito, and Eran Ben-Levi RadioGraphics 2001 21:6,page number 1409-1424

Sample MCQ #6

Breast within a Breast on Mammography is seen in?

AFibrosarcoma
BDuctal carcinoma
CLobular carcinoma
DFibroadenolipoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Fibroadenolipoma

The breast within a breast appearence refers to the mammographic appearance of breast hamartomas (fibroadenolipomas). These benign lesions are well-circumscribed and as the name goes fibro-adeno-lipomas contain a mixture of fibrous, glandular and fatty tissue resembling a normal breast which also has fibroglandular and fatty tissue.
