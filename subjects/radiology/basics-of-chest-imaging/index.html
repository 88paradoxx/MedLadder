Sample MCQ #1

MRI is based on the principles of ?

ANuclear magnetic resonance
BMagnetic axis
CRandom magnetic motion
DPiezoelectric effect
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Nuclear magnetic resonance

Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) is based on the principles of nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR). Ultrasound is based on Piezoelectric effect

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following arteries is not visualised in 'triple rule out' CT?

AThoracic aorta
BPulmonary artery
CCoronary artery
DSubclavian artery
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Subclavian artery

Triple-rule-out CT (TRO CT) angiography may be ordered in the setting of acute chest pain to examine the thoracic aorta and the coronary and pulmonary arteries. The protocol helps exclude life-threatening causes of acute chest pain, especially if atypical, or if alternative causes to acute coronary syndrome (ACS) are being entertained Reference : Czarniecki, M., Iqbal, S. Triple-rule-out CT. Reference article, Radiopaedia.org. https://doi.org/10.53347/rID-54142

Sample MCQ #3

An anechoic cystic lesion in the liver which shows few peripheral specks of calcification is seen in?

ALiver abscess
BLiver hemangioma
CLiver hydatid cyst
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Liver hydatid cyst

Hydatid cysts result from infection by the Echinococcus tapeworm species and can result in cyst formation The USG features of hydatid cyst are: The Gharbi ultrasound classification consists of five stages: stage 1: homogeneously hypoechogenic cystic thin-walled lesion stage 2: septated cystic lesion stage 3: cystic lesion with daughter lesions stage 4: pseudo-tumor lesion stage 5: calcified or partially calcified lesion (inactive cyst) Reference: AIIMS-MAMC-PGI's Comprehensive textbook of diagnostic radiology page number 1893

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following finding is not seen in hepatitis?

AHypoechoic liver
BGall bladder wall edema
CIrregular mucosal surface of gall bladder
DFree fluid in abdomen
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Irregular mucosal surface of gall bladder

Acute hepatitis USG features include: hepatomegaly (most sensitive sign) >15.5 cm at the midclavicular line starry sky appearance with decreased echotexture of liver and increased brightness of portal radicles gallbladder wall thickening the overall echotexture is often decreased Reference: AIIMS-MAMC-PGI's Comprehensive textbook of diagnostic radiology page Number 1847

Sample MCQ #5

Which of the following imaging findings is not seen in biliary atresia?

AIrregular mucosal surface of gall bladder
BTriangular cord sign
CGall bladder wall edema
DEchogenic wall of right portal vein
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Gall bladder wall edema

Biliary atresia (BA) is a congenital biliary disorder, which is characterized by an absence of the extrahepatic biliary tree. It is one of the most common causes of neonatal cholestasis, often causing cirrhosis immediately and leading to death THe USG features include: echogenic fibrous tissue anterior to the right portal vein: triangular cord sign hepatic artery changes larger hepatic arterial caliber gallbladder ghost triad atretic gallbladder, length less than 19 mm irregular or lobular contour lack of smooth/complete echogenic mucosal lining with an indistinct wall Reference: AIIMS-MAMC-PGI's Comprehensive textbook of diagnostic radiology page number 1210

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following is not an indication for MRI breast?

AScreening of high risk patients
BAssessment of chest wall or pectoralis muscle involvement
CAssessment of silicone implant integrity
DAssessment of fibroadenoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Assessment of fibroadenoma

Indications for breast MRI include screening of high risk patients, assessment of extent of ipsilateral disease in newly diagnosed breast cancer patients, assessment of chest wall or pectoralis muscle involvement, evaluation of the contralateral breast, surveillance for residual/recurrent disease after breast conservation surgery, evaluation of response to chemotherapy, further evaluation of mammographic or palpable abnormality, evaluation of axillary carcinoma with unknown primary, and assessment of silicone implant integrity Reference: Primer of diagnostic Imaging, sixth edition Pg 604

Sample MCQ #7

When is a mass said to be present in the breast parenchyma according to BI-RADS lesion?

AWhen a lesion is palpable
BWhen it is seen in two different projections
CWhen it is associated with nipple discharge
DWhen it is associated with pain
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. When it is seen in two different projections

A mass is defined by the BI-RADS lexicon as a space-occupying lesion that is seen in two different projections. The BI-RADS lexicon is employed to describe features of masses including shape, margins, and density. Additional features to consider include associated calcifications, effects on surrounding tissue, architectural distortion, "halo" sign, satellite lesions, multiple lesions, and stability. Reference: Primer of diagnostic Imaging, sixth edition Page number - 612

Sample MCQ #8

Which of the following is not a suspicious finding of breast malignancy on USG?

ASpiculated margins
BTaller than wide
CAcoustic shadowing
DAcoustic enhancement
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Acoustic enhancement

The suspicious sonographic findings can include: surface characteristics (spiculation, angular margins, and micro lobulations); shapes (taller than wide, duct extension, and branch pattern); and internal characteristics (acoustic shadowing, hypoechoic echotexture, and calcifications). Reference: Diagnostic ultrasound, Carol M. Rumack, Deborah Levine, 5th edition page number - 792
