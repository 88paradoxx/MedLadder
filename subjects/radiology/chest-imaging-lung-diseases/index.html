Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following type of mucor mycosis is commonly seen in COVID 19 pneumonia

AGIT
BLung
CRhino cerebral mucor mycosis
DHeart
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Rhino cerebral mucor mycosis

Mucor mycosis can involve nose, sinuses, orbit, central nervous system (CNS), lung (pulmonary), gastrointestinal tract (GIT), skin, jaw bones, joints, heart, kidney, and mediastinum (invasive type), but ROCM is the commonest variety. It should be noted that term ROCM refers to the entire spectrum ranging from limited sino nasal disease (sino-nasal tissue invasion), limited rhino-orbital disease (progression to orbits) to rhino-orbital-cerebral disease (CNS involvement). The area of involvement may differ due to underlying condition. Reference: Singh, Awadhesh Kumar et al. &ldquo;Mucor mycosis in COVID-19: A systematic review of cases reported worldwide and in India.&rdquo; Diabetes & metabolic syndrome vol. 15,4 (2021): 102146. doi:10.1016/j.dsx.2021.05.019

ALocalized area of dilation highly suggestive of a foreign body./removed with flexible bronchoscopy.
BFocal area of decrease air entry confirms the diagnosis
CIn complete obstruction, ball valve mechanism to such condition
DPrefer way of removing is rigid bronchoscopy

✓ Correct Answer: D. Prefer way of removing is rigid bronchoscopy

Unilateral hemithorax in a child with sudden breathing difficulty suggests foreign body in lungs. It is removed by rigid bronchoscope, not partial. Focal areas of reduced air entry can also occur in collapse; it doesn't confirm the diagnosis. Ball valve mechanism occurs in partial obstruction not complete.

Sample MCQ #3

All are features of Kerley A lines except?

AProminent Interlobular Septa
BDue to lymphatic engorgement and edema of septa
CLocated At Base Of Lungs
DSeen In CHF
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Located At Base Of Lungs

Kerley A lines are Perihilar Lines Kerley B lines are thin horizontal parallel lines perpendicular to pleura at base of Lung Kerley lines on a chest X-ray are thin linear opacities that can be associated with congestive heart failure (CHF). They are often classified into three types: Interstitial Kerley B Lines: Appear as short, horizontal lines at the lung periphery. Indicate interstitial edema, common in CHF. Septal Lines (Kerley A Lines) Vertical lines extending from the hila to the periphery. Result from fluid accumulation in the interlobular septa. Peribronchial Cuffing (Kerley A Lines): Short lines near the hila, representing fluid around bronchi. Suggestive of peribronchial edema. In CHF: X-ray findings often include cardiomegaly (enlarged heart). Increased vascular markings due to fluid retention. Pleural effusions may be present.

Sample MCQ #4

All are features of TOF on chest x ray except

ABoot shaped heart
BUpward pointed cardiac apex
CPulmonary plethora
DRight ventricular hypertrophy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Pulmonary plethora

In Tetralogy of Fallot (TOF), there is a characteristic decrease in pulmonary vascularity(pulmonary oligemia ) rather than pulmonary plethora. This is due to the obstruction of the right ventricular outflow tract (pulmonary stenosis), which results in decreased blood flow to the lungs. As a result, there is reduced vascularity in the lung fields, which can be seen on a chest X-ray. The "boot-shaped heart" appearance on chest X-ray is due to the right ventricular hypertrophy and narrowing of the pulmonary artery, which causes the heart to take on a boot-like shape. Right ventricular hypertrophy occurs because the right ventricle has to work harder to pump blood through the narrowed pulmonary valve. The upward pointed cardiac apex is typically seen in TOF with displaced to the left and may appear rounded or boot-shaped.

Sample MCQ #8

A 2 year old child with sudden onset of breathlessness, wheezing and decreased breath sounds in rt lung. Chest radiograph shows diffuse opacity in right hemithorax. What is the probable diagnosis?

ABronchial asthma
BPneumonia
CForeign body aspiration
DPneumothorax
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Foreign body aspiration

FB aspiration is most often seen in infants and toddlers (8 months-3 years). The clinical presentation may be acute while more often the symptoms remain indolent. The symptoms and signs can mimic asthma, upper respiratory infection, or pneumonia. The history of aspiration is often not elicited. FB may lead to partial ("ball-valve" effect) or complete obstruction, resulting in hyperinflation or collapse, respectively. Chest radiograph findings include asymmetric lung aeration, lung consolidation or atelectasis, and even pneumothorax or pneumomediastinum. Inspiratory radiographs alone may be normal in up to a third of patients (14-35%) with FBs. The lung volume of the affected lung/segment may be normal, increased, or decreased. Reference: AIIMS-MAMC-PGI's Comprehensive textbook of diagnostic radiology, pg 1052
