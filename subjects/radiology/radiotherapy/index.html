Sample MCQ #1

Remote after loading is used in:-

ABrachytherapy
BElectron beam
CProton beam
DCyber knife therapy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Brachytherapy

In afterloading procedures, an unloaded applicator is inserted first and the radioactive sources are introduced later . Afterloading has been utilized in many recently described uterine applicators, but the radiation exposure of physicians, nurses, and visitors during the treatment remains a serious problem. In principle, complete elimination of all unnecessary radiation exposure is possible by 'remote after-loading ,' which has been used for small teleradium bombs for more than twenty-five years and is widely applied in industrial radiography with gamma-ray sources. KEY TAKEAWAY - In remote after loading radiation exposure to the surrounding tissue is reduced. Reference: Remote Afterloading with Intracavitary Applicators Ulrich K. Henschke, Basil S. Hilaris, and G.D. Mahan Radiology 1964 83:2, 344-345

Sample MCQ #5

What is clinically target volume?

AAll gross disease including the abnormally enlarged lymph nodes
BGross disease and the regions which harbour the microscopic disease
CMargin around the tissue that allow the variations in treatment set up
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Gross disease and the regions which harbour the microscopic disease

Gross tumour volume (GTV) is all known gross disease including abnormally enlarged lymph nodes. The clinical target volume (CTV) encompasses the GTV plus regions considered to harbour potential microscopic disease. The planning target volume (PTV) provides a margin around the CTV to allow for variations in treatment setup and other anatomic motions during treatment such as respiration. In order to minimize any variation in patient positioning, meticulous immobilization is essential. Reference : Mehta, SR & Suhag, Virender & Semwal, Manoj & Sharma, Neelam. (2010). Radiotherapy: Basic Concepts and Recent Advances. Medical Journal Armed Forces India. 66. 158-162. 10.1016/S0377-1237(10)80132-7 page 5

Sample MCQ #8

What does a Linear accelerator (LINAC) produce in radiotherapy?

AAlpha and beta rays
BX-Rays and gamma rays
CX-rays and electrons
DNeutrons and positrons
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. X-rays and electrons

Explanation: LINACs produce both X-rays and electrons , making them versatile for treating different tumor sites. A Linear Accelerator (LINAC) is the most widely used machine for external beam radiotherapy. It works by accelerating electrons at high speed and then using them in two ways: The electrons themselves can be used directly for treatment. Or they can be made to strike a target, producing high-energy X-rays . These X-rays or electrons are then shaped and directed precisely at the patient's tumour to destroy cancer cells while minimising damage to surrounding healthy tissue. LINACs can deliver radiation using modern techniques like IMRT, VMAT, IGRT, SRS, and SBRT .
