Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following parameter gives accurate assessment of Gestational Age in first trimester?

ABiparietal Diameter
BAbdominal Circumference
CFemur Length
DCrown Rump Length
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Crown Rump Length

Biparietal Diameter is for Gestational age in second trimester Abdominal Circumference is to evaluate fetal growth -IUGR/macrosomia Femur length is used to measure gestational age in late third trimester Crown rump length is most accurate for gestational age in first trimester and most accurate for Expected Date of Delivery Reference: David sutton textbook of radiology edition 8 chapter 47

Sample MCQ #2

Wormian bones are seen with all except?

ARickets
BScurvy
CDown's syndrome
DHypothyroidism
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Scurvy

Wormian bones (also known as intrasutural bones ) is the name given to the additional small bones sometimes found between the cranial sutures of the bones of the skull vault, most commonly in relation to the lambdoid suture Mnemonic P: pyknodysostosis O: osteogenesis imperfecta R: rickets K: kinky hair syndrome C: cleidocranial Dysostosis H: hypothyroidism/hypophosphatemia O: otopalatodigital syndrome P: primary acroosteolysis (Hajdu-Cheney)/pachydermoperiostosis/progeria S: syndrome of Downs Reference: Yochum and Rowe's essentials of skeletal radiology, third edition, volume 1 page A-2

Sample MCQ #3

Bone marrow scintigraphy is done using?

ATc-99m sulphur colloid
BTc-99m MDG
CTc-99m Na
DTc-99m IOP
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Tc-99m sulphur colloid

Tc-99m sulphur colloid is taken up by Reticuloendothelial system like macrophages and bone marrow Reference: Mc-Graw Hill speciality Board review Radiology page number 727

Sample MCQ #4

What is used in GFR assessment?

ATc-99m DSMA
BTc-99m DTPA
CTc-99m sulphur colloid
DTc-99m MBG
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Tc-99m DTPA

99mTc diethylenetriamine penta-acetic acid (99mTc DTPA) is almost entirely filtered through the glomerulus, and therefore is useful to measure glomerular filtration rate (GFR). Reference: Mc-Graw Hill speciality Board review Radiology page number 749

Sample MCQ #5

'Creeping fat' sign is seen in?

AUlcerative colitis
BCrohn's disease
CStricture
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Crohn's disease

The creeping fat sign or wrapping fat refers to the fibrofatty proliferation of the mesenteric tissue adjacent to chronic inflamed bowel loops and has been considered to be a finding characteristic of Crohn disease. Reference: Berry radiology book, page number 1786

Sample MCQ #6

MRI is considered better than CT because?

AIt is less time consuming
BIt does not utilise ionising radiation
CIt is cheap
DIt can be used in emergency situations.

✓ Correct Answer: B. It does not utilise ionising radiation

MRI is Non ionising technique using Radiofrequency waves MRI has Long Acquisition time and is time consuming,hence cannot be used in emergencies. Reference: Pathobiology of human disease, 2014

Sample MCQ #7

A 70-year-old male patient named Alkeshbhai, a known case of bronchial asthma, presented to the emergency room with acute exacerbation, with severe breathlessness, chest tightness & dizziness. Physician immediately prescribed him salbutamol nebulization. Salbutamol inhalation did not improve the condition of the patient. Later, intravenous corticosteroids and aminophylline were added and the condition improved. What is the mechanism of action of corticosteroids in this condition?

AThey cause bronchodilation when given with xanthenes
BThey increase bronchial responsiveness to salbutamol
CThey increase the action of aminophylline on adenosine receptors
DThey increase the mucociliary clearance
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. They increase bronchial responsiveness to salbutamol

Mechanism of action of steroids in acute attack of bronchial asthma is to increase the bronchodilatory effect of beta 2 agonists, whereas in prophylaxis, their major mechanism is anti-inflammatory. Glucocorticoids are useful in both treatment and prophylaxis of bronchial asthma. Reference: Essentials of Medical Pharmacology by KD Tripathi, 8 th Edition, Page Number-249.

Sample MCQ #8

A 23-year-old young man with a known history of heroin addiction is brought to the emergency room in an unconscious state. On examination, the patient has decreased bowel sounds, depressed respiration, hypothermia, shallow breathing, and pinpoint pupils. Which of the following is the best and fast onset treatment of choice for this patient?

AOral naltrexone
BIV naloxone
COral diazepam
DOral buprenorphine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. IV naloxone

The decreased bowel sounds (constipation), respiratory depression, pinpoint pupils and history of heroin addiction strongly points toward the diagnosis of acute opioid poisoning. The drug of choice for acute opioid poisoning is intravenous naloxone. Reference: Kaplan & Sadock's Comprehensive Textbook of Psychiatry, 9th Edition, Page Number 1375-76,1384; Essentials of Medical Pharmacology by KD Tripathi, 8th Edition, Page Number 511-512.
