Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is used in cancer staging ?

APET-CT
BUSG
CX rays
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. PET-CT

PET-CT is preferred for cancer staging as it evaluates lymph node involvement and metastatic disease in cancer patients. Reference: Jacobson FL, McKean SC. Chapter 105. Introduction to Radiology. In: McKean SC, Ross JJ, Dressler DD, Brotman DJ, Ginsberg JS. eds. Principles and Practice of Hospital Medicine.

Sample MCQ #2

Gastric emptying studies using radionuclides are used in?

AGERD
BGastroparesis
CPostprandial nausea and vomiting
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Gastric emptying studies (GES) using radionuclides are performed to evaluate gastric motor function. They are often considered the "gold standard", and "most physiologic" examinations, because of their noninvasive nature, use a physiologic liquid or solid meal, and the resultant quantitative information. Typical indications for GES are evaluation of symptoms such as (postprandial) nausea, vomiting, or abdominal pain, gastroesophageal reflux disease, difficult glycemic control in diabetic patients, suspected or known gastroparesis, and assessing response to therapy. Reference: Mc-Graw Hill speciality Board review Radiology page number 739

Sample MCQ #3

What is the investigation of choice for HCC?

ANCCT
BCECT
CUSG
DCEUS
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. CECT

The investigation of choice for HCC is CECT abdomen. CT evaluation of patients with suspected HCC should be done using multiphasic contrast imaging of the liver. HCC shows early arterial enhancement with Rapid Washout

Sample MCQ #4

A 18 year old female patient with primary amenorrhea. On USG examinaltion an echogenic tubular cystic mass with internal echoes was obtained. What is the most probable diagnosis?

AImperforate hymen
BUterine agenesis
COvarian agenesis
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Imperforate hymen

Imperforate hymen is a congenital condition in which the hymen lacks a normal opening. The usual initial imaging modality of diagnosis, it shows a distended fluid-filled vagina (hematocolpos) and uterus (hematometra) with internal echoes representing hemorrhage of various ages. Reference: Imaging the Endometrium: Disease and Normal Variants Kenneth M. Nalaboff, John S. Pellerito, and Eran Ben-Levi RadioGraphics 2001 21:6, page number 1409-1424

Sample MCQ #5

A person is sitting on a 'fast unto death'. As a doctor, you will not allow the person to die because of

ADeclaration of Malta
BDeclaration of Sydney
CArticle 21 of Indian Constitution
DArticle 14 of Indian Constitution
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Article 21 of Indian Constitution

Article 21 of Constitution of India: This ensures 'Right to life,' but does not include the right to die. Hence, forcible feeding of persons going on hunger strike is lawful in India. Declaration of Malta: This deals with respecting the autonomy of the patient and suggests that the doctor should not feed forcibly in such cases. Feeding is allowed when the patient has lapsed into a coma. Article 14 of the Constitution of India: "The State shall not deny to any person equality before the law or the equal protection of the laws within the territory of India." Declaration of Sydney: Deals with the determination of death and the recovery of organs. Reference: The Essentials of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology by KS Narayan Reddy, 35th Edition, Page Number-260.

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following is not radio-opaque?

AIodine
BBarium
CSaline
DSteel
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Saline

The saline is a radio-lucent substance and is not seen on CXR. Iodine, barium and steel are radio-opaque and are easily seen on x rays. Reference: Bell, D. Contrast medium. Reference article, Radiopaedia.org. https://doi.org/10.53347/rID-69081

Sample MCQ #7

Sion procedure is used for visualisation of which of the following structures?

AUterus
BFallopian tubes
COvaries
DCervix
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Fallopian tubes

Normal tubes are isoechoic and are not usually visualised by TVS unless there is fluid contrast. The use of fluid contrast for evaluation of tubes has been described in literature as SION TEST from Mumbai or as SION PROCEDURE of sonosalpingography. Reference: Ultrasound in obstetrics and gynecology by Dr. P. K. shah, Dr. Mandakini megh, Dr. Nandita palshetkar page number 17

Sample MCQ #8

A patient presented with left iliac fossa pain. On USG examination, a large well defined anechoic cystic lesion was found arising from the left ovary. What is the most probable diagnosis?

ASerous cystadenoma
BMucinous cystadenoma
CDermoid cyst
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Serous cystadenoma

Benign serous cystadenomas appear as sharply marginated, anechoic masses that may be large and are usually unilocular. Internal thin walled septations and papillary projections may be seen (in borderline tumours). Reference: Ultrasound in obstetrics and gynecology by Dr. P. K. shah, Dr. Mandakini megh, Dr. Nandita palshetkar page number 41
