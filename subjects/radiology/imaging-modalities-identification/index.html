Sample MCQ #3

On which of the following modalities is junctional zone of endometrium best visualised

ACT Scan
BMRI
CUSG
DHSG
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. MRI

MRI was the first imaging technique to permit the visualization of the uterine junctional zone and remains the imaging method of choice to evaluate it and its associated pathology. Junctional zone is a region representing the inner myometrium and is a very important imaging feature in pelvic MR imaging for interpretation of various pathologies. In its intact state, it is usually visualized on MRI as a low T2 signal layer beneath the endometrium. Reference: AIIMS-MAMC-PGI&rsquo;s Comprehensive textbook of diagnostic radiology page number 224

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following modalities is most useful in assessing the metastasis of colorectal carcinomas?

AMRI
BCECT
CXRAY
DUSG
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. CECT

For detecting metastasis of colorectal carcinomas, CECT is helpful as it covers the whole abdomen in it's field and hepatic metastasis can be seen. MRI is used for local staging of disease and in evaluation of local extension into mesorectal fascia. Reference: AIIMS-MAMC-PGI&rsquo;s Comprehensive textbook of diagnostic radiology page number 1760

Sample MCQ #5

Investigation of choice in pheochromocytoma is:

ACT scan
BUrinary catecholamines
CMIBG scan
DMRI Scan
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Urinary catecholamines

Answer is B (Urinary Catecholamines): Pheochromocytomas synthesize and store catecholamines which include norepinephrine, epinephrine and dopamine. The investigation of choice for diagnosis of Pheochromocytomas is determination of elevated levels of catecholamines and their methylated metabolites (metanephrines) in the plasma and urine. CT scan, MRI and MBIG Scintography are all useful diagnostic modalities for localization of pheochromocytoma once the diagnosis is established.

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following investigation modalities help in categorising the focal hepatic lesions into benign and malignant?

ACT Scan
BUSG
CMRI
DXray fluoroscopy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging, MRI has more advantages than ultrasound, computed tomography, CT, positron emission tomography, PET, or any other imaging modality in diagnosing focal hepatic masses. With a combination of basic T1 and T2 weighted sequences, diffusion weighted imaging, DWI, and hepatobiliary gadolinium contrast agents, that is gadobenate dimeglumine (Gd-BOPTA) and gadoxetic acid (Gd-EOB), most liver lesions can be adequately diagnosed and differentiated into benign and malignant lesions. Reference: AIIMS-MAMC-PGI's Comprehensive textbook of diagnostic radiology page number 1656

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following imaging modalities is used to evaluated tube patency?

ASIS
BHSG
CInfusion of saline agitated with air bubbles
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Hysterosalpingography (HSG) has been the traditional imaging modality of choice to evaluate tube patency, newer techniques involving agitated saline and ultrasound contrast agents injected during sono hysterography (SIS) have shown promise as an alternate imaging technique. Although the injection of saline agitated with air bubbles when performing SIS can confirm tube patency Ultrasound contrast "microbubbles" last longer and can be imaged with real-time cine clips and reconstructed two-dimensional and three-dimensional images to confirm tube patency Reference: Callen's Ultrasonography in Obstetrics and Gynecology, sixth edition page number 949

Sample MCQ #8

Which of the following modalities is now preferred for pretreatment staging of rectal cancer?

ACT scan
BMRI
CBarium Enema
DEUS
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. MRI

MRI is the modality of choice for rectal cancer staging. The high soft-tissue contrast of MRI accurately assesses the extramural tumor spread and relation to mesorectal fascia and the sphincter complex. MRI is the modality of choice for staging rectal cancer to assist surgeons in obtaining negative surgical margins. MRI facilitates the accurate assessment of mesorectal fascia and the sphincter complex for surgical planning. Reference: AIIMS-MAMC-PGI&rsquo;s Comprehensive textbook of diagnostic radiology page number 1766
