Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is not an ionising radiation?

AX rays
BGamma rays
CBeta rays
DUltrasound waves
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Ultrasound waves

Ionizing radiation is the term given to forms of radiation that are energetic enough to displace orbiting electrons from the atoms in the absorbing medium, thus forming positive ions. High Energy Electromagnetic Radiation like X Rays, Gamma Rays, and Cosmic Rays are Ionising Radiation Particulate Radiation Like Alpha, Beta, Protons, Neutrons, and Electrons are Ionising Radiation. Most other electromagnetic radiation like UV rays, Visible light, Infrared Rays, Radiowaves are Nonionising Sound waves used in Ultrasound are also Nonionising. Reference: Bell, D. Ionizing radiation. Reference article, Radiopaedia.org. https://doi.org/10.53347/rID-59177

Sample MCQ #3

Greatest risk to foetus for malformations due to radiation exposure is between ______ weeks.

A2 to 7
B10-15
C15-25
D>25
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 2 to 7

The embryo/foetus is most susceptible to teratogenic effects of radiation during 2-7 weeks as this is the period of organogenesis The foetus is more resistant to the radiation during the second and third trimester. Reference : Yoon I, Slesinger TL. Radiation Exposure In Pregnancy. [Updated 2022 May 8]. In: Stat Pearls [Internet]. Treasure Island (FL): Stat Pearls Publishing; 2022 Jan.

Sample MCQ #4

A 7 week pregnant lady underwent a Chest X ray by mistake, what is to be done?

AImmediate Termination of Pregnancy
BDo Preinvasive Test
CDo Chromosomal Testing
DContinue Pregnancy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Continue Pregnancy

Accidental Exposure of Radiation during pregnancy-reassure the patient and continue the pregnancy as the threshold required for fetal damage is very high and termination is usually not advised unless very high exposure Above 10RAD, which is highly unlikely with routine diagnostic modalities.

Sample MCQ #5

All are True about modern Xray tube except?

AIt has a rotatory Cathode
BHeat loss is mainly by Radiation
CRhenium is added to tungsten target at Anode
DChief source of Xray generation is by Bremstrahlung effect
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. It has a rotatory Cathode

Modern X ray tube has a rotatory anode, not cathode Motor is attached to the anode to make it rotatory The Cathode is made of Tungsten alloyed with Thorium The Target of Anode is made of Tungsten Alloyed with Rhenium The major source of X RAY Generation in Modern X ray tube is Bremstrahlung Radiation/Braking Radiation

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following is true statement regarding radiation ?

ARapidly dividing cells are resistant to Radiation
BGI mucosa is one of the most radioresistant tissue in the body
CThe intensity of Radiation is inversely proportional to the square of distance from the source
DSmall blood vessels are least resistant to radiation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. The intensity of Radiation is inversely proportional to the square of distance from the source

According to Inverse square law in radiation, the intensity of radiation is inversely proportional to the square of distance from the source. Rapidly dividing cells , which are less differentiated are actually more radiosensitive Amongst the body cells, the most sensitive are spermatogonia and the least sensitive are nerve cells and muscle fibres Gi mucosa is Radiosensitive Small blood vessels are generally radioresistant
