Sample MCQ #1

Central scar in pancreas is seen in?

AMucinous neoplasm
BSerous cystadenoma of pancreas
CAcute pancreatitis
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Serous cystadenoma of pancreas

Serous cystadenoma of the pancreas On CT, typically demonstrates a multicystic, lobulated mass in the pancreatic head (like bunch of Grapes) the individual cysts are typically a characteristic enhancing central scar may be present which can show associated stellate calcification. Reference: AIIMS-MAMC-PGI's Comprehensive textbook of diagnostic radiology page number 2067

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following are seen in MRCP?

AHepatic veins and portal vein
BPancreatic ducts and bile ducts
CSplenic vessels
DIVC and aorta
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Pancreatic ducts and bile ducts

Magnetic resonance cholangiopancreatography (MRCP) is a non-invasive imaging technique to visualize the intra and extrahepatic biliary tree and pancreatic duct. Reference: AIIMS-MAMC-PGI's Comprehensive textbook of diagnostic radiology page number 1974

Sample MCQ #3

'Chain of lakes appearance' is seen in?

AAcute pancreatitis
BChronic pancreatitis
CWalled off necrosis
DPseudocyst
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Chronic pancreatitis

Dilatation and beading of the pancreatic duct often with intraductal calcifications, could give a 'chain of lakes' appearance in chronic pancreatitis on MRCP/ERCP images. Reference: AIIMS-MAMC-PGI's Comprehensive textbook of diagnostic radiology page Number 2032
