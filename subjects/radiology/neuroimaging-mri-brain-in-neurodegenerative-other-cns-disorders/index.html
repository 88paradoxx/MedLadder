Which MRI Technique is used to localize Speech Centres in Brain?

ADiffussion weighted MRI
BDiffussion Tensor Imaging
CFunctional MRI
DMR-Spectroscopy

✓ Correct Answer: C. Functional MRI

Functional MRI is based on BOLD technique, Blood O2 Level Dependent technique and is used to localize speech centres, fine motor areas and Brain Mapping. Sample MCQ #2

Some of the other contrast agents used in MRI are ___ and ___

AManganese and tin oxide
BManganese and iron oxide
CMagnesium and tin oxide
DMagnesium and iron oxide
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Manganese and iron oxide

Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) contrast agents are either paramagnetic ion complexes or superparamagnetic magnetite particles and contain lanthanide elements such as gadolinium (Gd3+) or transition metal manganese (Mn2+). These elements shorten the T1 or T2 relaxation time, thereby causing increased signal intensity on T1-weighted images or reduced signal intensity on T2-weighted images. Most paramagnetic contrast agents are positive agents. These agents shorten the T1, so the enhanced parts appear bright on T1-weighted images. Dysprosium, superparamagnetic agents and ferromagnetic agents are negative contrast agents. The enhanced parts appear darker on T2-weighted images. Reference: AIIMS-MAMC-PGI's Comprehensive textbook of diagnostic radiology page number 772
