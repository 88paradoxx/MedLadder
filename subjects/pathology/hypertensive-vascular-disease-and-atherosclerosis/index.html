DSegmental nature of the involvement

✓ Correct Answer: C. Most commonly involved artery is abdominal aorta

Ans. is 'c' i.e., Most commonly involved artery is abdominal aorta It is the most common form of systemic vasculitis in adults, is an acute and chronic, often granulomatous, inflammation of arteries of large to small size. It affects principally the arteries in the head-especially the temporal arteries— but also the vertebral and ophthalmic arteries and the aorta, where it may cause thoracic aortic aneurysm

Sample MCQ #3

Which of following gross features is/are seen in specimen of benign nephrosclerosis? i. Flea-bitten appearance ii. U shape scar on cortical surface iii. Pale yellow abscess formation iv. Grain leather appearance

AOnly i, ii, and iii are correct
BOnly i and iii are correct
COnly ii and iv are correct
DOnly iv is correct
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Only iv is correct

Nephrosclerosis refers to sclerosis of small renal arteries and arterioles that is strongly associated with hypertension. The characteristic morphologic alterations of the small arterioles are called hyaline arteriolosclerosis, while the arteries show fibrous intimal and medial thickening often resulting in luminal narrowing. The vascular changes cause ischemia, which leads to various combinations of interstitial fibrosis, tubular atrophy, and focal global glomerulosclerosis. Grossly, the kidneys are symmetrically atrophic. Typically the renal surface shows diffuse, fine granularity that resembles grain leather. Others are the features of malignant nephrosclerosis. Reference : Robbins Basic Pathology, 10th edition, Page- 570

Sample MCQ #5

A 7-year-old male patient having 3-year history of coughing and intermittent respiratory distress. He now presented in casualty with respiratory distress, he also feels chest tightness, cough with sputum and wheezing. He uses accessory muscle during distress. Laboratory findings revealed an elevated serum IgE level and peripheral blood eosinophilia. A sputum sample examined microscopically shows increased numbers of eosinophils. Which of the following histologic features is most likely to characterize the disease he is suffering?

ADilation of respiratory bronchioles with loss of elastic fibers
BInflammatory destruction of bronchial walls
CInterstitial and alveolar edema with hyaline membrane formation
DRemodeling of airways with smooth muscle hyperplasia and increased submucosal glands
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Remodeling of airways with smooth muscle hyperplasia and increased submucosal glands

(d) Patients long history and classic symptoms of chest tightness, cough with sputum and wheezing along with elevated serum IgE level, peripheral blood eosinophilia and eosinophils in sputum are suggestive of asthma. Characteristic histopathological findings in asthma are: Thickening of airway wall Sub-basement membrane fibrosis (due to deposition of type I and III collagens) Increased vascularity Increase in the size of the submucosal glands and number of airway goblet cells Hypertrophy and/or hyperplasia of the bronchial wall muscle with increased extracellular matrix Reference: Robbins 10th Ed, pg 686

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following is seen in kidney in malignant hypertension?

AHyaline necrosis
BFibrinoid necrosis
CMedial wall hyperplasia
DMicroaneurysm
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Fibrinoid necrosis

Hyperplastic arteriolosclerosis. This lesion occurs in severe hypertension; vessels exhibit concentric, laminated ("onionskin") thickening of the walls with luminal narrowing The laminations consist of SMCs with thickened, reduplicated basement membrane; in malignant hypertension, they are accompanied by fibrinoid deposits and vessel wall necrosis (necrotizing arteriolitis) , particularly in the kidney Reference : Robbins 10th/pg 493

Sample MCQ #8

A 12-year-old boy develops fever, accompanied by occasional headaches, malaise, fatigue, and nausea a month after being bitten by a dog. One day later, he experiences episodes of rigidity, hallucinations, breath holding, and difficulty swallowing because of uncontrollable oral secretions. Neurologist were consulted and immediately treatment started with some injectable medicines. Which of the pathological findings is the hallmark of the disease?

ANegri bodies
BPerivascular lymphocytes
CPseudocysts with bradyzoites
DSpongiform change
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Negri bodies

The child had rabies, an infectious disease in which the virus travels from the site of an infected animal bite up nerves to the CNS. Pathognomonic Negri bodies are cytoplasmic inclusions found in the hippocampal pyramidal cells and cerebellar Purkinje cells. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 28 pg 1266
