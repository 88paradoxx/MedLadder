Sample Lung Tumors MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A 62-year-old male patient presents with increasing chest pain, bloody sputum, and weight loss over the past 3 months. An HRCT scan reveals a mass, circumscribing the right main bronchus protruding into its lumen. Endobronchial lung biopsy was taken, based on the histopathological findings diagnosis of carcinoid tumor was given. All of the below mentioned IHC markers will shows, positivity in this tumor except:

ANSE
BSynaptophysin
CChromogranin
Dp40
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. p40

Specific markers associated with adenocarcinoma (TTF-1, napsin A) or squamous cell carcinoma (p40, p63), neuroendocrine tumor (Synaptophysin, NSE, chromogranin). Reference: Robbins 10th Ed, pg 719

Sample MCQ #2

A 63-year-old male patient having smoking history presented with shortness of breath and hemoptysis since 4 months. He is also complaining of productive cough and chest pain. Chest X-ray performed shows small mass in the right lung in the periphery. A transbronchial lung biopsy was performed and diagnosis of adenocarcinoma was made. Which IHC marker will be used to confirm the diagnosis?

Ap63
BNapsin A
CSynaptophysin
DPanCK
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Napsin A

Specific markers associated with adenocarcinoma (TTF1, napsin A) or squamous cell carcinoma (p40, p63), neuroendocrine tumor (Synaptophysin, NSE). Reference: Robbins 10th Ed, pg 719

Sample MCQ #3

A 63-year-old female with an 80-pack-per year smoking history has been complaining of weakness and constipation. She also has abdominal pain and episodes of seizure. Laboratory testing reveals a total calcium of 13.0 mg/dL. On chest X-ray, she has a probable mass in the left upper lobe of the lung near the hilum. Of the following, what would a biopsy of the mass most likely reveal?

ANeoplastic glands
BSmall neoplastic cells with a high nuclear to cytoplasm ratio
CKeratin pearls
DNoncaseating granulomas
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Keratin pearls

The patient has symptoms consistent with hypercalcemia: weakness, constipation, abdominal pain, and psychiatric symptoms. Seizures can also occur. Squamous cell carcinoma, which has keratin pearls, can produce a parathyroid-hormone-like protein that will result in hypercalcemia (C). Adenocarcinoma (A) and small cell carcinoma (B) are not usually associated with PTH production. The mass is not a pulmonary sarcoidosis (D). Reference: Robbins Basic Pathology, 10 th ed, Pg-539
