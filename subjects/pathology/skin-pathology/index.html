Sample Skin Pathology MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Basal cell layer dysplasia associated with hyper and parakeratosis is seen in

ACylindroma
BTrichoepithelioma
CActinic keratosis
DPsoriasis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Actinic keratosis

Actinic keratosis may show dysplastic changes that lead to cutaneous SCC. The stratum corneum is thickened (hyperkeratosis) and cells in this layer retain their nuclei (parakeratosis). Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition Chapter 25, pg 1145

Sample MCQ #2

Regarding histology of dysplastic nevi, all are correct except

ALinear fibrosis occurs around epidermal rete ridges involved by the nevus
BLentiginous hyperplasia is seen
CLymphocytic infiltrate may be seen in the epidermis
DPresence of cytological atypia in form of irregular nuclear contours and hyperchromasia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Lymphocytic infiltrate may be seen in the epidermis

Lymphocytic infiltrates may be seen in superficial dermis. Release of melanin from dead nevus cells into dermis may occur (melanin incontinence) and this is phagocytosed by dermal macrophages leading to a peculiar fibrosis surrounding epidermal rete ridges that are involved by nevus. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition Chapter 25, pg 1137

Sample MCQ #3

False statement regarding melanocytic nevus

AThese are benign neoplasms that have mutation leading to constitutive activation of RAS or BRAF
BEarliest lesion is a junctional nevus
CMay become more prominent in pregnancy
DLack neurotization, a phenomenon present in melanoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Lack neurotization, a phenomenon present in melanoma

At the deepest extent of melanocytic nevi, nevus cells often acquire fusiform contours and grow in fascicles resembling neural tissue, this phenomenon is called neurotization and it is absent in melanoma. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition Chapter 25, pg 1136

Sample MCQ #4

True statements about dysplastic nevus syndrome are

AAlso called familial atypical multiple mole and melanoma syndrome
BAutosomal dominant in inheritance
CIncreases risk of pancreatic cancer
DAll the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All the above

FAMMM is an autosomal dominant syndrome characterized by multiple dysplastic nevi and melanomas and in 50% cases melanomas develop by the age of 60. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition Chapter 25, pg 1137

Sample MCQ #5

Tumor depth of invasion is denoted by Breslow thickness and it is calculated from

ADeepest margin of tumor cells to upper extent of tumor
BEpidermal granular layer to deepest intraepidermal tumor cells
CStratum corneum to deepest intradermal tumor cells
DStratum Basale to deepest intradermal tumor cells
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Epidermal granular layer to deepest intraepidermal tumor cells

It is calculated from top of granular layer to deepest intradermal cells if overlying skin is intact, if ulcerated start from base of ulcer. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition Chapter 25, pg 1135

Sample MCQ #6

True statements regarding Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans

AComposed of fibroblasts arranged in storiform pattern
BMay extend to subcutis and rarely metastasizes
CImatinib can be used for unresectable tumors
DAll the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All the above

There is overexpression of PDGF beta due to translocation involving genes encoding collagen 1A1 (COL1A1) and platelet-derived growth factor-beta (PDGFB), so tyrosine kinase inhibitors are used. Although they are locally aggressive and can recur, they rarely metastasize, metastasis is seen with tumors that exhibit greater cytologic atypia. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition Chapter 25, pg 1150

Sample MCQ #7

Dermatosis Papulosa Nigra refers to

AAcanthosis nigricans
BSeborrheic keratosis
CPlaque psoriasis
DPilomatricoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Seborrheic keratosis

Multiple small seborrheic keratosis on face are termed as dermatosis papulosa nigra. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition Chapter 25, pg 1158

Sample MCQ #8

Sezary syndrome is included in the category of?

AT cell leukemia
BLymphoma
CB cell leukemia
DPigmented disorder of skin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. T cell leukemia

Mycosis fungoides is the most common form of a type of blood cancer called cutaneous T-cell lymphoma. Cutaneous T-cell lymphomas occur when certain white blood cells, called T cells , become cancerous; these cancers characteristically affect the skin, causing different types of skin lesions. Reference : Robbins Basic Pathology, 8 th edition, page number - 616
