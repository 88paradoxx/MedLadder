Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following regulates warfarin oral anticoagulant therapy?

APT
BAPTT
CBleeding time
DThrombin time
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. PT

PT is the standard test for monitoring treatment with oral anticoagulant (warfarin) inhibit carboxylation of vitamin K dependent factors (Factor II, VII, IX and X) and make these factors inactive. In a patients receiving oral anticoagulants, PT should be reported as a ratio of PT of patient to PT of control; it should not be reported as percentage. Various types of thromboplastin reagents obtained from different sources (like ox brain, rabbit brain, rabbit lung) are available commercially for PT test. Reference : Essentials of clinical pathology, 2nd edition ,page number - 332

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following enzyme is involved in ubiquitin proteosome pathway of atrophy?

AUbiquitin ligases
BUbiquitin lipases
CUbiquitin lyases
DUbiquitin hydrolases
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Ubiquitin ligases

The degradation of cellular proteins occurs mainly by ubiquitin proteosome pathway. Nutrient deficiency and disuse may activate ubiquitin ligases, which attach the small peptide ubiquitin to cellular proteins for degradation in proteasomes. This pathway is thought to be responsible for various catabolic conditions. Reference: Robbins and Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases, 19 th ed Pg:37

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is true regarding Factor V Leiden mutation?

AIncreased risk of deep vein thrombosis
BFactor V becomes resistant to cleavage by protein C
CGlutamine to Arginine substitution at 506
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Factor V Leiden mutation results in glutamine to arginine substitution at amino acid residue 506 that renders factor V resistant to cleavage and inactivation by protein C. As a result, an important antithrombotic counterregulatory pathway is lost and chances of thrombosis are highly raised Reference : Robbins 9th/pg 123

Sample MCQ #4

An 8-year-old girl experiences sudden severe dyspnea. On examination, she has upper airway obstruction from soft tissue swelling in her neck. A radiograph shows a hematoma compressing the trachea. She was then diagnosed to have hemophilia A. Both parents and two female siblings are unaffected by this problem, but a male sibling has experienced a similar episode. Which of the following genetic abnormalities is most likely to account for the findings in this girl?

AAutosomal dominant mutation
BGenomic imprinting
CGermline mosaicism
DRandom X inactivation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Random X inactivation

This girl has features of hemophilia A. This X-linked recessive condition is expected to occur in males who inherit the one maternal X chromosome with the genetic mutation, and they do not have another X chromosome with a normal functional allele, as is the case in her brother. Hemophilia in a female can be explained by the Lyon hypothesis, which states that only one X chromosome in a female is active (the &ldquo;turned off&rdquo; X chromosome is the Barr body) for most genes, but this inactivation is a random event. Some unlucky females are out on the tail end of the Poisson distribution of random events and have few active X chromosomes with the normal allele, leading to markedly diminished factor VIII activity. The other choices do not explain this phenomenon. Reference: Robins Basic Pathology 10th ed/pg 267

Sample MCQ #5

Which among the following laboratory investigation is best to reveal about bleeding in Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC)?

AIncreased PT
BIncreased aPTT
CDecreased fibrinogen
DIncreased FDPs
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Increased FDPs

Two types of DIC are acute (decompensated and chronic compensated). In acute DIC, there is rapid activation of coagulation leading to bleeding from consumption of coagulation factors. Findings in acute DIC are low platelet, prolonged PT and APTT, low fibrinogen, and increased FDP and D-dimer. In chronic DIC, there is slow activation of coagulation with slow consumption of coagulation factors. Findings are normal platelet count, normal PT and APTT. However, FDP and D-dimer are increased. So in acute DIC, all the four options are correct, while if we consider the question as chronic DIC, then best possible answer is increased FDP. Reference: Robbins & cotran pathologic basis of diseases 10 th edition, Chapter 14, pg 667

Sample MCQ #6

The following is not a contraindication for heparin in DIC

AIf the platelet count cannot be maintained at >50,000/ mcL
BIn cases of central nervous system/gastrointestinal bleeding
CPlacental abruption
DDIC associated with malignancy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. DIC associated with malignancy

About use of Heparin in DIC: Low doses of continuous infusion heparin (5-10 U/ kg per h) may be effective in patients with low-grade DIC associated with solid tumor, acute promyelocytic leukemia, or in a setting with recognized thrombosis. Heparin is also indicated for the treatment of purpura fulminans during the surgical resection of giant hemangiomas and during removal of a dead fetus. In acute DIC, the use of heparin is likely to aggravate bleeding. To date, the use of heparin in patients with severe DIC has no proven survival benefit. Reference : Robbins 9th/pg 662; 8th/pg 672

Sample MCQ #8

A 10-day infant presented with large cephalohematoma. His parents gave no history of trauma but severe umbilical bleed during his childbirth. What is the most likely factor deficiency associated?

AvWF
BFactor I def
CFactor VIII def
DFactor XIII def
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Factor XIII def

Abnormal bleeding manifests shortly after birth, when bleeding from the healthy umbilical cord remnant occurs. Umbilical stump bleeding is an uncommon presentation for other congenital bleeding disorders. Rebleeding at circumcision is also common. The most life-threatening complication of factor XIII deficiency is spontaneous intracranial hemorrhage. Reference : Robbins 9th/pg 662
