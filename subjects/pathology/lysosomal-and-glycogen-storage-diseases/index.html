Sample MCQ #2

Accumulation of sphingomyelin in phagocytic cells is feature of:

ATay-sachs disease
BGaucher's disease
CNiemann-Pick disease
DDown's syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Niemann-Pick disease

Niemann-Pick Disease Autosomal Recessive Biochemical defect Deficient activity of acid sphingomyelinase, a lysosomal enzyme encoded by a gene on chromosome 11 Accumulation of sphingomyelin and other lipids in the monocyte-macrophage system. Clinical features Failure to thrive Hepatosplenomegaly Rapidly progressive neurodegenerative course. Treatment Orthotopic liver transplantation Amniotic cell transplantation Bone marrow transplantation Miglustat A phase I trial of enzyme replacement therapy for type B NPD. Reference: Harper's 32nd edition, page 250-251

Sample MCQ #3

Lysosomal enzymes are tagged with which residue in Golgi apparatus to direct them to lysosome?

AGlucose 6 phosphate
BMannose 6 phosphate
CLecithin
DPhosphatidylserine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Mannose 6 phosphate

Mannose 6- phosphate is marker for delivering lysosomal enzymes Lysosomes are membrane-bound organelles containing roughly 40 different acid hydrolases (i.e., enzymes that function best in acidic pH - 5). These hydrolases include proteases, nucleases, lipases, glycosidases, phosphatases, and sulfatases. Lysosomal enzymes are initially synthesized in the ER lumen and then tagged with a mannose-6-phosphate (M6P) residue within the Golgi apparatus. Such M6P-modified proteins are subsequently delivered to lysosomes through trans-Golgi vesicles that express M6P receptors. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 1 pg 13
