Sample MCQ #2

AMeningioma
BMultiple myeloma
CGastric cancer
DPancreatic cancer

✓ Correct Answer: B. Multiple myeloma

Explanation: Cancers with increased incidence in obesity due to: Hyperinsulinemia, increased IGF-1 , Chronic inflammation , Increased estrogen (from aromatization in adipose tissue) . Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following growth factors is associated with cancer cachexia? i. PDGF ii. EGF iii. VEGF iv. TNF

AOnly i, ii and iii are correct
BOnly i and iii are correct
COnly ii and iv are correct
DOnly iv is correct
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Only iv is correct

It is suspected that TNF and other cytokines produced by macrophages in response to tumour cells or by the tumour cells themselves mediate cachexia. TNF suppresses appetite and inhibits the action of lipoprotein lipase, preventing the release of free fatty acids from lipoproteins. TNF-&alpha; is chiefly secreted by macrophages in response to stimuli for the induction of systemic inflammation. The binding of the ligand TNF-&alpha; to the TNF receptor(TNFR1) initiates the pro-inflammatory and pro-apoptotic signaling cascades. Reference: Robbins and Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases, 10 th ed, Pg: 236.

Sample MCQ #4

Acanthosis nigricans is not associated with

AGastric cancer
BRenal carcinoma
CLung cancer
DUterine carcinoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Renal carcinoma

Acanthosis nigricans can occur as paraneoplastic syndrome caused by growth factors released from tumors. RCC is rarely associated with acanthosis nigricans. Most common associations are with obesity and diabetes. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition Chapter 25, pg 1143

Sample MCQ #5

Hyperglycemia is associated with:

AOsteosarcoma
BChondroblastoma
CChondrosarcoma
DOsteoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Chondrosarcoma

Hypoglycemia as Paraneoplastic syndrome in some tumors: Mesenchymal tumors, sarcomas, adrenal, hepatic, gastrointestinal, kidney, prostate, Cervix (small-cell carcinoma) The first association of hyperglycemia was made by Glicksman and Rawson who noted a 25% incidence of "diabetes" in patients with sarcomas of bone. Marcove and Francis found hyperglycemia in 85% of their chondrosarcoma patient and Turner and Horne reported similar abnormalities associated with fibrosarcoma Reference: Cancer 42:603-610, 1978;

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following are AIDS defining cancers?

ABurkitt's lymphoma
BNon Hodgkin's Lymphoma
CCa esophagus
DPrimary lymphoma of brain e. Invasive cancer of uterine cervix
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Primary lymphoma of brain e. Invasive cancer of uterine cervix

The neoplastic diseases considered to be AIDS-defining conditions are Kaposi's sarcoma, non-Hodgkin's lymphoma and invasive cervical carcinoma. In addition, there is also an increase in the incidence of a variety of non- AIDS-defining malignancies: Hodgkin's disease; Multiple myeloma; Leukemia; Melanoma; and Cervical, brain, testicular, oral, lung, gastric, liver, renal and anal cancers. Reference: Robbins, 9th ed, Pg: 253.
