Sample MCQ #2

Flow cytometry is routinely utilized for:

AClassification of lymphomas and leukaemias
BDiagnosis of melanoma
CDiagnosis of squamous cell carcinoma
DDiagnosis of adenocarcinoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Classification of lymphomas and leukaemias

Flow cytometry is used routinely in the classification of leukemias and lymphomas. In this method, fluorescently labeled antibodies against cell surface molecules and differentiation antigens are used to obtain the phenotype of malignant cells. Flow cytometry is a technology that rapidly analyzes single cells or particles as they flow past single or multiple lasers while suspended in a buffered salt-based solution. Each particle is analyzed for visible light scatter and one or multiple fluorescence parameters. Reference: Robbins and Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases, 10 th ed, Pg: 238.

Sample MCQ #3

What is/are use's of DNA Microarrays?

AClassify tumours
BPredict metastatic potential
CPredict Prognosis
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Classify tumours

The most common method for large-scale analysis of RNA expression in use in research laboratories has been based on DNA microarrays. DNA microarray analysis helps as the bacterial genomic DNA often outlasts the viability of the bacteria and a diagnosis can be made using a small amount of DNA, as opposed to the large numbers of bacteria needed for culture. Reference: Robbins and Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases, 10 th ed, Pg: 240.
