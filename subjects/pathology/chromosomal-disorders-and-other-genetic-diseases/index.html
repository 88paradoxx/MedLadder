Sample MCQ #1

In Zellweger syndrome,there is ?

AAccumulation of long fatty acids
BAccumulation of short chain fatty acids
CAccumulation of very long chain fatty acids
DAccumulation of medium chain fatty acids
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Accumulation of very long chain fatty acids

Ans. is 'c' i.e., Accumulation of very long chain fatty acids [Ref Harper 29m/e p. 554; Chatterjea 6th le p. 412] Zellweger Syndrome is a rare inborn error of peroxisomal fatty acid oxidation° due to absence of functional peroxisomes in all tissues. As a result, the long chain fatty acids are not oxidized in peroxisomes and accumulate in tissues particularly in brain, liver, kidney and muscle and usually result in death by age six.

Sample MCQ #2

A patient was diagnosed with isolated increase in LDL. His father and brother had the same disease with increased cholesterol. The likely diagnosis is:

AFamilial type III hyperlipoproteinemia
BAbetalipoproteinemia
CFamilial LPL deficiency (type1)
DLDL receptor mutation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. LDL receptor mutation

Disease:- Familial hypercholesterolemia (type IIa) Defect:- Defective LDL receptors or mutation in ligand region of apo B-100. Remarks:- Elevated LDL levels and hypercholesterolemia, resulting in atherosclerosis and coronary disease. Reference: Harper's illustrated biochemistry 32 nd edition, page 275

Sample MCQ #3

A child presents with micrognathia and low-set ears. These clinical features are commonly associated with which type of genetic abnormality?

ADeletion
BTranslocation
CAmplification
DInversion
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Deletion

Explanation: Micrognathia (small jaw) and low-set ears are classic dysmorphic features seen in several chromosomal deletion syndromes, such as: Cri-du-chat syndrome (5p deletion) Wolf -Hirschhorn syndrome (4p deletion) 2 deletion syndrome (DiGeorge syndrome) 13q deletion syndrome T Translocations, inversions, or amplifications may also cause genetic disorders, but specific features like micrognathia and low-set ears are most characteristically seen with deletions. (option B, C, D) Key Takeaways: Deletions consistently produce the distinct facial dysmorphisms (micrognathia, low-set ears) due to loss of developmental genes.
