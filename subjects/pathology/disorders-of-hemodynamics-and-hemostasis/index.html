Sample MCQ #1

Assertion: Septic infarctions occur when infected cardiac valve vegetations embolize Reason: Septic infarct gets converted into a granuloma.

ABoth statements are true and causally related
BBoth statements are true but not causally related
CThe first statement is true and second is false
DThe first statement is false and second is true
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. The first statement is true and second is false

Septic infarcts occur when infected cardiac valve vegetations embolize, or when microbes seed necrotic tissue. In these cases, the infarct is converted into an abscess, with a correspondingly greater inflammatory response and healing by organization and fibrosis. Reference: Robbins and Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases, 10&#7511;&#688; ed Pg:115

Sample MCQ #2

Elevated D-dimer levels are specific for:

ADeep vein thrombosis
BPulmonary thromboembolism
CSystemic embolism
DDisseminated intravascular coagulation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Disseminated intravascular coagulation

Levels of fibrin degradation products, including crosslinked fibrin degradation products (D-dimer), are usually increased in the presence of acute venous thromboembolism. Absence of an elevated level of D-dimer in patients undergoing an evaluation for acute DVT or PE has an excellent negative predictive value for thrombosis. Reference : Wintrobe's 12th/pg 1437

Sample MCQ #3

Which is the commonest site of thrombosis?

AVein
BArtery
CHeart
DCapillary
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Vein

Venous thrombi (phlebothrombosis) are almost invariably occlusive; they frequently propagate some distance toward the heart, forming a long cast within the vessel lumen that is prone to give rise to emboli. Because these thrombi form in the sluggish venous circulation, they tend to contain more enmeshed red cells, leading to stasis or thrombi. The veins of the lower extremities are most commonly affected (90% of venous thromboses); however, venous thrombi also can occur in the upper extremities, periprostatic plexus, or ovarian and periuterine veins, and under special circumstances they may be found in the dural sinuses, portal vein, or hepatic vein. Reference: Robbins and Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases, 10&#7511;&#688; ed Pg:110
