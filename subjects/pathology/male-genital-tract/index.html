Sample MCQ #1

Which is not a germ cell tumor derived from germ cell neoplasia in situ

ASpermatocytic tumor
BSeminoma
CEmbryonal carcinoma
DChoriocarcinoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Spermatocytic tumor

Germ cell neoplasia in situ is a precursor lesion found in association with 90% of germ cell neoplasms and with every type of GCT except spermatocytic tumor and unusual types that arise in infancy. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 21 pg 969

Sample MCQ #2

On IHC seminoma cell is positive for

AOCT3/4
BKIT
CPodoplanin
DAll the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All the above

Seminoma cells are negative for cytokeratin. Placental alkaline phosphatase (PLAP) is also positive in seminoma Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 21 pg 969

Sample MCQ #3

Teratoma with somatic type malignant transformation can lead to

ASCC
BAdenocarcinoma
CSarcoma
DAll the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All the above

Rarely malignant non-germ cell tumors arise in post-pubertal teratomas, a phenomenon referred to as teratoma with somatic type malignant transformation. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 21 pg 969
