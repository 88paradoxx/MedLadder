Sample MCQ #1

Which is not a germ cell tumor derived from germ cell neoplasia in situ

ASpermatocytic tumor
BSeminoma
CEmbryonal carcinoma
DChoriocarcinoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Spermatocytic tumor

Germ cell neoplasia in situ is a precursor lesion found in association with 90% of germ cell neoplasms and with every type of GCT except spermatocytic tumor and unusual types that arise in infancy. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 21 pg 969

Sample MCQ #2

On IHC seminoma cell is positive for

AOCT3/4
BKIT
CPodoplanin
DAll the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All the above

Seminoma cells are negative for cytokeratin. Placental alkaline phosphatase (PLAP) is also positive in seminoma Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 21 pg 969

Sample MCQ #3

Teratoma with somatic type malignant transformation can lead to

ASCC
BAdenocarcinoma
CSarcoma
DAll the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All the above

Rarely malignant non-germ cell tumors arise in post-pubertal teratomas, a phenomenon referred to as teratoma with somatic type malignant transformation. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 21 pg 969

Sample MCQ #4

Most common genetic alteration in prostate cancer

AChromosomal rearrangement that juxtaposes ETS next to TMPRSS2
BMYC amplification
CSilencing of p27 gene
DLoss of TP53
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Chromosomal rearrangement that juxtaposes ETS next to TMPRSS2

All these alterations are seen in prostate cancer, but chromosomal rearrangement that juxtaposes coding sequence of ETS family transcriptor gene next to androgen-regulated TMPRSS2 promoter is the most common of these, such that ETS oncogene is overexpressed in an androgenic fashion. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 21 pg 977

Sample MCQ #5

False statement about embryonal carcinoma

AMore aggressive than seminoma
BPleomorphism is seen within tumor cells
CVascular-lymphatic invasion is common
DTumor cells are negative for OCT3/4
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Tumor cells are negative for OCT3/4

Tumor cells are positive for OCT3/4 and cytokeratin, and negative for KIT and podoplanin. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 21 pg 969

Sample MCQ #6

Reduplication of which chromosome is found invariably in invasive GCTs regardless of histologic subtype

A13p
B17p
C12p
D10p
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 12p

Isochromosome 12p, a cytogenetic alteration that is invariably in invasive GCTs regardless of histologic subtype. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 21 pg 969

Sample MCQ #7

Gleason grade group 1 has a score of less than or equal to

A4
B5
C6
D7
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 6

Gleason system stratifies prostate cancer into 5 grades on basis of glandular patterns of growth. Grade 1 corresponds to well-differentiated tumors and grade 5 to poorly differentiated tumors. Most tumors contain more than one pattern, in these cases a primary grade is assigned to the dominant pattern, and a secondary grade is assigned to the second most frequent pattern. Gleason grade groups Group 1: score less than equal to 6 Group 2: 3 + 4 Group 3: 4 + 3 Group 4: 4 + 4/3 + 5/5 + 3 Group 5: 4 + 5/5 + 4/5 + 5 Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 21 pg 977

Sample MCQ #8

Components of testicular dysgenesis syndrome are all except

ATesticular GCTs
BCryptorchidism
CEpispadias
DPoor sperm quality
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Epispadias

Hypospadias is a component, not epispadias. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, Chapter - 21 pg 969
