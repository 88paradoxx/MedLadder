Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following shows BCL-2 positive marker?

AFollicular lymphoma
BMycosos fungoidosis
CB cell lymphoma
DMantle cell lymphoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Follicular lymphoma

Greater than 85% of follicular lymphomas have a characteristic (14;18) translocation that fuses the BCL2 gene on chromosome 18 to the IgH locus on chromosome 14. This chromosomal rearrangement results in the inappropriate 'overexpression' of BCL2 protein, which you will recall is an inhibitor of apoptosis that contributes to cell survival. Immunophenotype. These tumors express B cell markers such as CD20 and the germinal center B cell markers CD10 and BCL6; of note, BCL6 is a transcription factor that is required for the generation of germinal center B cells. Reference : Robbins, 10 th edition, page number - 468

Sample MCQ #3

Primary extranodal neoplasms is/are:

ABurkitt lymphoma
BWaldeyer's ring lymphoma
Cretro peritoneal lymphoma
DThyroid lymphoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Thyroid lymphoma

At least one quarter of non-Hodgkin&rsquo;s lymphomas (NHL) arise from tissue other than lymph nodes and even from sites which normally contain no lymphoid tissue. These forms are referred to as primary extranodal lymphomas. Gastrointestinal localizations, CNS, skin (Mycosis fungoidosis), thyroid, testis. Primary nodal NHL have presentation in lymph node, Waldeyer&rsquo;s ring, spleen or bone marrow Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, chapter 13, pg 602
