Sample MCQ #3

ABone marrow with reticulin stain
BJAK STAT mutation assessment
CPhiladelphia chromosome
DErythropoietin levels
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Erythropoietin levels

In this case, the clinical features are suggestive of polycythemia. Since, he is from hilly area, it may be due to secondary causes. To rule out primary (low epo) vs secondary cause (high epo), erythropoietin must be the next logical step. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, chapter 13, pg 624

Sample MCQ #4

A 53-year-old man has had increasing fatigue for the past 6 months. On physical examination he has a palpable spleen tip. Laboratory studies show a WBC count of 189,000/microliter. The peripheral blood smear shows many mature and immature myeloid cells present. Cytogenetic analysis of cells obtained via bone marrow aspiration reveals a t(9:22) translocation. This translocation leads to formation of a hybrid gene that greatly increases tyrosine kinase activity. Which of the following genes is most likely translocated to cause these findings?

Ap53
BRb
Cc-abl
DNF-1
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. c-abl

The above history shows organomegaly with increased WBC counts. Peripheral smear with mature, immature myeloid cells and t(9:22) translocation is case of chronic myeloid leukemia. BCR-ABL fusion gene formation is an early, perhaps initiating, event that drives leukemogenesis. The crucial role of BCR-ABL in cancer has been confirmed by the dramatic clinical response of patients with chronic myeloid leukemia to BCR-ABL kinase inhibitors. Reference: Robbins and Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases, 10 th ed, Pg: 207.

Sample MCQ #5

A 3 year old child presents with scattered lesions in the skull. Biopsy revealed Langerhans giant cells. The most commonly associated marker with this condition-

ACD 1A
BCD 57
CCD 3
DCD 68
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. CD 1A

Langerhans cell proliferations take on different clinical forms, but all are believed to be variations of the same basic disorder. The proliferating Langerhans cells express MHC class II antigens, CD1a, and langerin. Langerin is a transmembrane protein found in Birbeck granules, cytoplasmic penta laminar rodlike tubular structures that in electron micrographs have a characteristic periodicity and sometimes a dilated terminal end ('tennis racket' appearance). Under the light microscope, the proliferating Langerhans cells do not resemble their normal dendritic counterparts. Instead, they have abundant, often vacuolated cytoplasm and vesicular nuclei, an appearance more akin to that of tissue macrophages (called histiocytes by morphologists) hence the term Langerhans cell histiocytosis. Reference : Robbins, 10 th edition, page number - 484

Sample MCQ #6

A 24 - year - old woman who presented with aquagenic pruritic had a Hb level of 20g/dL . Her lab investigations are given below, Which of the following is not seen in this condition ? Hb - 19g/dl Hematocrit - 52% S. erythropoietin - decreased JAK2 mutation - positive

AACUTE MYELOID LEUKEMIA
BBUDD - CHIARI SYNDROME
CMYELOFIBROSIS
DHEPATIC FAILURE
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. HEPATIC FAILURE

The given clinical scenario is suggestive of polycythemia vera. It is a myeloproliferative neoplasm, presents with history of hepatic vein thrombosis (Budd Chiari syndrome ) and can progress to Myelofibrosis. It can also undergo clonal evolution to AML. Hepatic failure is not seen in this condition. Polycythemia vera is strongly associated with activating point mutations in the tyrosine kinase JAK2. In this condition, clonal erythrocytosis is taking place autonomously i.e, independent of erythropoietin. Clinical features of polycythemia vera : Headache Hypertension Dizziness

ABirbecks granules

✓ Correct Answer: A. Birbecks granules

Birbeck granules Also known as Langerhans bodies or X granules Seen on Electron microscopy Rod-shaped organelles with a central striation and terminal vesicular dilation, giving them a 'tennis racket' appearance. Reference: Robbins 9th/pg 531
