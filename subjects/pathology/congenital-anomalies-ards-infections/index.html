Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following agent causes atypical pneumonia?

AStaphylococcus aureus
BKlebsiella pneumoniae
CMoraxella catarrhalis
DHuman corona virus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Human corona virus

Causes of atypical pneumonia: Mycoplasma, Chlamydia pneumonia, Influenza, Adenovirus, RSV, Legionella, Coxiella burnetti and Pneumocytis carnii. Reference: Robbins Basic Pathology, 9 th ed, Pg-703-05

Sample MCQ #2

In primary tuberculosis, all of the following may be seen EXCEPT

ACavitation
BCaseation
CCalcification
DLanghans giant cells
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Cavitation

Primary tuberculosis is the form of disease that develops in a previously unexposed and therefore unsensitized patient. Ghon focus, ghon complex and ranke complex are features of primary tuberculosis. Secondary tuberculosis is the pattern of disease that arises in a previously sensitized host. It may appear shortly after primary tuberculosis, but more commonly arises from reactivation of dormant primary lesion. The upper parts of both lungs are riddled with gray-white areas of caseation and multiple areas of softening and cavitation. Reference: Robbins Basic Pathology, 10 th ed, Pg-528

Sample MCQ #3

Cotton candy exudates are characteristic of?

AStaphylococcus aureus
BKlebsiella pneumoniae
CMoraxella catarrhalis
DPneumocystis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Pneumocystis

Involved areas of the lung contain a characteristic intraalveolar foamy, pink-staining exudate with hematoxylin-eosin (H&E) stain ("cotton candy" exudate). The septa are thickened by edema and a minimal mononuclear infiltrate. Special stains are required to visualize the organism. Silver stain of tissue sections reveals round- to cup-shaped cysts (4 to 10 &micro;m in diameter) within the alveolar exudates. Reference: Robbins Basic Pathology, 10 th ed, Pg-534
