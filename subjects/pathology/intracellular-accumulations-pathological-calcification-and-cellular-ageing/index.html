Sample MCQ #1

A 65-year-old male patient having long-standing cigarette smoking (2 pkt/day) and nonfebrile presented to OPD complaints of shortness of breath and cough since many months. His dyspneic duration and intensity increasing these days. His chest is hyperresonant on percussion. Clubbing is also present. X-ray revealed overinflation of lung. Based on the above findings, what will be the histopathological findings in the lung?

ADestruction of the walls of airspaces without fibrosis
BInterstitial fibrosis of the lung parenchyma
CLymphocytes restricted to the interstitium
DProminent bronchial smooth muscle cell hyperplasia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Destruction of the walls of airspaces without fibrosis

Emphysema is a chronic lung disease characterized by enlargement of the airspaces distal to the terminal bronchioles, with destruction of their walls, but without fibrosis or inflammation. Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition Chapter-15, Pg 678

Sample MCQ #3

Which pathological process is the reason of release of cardiac specific enzymes in blood?

AAutophagy
BNecroptosis
CApoptosis
DNecrosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Necrosis

The biochemical mechanisms of necrosis vary with different injurious stimuli. These mechanisms include: Failure of energy generation in the form of ATP because of reduced oxygen supply or mitochondrial damage Damage to cellular membranes, including the plasma membrane and lysosomal membranes, which results in leakage of cellular contents including enzymes Irreversible damage to cellular lipids, proteins and nucleic acids, which may be caused by reactive oxygen species (ROS); and others. Reference: Robbins and Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases, 10 th ed Pg:35
