Sample MCQ #1

A 45-year-old male patient who had long duration smoking history and diagnosed as a case of small cell carcinoma of lung and on chemotherapy regimen develops a recently developed heart murmur. Undergoes some laboratory tests which revealed normal TLC and negative blood culture. No growth seen on urine culture examination. Possibly noninfective endocarditis was the reason for the heart murmur. All of the following statements are true regarding nonbacterial thrombotic endocarditis; except:

ACharacteristic deposition of sterile Thrombi on the leaflet of cardiac valves
BHistologically these are bland thrombi, loosely attached to the underlying valve
CVegetation are destructive and shows marked inflammatory response
DThese vegetation have trivial effect but can be a source of emboli
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Vegetation are destructive and shows marked inflammatory response

(a) NBTE is characterized by the deposition of small (1 - 5 mm) sterile thrombi on the leaflets of the cardiac valves (b) Histologically, these are bland thrombi, loosely attached to the underlying valve (c) The vegetations are nondestructive and do not elicit any inflammatory reaction. (d) The local effect of the vegetations is usually trivial, they can be the source of systemic emboli that produce significant infarcts in the brain, heart, or elsewhere. Reference : Robbins 10th Ed pg 564

Sample MCQ #2

A 9-year-old girl presented with subcutaneous nodules over the skin of her arms and torso 3 weeks after a bout of acute pharyngitis. She manifests some abnormal movements and begins to complain of pain in her knees and hips, particularly with movement. A friction rub is heard on auscultation of her chest. An abnormality detected by which of the following serum laboratory findings is most characteristic of the disease affecting this girl?

AAntistreptolysin O antibody titer
BAntinuclear antibody titer
CCreatinine level
DTroponin I level
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Antistreptolysin O antibody titer

(a) Acute rheumatic fever can involve any or all layers of the heart. Because rheumatic fever follows group A streptococcal infections, the antihyaluronidase, anti- DNase, and antistreptolysin O (ASO) titers are often elevated. (b) The ANA level could be elevated in systemic lupus erythematosus, which is most likely to produce a serous pericarditis. (c) The strains of group A streptococci that lead to acute rheumatic fever are less likely to cause glomerulonephritis, so an elevated creatinine level is unlikely. (d) Cardiac troponins are markers for ischemic myocardial injury. Although their levels may be elevated because of the acute myocarditis that occurs in rheumatic fever,this change is not a characteristic of rheumatic heart disease. Reference : Robbins 10th Ed pg 563

Sample MCQ #3

Vegetations of the following endocarditis has the maximum chances of embolization:

ARheumatic heart disease
BInfective endocarditis
CLibman-Sacks endocarditis
DSubacute bacterial endocarditis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Infective endocarditis

Vegetations on heart valves are the classic hallmark of IE; these are friable, bulky, potentially destructive lesions containing fibrin, inflammatory cells, and bacteria or other organisms. Vegetations are prone to embolization; because the embolic fragments often contain virulent organisms, abscesses frequently develop where they lodge, leading to sequelae such as septic infarcts or mycotic aneurysms. Reference: Robin's basic pathology 10 th edition, pg 564
