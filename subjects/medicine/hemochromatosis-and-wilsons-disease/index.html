Sample MCQ #3

A 15-year male child presented with hepatomegaly, involuntary movements, poor scholastic performance. His sister died at 5 years of age. Eye examination is shown below. What is the diagnosis?

AGlutaric acidemia
BWilson's disease
CHepatitis A
DWestphal variant of HD
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Wilson's disease

The presence of hepatomegaly, involuntary movements points to Wilson's disease. Wilson Disease is an inherited condition that causes the body to retain excess copper. The copper buildup leads to damage in the kidneys, brain, and eyes. If not treated, Wilson Disease can cause severe brain damage, liver failure, and death.

The ground glass appearance of the stones suggests cystine stones . Hexagonal crystals are seen in Cystinuria. Autosomal recessive disorder. Sample MCQ #7

The KF ring is associated with which of the following conditions?

AHemochromatosis
BWilson's disease
CAlzheimer's disease
DHuntington's disease
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Wilson's disease

Explanation: The Kayser-Fleischer (KF) ring is due to copper deposition in Descemet's membrane of the cornea . It is a hallmark feature of Wilson's disease , an autosomal recessive disorder of copper metabolism characterised by hepatic, neurological, and psychiatric manifestations . Slit-lamp examination is the best method to visualise KF rings. Explanation of other options: Hemochromatosis (Option A): Characterised by iron overload affecting the liver, pancreas, heart, and skin ("bronze diabetes"), but no KF ring is seen. Alzheimer's disease (Option C): A neurodegenerative disorder with memory loss and amyloid plaques, not associated with copper deposition. Huntington's disease (Option D): A genetic movement disorder due to CAG repeats, with chorea and psychiatric features, but no ocular signs like KF rings.
