Sample MCQ #1

Transferrin saturation is calculated by

ATIBC &times; 100 &divide; serum iron
BSerum iron &times; 100 &divide; TIBC
CSerum ferritin &times; 100 &divide; TIBC.
DSerum ferritin &times; 100 &divide; Serum Iron
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Serum iron &times; 100 &divide; TIBC

Transferrin saturation , which is normally 25-50%, is obtained by the following formula: Transferrin saturation = (Serum iron &times; 100) &divide; TIBC, where TIBC is total iron binding capacity. Iron-deficiency states are associated with saturation levels Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 63, Pg: 436

Sample MCQ #2

Microcytic erythrocytosis is seen in all except

A&beta;-thalassemia trait
BHypoxic erythrocytosis
CEssential Thrombocytosis
DPolycythemia vera
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Essential Thrombocytosis

Only three situations cause microcytic erythrocytosis: &beta;-thalassemia trait, hypoxic erythrocytosis, and PV. With &beta;-thalassemia trait, the RDW is usually normal, whereas with hypoxic erythrocytosis and PV, the RDW may be elevated due to associated iron deficiency. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 103, Pg: 804

Sample MCQ #3

Treatment of Sideroblastic Anemia:

AVitamin B12
BVitamin B6
CVitamin C
DIron
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Vitamin B6

Vitamin B6 dependency syndromes that require pharmacologic doses of vitamin B6 are cystathionine &beta;-synthase deficiency, pyridoxine-responsive (primarily sideroblastic) anemias, and gyrate atrophy with chorioretinal degeneration due to decreased activity of the mitochondrial enzyme ornithine aminotransferase. In these situations, 100-200 mg/d of oral vitamin B6 is required for treatment.
