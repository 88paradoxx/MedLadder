Sample MCQ #1

Transferrin saturation is calculated by

ATIBC &times; 100 &divide; serum iron
BSerum iron &times; 100 &divide; TIBC
CSerum ferritin &times; 100 &divide; TIBC.
DSerum ferritin &times; 100 &divide; Serum Iron
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Serum iron &times; 100 &divide; TIBC

Transferrin saturation , which is normally 25-50%, is obtained by the following formula: Transferrin saturation = (Serum iron &times; 100) &divide; TIBC, where TIBC is total iron binding capacity. Iron-deficiency states are associated with saturation levels Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 63, Pg: 436

Sample MCQ #2

Microcytic erythrocytosis is seen in all except

A&beta;-thalassemia trait
BHypoxic erythrocytosis
CEssential Thrombocytosis
DPolycythemia vera
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Essential Thrombocytosis

Only three situations cause microcytic erythrocytosis: &beta;-thalassemia trait, hypoxic erythrocytosis, and PV. With &beta;-thalassemia trait, the RDW is usually normal, whereas with hypoxic erythrocytosis and PV, the RDW may be elevated due to associated iron deficiency. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 103, Pg: 804

Sample MCQ #3

Treatment of Sideroblastic Anemia:

AVitamin B12
BVitamin B6
CVitamin C
DIron
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Vitamin B6

Vitamin B6 dependency syndromes that require pharmacologic doses of vitamin B6 are cystathionine &beta;-synthase deficiency, pyridoxine-responsive (primarily sideroblastic) anemias, and gyrate atrophy with chorioretinal degeneration due to decreased activity of the mitochondrial enzyme ornithine aminotransferase. In these situations, 100-200 mg/d of oral vitamin B6 is required for treatment.

Sample MCQ #4

Sympathetic segments? supply to the heart arises from which of the following spinal

AT1 to T5
BT2 to T6
CT3 to T7
DT4 to T8
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. T1 to T5

Sympathetic nerve cells supplying the heart are located in the intermediolateral horn of spinal segments T1 to T5. Sympathetic supply is cardio-acceleratory, and on stimulation, they increase the heart rate, and also dilate coronary arteries. Both parasympathetic and sympathetic nerves form the superficial and deep cardiac plexuses, the branches of which run along the coronary arteries to reach the myocardium.

Sample MCQ #5

What is the Most common site of spinal TB in adults?

AUpper thoracic spine
BLower thoracic spine
CCervical spine
DSacrum
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Lower thoracic spine

Weight-bearing joints (the spine in 40% of cases, the hips in 13%, and the knees in 10%) are most commonly affected in skeletal TB The upper thoracic spine is the most common site of spinal TB in children The lower thoracic and upper lumbar vertebrae are usually affected in adults Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st Ed, Ch: 178, Pg: 1365

Sample MCQ #7

A 38 year old alcoholic presents with unsteadiness whilst walking. On examination he has increased tone and brisk reflexes in the right leg. Proprioception is abnormal in the right leg. There is loss of vibration sense in the right leg. There is decrease in pain and temperature sensation in the left leg. Which one of the following conditions is most likely to be responsible for his weakness?

ASyringomyelia
BSubacute combined degeneration of cord
CFriedrich's ataxia
DBrown Sequard syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Brown Sequard syndrome

Brown Sequard syndrome which describes hemisection of the spinal cord, causes ipsilateral UMN signs and proprioception loss (corticospinal tract and dorsal column decussate at the medulla), and contralateral sensory loss in pain and temprature (the spinothalamic tracts decussate at the same level). The rest of the conditions (syringomyelia, subacute degeneration of cord, Friedrich's ataxia) can cause cerebellar signs or patchy sensory loss but should be bilateral. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 442, Pg: 3446

Sample MCQ #8

Enterococci are commonly associated with all of these clinical manifestations except:

AWound infection.
BUrinary tract infection
CBacteremia
DOsteomyelitis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Osteomyelitis

Enterococci act as opportunistic pathogens in severely ill and compromised patients Enterococci are known to cause urinary tract infections; intra abdominal abscesses; wound infections; bacteremia, including catheter-associated blood stream infections; and endocarditis; less commonly, they cause osteoarticular infections and meningitis. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st Ed, Ch: 149 Pg: 1200
