Sample MCQ #1

A 75-year-old man visits his GP for a new-patient screen. His only previous complaints have been type-2 diabetes and mild long-standing back pain. Screening tests reveal an elevated serum creatinine of 2.15 mg/dl and anaemia with Hb of 10.1 g/dl. A back X-ray shows collapse of the lumbar spine and there is a monoclonal band on serum protein electrophoresis. What is the most likely cause of his deteriorated renal function?

ADiabetic nephropathy
BMembranous glomerulonephritis
CMetformin toxicity
DAmyloidosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Amyloidosis

This patient is suffering from Myeloma as evidenced by the presence of anemia, renal impairment and bone involvement. Myeloma can affect the kidney in several ways - the filtered light chains are toxic to the tubules and can cause acute obstruction: Cast nephropathy The light chain impairs the proximal tubular function and cause RTA Hypercalcemia can cause tubulointerstitial disease Amyloidosis LCDD All forms of renal involvement is reversible to a certain extend with treatment of myeloma. Among the given options Amyloidosis is the best pick Amyloidosis presents predominantly between the ages of 60 and 70 years. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st edition, Ch:111, Pg:870.

Sample MCQ #2

A 55-year-old man with a history of hypertension has renal impairment. His creatinine is 2.8 mg/dl. Urine dipstick shows blood ++ and protein +. What is the most useful investigation?

AIntravenous urogram
BRenal ultrasound
CRenal biopsy
DDTPA scan
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Renal biopsy

A urinalysis of 2+ blood and 1+ protein are strong pointers towards glomerular disease. Hence among the options, biopsy is the most useful investigation. Renal ultrasound may be useful in assessing the kidney size and echoes, but it may not help us arrive at a diagnosis. IV urogram is used in the investigation of Obstruction to urinary tract and DTPA nucleide scan is used to asses the functional status of kidneys. Indications of Renal biopsy(when suspected): Glomerulonephritis Nephrotic Syndrome Vasculitis Interstitial nephritis Myeloma kidney HUS and TTP Allograft rejection Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Principles, 21 st edition, Ch: 310, Pg: 2305.

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following drugs cause renal impairment by causing interstitial nephritis?

AAminoglycosides
BVancomycin
CAmphotericin B
DQuinolones
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Quinolones

Quinolones, penicillins, cephalosporins, sulphonamides and rifampicin are antibiotics that cause interstitial nephritis. Vancomycin, Aminoglycosides, Amphotericin B, Acyclovir, Foscarnet, Tenofovir and Cidofovir are toxic to the tubules of kidney. Vancomycin and acyclovir cause intra tubular obstruction. Amphotericin B causes RTA like picture with hypomagnesemia, hypocalcemia. Hypomagnesemia is also seen with Aminoglycosides. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21st ed, Chap 310, Pg. 2300
