Sample MCQ #1

A 22 year old female patient presents with a 5-day history of ascending muscle weakness in both hands and feet. EMGs confirm acute demyelinating sensory and motor neuropathy. What treatment should be started?

ACorticosteroids
BTacrolimus
CAmitriptyline
DIntravenous immunoglobulin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Intravenous immunoglobulin

The diagnosis is Guillain Barre syndrome . It is preceded by diarrhea (e.g. campylobacter) or chest infections (e.g. mycoplasma) in two thirds of cases. Autonomic dysfunction and hyporeflexia are associated. P rogressive symmetric ascending weakness is characteristic . Management: Plasma exchange (OR) IVIG (IV immunoglobulins) - 2 g/kg/day for 5 days. As effective as plasma exchange. Easier to administer and fewer side effects. No benefit in combining both treatments. Steroids and immunosuppressants have not been shown to be beneficial. Regular FVC (Forced vital capacity ) to monitor respiratory function and assess need for ventilation. ICU admission is recommended when FVC is less than 20 mL/kg and intubation is recommended in most cases when FVC is less than 15 mL/kg . Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 ST Ed, Ch: 447, Pg: 3503

Sample MCQ #3

The basic abnormality in increased fasting blood glucose is:

ADecreased first phase insulin secretion
BDecreased glucose uptake in muscle
CIncreased hepatic glucose output
DIncreased glucagon levels
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Increased hepatic glucose output

The basic abnormality in increased fasting glucose is high hepatic glucose output mainly due to reduced basal secretion of insulin or hepatic insulin resistance Raised post-prandial glucose values is due to decreased peripheral utilization (muscle and adipose tissue) First and second phase insulin secretion occurs in response to meals Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition page 3096-3099

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following is not an FDA approved drug for Diabetic peripheral neuropathy?

ADuloxetine
BPregabalin
CTapentadol
DAmitriptyline
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Amitriptyline

Though tricyclic antidepressants like amitriptyline are commonly employed for managing positive symptoms of diabetic neuropathy, they are not FDA approved Tapentadol is a central acting opioid analog with lesser evidence which is FDA approved Capsaicin patch has also been approved by FDA which works by interfering with the function of TRPV1 Venlafaxine, Oxcarbexapine, tricyclic antidepressants, gabapentin etc are others useful in the symptomatic treatment of neuropathy Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition page 3124

Sample MCQ #5

Which of the following clinical manifestations suggests Guillain-Barre syndrome (GBS)?

AAsymmetrical involvement of distal muscles
BBrisk tendon reflexes
CBulbar involvement in about 50% of cases
DNormal cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) protein
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Bulbar involvement in about 50% of cases

GBS is associated with bulbar involvement occurs in 50%, with a risk of aspiration and respiratory insufficiency. In GBS, Symmetric involvement is typical. Asymmetrical involvement is present in only 9% of patients. CSF protein is elevated to more than twice the upper limit of normal, with normal glucose and no pleocytosis. Areflexia is commonly present. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 447, Pg: 3501

Sample MCQ #6

A 57-year-old female is admitted with progressive weakness following a trivial flu-like illness. Which of the following would exclude Guillain-Barre syndrome (GBS) as the diagnosis?

AAutonomic dysfunction
BElevated protein on CSF examination
COphthalmoplegia
DSensory level below T1
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Sensory level below T1

A sensory level is not a feature of GBS and would suggest cervical myelopathy. Guillian Barre Syndrome (GBS) is a post-infectious acute polyneuritis typified by elevated CSF protein with few cells and often normal glucose. There is a profound weakness associated with areflexia and peripheral sensory neuropathy. Ophthalmoplegia is associated in particular with the Miller-Fisher variant. Muscle wasting is typical with prolonged illness. Autonomic dysfunction can also occur. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st ed, Ch: 447, Pg: 3501

Sample MCQ #7

Guillain Barre Syndrome treatment consists of all except?

ASteroid
BI.V immunoglobulin
CPlasmapheresis
DVentilatory support
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Steroid

Steroids have no role in the treatment of GBS. Plasma exchange and I.V immunoglobulins are the mainstay treatment whereas ventilatory support may be needed during the course of GBS.
