Sample MCQ #1

A 44 year old man has a diagnosis of Ulcerative Colitis. He was incidentally found to have positive anti smooth muscle antibodies by the GP who sent an autoimmune screen. Which is the next recommended test for this patient?

ACT of the abdomen
BColonoscopy
CEndoscopy
DLiver function tests
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Liver function tests

A positive anti smooth muscle antibody test is suggestive of autoimmune hepatitis . Liver function tests may demonstrate elevated levels of bilirubin, AST and ALT. A liver biopsy may then be warranted. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st Ed, Ch: 326 Pg: 2482

Sample MCQ #3

A 43 year old man has recurrent episodes of bloody diarrhea and lower abdominal cramping. Histologic evaluation of a biopsy from flexible sigmoidoscopy reveals acute inflammation without architectural distortion consistent with ulcerative colitis.Which of the following conditions is associated with the condition?

APrimary biliary cirrhosis
BSclerosing cholangitis
CErythema nodosum
DRenal calculi
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Sclerosing cholangitis

75% of patients with primary sclerosing cholangitis have inflammatory bowel disease, especially ulcerative colitis. Primary sclerosing cholangitis (PSC) is a chronic cholestatic liver disease of unknown etiology. In the absence of underlying bile duct abnormalities, a generalized beading and stenosis of the intrahepatic and extrahepatic biliary tree characterize PSC. PSC is usually progressive, leading to cirrhosis, portal hypertension, and liver failure.Typically, a cholestatic jaundice picture is seen with the liver function tests. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, ch: 344, Pg: 2627-2628
