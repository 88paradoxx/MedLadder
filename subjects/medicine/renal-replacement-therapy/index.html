Sample MCQ #1

A 27-year-old woman is in end-stage renal disease. She has been started on regular haemodialysis. She complains of pain in her fingers. An X-ray shows digital subperiosteal erosions. What is the primary metabolic cause for her bony condition?

AIncreased serum phosphate levels
BIncreased parathyroid hormone levels
CIncreased renal 1a-hydroxylase enzyme levels
DIncreased serum calcium levels
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Increased serum phosphate levels

Digital sub periosteal resorption is classical of Osteitis fibrosa cystica of hyperparathyroidism. In CKD secondary hyperparathyroidism may progress to tertiary hyperparathyroidism. The series of events that happen in CKD is: Declining GFR leads to reduced excretion of phosphate and thus, phosphate retention The retained phosphate stimulates increased synthesis of both FGF-23 by osteocytes and of PTH and also stimulates growth of parathyroid gland mass PTH production is stimulated by decreased levels of ionized calcium, which in turn, result from decreased levels of renal calcitriol production with reduced kidney mass and suppression of calcitriol production due to phosphate retention and elevated levels of FGF-23, which also increases degradation of calcitriol. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st edition, Ch:311, Pg:2313

Sample MCQ #2

A 60-year-old man with long-standing type-2 diabetes, who has recently started on peritoneal dialysis, is admitted with a fractured femoral neck. His serum calcium concentration is 2.9 mmol/l, phosphate is 2 mmol/l and PTH is 5.6 pg/ml(normal range 25-65). Medications include alfacalcidol 0.5 mg daily and calcium carbonate 500 mg three times daily. What would a bone biopsy show?

AAdynamic bone disease
BAluminium deposition at the osteoid bone interface
CAn increase in plasma cells
DOsteitis fibrosa cystica
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Adynamic bone disease

Referring to the above question, we can clearly say that this patient is suffering from adynamic bone disease. Note that the calcium and phosphorous given here are in mmol/l. Ca value has to be multiplied by 4. So the Ca is 11.6mg/dl. Phosphorous has to be multiplied by 3. So the value is 6mg/dl. Aluminium deposited can cause osteomalacia like picture and is seen with use of aluminium as phosphate binder and Al in dialysate. Osteitis fibrosa cystica is severe secondary hyperparathyroidism. An increase in plasma cells cause hypercalcemia and hypophosphatemia Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal medicine, 21 st edition, Ch:410, Pg:3181, 3182.

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following statements are true regarding the management of Lupus nephritis? 1. HCQS should be included as treatment for all classes since it reduces the accrual of tissue damage. 2. Management of class I and Class II should be guided by the extra renal manifestations. 3. Class III and IV requires aggressive immunosuppression. 4. Isolated Class V has got better prognosis than class IV. 5. Isolated class V without nephrotic range proteinuria requires only supportive management.

AAll are correct
B1, 2 and 3
C1, 3 and 5
D1, 2 and 4
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. All are correct

The question deals with the management and prognosis of Lupus Nephritis. HCQ should be in the management of allclasses of lupus nephritis. Class I and II should be treated mainly based on the extra renal manifestations. Class III and IV are the proliferative and serious forms and require aggressive immunosuppression with high dose glucocorticoid with either MMF or Cyclophosphamide. Isolated Class V presents with proteinuria alone and has got better outcome than class IV. But class V often co exists with class III and IV. Class V with nephrotic range proteinuria should be treated with immunosuppression, usually steroid with calcinurin inhibitors. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21st edition Ch:356 Pg:2739-2747 KDIGO guidelines
