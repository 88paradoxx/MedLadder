Sample MCQ #1

A 13-year-old boy is brought to your clinic with a complaint of delayed pubertal changes. While examining the patient, which of the following features is the first change to indicate that pubertal change may have commenced?

AIncrease in testicular volume
BGrowth of external genitalia
CGrowth of pubic hair
DChange in voice
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Increase in testicular volume

The first clinical sign of the onset of puberty in boys is an increase in testicular volume (> 4 ml). This is followed by growth of external genitalia, pubic and axillary hair growth, growth of external genitalia and deepening of voice The first sign of pubertal onset in girls is thelarche (breast development) followed by pubic hair growth and menarche. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 3011

Sample MCQ #2

A 48-year-old man presents to the clinic complaining of a lump in the neck which becomes particularly more prominent when she swallows. On examination there is a thyroid nodule at the base of the neck. Leukocyte screening of two other family members who suffered thyroid carcinoma has revealed the ret proto-oncogene. Which type of thyroid carcinoma is linked to the ret-proto-oncogene?

APapillary thyroid carcinoma
BAnaplastic thyroid carcinoma
CFollicular thyroid carcinoma
DMedullary thyroid carcinoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Medullary thyroid carcinoma

The ret proto-oncogene encodes for a receptor tyrosine kinase. In medullary thyroid carcinoma the mutation is associated with hyperfunction. Ret is associated with familial cases of medullary thyroid carcinoma and the MEN-2 syndrome. Formerly the condition was screened for with the pentagastrin suppression test, although a DNA probe for ret is now readily available and patients can be screened via a leukocyte sample. Reference: Harrison&rsquo;s Principles of Internal Medicine 20 th edition Ch:378 Pg:2714-17

Which is the most likely cancer in this patient?

APapillary thyroid carcinoma
BAnaplastic thyroid carcinoma
CFollicular thyroid carcinoma
DMedullary thyroid carcinoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Medullary thyroid carcinoma

The RET proto-oncogene encodes for a receptor tyrosine kinase. Activating mutation in RET gene can lead to MEN 2A and MEN 2B syndromes Medullary thyroid cancer is present in 100% of MEN2A and MEN 2B syndrome cases Serum Calcitonin is used as a tumor marker in screening patients with suspected Medullary thyroid cancer. Sample MCQ #4

You review a 28-year-old girl who has been referred with amenorrhoea. She is noted on routine screening to have a raised prolactin level. Patient is keen to know about prolactin physiology. Thinking of hormones in general, which of the following hormones is under continuous inhibition?

AProlactin
BGrowth hormone
CAdrenocorticotrophic hormone
DThyroid-releasing hormone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Prolactin

Prolactin is under predominantly inhibitory control , by levels of dopamine. Prolactin levels are known to rise during pregnancy, lactation, severe stress, sleep and coitus. Mildly increased prolactin levels in the range of 400-600 mU/litre may be physiological, but higher levels require a diagnosis. Levels above 1000 imply the possibility of microprolactinoma, and levels above 5000 imply the presence of macroprolactinoma. Drug causes of Hyperprolactinaemia are, not surprisingly, the dopamine antagonists, which include metoclopramide, domperidone and the phenothiazines. Features of Hyperprolactinaemia in women include amenorrhoea, galactorrhoea, loss of libido, subfertility and features of androgen deficiency. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 20 th edition Chapter:371, Page number:2660-61

Sample MCQ #5

Which of the following is not true regarding Pendred syndrome?

ACongenital hypothyroidism
BPendrin is the sodium-iodide symporter present in thyroid follicular cell
CSensorineural hearing loss
DAutosomal recessive inheritance

✓ Correct Answer: B. Pendrin is the sodium-iodide symporter present in thyroid follicular cell

Pendred syndrome is an autosomal recessive disorder causing congenital hypothyroidism with sensorineural hearing loss Mutation in Pendrin (SLC26A4) which is a chloride-iodide transporter Gene coding Pendrin is located in Chromosome 7 Pendrin, located in the apical surface of follicular cell, mediates the secretion of iodine into the colloid Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition page 2935

Sample MCQ #6

Titration of doses of anti-thyroid drugs is best based on?

ATotal T4 levels
BUnbound T4 levels
CTSH
DTSH and Total T3
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Unbound T4 levels

Thyroid function tests and clinical manifestations are reviewed 4-6 weeks after starting treatment, and the dose is titrated based on unbound T4 levels. Most patients do not achieve euthyroidism until 6-8 weeks after treatment is initiated. TSH levels often remain suppressed for several months and therefore do not provide a sensitive index of treatment response. When TSH suppression is alleviated, TSH levels can also be used to monitor therapy. Though total T3 and T4 can also be used, free hormones would be the better option. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page:2931

Sample MCQ #7

A 44-year-old woman presents to the Emergency department with palpitations and shortness of breath. Recent thyroid function tests on the hospital computer reveal thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH) of Which of the following findings would be most consistent with Grave's disease?

ARespiratory acidosis
BDecreased Pa(O2)
CIncreased Pa (CO2)
DDecreased Pa (CO2)
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Decreased Pa (CO2)

Compared with normal subjects, hyperthyroid patients show significantly lower resting arterial CO2 tension, tidal volume and significantly higher mean inspiratory flow and pa(O2). This may of course lead to misdiagnosis of patients with hyperthyroidism as having hyperventilation syndrome. Patients with hyperthyroidism also show a greater ventilatory response to hypoxia than normal patients. These ventilatory changes are significantly correlated with T3 levels. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page:2939

Sample MCQ #8

The average daily production of T4 in the body is around _______ mcg/kg

A1
B1.6
C2
D2.4
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 1.6

The average daily production of T4 is around 80-85 mcg/day (1.6-1.8 mcg/kg) In a patient with no endogenous thyroid hormone production, the replacement dose is typically 1.6-1.8 mcg/kg The dose of thyroxine is adjusted based on the TSH value done after 6 to 8 weeks Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition page 2927
