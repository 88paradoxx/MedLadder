Sample MCQ #1

A 14 year old boy develops red eye and rhinitis frequently during the start of the summer. What is the likely triggering agent?

AHouse dust mite
BGrass pollen
CWillow pollen
DIsocyanates
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Grass pollen

Grass pollen is released late May till August, and willow pollen is released from March till June. As the allergic rhinitis is triggered during the start of summer, this is most likely to be due to grass pollen. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal medicine, 21st edition Ch:288 Pg 2160

Sample MCQ #2

Diurnal variation of ACTH depends on ?

ASuprachiasmatic nucleus
BSupraoptic nucleus
CVentrolateral nucleus
DThalamus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Suprachiasmatic nucleus

Ans. A. Suprachiasmatic nucleus The biologic clock responsible for the diurnal ACTH rhythm is located in the suprachiasmatic nuclei of the hypothalamus.

Sample MCQ #3

A 14-year-old girl is seen at the endocrine clinic because her mother is concerned that her daughter's periods have not yet started. In reviewing her history, which of the following would be most typical of the first sign of onset of puberty?

APubic hair growth
BBreast-bud development
CAxillary hair growth
DMenarche
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Breast-bud development

Breast-bud enlargement (thelarche) is the first sign of puberty in girls. The average age of pubertal onset is earlier in girls than boys. The order of pubertal progression in females is thelarche, pubarche followed by menarche. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 3031

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following is not a cholinergic symptom of hypoglycemia?

ASweating
BPalpitation
CHunger
DParesthesias
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Palpitation

Cholinergic symptoms of hypoglycemia, mediated by acetylcholine which is released from sympathetic postganglionic neurons are sweating, hunger and paresthesia. Adrenergic symptoms mediated by norepinephrine which is released from sympathetic postganglionic neurons and epinephrine released from the adrenal medulla are the tremor, anxiety, palpitations. Reference: Harrison 20 th chap 399 pg. 2884

Sample MCQ #8

A patient who is on Amphotericin B developed weakness and cramps.The serum potassium was 2.3 mEq/dl. Calculate the total parenteral potassium supplementation to be prescribed to the patient over the next 24 hours?

A60mEq
B80mEq
C100mEq
D140 mEq
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 140 mEq

For causing a rise of potassium in blood by 1mEq/dl ,200 mEq of potassium supplementation is needed.Since symptoms develop with serum potassium less than 3mEq/dl the clinician should provide sufficient potassium to rise up to 3mEq/dl through the IV route. Following oral potassium supplement will suffice.Therefore, mathematically speaking this patient needs approximately 0.7 mEq/dl of potassium to rise in the blood resulting in a requirement of 0.7 x 200 = 140 mEq OVER 24hrs. Reference: Harrison 20 th chap 49 pg.308
