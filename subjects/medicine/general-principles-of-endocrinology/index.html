Sample General Principles of Endocrinology MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A 14 year old boy develops red eye and rhinitis frequently during the start of the summer. What is the likely triggering agent?

AHouse dust mite
BGrass pollen
CWillow pollen
DIsocyanates
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Grass pollen

Grass pollen is released late May till August, and willow pollen is released from March till June. As the allergic rhinitis is triggered during the start of summer, this is most likely to be due to grass pollen. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal medicine, 21st edition Ch:288 Pg 2160

Sample MCQ #2

Diurnal variation of ACTH depends on ?

ASuprachiasmatic nucleus
BSupraoptic nucleus
CVentrolateral nucleus
DThalamus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Suprachiasmatic nucleus

Ans. A. Suprachiasmatic nucleus The biologic clock responsible for the diurnal ACTH rhythm is located in the suprachiasmatic nuclei of the hypothalamus.

Sample MCQ #3

A 14-year-old girl is seen at the endocrine clinic because her mother is concerned that her daughter's periods have not yet started. In reviewing her history, which of the following would be most typical of the first sign of onset of puberty?

APubic hair growth
BBreast-bud development
CAxillary hair growth
DMenarche
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Breast-bud development

Breast-bud enlargement (thelarche) is the first sign of puberty in girls. The average age of pubertal onset is earlier in girls than boys. The order of pubertal progression in females is thelarche, pubarche followed by menarche. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 3031
