Sample MCQ #3

A 34 year old lady has developed worsening pruritus and jaundice over the past year. Investigations revealed a positive antimitochondrial antibody and a liver biopsy confirmed the diagnosis of primary biliary cirrhosis. Which of the following is associated with the condition?

ARaised IgA
BMale
CXanthelasma
DAnti gliadin antibody
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Xanthelasma

Primary biliary cirrhosis is more common in females and is usually associated with raised IgM levels. Xanthelasma are present due to cholestasis. Connective tissue disorders such as Sjogren's syndrome are associated. A positive antimitochondrial antibody and liver biopsy are the best ways to confirm the diagnosis. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine , 21 st Ed, Ch: 344 Pg: 2627

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following is NOT fitting criteria for APLA?

ASmall vessel thrombosis
BDeath of normal fetus at 12th week of gestation
CPremature birth at 35th week of gestation
DFour abortions before the 10th week
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Premature birth at 35th week of gestation

Clinical criteria include vascular thrombosis, defined as one or more clinical episodes of arterial, venous, or small vessel thrombosis in any tissue or organ; and pregnancy morbidity, defined as one or more unexplained deaths of a morphologically normal fetus at or beyond the 10th week of gestation; one or more premature births of a morphologically normal neonate before the 34th week of gestation because of eclampsia, severe preeclampsia, or placental insufficiency; or three or more unexplained consecutive spontaneous abortions before the 10th week of gestation. Laboratory criteria include LA, anticardiolipin (aCL), and/or anti-&beta;2 GPI antibodies, at intermediate or high titres on two occasions 12 weeks apart. Reference: HARRISON'S PRINCIPLES OF INTERNAL MEDICIENE, ED 21 st , CHAPTER 357 pg 2749

Sample MCQ #7

The first-line therapy for a 50 year old man with severe aplastic anemia is best described as follows?

AA myeloablative allogeneic stem cell transplant from an unrelated donor
BImmunosuppressive therapy with high-dose glucocorticoids
CImmunosuppressive therapy with horse anti-thymocyte globulin (ATG) followed by ciclosporin
DSupportive care only with red cells, platelets and G-CSF
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Immunosuppressive therapy with horse anti-thymocyte globulin (ATG) followed by ciclosporin

The treatment of choice is immunosuppression with horse ATG, which is safer than currently available rabbit ATG. Horse ATG should be followed by ciclosporin as longer-term immunosuppression. It induces hematologic recovery (independence from transfusion and a leukocyte count adequate to prevent infection) in 60-70% of patients. Allogeneic stem cell transplantation is reserved as first -line treatment for young patients ( Supportive care is important but not as sole therapy and there is no proven useful role for G-CSF. Long-term glucocorticoids dramatically increase the risk of fatal fungal infection in neutropenic patients. Reference: Harrison's Principles of internal med 21st ed chap 102, pg. 797

Sample MCQ #8

The pathogenesis of most cases of acquired aplastic anemia can be attributed to:

AEnvironmental exposure to benzene and benzene derivatives
BInherited defects of DNA repair
CRadiation exposure
DAutoimmunity
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Autoimmunity

Environmental exposure to myelosuppressive chemicals and radiation are now uncommon causes of aplastic anemia. Fanconi anemia and dyskeratosis congenita (disorders of DNA repair and telomere maintenance, respectively, account for no more than 20% of cases). The majority of patients with acquired aplastic anemia have an autoimmune disease characterized by oligoclonal T-cell populations that function to repress stem cell function. This accounts for the favorable responses to immunosuppressive therapy in such patients. Reference: Harrison's Principles of internal medicine 21st ed chap 116 pg. 911
