Sample MCQ #1

Which is the antibody associated with Hashimoto's thyroiditis?

AAnti-CCP
BAnti-TSH
CAnti-thyroglobulin receptor
DAnti-TPO
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Anti-TPO

Explanation: Hashimoto's thyroiditis is an autoimmune thyroid disease characterized by marked lymphocytic infiltration of the thyroid, follicular atrophy, and fibrosis . The disease is primarily mediated by T-cell-induced thyroid cell destruction , but autoantibodies serve as important clinical markers . The most relevant antibodies in Hashimoto's thyroiditis are: Anti-thyroid peroxidase (Anti-TPO) antibodies &rarr; most commonly present, clinically useful for diagnosis. Anti-thyroglobulin (Tg) antibodies &rarr; also present in many cases. (vs Option C) TSH-receptor blocking antibodies &rarr; seen in some patients, especially in thyroid atrophy, but less common. (vs Option B) Among these, anti-TPO antibodies are the most strongly associated and are the standard diagnostic marker. Anti-CCP (Option A): Seen in rheumatoid arthritis , not thyroid disease.

Sample MCQ #2

A 66 year old man is investigated for lower back pain. He also complains of weight loss and fevers. On reviewing his history, he has been told before that he has ankylosing spondylitis. On admission, his blood tests show a urea of 66 mg/dl and a creatinine of 3.50 mg/dl. An ultrasound of the kidneys shows hydronephrosis bilaterally. CT scan shows fibrotic para-aortic masses. Which of the following is the diagnosis?

ACarcinoid syndrome
BRetroperitoneal fibrosis
CAmyloidosis
DMetastatic bladder cancer
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Retroperitoneal fibrosis

The symptoms of fevers, weight loss and lower back pain are classical for retroperitoneal fibrosis. There is an association with inflammatory conditions such as SLE, rheumatoid arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, Hashimoto's thyroiditis and glomerulonephrosis. CT or MRI shows fibrotic para-aortic masses causing ureteral obstruction. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 319 Pg: 2375

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following drugs used in RA is given orally?

ATofacitinib
BInfliximab
CEtanercept
DRituximab
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Tofacitinib

Among the newer agents, JAK inhibitors are the drugs that are given orally among the options. Conventional DMARDS are also oral agents. Biologicals are all parenteral agents. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21st ed, Ch: 358, Pg: 2761-2764

Sample MCQ #4

A 15 year old female who is diagnosed with SLE has come to your OP for review. Which of the following statements is true? 1. Permanent remission is seen only in 2. Aim of treatment is disease remission on treatment or Low Lupus Disease Activity. 3. Anti ds DNA or C3 levels are used as markers of disease flare 4. SLEDAI 2k score of >3 indicates active disease.

AAll are true
B1, 3 and 4
C1, 2 and 3
D1, 2 and 4
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 1, 2 and 3

The question deals with treatment endpoints in SLE. Complete permanent remission(no disease activity without any treatment) is seen in Aim of treatment in SLE is disease remission on treatment or low level disease activity. Disease activity is measured using SLEDAI 2k score and a score of > 4 indicates an active disease. Anti ds DNA titres and C3 complement levels are used as markers of disease flare. From among the given options, SLEDAI2K score of > 3 does not indicate active disease. Hence it is wrong. So statements 1,2 and 3 are correct. Reference: Harrison's principles of Internal Medicine 21st edition Ch : 356 Pg: 2740, 2746

Sample MCQ #5

Which of the following is a ts DMARD used in treatment of RA?

AAnakinra
BTofacitinib
CInfliximab
DRituximab
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Tofacitinib

TS DMARDS or targeted synthetic DMARDS are inhibitors of JAK and are therapeutically used in RA. The JAK family links the extracellular domains of the cytokines involved in RA to the intra cellular signals. They include Tofacitinib : JAK 1 and JAK 3 inhibitor Baricitinib: JAK 1 and JAK 2 inhibitors Upadacitinib: JAK 1 inhibitor They can be used as monotherapy or in combination with methotrexate. They cause neutropenia, increase in cholesterol levels and rise in serum creatinine and transaminases. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21st ed, Ch: 358, Pg: 2761-2764

Sample MCQ #6

A 55 year old woman with psoriasis has significant joint pains.Which one of the following is effective in the treatment of psoriatic arthropathy?

ACodeine phosphate
BMethotrexate
CBeta interferon
DCapecitabine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Methotrexate

The conventional DMARDs that are useful in Psoriatic Arthritis are: Methotrexate, Sulfasalazine, Leflunomide. Biologicals that are used include: anti TNF&alpha; agents, anti IL17/23 agents and anti JAK agents like Tofacitinib. Phosphodiesterase inhibitor Apremilast is also used. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st ed, Ch: 362, Pg: 2800

Sample MCQ #8

What are the parameters that you use in a patient with SLE for assessing the disease activity?

AAnti ds DNA and serum Complement levels
BESR
CCRP
DANA
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Anti ds DNA and serum Complement levels

The disease activity in SLE can be assessed by measuring the Serum ds DNA activity and a decrease in Serum complement levels(C3 or C4). ESR and CRP can be raised in disease flare or during infections and hence cant be used to differentiate them. Reference : Harrisons Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed Ch: 356, Pg:2745
