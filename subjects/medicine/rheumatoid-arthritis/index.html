Sample MCQ #1

Which is the antibody associated with Hashimoto's thyroiditis?

AAnti-CCP
BAnti-TSH
CAnti-thyroglobulin receptor
DAnti-TPO
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Anti-TPO

Explanation: Hashimoto's thyroiditis is an autoimmune thyroid disease characterized by marked lymphocytic infiltration of the thyroid, follicular atrophy, and fibrosis . The disease is primarily mediated by T-cell-induced thyroid cell destruction , but autoantibodies serve as important clinical markers . The most relevant antibodies in Hashimoto's thyroiditis are: Anti-thyroid peroxidase (Anti-TPO) antibodies &rarr; most commonly present, clinically useful for diagnosis. Anti-thyroglobulin (Tg) antibodies &rarr; also present in many cases. (vs Option C) TSH-receptor blocking antibodies &rarr; seen in some patients, especially in thyroid atrophy, but less common. (vs Option B) Among these, anti-TPO antibodies are the most strongly associated and are the standard diagnostic marker. Anti-CCP (Option A): Seen in rheumatoid arthritis , not thyroid disease.

Sample MCQ #2

A 66 year old man is investigated for lower back pain. He also complains of weight loss and fevers. On reviewing his history, he has been told before that he has ankylosing spondylitis. On admission, his blood tests show a urea of 66 mg/dl and a creatinine of 3.50 mg/dl. An ultrasound of the kidneys shows hydronephrosis bilaterally. CT scan shows fibrotic para-aortic masses. Which of the following is the diagnosis?

ACarcinoid syndrome
BRetroperitoneal fibrosis
CAmyloidosis
DMetastatic bladder cancer
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Retroperitoneal fibrosis

The symptoms of fevers, weight loss and lower back pain are classical for retroperitoneal fibrosis. There is an association with inflammatory conditions such as SLE, rheumatoid arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, Hashimoto's thyroiditis and glomerulonephrosis. CT or MRI shows fibrotic para-aortic masses causing ureteral obstruction. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 319 Pg: 2375

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following drugs used in RA is given orally?

ATofacitinib
BInfliximab
CEtanercept
DRituximab
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Tofacitinib

Among the newer agents, JAK inhibitors are the drugs that are given orally among the options. Conventional DMARDS are also oral agents. Biologicals are all parenteral agents. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21st ed, Ch: 358, Pg: 2761-2764
