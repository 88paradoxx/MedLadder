Sample MCQ #3

A 71 year old patient has a set of investigations due to lethargy. She complains of polyuria and generalised back pains. Upon investigation, she has the following results: Hb 8 g/dl MCV 100 fl Platelets 190 x 10^9/L total protein 9 g/dl Albumin 3.4 (3.7-4.9) g/dl calcium 12 mg/dl phosphate 1.8 mg/dl What is the appropriate next investigation?

AComplete liver function tests
BUrinary albumin
CSerum Protein electrophoresis
D24 hour urine protein
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Serum Protein electrophoresis

The patient in this question has bone pains, hypercalcemia with impaired renal function and AG reversal, all pointing to the diagnosis of Myeloma. Hence the next best step would be a serum protein electrophoresis. 24 hour urine albumin or protein and LFT is of little use in this clinical scenario. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 111 Pg : 865-867

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following diseases presents with Thrombotic microangiopathy without Microangiopathic hemolytic anemia?

AMalignant Hypertension.
BPOEMS Syndrome
CHemolytic Uremic syndrome
DHELLP syndrome.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. POEMS Syndrome

Thrombotic microangiopathy(TMA) is a pathological lesion characterized by the presence of endothelial injury in the terminal arteriole and capillaries of organs, causing hyaline platelet thrombi, resulting in partial or complete occlusion of vessels. In kidneys it leads to a membranoproliferative pattern in glomerulus. Usually TMA is associated with Micro angiopathic hemolytic anemia(MAHA) characterized by schistocytes and thrombocytopenia. Typical examples of TMA include: Hemolytic uremic syndrome Thrombotic Thrombocytopenic Purpura Malignant hypertension Scleroderma renal crisis APLa syndrome HELLP syndrome HIV infection Radiation nephropathy TMA without MAHA is seen in POEMS Syndrome TMA associated with Myeloproliferative neoplasias Reference: Harrison's Principles of internal medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 217, Pg: 2364.

A 23-year-old woman was referred for investigation of iron deficiency anemia.Her mother died aged 28 years from colonic carcinoma complicating Peutz-Jegher syndrome.Which is the most likely mode of inheritance of Peutz-Jegher syndrome?

AAutosomal dominant
BAutosomal recessive
CMitochondrial
DX Linked
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Autosomal dominant

Peutz-Jegher syndrome is a condition characterized by perioral pigmentation and numerous hamartomas of the bowel.Originally it was assumed that these did not predispose to malignancy but studies now suggest the contrary.The condition is autosomal dominant. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st Ed, Ch: 81, Pg: 638

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following is a cause of macroglossia?

AAmyloidosis
BCrohn's disease
CGlossitis
DPeutz-Jeghers syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Amyloidosis

Macroglossia, Raccoon eyes, hepatomegaly, anemia are features of amyloidosis Crohn's disease can affect any part of the gastrointestinal tract from the mouth to the anus. Aphthous ulceration is common in active disease. Aphthous ulcers and glossitis are both features of celiac disease. Patients with Peutz-Jeghers syndrome have characteristic mucocutaneous pigmentation, that leads to perioral freckling. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st ed, Ch: 112, Pg: 880

Sample MCQ #8

Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura is a syndrome characterized by:

AThrombocytosis, anemia, neurologic abnormalities, progressive renal failure and fever.
