Sample MCQ #2

A 35-year man has been referred for management of hypertension. On examination, he had palpable kidneys. This was confirmed by abdominal ultrasound which showed multiple cysts. Which of the following is likely to be associated?

APolycythaemia
BNail pitting
CHypermobility
DMitral stenosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Polycythaemia

A young hypertensive with palpable kidneys and cysts on ultrasound points to the diagnosis of ADPKD (autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease). Polycystic kidney disease is associated with increased levels of EPO which lead to polycythemia. Associations of ADPKD: Cardiovascular: Intracranial aneurysm (5 times more common than general population), can rupture to cause subarachnoid hemorrhage. Diffuse arterial dolichoectasia of the anterior and posterior cerebral circulation &rarr; predisposed to arterial dissection and stroke. Mitral valve prolapse. Insufficiency of the mitral, aortic, and tricuspid valves. Gastrointestinal: Colonic diverticula Abdominal wall hernia Polycystic liver disease Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st Edition, Page Number 2350-2354.

Sample MCQ #3

A 52 year old man presents with lower back pains and lethargy. Investigations subsequently confirmed obstructive nephropathy and retroperitoneal fibrosis. His medication is reviewed on the ward round. Which medication is well known to cause retroperitoneal fibrosis?

AIsoniazid
BRifampicin
CAzathioprine
DMethysergide
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Methysergide

Retroperitoneal fibrosis is a disease featuring the proliferation of fibrous tissue in the retroperitoneum, the compartment of the body containing the kidneys, aorta, renal tract and various other structures. It may present with lower back pain, renal failure, hypertension, deep vein thrombosis and other obstructive symptoms. It may have an autoimmune etiology. 1/3 rd cases are secondary to malignancy, medications, aortic aneurysm or certain infections. Methyldopa, methysergide, amphetamines, beta blockers, cocaine and LSD are drugs which cause retroperitoneal fibrosis. Azathioprine is used to treat the condition. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st ed, Ch: 319 Pg: 2375
