Sample MCQ #1

A 30 year old man gives a six-month history of worsening breathlessness and coughs up half a cupful of sputum daily. He was occasionally wheezy with viral illnesses as a young child, works in a factory and has smoked 25 cigarettes per day for the last four years. The most likely diagnosis is:

ALung carcinoma
BBronchiectasis
CAsthma Asbestosis
DChronic obstructive pulmonary disease
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Bronchiectasis

Bronchiectasis is most likely due to the extensive amounts of sputum production. In a young person, cystic fibrosis and hypogammaglobulinemia should be considered. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal medicine, 21st edition Ch: 290 Pg 2173

Sample MCQ #2

The most common extra intestinal manifestation of Amebiasis is?

APainful genital ulcers
BCerebral involvement
CPleuropulmo involvement
DLiver abscess

✓ Correct Answer: D. Liver abscess

Extraintestinal infection by E. histolytica Most often involves the liver. Of travelers who develop an amebic liver abscess after leaving an endemic area, 95% do so within 5 months. Sample MCQ #3

The most common complication of Amebiasis is?

ARupture into the pericardium
BAcute abdomen
CHepato Bronchial fistula
DPleuropulmo involvement
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Pleuropulmo involvement

Pleuropulmonary involvement, which is reported in 20-30% of patients, is the most frequent complication of amebic liver abscess. Rupture into the pericardium, usually from abscesses of the left lobe of the liver, carries the gravest prognosis; it can occur during medical therapy and requires surgical drainage. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal medicine, 21st edition Ch: 223Pg 1717
