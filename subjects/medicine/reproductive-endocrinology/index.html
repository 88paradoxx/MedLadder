Sample MCQ #1

A 12-year-old girl presents with short stature, webbed neck and primary amenorrhoea.Given the likely clinical diagnosis, which hormone would be most appropriate to treat this condition?

AGrowth hormone
BProgesterone
CEstrogen
DPulsatile GnRH
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Estrogen

This girl most probably has Turner's syndrome , which is most commonly associated with absence of one X chromosome (XO pattern). Characteristic features in this condition include streak ovaries and absent internal genitalia. Estrogen administration will develop female secondary sex characters and life-long therapy will be required. Growth hormone may be useful for a limited period to increase her height. As the ovaries are absent, pulsatile GnRH and luteinising hormone would be of no use. Progesterone would be useful for inducing withdrawal bleed in females sufficiently treated with estrogen. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 3000

Sample MCQ #2

Kallmann's syndrome is due congenital synthesis defect of?

ALH
BFSH
CTestosterone
DGnRH
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. GnRH

Kallmann's syndrome is hypogonadotropic hypogonadism with anosmia. It is due to defective hypothalamic gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH)synthesis. KAL gene defect on chromosome Xp22.3 is the culprit. Embryonic migration of GnRH neurons from hypothalamic olfactory placode to hypothalamus is prevented due to this genetic defect. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 3013

Sample MCQ #3

Most common cause of anovulation in females is:

AFunctional hypothalamic amenorrhea
BPCOS
CPremature ovarian insufficiency
DOvarian tumors

✓ Correct Answer: B. PCOS

Five most important causes for anovulation in females include PCOS and other hyperandrogenic disorders, hypothalamic anovulation, hyperprolactinemia, POI and chronic illnesses Reference : Williams Textbook of Endocrinology 14th edition Page 601
