Sample MCQ #1

Which blood group is more prone to helicobacter and cholera infection?

AA
BB
CO
DNone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. O

In most people (80%), A, B, and H antigens are secreted by the cells and are present in the circulation as well as in various secretions such as saliva ( Se phenotype ). Others, called "nonsecretors" do not secrete A, B, and H antigens ( se phenotype ). ABO and Se/se systems influence the susceptibility to a variety of diseases. Malaria is less severe in O than non O patients. Group O is associated with enhanced susceptibility to Helicobacter pylori (and gastric ulcer) as well as to cholera bacillus or to norovirus . Group O individuals exhibit a lesser procoagulation phenotype when compared to non O individuals. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 113, Pg: 884

Sample MCQ #2

A patient has been diagnosed as having epidemic typhus. By which vector is he most likely to have become infected?

AHard tick
BHuman body louse
CTrombiculid mite
DAedes aegypti mosquito
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Human body louse

The chief vector of epidemic typhus is the human body louse. The hard tick is a vector for a number of rickettsial diseases. Larval trombiculid mites spread scrub typhus. Rat fleas are responsible for the spread of endemic (murine) typhus. The Aedes aegypti mosquito is the vector for dengue fever Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition, Ch:187 Pg:1435
