Sample Sleep Apnea MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A 36-year-old man visits his doctor because his wife has long complained of his snoring, but recently observed that his breathing stops for a couple of minutes at a time while he is sleeping. He undergoes polysomnography and ventilatory response testing to ascertain the extent and cause of his sleep apnea. The activity of the central chemoreceptors is stimulated by which of the following?

AAn increase in the PCO2 of blood flowing through the brain
BA decrease in the PO2 of blood flowing through the brain
CAn increase in the pH of the CSF
DA decrease in the metabolic rate of the surrounding brain tissue
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. An increase in the PCO2 of blood flowing through the brain

The central chemoreceptors, located at or near the ventral surface of the medulla, are stimulated to increase ventilation by a decrease in the pH of their extracellular fluid (ECF). The pH of this ECF is affected by the PCO2 of the blood supply to the medullary chemoreceptor area, as well as by the CO2 and lactic acid production of the surrounding brain tissue. The central chemoreceptors are not stimulated by decreases in PaO2 or blood oxygen content but rather can be depressed by long-term or severe decreases in oxygen supply. Reference: Ganong, pp 675-678. Levitzky, pp 206-208.

Sample MCQ #2

A 30-year-old male presents with hematuria and hemoptysis of two weeks duration. His urinalysis showed 2+ protein and dysmorphic RBC. His physician suspected a pulmonary renal syndrome and performs a renal biopsy. The pathologist uses a special stain to visualize the basement membrane. What is it?

ACongo Red
BMasons Trichrome
CPer-iodic Acid Schiff
DJone's Methanamine silver.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Jone's Methanamine silver.

Referring to the previous question, this patient is also suffering from a pulmonary renal syndrome. If the pathologist wants to stain the basement membrane, he might be suspecting Goodpasteur's syndrome. Jone's Methanamine silver is the stain used for basement membrane. Congo red is used for Amyloid. Masons Trichrome for collagen PAS for staining carbohydrate moiety in the cell membranes. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 314, Pg: 2336.
