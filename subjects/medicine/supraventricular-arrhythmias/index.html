Sample MCQ #1

Mr. Kohli is a 26-year-old cricketer comes to you for regular check-up. On your assessment, you note a resting heart rate or 45 bpm with an occasional pause of up to 2 seconds. His blood pressure is 110/70 mmHg. He feels well and reports no syncopal or presyncopal episodes at rest or during exercise. ECG is normal. Which of the following would be next step ?

AAtropine
BFollow-up after 1 week. Avoid cricket for a week
CNo further follow-up
DHe should undergo a treadmill stress ECG to determine the presence of chronotropic competence.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. No further follow-up

Sinus bradycardia and pauses up to 3 seconds are common in young individuals, particularly in highly trained athletes. (You know Mr. Kohli) Given the normal ECG, lack of symptoms, and normal physical examination, no further testing is warranted. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 245

Sample MCQ #2

A 74-year-old woman presents with a feeling of shortness of breath and choking on lying down. Around 4 months earlier she had been diagnosed with atrial fibrillation and was started on aspirin and digoxin by her physician. On examination her physician could feel a goitre. Plain radiography confirmed retrosternal extension, which was presumed to be contributing to her shortness of breath. Her TSH level was less than 0.05 mU/l. Thyroid autoantibodies were negative. What diagnosis best fits with this clinical picture?

AHashimoto's thyroiditis
BToxic, multinodular goitre
CDequervain thyroiditis
DGraves disease
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Toxic, multinodular goitre

Toxic multinodular goitre usually occurs in women over 55 years of age and is more common than Grave's disease in the elderly. Her atrial fibrillation may well be related to this. Her goitre is obstructing, with significant retrosternal extension, and surgery is the treatment of choice. Initially, however, she should be rendered euthyroid with antithyroid drugs such as carbimazole. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page:2948

Sample MCQ #3

A 56 year old chronic alcoholic is found on the street with a low conscious level. His GCS is 3/15 on arrival in casualty. On examination, his BP is 110/65 and he is jaundiced. There is marked ascites and he has 7 spider naevi. ECG shows atrial fibrillation with a ventricular rate of 120. Which is the most practical test to elucidate why he is unconscious?

AEEG
BUltrasound of abdomen
CRandom blood sugar
DPhenytoin and carbamazepine levels
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Random blood sugar

In a patient with liver disease, it is important to exclude hypoglycemia as a cause of unconsciousness. Following confirming that baseline observations and BM is normal, the next best step is to perform a CT of his head to exclude a central neurological cause of decreased conscious level. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 28, Pg: 187

AWarfarin therapy

Sample MCQ #5

A 66 year man who is on warfarin for atrial fibrillation is complaining of worsening breathlessness over the last 2 months. On examination, he has an irregular heart beat, blood pressure of 110 / 60 mmHg and he looked pale. There was no evidence of organomegaly. Investigations show: Haemoglobin 8.5 g/dL MCV 70 fL PLT 160 x 10^9/L His upper gastrointestinal tract endoscopy and colonoscopy were normal. What is the next best investigation?

ABarium enema
BSmall bowel enema
CCapsule endoscopy
DMesenteric angiography
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Capsule endoscopy

This patient has microcytic anaemia and is also on warfarin. So the priority is to rule out bleeding. Since upper GI endoscopy and colonoscopy were normal, the next step would be capsule endoscopy which helps to see bleeds in small bowel . In this case angiodysplasia of small bowel could be a cause of bleeding. In capsule endoscopy, a patient swallows a small pill which emits a radio frequency which is picked up by a sensor that the patient wears over several hours. Images are then downloaded as a continuous movie. Another possible cause of GI bleed in a patient with AF is mesenteric insufficiency, which will be characterized by abdominal pain post prandial. In that case mesenteric angiography will be the investigation. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 322, Pg:2417

Sample MCQ #6

A 35 year old patient with rheumatic mitral stenosis has history of palpitations and irregular heartbeat. She presents unwell with fast Atrial Fibrillation and was DC cardioverted successfully. Which of the following medications is most effective for maintenance of sinus rhythm?

ADigoxin
BAmiodarone
CAtenolol
DDiltiazem
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Amiodarone

Amiodarone is most effective for maintenance of sinus rhythm. Beta-blockers and non-dihydropyridine calcium channel blockers are used to control ventricular rate in patients with Atrial fibrillation. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 1907

Sample MCQ #7

A 50-year-old politician presented with a strange fluttering sensation in his chest, but no chest pain. The symptoms lasted 24 hours. An ECG revealed atrial fibrillation with a ventricular rate of 130 beats per minute. His blood pressure is 110/70 mm Hg. Which one of the following drugs is most likely to restore sinus rhythm?

AAdenosine
BBisoprolol
CDigoxin
DFlecainide
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Flecainide

A patient with atrial fibrillation and hemodynamic instability (hypotension/shock/impaired consciousness/heart failure/angina) is treated with DC cardioversion. In a patient with AF without hemodynamic instability, the two approaches to management include controlling the heart rate or restoring sinus rhythm. Flecainide is the drug most likely to restore sinus rhythm in atrial fibrillation. Other drugs include Amiodarone, sotalol, dronaderone, propafenone etc. Adenosine is used in patients with supraventricular tachycardia (SVT). Digoxin and Bisoprolol are indicated for rate control and are not normally associated with the restoration of sinus rhythm. Verapamil can be used for rate control in atrial fibrillation, and may be used in the treatment of SVT. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 1907

Sample MCQ #8

A 71 year old man who had multiple episodes of unilateral amaurosis fugax. He has risk factors of being a smoker and hypertension. He is on atenolol and aspirin. An ECG shows atrial fibrillation. Carotid dopplers show 80% right carotid stenosis. What is the most appropriate management?

AWarfarin
BCarotid Endarterectomy, then warfarin
CClopidogrel
DHigh dose aspirin 300mg with a proton pump inhibitor
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Carotid Endarterectomy, then warfarin

As there is > 70% carotid artery stenosis and symptoms suggestive of emboli, endarterectomy is recommended. The patient should also be anticoagulated afterwards in view of atrial fibrillation. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 427, Pg: 3347
