Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is the most common sexually transmitted pathogen?

ATrichomonas vaginalis
BHuman immunodeficiency virus
CNeisseria gonorrhoeae
DHuman Papillomavirus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Human Papillomavirus

Genital HPV remains the most common sexually transmitted pathogen in the United States, infecting 60% of a cohort of initially HPV-negative, sexually active Washington state college women within 5 years in a study conducted from 1990 to 2000-i.e., during the pre-HPV immunization era. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 136, Pg: 1079

Sample MCQ #2

What is the Most common cause of nongonococcal urethritis ?

AC.trachomatis
BHerpes simplex
CTrichomonas
DCandida
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. C.trachomatis

Most common cause of urethritis in males is N. gonorrhoea. The most common cause of Non-Gonococcal urethritis in males is Chlamydia trachomatis. The most common cause of urethritis in females is Chlamydia. Hence chlamydia is the most appropriate answer. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st Ed, Ch:136 Pg: 1081, 1083

Sample MCQ #3

Ichy purple papule followed by hayperpigmentation on resolution, is seen in?

AAddison's disease
BDM
CHypothyroidism
DLichen planus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Lichen planus

Ans. D. Lichen planus LP is characterized by shiny, violaceous, flat-topped polygonal papules which retain the skin lines. White lines, known as Wickham's striae, may traverse the surface of the papules. Linear lesions often appear along scratch marks or in scars (Koebner phenomenon). In most cases, the papules eventually flatten after a 6 months to 2 yrs, often to be replaced by an area of hyperpigmentation.
