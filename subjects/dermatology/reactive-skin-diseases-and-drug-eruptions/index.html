Sample MCQ #1

A patient developed bullae without erythema on elbows, knee & sacral area fib crust formation, scarring & milia. He had no photo sensitivity and negative family history for bullous diseases. On DIF IgG deposition at DEJ with no blood vessel involvement is seen probable diagnosis is:

AEB dystrophic
BEB acquisita
CPorphyria CT
DPemphigoid bullous
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. EB acquisita

Epidermolysis Bullosa Acquisita (EBA) Epidermolysis bullosa - congenital, present from birth EBA is not inherited and usually presents in adult life EBA is a rare disease in which tense blisters appear at sites of trauma EBA blisters tend to be localized to areas that are easily injured such as the hands, feet, knees, elbows, and buttocks Sometimes there is mucosal involvement with blisters forming in mouth, nose and eyes EBA may mimic Bullous pemphigoid Salt split immunofluorescence used to differentiate between EBA and BP Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 50, Page 45

Sample MCQ #5

False regarding dermatitis herpetiformis is/are? 1. Associated with gluten sensitive enteropathy 2. Caused by herpes virus 3. Mucosal involvement is common 4. Deposition of IgG in dermis 5. Dapsone is used in the treatment

A1, 2
B2, 5
C2, 3, 4
D1, 4, 5
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 2, 3, 4

Dermatitis herpetiformis is a complex disease of the skin caused by the deposition of IgA in the papillary dermis which triggers an immunologic cascade. It has no association with viral or bacterial etiology. Dermatitis herpetiformis aka Duhring's disease is commonly a/w genetic predisposition for gluten sensitivity, l/t formation of IgA antibodies against gluten-tissue transglutaminase. Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 50, Page 53

Sample MCQ #6

A 17-year-old male presents to the clinic due to an intermittent pruritic rash. He has a past medical history of asthma. On physical examination, the rash can be reproduced by drawing on the skin with a blunt object. Which of the following is the phenomenon is elicited here?

ADermatographism
BKoebners phenomenon
CReverse koebners phenomenon
DDarriers sign
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Dermatographism

The examination finding is s/o symptomatic dermographism which is seen in chronic urticaria Prevention and avoidance of precipitating factors such as physical stimuli and stressors are important in managing dermatographism. Choice therapy includes treatment with H1 antihistamines such as cetirizine or loratadine. Reference : Rooks textbook of dermatology, 9 th edition, chapter 42, Page no 11

Sample MCQ #7

All the following are TRUE about erythema multiform EXCEPT :

AVesicle and bullae are not seen
BAssociated with internal malignancy
CTarget lesion with bull's eye lesion seen over palms and soles
DAssociated with adverse reaction to drugs
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Vesicle and bullae are not seen

Erythema multiforme - manifests as very characteristic 1-2 cm circular lesions consisting of 3 concentric zones - a pinkish raised periphery, pallor in middle and a dark violaceous centre thickened to the iris of the eye or the target of the shooting range. Such a lesions are therefore called iris lesions or target lesions Some case may develop tense blister at the centre of the target lesions Approximately 90% of cases of erythema multiforme minor follow outbreaks of herpes simplex (HSV 1 infection)
