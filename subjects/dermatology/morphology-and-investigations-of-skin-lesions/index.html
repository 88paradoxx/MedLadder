Sample MCQ #1

Which bedside tests helps to differentiate nevus anemicus and nevus depigmentosus ?

AWoods lamp
BDiascopy
CDermoscopy
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Diascopy

Pressing the lesion with glass slide - diascopy Helps differentiate between ND and NA. The margins of ND remain distinct from normal skin during diascopy. While margins of NA blend in with surrounding skin as normal skin also blanches on pressing with the slide

Sample MCQ #2

The cell seen in Tzanck smear in pemphigus is:

ACell with hyperchromatic nuclei and perinuclear halo
BCell with hypochromatic nuclei and perinuclear halo
CMultinucleated cells
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Cell with hyperchromatic nuclei and perinuclear halo

Acantholytic cells appear as round cells with perinuclear halo in Tzanck smear: Characteristic of Pemphigus Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 50, page 3

Sample MCQ #3

A patient presented to the OPD with dome shaped papules with central umbilication, on face and cytology smear was made. What is the finding observed?

AHanderson-Peterson bodies
BKoilocytes
CTzanck cells
DMultinucleate giant cells
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Handerson-Peterson bodies

Dome shaped papules with central umbilication - diagnosis of molluscum contagiosum Tzanck smear can be made from the white pasty core of the molluscum lesion - Giemsa stain done. Tzanck smear reveals the presence of diagnostic intracytoplasmic molluscum bodies (Henderson-Patterson bodies), the largest known inclusion bodies (30-35 m) Reference : Rook&rsquo;s textbook of dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 25, Page 12

AScar
BScale

DErosion

AMacule
BPatch
CPlaque
DPapule

Flat, non-palpable, Change in color of skin 1cm &rarr; patch Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th edition, Chapter 4, page3

Sample MCQ #7

Break in continuity of epidermis is called:

AUlcer
BExcoriation
CErosion
DAtrophy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Ulcer

Full thickness loss of epidermis BM: not intact Reaches dermis Heals with scarring Erosion - partial loss of epidermis. Excoriation - linear scratch marks Atrophy - loss of substance in skin Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th edition, Chapter 4, page 11

Sample MCQ #8

Application of pressure from side on an intact bulla is called as the elicitation of:

ANikolsky sign
BBullae spread sign
CAsboe Hansen sign
DHutchinsons sign
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Bullae spread sign

Nikolsky sign: Application of tangential pressure on intact skin leads to formation of erosion Bullae spread sign: Application of pressure from side on an intact bulla leads to extension of bullae Asboe Hansen sign: Application of pressure on the bullae from the top/centre Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 50, page 5
