Sample Dermatitis MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Profilaggrin is found in the ______.

AStratum corneum
BStratum granulosum
CStratum spinosum
DStratum basale
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Stratum granulosum

Profilaggrin is found in the keratohyalin granules present in the stratum granulosum. Profilaggrin breaks down to yield individual filaggrin monomers at the transition between the stratum granulosum and stratum corneum. Deficiency of filaggrin: Atopic dermatitis & Ichthyosis vulgaris Reference : Rook's Textbook of Dermatology, 9th Edition, Page Number-7.

Sample MCQ #2

In which of the following conditions Parakeratosis most frequently occurs -

AActinic keratoses
BSeborrheic keratoses
CMolluscum contagiosum
DBasal cell carcinoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Actinic keratoses

Normally, nuclei are absent in stratum corneum. Retention of nuclei within the cells of stratum corneum - Parakeratosis. Parakeratosis is seen in - Normally &rarr; Mucous membrane of mouth and vagina. Disorders &rarr; Benign (Psoriasis, Seborrheic dermatitis) Malignant (Actinic keratosis, Squamous cell carcinoma) Reference: Rooks textbook of dermatology, chapter 3, page 34

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is characteristically associated with Acute eczema?

ASpongiosis
BOrthokeratosis
CHyper granulosis
DAcanthosis

✓ Correct Answer: A. Spongiosis

Spongiosis: Intraedpidermal intercellular edemaDermal inflammatory infiltrates around the dilated venules of the superficial plexus, edema, and spongiosis Fully developed spongiosis becomes organized in focal micro vesicles Reference: Rooks textbook of dermatology 9 th edition, chapter 39, page 39.3
