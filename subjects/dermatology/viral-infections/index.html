Sample MCQ #1

A 25-year-old female presents with red crusted lesions around the mouth and finger pulps, three months after having had small bowel resection for Crohn's disease. What is the most likely cause of her skin condition?

ANicotinamide deficiency
BThiamine deficiency
CVitamin B12 deficiency
DZinc deficiency
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Zinc deficiency

The features of this patient who had bowel resection suggest zinc deficiency . Zinc deficiency can lead to a condition called acrodermatitis enteropathica which presents with diarrhea, alopecia, muscle wasting, depression, irritability, and a rash involving the extremities, face (perioral), and perineum . The rash is characterized by vesicular and pustular crusting with scaling and erythema. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st Ed, Ch:333 Pg: 2532

Sample MCQ #2

In Tzanck smear, multinucleated giant cells are seen in:

AHerpes simplex
BPemphigoid
CMolluscum contagiosum
DPemphigus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Herpes simplex

A Tzanck smear is a cytologic technique commonly used in the diagnosis of herpes virus infections (simplex, genitalis, varicella-zoster). An early vesicle is deroofed, and the base of the lesion is scraped gently with a scalpel blade. The material is then placed on a glass slide, air-dried, and stained with Giemsa stain. Multinucleated giant cells suggest the presence of herpes. Another finding - presence of acantholytic cells Reference : Rook&rsquo;s textbook of dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 4, Page 22

Sample MCQ #3

Ballooning degeneration is a characteristic feature of:

AInsect bite
BBullous impetigo
CHerpes zoster
DPemphigus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Herpes zoster

Vesicles in herpetic infections (simplex, genitalis, varicella-zoster ) show degenerative changes characterized by ballooning, the presence of multinucleated giant cells, and eosinophilic intranuclear inclusions. Reference : Rook&rsquo;s textbook of dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 25, Page 27
