Sample MCQ #1

SAPHO Syndrome components are all except:

AAcne
BHyperostosis
COsteitis
DAcantholysis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Acantholysis

Acronym for the combination of Synovitis, Acne, Pustulosis, Hyperostosis, and Osteitis Chronic disorder that involves the skin, bone, and joints Some researchers feel that the SAPHO syndrome is related to the group of arthritis conditions which typically affect the spine, called Spondyloarthropathies Treatment typically involves medications which reduce inflammation Reference: Rook&rsquo;s Textbook of Dermatology, 9th Edition, Chapter 90, Page 54

Sample MCQ #2

Loss of Intercellular cohesion between keratinocytes is called as ?

AAcanthosis
BAcantholysis
CKeratinolysis
DSpongiosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Acantholysis

Ans. B. Acantholysis Separation of epidermal keratinocytes due to loss of intercellular bridge is referred to as acantholysis. Acantholysis is seen in the epidermis (especially in basal layer).

Sample MCQ #3

Tzanck smear in bullous pemphigoid shows:

AAcantholytic cells
BNeutrophils
CEosinophils
DMast cells
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Eosinophils

Cytologic examination of Tzanck smear in bullous pemphigoid and pemphigoid gestationis shows numerous eosinophils without Tzanck cells (acantholytic cells). Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 50, Page 18

Sample MCQ #4

AThalidomide
BRifampicin

DClofazimine

Dermatitis Herpetiformis: Sub-epidermal immunobullous disorder Association: Gluten sensitive enteropathy (GSE) Treatment: Gluten-free diet Dapsone (Drug of choice) Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 50, Page 54

Sample MCQ #6

Pemphigus vulgaris is a which type of hypersensitivity reaction?

AType 2
BType 1
CType 3
DType 4
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Type 2

The pathogenesis of pemphigus revolves around the formation of antibodies anti dsg1 and anti dsg3, and antibodies binding to the antigen dsg1 and dsg3 at their site. This mechanism is explained in type 2 hypersensitivity reaction. Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 50, page 3

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following is the correct pair?

APemphigus erythematosus, suprabasal
BPemphigus foliaceous, suprabasal
CPemphigus vegetans, sub corneal
DPemphigus vulgaris, suprabasal
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Pemphigus vulgaris, suprabasal

Separation of epidermis - intra epidermal split Pemphigus vulgaris - intraepidermal split, suprabasal level, row of tombstones appearance Pemphigus vegetans (Subtype of PV) - suprabasal Pemphigus foliaceous - sub corneal Pemphigus erythematosus (Subtype of PF) - sub corneal Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 50 page 3

Sample MCQ #8

Following is true about pemphigus erythematosus except:

AIt is the rarest type of pemphigus
BIt is considered as a variant of Pemphigus foliaceous
CIt is also called as Senear Usher Syndrome
DIt has features of Lupus erythematosus and pemphigus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. It is the rarest type of pemphigus

Senear-Usher syndrome is a disorder which results from combination of lupus erythematosus (photosensitivity) and pemphigus foliaceus. Rarest variant of pemphigus: P. vegetans Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 50, page 6
