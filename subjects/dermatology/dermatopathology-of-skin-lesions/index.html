Sample MCQ #1

The epidermis contains multiple specialized cell types with distinct functions, locations, and embryological origins. Understanding where each cell type resides is essential for dermatology and pathology. Which of the following statements about skin structure is NOT true?

ALangerhans cells originate from bone marrow and are present in granular layer.
BMelanocytes are present in stratum basale.
CStratified squamous epithelium with hallmark keratin intermediate filaments.
DMerkel cells are present in stratum basale.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Langerhans cells originate from bone marrow and are present in granular layer.

Explanation: Langerhans cells do originate from bone marrow, but they are primarily located in the stratum spinosum, not the granular layer. Key epidermal cell types and their locations: Langerhans cells: Antigen-presenting dendritic cells derived from bone marrow; mostly in stratum spinosum. Melanocytes: Neural crest-derived pigment cells; found in stratum basale. Merkel cells: Mechanoreceptors involved in touch sensation; located in stratum basale. Keratinocytes: Main epidermal cells with abundant keratin intermediate filaments, forming stratified squamous keratinized epithelium.

AMental retardation
BThrombocytopenia
CCataract
DNephropathy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Thrombocytopenia

Combination of giant hemangioma, thrombocytopenia, and consumption coagulopathy KMS is an infrequent but potentially fatal complication of rapidly growing vascular lesions in infants The low platelet count in Kasabach-Merritt syndrome is due to a combination of increased destruction and sequestration of platelets within the expanding vascular tumour. The tumor here is infantile haemangioma. MC vascular tumor in infants Reference: Rook's dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 101, Page 4

Sample MCQ #5

Which of the following genetic mutation leads to Darier's disease?

AATP 202
BATP 2A2
CATP 3A2
DATP 201
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. ATP 2A2

Darier disease, previously known as keratosis follicularis, is a rare autosomal dominant genodermatosis characterized by keratotic papules and longitudinal eyrthronychia; it is caused by mutations in the ATP2A2 gene, encoding a calcium pump within the endoplasmic reticulum. Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 66, Page 2

Sample MCQ #6

Desmosomes are helpful in connecting ____

AKeratinocytes
BMelanocytes
CDermis and epidermis
DLangerhans cells
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Keratinocytes

Desmosomes are the intra-epidermal inter-cellular adhesions that are helpful in connecting keratinocytes in the epidermis. Max concentration is in spinous cells. Desmosomes contain various proteins : Desmogleins, desmocollin and desmoplakin. Damage to the desmosomes leads to separation of keratinocytes leading to acantholysis. The cells separate from each other, become round with large nucleus and perinuclear halo called as acantholytic cells. Reference: Rooks textbook of dermatology 9th edition, chapter 2, page 18

Sample MCQ #8

Miliaria is a disorder of_____

ASebaceous glands
BApocrine glands
CHolocrine glands
DEccrine glands
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Eccrine glands

Miliaria, or eccrine miliaria, - Blocked eccrine sweat glands and ducts. The obstruction leads to leakage of sweat into the epidermis or dermis, resulting in cellular overhydration, swelling, and further occlusion of the ducts. It is most common in warm, humid climates during the summer months. The 3 main types of miliaria are crystallina, rubra, and profunda. Reference: Rook&rsquo;s Textbook of Dermatology, 9th Edition, Chapter 94, Page 12
