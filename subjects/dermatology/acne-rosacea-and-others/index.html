Sample MCQ #1

Eccrine glands are involved in which of the following:

AFordyce spots
BSyringoma
CHidradenitis suppurativa
DFox - Fordyce disease
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Syringoma

Syringomas are benign, eccrine sweat duct tumours, typically found clustered on eyelids in a symmetric pattern. Syringomas are skin-coloured or yellowish, generally small, dermal papules. Reference: Rook's Textbook of Dermatology, 9th Edition, Page Number-30.

Sample MCQ #2

Comedones are characteristic of:

APsoriasis
BAcne vulgaris
CLichen planus
DPemphigus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Acne vulgaris

Clinical Presentation of Acne Sites: Face, shoulders, upper trunk Polymorphic eruption: Comedones, papules, nodules, cysts Comedones: Pathognomonic lesions of acne vulgaris Open comedones (Black heads): Block on skin surface Closed comedones (White heads): Block below skin surface Reference: Rook&rsquo;s Textbook of Dermatology, 9th Edition, Chapter 90, Page 1

Sample MCQ #3

All of the following are Premalignant lesions except:

AArsenical Keratosis
BCutaneous horn
CGiant Hairy Naevus
DRhinophyma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Rhinophyma

Rhinophyma is a disfiguring nasal deformity due to the proliferation of sebaceous glands and underlying connective tissue. The name itself is broken down into "rhis," derived from Greek meaning nose, and "phyma," also Greek, for skin tumor. Rosacea is the precursor condition to the later development of rhinophyma Rhinophyma has no malignant potential Reference: Rooks textbook of dermatology, chapter 142, page 142.1

Sample MCQ #4

Following does not form a part of follicular occlussion tetrad?

APilonidal cysts
BAcne inverse
CAcne conglobata
DAcne fulminans
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Acne fulminans

Acne inversa is another name for Hidradenitis Suppurativa HS can be associated with other follicular occlusion diseases such as pilonidal cysts (PCs) of the sacrum and buttocks, dissecting cellulitis (DC), and acne conglobata (AC), which usually involves the face, chest. PCs, DC, and/or AC - the follicular occlusion triad When HS occurs with PCs, DC, and/or AC it is referred to as the follicular occlusion tetrad. Reference: Rook&rsquo;s Textbook of Dermatology, 9th Edition, Chapter 92, Page 2

Sample MCQ #5

Among the following alternatives, which is not a feature of rosacea?

AFlushing
BTelangiectasia
CMucosal ulcerations
DRhinophyma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Mucosal ulcerations

Rosacea is defined as a chronic disease that impacts fair skinned middle aged adults. This chronic disease is characterized as telangiectasia and flushing in early stage, pustules and papules in inflammatory stage, papules and induction and rhinophyma in chronic stage Reference: Rook&rsquo;s Textbook of Dermatology, 9th Edition, Chapter 91, Page 2

Sample MCQ #6

Differential diagnosis of Rosacea includes all of the following except:

AAcne vulgaris
BLupus erythematosus
CBCC
DMelasma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Melasma

Differential diagnosis of Rosacea includes Acne vulgaris, Seborrheic dermatitis, Perioral dermatitis, Carcinoid syndrome, Lupus erythematosus, Basal cell carcinoma Most common potential diagnostic confusion is between rosacea and acne vulgaris, owing to the presence of pustules and papules. Reference: Rook&rsquo;s Textbook of Dermatology, 9th Edition, Chapter 91, Page 10

Local side effects of topical corticosteroids among the following: 1. Blue discolouration of skin 2. Atrophy 3. Acneiform eruption 4. Hypertrichosis

A1, 2, 3
B2, 3, 4
C1, 2, 4
D2, 4

✓ Correct Answer: B. 2, 3, 4

Explanation: Blue discolouration of skin is not a side effect of corticosteroids Topical corticosteroids cause a variety of local adverse effects due to dermal atrophy and altered follicular activity. (Mneumonic - STEROID ) S - Skin thinning ( atrophy , striae) (Option 2 is true) T-; Telangiectasia E - Ecchymosis (easy bruising) R -; Rosacea-like dermatitis O - Opportunistic infections (folliculitis, tinea incognito) I - Induced acne ( steroid acne ), Increased hair growth ( hypertrichosis ) (Option 3 and 4 are true) D -; Decreased pigmentation and Delayed wound healing Key Takeaway: Bluish-Grey Discolouration of Skin (Ceruloderma) is caused by Antiarrhythmic: Amiodarone Antibiotics: Minocycline Antimalarials: Hydroxychloroquine, Chloroquine Silver compounds: Argyria Antipsychotic: Chlorpromazine

Sample MCQ #8

Which of the following drugs plays a role in chemoprevention of skin malignancies in albinism ?

AImiquimod
BIsotretinoin
CVismedogib
D5 fluoro-uracil
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Isotretinoin

Incidences of Squamous cell, carcinoma, Basal cell carcinoma, and Actinic keratosis are markedly increased in patients with Oculocutaneous albinism. Retinoids like isotretinoin are effective as chemopreventive agents in premalignant lesions of the skin. If retinoids are given at an early stage, they can cause regression of premalignant lesions of the skin. These can be recommended as they reduce the potential effects of carcinogenesis. Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th edition, chapter 70, page 8
