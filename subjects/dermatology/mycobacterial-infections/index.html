Sample MCQ #1

Facies leprosa is characterised by all except:

ALoss of upper incisors teeth
BResorption of nasal bone
CResorption of anterior nasal spine
DNodules on face
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Nodules on face

Nodules on face are not a feature of facies leprosa. Facies leprosa - Seen in lepromatous leprosy Characterized by the resorption of the nasal bone, anterior nasal spine, supra-incisive alveolar region, and anterior alveolar process of the maxilla, with associated sensory or motor damage depending on the type of nerves involved. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, pg. no 182 International Textbook Of Leprosy, Part I, Section II, Chapter 2.1, Page 16,17

Sample MCQ #2

Which form of leprosy is the Most common form of the disease encountered in India?

ATuberculoid
Blepromatous
CBorderline tuberculoid
DBorderline lepromatous
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Tuberculoid

Tuberculoid leprosy(TT) leprosy is the most common form of the disease encountered in India and Africa but is virtually absent in Southeast Asia, where borderline tuberculoid (BT) leprosy is frequent. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st Ed, Ch: 179, Pg: 1385

Sample MCQ #3

What is The Most commonly affected nerve trunk in leprosy?

AUlnar nerve
BPeroneal
CTransverse cervical
DSupra orbital
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Ulnar nerve

The most commonly affected nerve trunk in leprosy is the ulnar nerve at the elbow, whose involvement results in clawing of the fourth and fifth fingers, loss of dorsal interosseous musculature in the affected hand, and loss of sensation in these distributions Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st Ed, Ch: 179, Pg: 1387
