Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is/are correct regarding Hailey-Hailey disease? 1. Steroid not ffective 2. ATP2Cl impairment 3. Autosomal recessive 4. Flexural distribution 5. Benign pemphigus

A2, 3
B1, 3, 4
C2, 4, 5
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 2, 4, 5

Hailey-Hailey Disease Synonym: Benign familial pemphigus Autosomal dominant inheritance ATP2Cl enzyme insufficiency Flexural distribution: Painful erosions, vesico-pustules and scaly erythematous plaques appear at sites of friction such as the sides of the neck, the axillae, the groins and the perineum. Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 66, page 10
