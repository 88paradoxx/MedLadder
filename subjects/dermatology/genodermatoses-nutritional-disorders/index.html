Sample MCQ #4

The etiology of congenital epidermolysis bullosa is:

AGenetic
BInfection
CSenile
DMalignant
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Genetic

Epidermolysis bullosa is a group of mechanobullous disorders in which the skin breaks down and blister forms due to minor trauma. It is a genetic disorder with mutations in skin proteins. Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition Chapter 71, Page 2

Sample MCQ #5

A 8-day-old child with history of consanguinity in the parents, the mother reports blisters and peeling off of the skin at the site of handling and pressure. There was a similar history in the previous child, which proved to be fatal. The diagnosis is:

ABullous pemphigoid
BLetterer-Siwe disease
CCongenital syphilis
DCongenital epidermolysis bullosa
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Congenital epidermolysis bullosa

Diseases characterized under the general term epidermolysis bullosa are a heterogeneous group of hereditary blistering disorders. All of them are characterized by induction of blisters by trauma and exacerbation of blistering in warm weather. Disorders can be categorized in 3 main headings, viz. epidermolysis bullosa simplex, junctional epidermolysis bullosa and dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa. Affected infant is usually blistered at birth or develops lesions during the neonatal period. Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition Chapter 71, page 11

Sample MCQ #6

Direct Immunofluorescence is negative in all except

AEpidermolysis bullosa simplex
BHailey Hailey disease
CEpidermolysis bullosa acquisita
DTransient acantholytic dermatoses
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Epidermolysis bullosa acquisita

DIF is positive only in the immunobullous group of disorders due to presence of auto-antibody-antigen complexes in the skin. So, it will be positive in EB acquisita as it is a subepidermal IB disorder DIF will be negative in diseases which have a genetic etiology like Congenital EB, hailey hailey disease, darriers disease and Transient acantholytic dermatoses (Grovers disease). Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th Edition, Chapter 66, Page 1

✓ Correct Answer: B. Collagen type 7

Sample MCQ #8

Neurofibromatosis type 1 is a hereditary condition commonly associated with multiple caf&eacute;-au-lait spots on the skin. Which pattern of inheritance is seen in this?

AAutosomal dominant
BAutosomal recessive
CX linked dominant
DX linked recessive
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Autosomal dominant

Neurofibromatosis type 1 (NF-1) or Von Recklinghausen disease is an autosomal dominant disorder. It is the most common hamartoma neoplastic syndrome The gene for NF-1 is located on chromosome 17 and encodes a gene product called neurofibromin Neurofibromin, a GTPase-activating protein, normally inhibits the rat sarcoma (RAS) pathway. Mutations of the NF-1 gene result in a lack of expression of neurofibromin, thereby promoting tumorigenesis. Reference: Rooks textbook of Dermatology, 9 th edition, Chapter 80, page 1
