Sample MCQ #1

A baby boy born at 30 weeks of gestation is brought to you at 8 months of chronological age with complaint of not able to roll from prone to supine. What is the best next step of management?

AReassure the parents
BSend genetic studies to find congenital disease
CSend chromosomal array to find trisomy
DSend to development pediatrician and occupational therapist for early intervention
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Reassure the parents

Corrected gestational age = Chronological age- Preterm age = 32 weeks - 10 weeks = 22 weeks = 5.5 months Hence, the corrected age of the child is 5.5 months and usual age for the prone to supine is 6 months. Hence, the parents are to be reassured that child is normal for age. Till 2 years of age, correction of gestational age has to be applied. Tags : Topic : NORMAL DEVELOPMENT & ASSESSMENT Difficulty : MUST KNOW PYQ : NEET PG 2023 Reference : Ghai's Essential pediatrics, 8 th ed., pg. 50

Sample MCQ #2

Tanya had her first birthday party recently. During her party she was able to commando crawl with ease, but could not walk. She picked up all the gems from her cake. She could speak 2 words with meaning. She waved goodbye to her guests. Which are of Tanya's development is delayed?

AGross motor
BPersonal social and Language
CFine motor
DNone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. None

Commando crawl (gross motor), picking up gems from cake- pincer grasp (fine motor), speaking 2 meaningful words (language), waving good bye (personal-social); all are achieved latest by 12 months. Hence, she has achieved all her milestones needed for her age. MILESTONES AT 12 MONTHS GROSS MOTOR Stand without support Walk with support FINE MOTOR Mature pincer grasp (holding like a pen) PERSONAL SOCIAL Plays a simple ball game LANGUAGE 1-2 meaningful words Tags : Topic : NORMAL DEVELOPMENT & ASSESSMENT Difficulty : MUST KNOW PYQ : FMGE JUNE 2022; NEET PG 2021 Reference : Nelson textbook of pediatrics, 21 st ed., pg. 137

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is most suggestive for suspecting cerebral palsy in children?

AHand dominance at 9 months
BSmiling for the first time at 8 weeks
CSitting without support at 6 months
DHead control at 6 weeks
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Hand dominance at 9 months

Hand preference, or handedness, is the tendency to use one hand more naturally and efficiently than the other for fine motor tasks. This usually develops in early childhood, often becoming clear between the ages of 2 and 4 Hand preference or hand dominance before 12 months of age is a red flag sign and needs evaluation. All other development milestone mentioned are achieved at optimal age. KEY TAKEAWAYS: Identifying and supporting hand preference helps children develop motor skills effectively, which can aid in tasks like writing and drawing as they grow Tags: Topic: CEREBRAL PALSY Difficulty level: MAY KNOW PYQ: NEET PG 2021 Reference: Nelson textbook of pediatrics, 21 st ed., pg. 143

Sample MCQ #4

All of the following are true regarding development sequence in children, except?

ADevelopment occurs in spurts rather than being a continuous process
BCNS maturity is a major determinant of development
CProceeds cephalon-caudally
DPrimitive reflexes must be lost for complex milestones to be attained
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Development occurs in spurts rather than being a continuous process

Six rules of development in a child 1 st : Development is a continuous process - Begins in utero. 2 nd : CNS maturity is the major determinant. 3 rd : Cephalo-caudal progression is shown. 4 th : Primitive reflexes must be lost. 5 th : Initially reflexes or milestones are general or crude. As age progresses, they become more complex 6 th : Similar sequence occurs in all children. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Ed, Page 22

Sample MCQ #5

What is the rate of increase in head circumference in infants during the first 3 months of age?

A0.5 cm/month
B1 cm/month
C2 cm/month
D4 cm/month
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 2 cm/month

Explanation: Head circumference increases most rapidly during the first 3 months of life at a rate of 2 cm per month . (Option D ruled out) Head Circumference Growth Pattern: 0-3 months: 2 cm/month 3-6 months: 1 cm/month (Option B ruled out) 6-12 months: 5 cm/month (Option A ruled out) 1-3 years: 2 cm/month Developmental Milestones: Birth: HC = 33-35 cm (2.5 cm > chest circumference) 1 year: HC = Chest circumference After 1 year: HC Measurement Technique: Maximum head circumference measured from occipital protuberance to supraorbital ridge using cross-tape method.

Sample MCQ #6

Sigmoid-shaped growth curve is shown by which among the following tissues/organs?

ABrain growth
BLymphoid tissue growth
CGonadal growth
DSomatic growth
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Somatic growth

Somatic growth shows a typical sigmoid/S-shaped growth curve. There is rapid growth initially in the first 4-5 years, then there is a relatively stable growth, followed by pick-up again occurring around peri-pubertal period. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Pages 6-7.

Sample MCQ #7

70% of the adult head size is attained by which age?

AAt birth
BBy 6 months
CBy 12 months
DBy 24 months
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. At birth

At birth, about 70% of the adult head size is attained, while it becomes approximately 90% of the adult head size by 2 years of age. Thus, first 2 years of life are critical for brain growth. Maturation of functions occurs later in life. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Ed, Pages 6-7

Sample MCQ #8

Which of the following is not seen in Down's syndrome?

ACaudal Regression Syndrome
BShort Stature
CHypothyroidism
DConductive Hearing Loss
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Caudal Regression Syndrome

Caudal Regression Syndrome (CRS), also called Sacral Agenesis, is rare in the general population, but more common in infants of diabetic mothers (specific for these babies). It has not been described in Down's syndrome. Option B: Linear growth is retarded in Down's syndrome. Option C: About 13-34% patients with Down's syndrome have hypothyroidism. Option D: 40-60% patients have conductive hearing loss and are prone to Serous Otitis Media-commonly during the first year. Reference: OP Ghai Essential Pediatrics, 10th Edition, Page Number-667.
