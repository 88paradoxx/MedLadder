Sample Nutrition and Breastfeeding MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A neonate has not passed urine in the last 24 hours since birth. Next step in management is?

AContinue breastfeeding and observe
BAdmit to NICU
CStart formula feeds
DStart IV fluids
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Continue breastfeeding and observe

According to Cloherty & Stark, 90% neonates void urine within 24 hours and 99% do so by 48 hours. Neonates without any risk factor can pass urine anytime before 48 hours of life. Until then we need to observe and feed the child. If more than 48 hours have passed, the baby needs to be shifted to the ICU and evaluated for volume status & investigated. Reference: Cloherty and Starks manual of neonatal care, SA ed., pg. 128

Sample MCQ #3

Indication to use prednisolone in Henoch-Schoenlein Purpura is?

APrevention of nephritis
BSevere GIT involvement
CDependent purpuric rash
DCNS dysfunction
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Severe GIT involvement

Treatment for mild and self-limited HSP is supportive, with an emphasis on ensuring adequate hydration, nutrition, and analgesia. Corticosteroids are most often used to treat significant GI involvement or other life-threatening manifestations. Glucocorticoids such as oral prednisone (1-2 mg/kg/day), or in severe cases, intravenous (IV) methylprednisolone for 1-2 wk, followed by taper, reduce abdominal and joint pain but do not alter overall prognosis. Corticosteroids are not routinely recommended for prevention of complications such as nephritis. Corticosteroids may also be needed in any orchitis, CNS involvement and pulmonary hemorrhage Reference: Nelson 21 st Ed, Chapter 192, OP Ghai 10 th Ed, Page 661

Sample MCQ #4

When is it recommended to introduce solid foods to an infant while continuing breastfeeding?

A2 months
B6 months
C12 months
D18 months
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 6 months

Explanation: According to current recommendations by World Health Organization (WHO) and American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP), solid foods should be introduced to an infant while continuing breastfeeding at around 6 months of age. At 6 months, infants have typically developed the necessary oral motor skills to handle solid foods, including loss of the tongue-thrust reflex, ability to sit with support, and adequate head and neck control . Breast milk or formula remains the primary source of nutrition during the first year, with complementary foods gradually introduced to meet increasing nutritional demands, especially for iron and zinc. Breastfeeding can go up to 2 years of age or till the time the mother wants.

Sample MCQ #5

All are true about human colostrum; EXCEPT?

AThick and yellow colour
BIgA high
CHigh amino acids and minerals
DHigh sugar
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. High sugar

In human colostrum, sugar content, i.e., Lactose content is low. Types of breast milk Colostrum- Secreted in the first 72 hr of life. Small in quantity, yellow coloured, thick in consistency, contains IgA, Vit A, D, E and K. Contains good amino acids but has lower lactose content Transitional Milk- Secreted between 4 days to 14 days. Protein content falls while fat and lactose content increase Mature Milk- Secreted after 2 weeks. Has high lactose, low fat and very low protein. Watery in consistency but contains all nutrients needed for the growth and development of the child. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Page 144
