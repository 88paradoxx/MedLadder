Sample MCQ #1

Deficiency of which of the following vitamins is associated with increased risk of thrombosis?

AVitamin A
BVitamin C
CVitamin K
DVitamin E
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Vitamin E

Deficiency in vitamin E is associated with an increased risk of thrombosis, particularly in neonates, due to its role in platelet aggregation. Vitamin K deficiency leads to hemorrhagic disorders , since vitamin K is essential in the production of coagulation factors II, VII, IX, X, and proteins C and S. Increased bruising can be seen in vitamin C deficiency due to an inability to synthesize collagen. Vitamins A is not associated with either an increased bleeding or thrombosis risk. Sample MCQ #3

Vitamin K is given at birth to prevent the following?

AHemorrhagic disease of newborn
BNeonatal asphyxia
CNeonatal hypoglycemia
DHemolytic anemia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Hemorrhagic disease of newborn

Neonates have low Vitamin K levels due to Poor transplacental transfer Low Vitamin K in breast milk Lack of gut colonization with Vit K producing bacteria Thus, neonates are predisposed to Vitamin K deficiency, which can cause bleeding manifestations, called as Hemorrhagic Disease of newborn, also called as Vitamin K Deficiency Bleeding (VKDB). To prevent this, Government of India recommends one intramuscular dose of Vitamin K at birth to all neonates. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Page 183

Sample MCQ #4

When suspecting early vitamin D deficiency in the patient, which of the following test is standard for determining the patient's vitamin D status?

A25- Hydroxylase levels
B25 Hydroxy cholecalciferol levels
C1, 25- Dihydroxy cholecalciferol levels
DPTH levels
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 25 Hydroxy cholecalciferol levels

The initial laboratory tests in a child with rickets should include Serum calcium, Serum phosphorus Alkaline phosphatase Parathyroid hormone (PTH), 25-hydroxyvitamin D, 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D (1,25- D), Creatinine Electrolytes 25-hydroxy vitamin D is reduced in initial phase, while 1,25 dihydroxy vitamin D levels are maintained. 1,25 dihydroxy vitamin D levels fall in very severe condition only. Other lab tests help in confirmation of the type of rickets. Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following vitamin is the most important for free radical scavenging action?

AVitamin A
BVitamin B6
CVitamin C
DVitamin E
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Vitamin E

Out of all the options, Vitamin A, C and E have antioxidant property . Out of these, Vitamin E is the most important. It is mainly located within the cell membranes, preventing lipid peroxidation and formation of free radicals. Deficiency of vitamin E can cause, hemolysis and neurological symptoms especially in the posterior column. A vegan patient with MCV of 110 fL and Hb of 10 g% is most likely to have?

AIron deficiency anemia
BMegaloblastic anemia
CAplastic anemia
DThalassemia

Sample MCQ #8

A child developed sudden-onset cardiac arrest. CPR was started and rhythm was checked. Since there was asystole, it was decided to give injection epinephrine to the child. However, intravenous access could not be obtained immediately. What alternative site can you inject epinephrine in such a patient?

AIntracardiac
BIntramuscular
CIntraosseous
DTracheal
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Intraosseous

In pediatric cardiac arrest, if the central or peripheral venous access is not immediately achieved, intraosseous (IO) access should be secured. The usual site for IO access is upper end of tibia, medial to the tibial tuberosity. Other sites include distal femur, lower end of tibia above medial malleolus and anterior superior iliac spine. All drugs and infusions which can be given via intravenous route, can also be administered via intraosseous route. Tracheal route is not a preferred site even in emergencies. However, if even IO access is not possible, it can be considered for lipid-soluble agents like epinephrine, lidocaine, atropine and naloxone, if indicated. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Page 766
