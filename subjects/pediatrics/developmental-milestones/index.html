Sample Developmental Milestones MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Mouthing of objects tends to significantly reduce by what developmental age in a child?

A4-6 months
B6-9 months
C9-10 months
D12-15 months
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 12-15 months

According to OP Ghai 10 th Ed, at 6 months, as the ability to chew develops, the child can take a biscuit to his mouth and chew. At this age, he tends to mouth all objects offered to him. This is called mouthing of objects and can be a reason for inadvertent foreign body ingestion or aspiration. This tendency abates by around 1 year of age in most children. According to other texts and sources, mouthing reduces between 12-18 months. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Page 31

Sample MCQ #2

A child with developmental delay, can ride a tricycle, can copy a circle, can say name, knows own sex, but cannot narrate a story. What is the developmental age?

A2 years
B3 years
C4 years
D5 years
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 3 years

Age of attaining milestones normally Riding a tricycle comes normally at 3 years of age Saying full name and gender also comes at 3 years of age Copying a circle comes at 3 years of age Narrating a story comes at 4 years of age. Now, in this question, the child is able to perform the milestones corresponding to 3 years, but unable to do milestones related to 4 years. Hence, the developmental age of the child should be 3 years. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Tables 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4

Sample MCQ #3

A normal infant is able to sits leaning forward with hands, reaches for and grasps cube with one hand, transfers objects between hands, cannot wave bye-bye, cannot hold objects between finger and thumb. Most probable age is?

A4 months
B7 months
C9-10 months
D12-13 months
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 7 months

Let us evaluate the information given in the question. A normal infant can sit with own support from 6 months onwards, also called as Tripod position. Reaching for and grasping a cube with one hand, also called as Unidextrous grasp comes at 6 months of age Transferring objects hands to hands develops between 6 to 7 months of age. Waving bye-bye develops at 9 months Immature Pincer grasp comes by 9 months and mature one by 12 months. So, the child is able to do milestones of at or before 7 months of age, but not attained milestones related to 9 months and beyond. Hence the probable age of the child is 7 months. References: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Tables 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.4; Nelson textbook of pediatrics, 20 th edition, pg-69

Sample MCQ #4

A boy has now learnt to feed himself with spoon without spillage, can dress and undress himself with supervision, knows that he is a boy. These milestones are most likely developed between which age?

A2 to 3 years
B3 to 4 years
C4 to 5 years
D5 to 6 years
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 2 to 3 years

Let us evaluate the information given step-wise. Feeding self with spoon with spillage is achieved by 15 months & without spillage by 18 months of age. Can undress self with assistance or supervision by 2 years of age. Can dress self with assistance or supervision by 3 years of age. Knowing gender comes by 3 years. Hence best possible answer here is 2 to 3 years. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Table 3.3; Nelson textbook of pediatrics, 20 th edition, pg-76

Sample MCQ #5

An infant can sit leaning forwards, on his own hands. When made to stand, he bounces actively. He laughs loudly but becomes concerned when mother moves away and a new person enters the household. What is his most likely age?

A12 weeks
B16 weeks
C22 weeks
D28 weeks
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 28 weeks

Let us understand the information given here. The child is able to sit with own support, laughs loudly & has stranger anxiety. All of this is pointing towards a likely developmental age of 6 to 7 months. Thus the best answer is option D, i.e., 28 weeks. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Pages 28-35.

Sample MCQ #6

A 10-month-old female infant presents with jerky movement of limbs towards the body; EEG shows hypsarrhythmia. The baby also has delay in attaining developmental milestones. Drug of choice for management in this condition?

APhenytoin
BACTH
CLevetiracetam
DPhenobarbitone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. ACTH

The child in the question has West Syndrome. West syndrome starts between the ages of 2 and 12 months and consists of a triad of infantile epileptic spasms that usually occur in clusters (particularly in drowsiness or upon arousal), developmental regression, and a typical EEG picture called hypsarrhythmia. West syndrome is best treated with ACTH. However, if West Syndrome occurs as a part of Tuberous Sclerosis, then Vigabatrin is the preferred agent. Reference: Nelson textbook of Pediatrics, 20 th ed., pg. 2836

Sample MCQ #7

A 10-month-old female infant was admitted to ICU with complaints of recurrent, intermittent jerky movements of limbs on the body. There are delayed milestones in the child. Her EEG revealed a pattern of hypsarrhythmia. What shall be the anti-epileptic therapy of choice?

ALow-dose ACTH
BHigh-dose ACTH
CVigabatrin
DLevetiracetam
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. High-dose ACTH

Recurrent jerky movements + Delayed milestones + Hypsarrhythmia on EEG indicates a likely diagnosis of West Syndrome. West syndrome is best treated with ACTH. High-dose ACTH is preferred over low-dose ACTH Vigabatrin is considered by some as a drug of second choice for infantile spasms and by some even as an alternative to ACTH as a drug of first choice in Tuberous Sclerosis patients. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Page 582, AOCN 2021 Guidelines On West Syndrome

Sample MCQ #8

A 2-year-old boy was found to have hepatosplenomegaly and delayed milestones. The liver biopsy and bone marrow biopsy revealed PAS positive histiocytes. Electron microscope finding of these histiocytes will most likely be?

ABirbeck granules in cytoplasm
BMyelin figures in cytoplasm
CParallel rays of tubular structure in lysosomes
DElectron dense deposit in mitochondria
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Birbeck granules in cytoplasm

Hepatosplenomegaly with PAS positive histiocytes suggests diagnosis of Langerhans cell histiocytosis (LCH). The hallmark of LCH is clonal proliferation of monocyte lineage. The presence of birbeck granules in the cytoplasm of tumor cells is characteristic of LCH. Birbeck granules are tennis racket shaped bilaminar granules. They are not seen in all tumor cells by electron microscopy, hence detection of S-100 and CD1a by immunohistochemistry are needed for confirmation of diagnosis. Reference: Nelson textbook of Pediatrics, 1 st SA edition, pg. 2487
