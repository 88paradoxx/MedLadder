Sample Developmental Milestones MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Mouthing of objects tends to significantly reduce by what developmental age in a child?

A4-6 months
B6-9 months
C9-10 months
D12-15 months
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 12-15 months

According to OP Ghai 10 th Ed, at 6 months, as the ability to chew develops, the child can take a biscuit to his mouth and chew. At this age, he tends to mouth all objects offered to him. This is called mouthing of objects and can be a reason for inadvertent foreign body ingestion or aspiration. This tendency abates by around 1 year of age in most children. According to other texts and sources, mouthing reduces between 12-18 months. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Page 31

Sample MCQ #2

A child with developmental delay, can ride a tricycle, can copy a circle, can say name, knows own sex, but cannot narrate a story. What is the developmental age?

A2 years
B3 years
C4 years
D5 years
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 3 years

Age of attaining milestones normally Riding a tricycle comes normally at 3 years of age Saying full name and gender also comes at 3 years of age Copying a circle comes at 3 years of age Narrating a story comes at 4 years of age. Now, in this question, the child is able to perform the milestones corresponding to 3 years, but unable to do milestones related to 4 years. Hence, the developmental age of the child should be 3 years. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Tables 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4

Sample MCQ #3

A normal infant is able to sits leaning forward with hands, reaches for and grasps cube with one hand, transfers objects between hands, cannot wave bye-bye, cannot hold objects between finger and thumb. Most probable age is?

A4 months
B7 months
C9-10 months
D12-13 months
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 7 months

Let us evaluate the information given in the question. A normal infant can sit with own support from 6 months onwards, also called as Tripod position. Reaching for and grasping a cube with one hand, also called as Unidextrous grasp comes at 6 months of age Transferring objects hands to hands develops between 6 to 7 months of age. Waving bye-bye develops at 9 months Immature Pincer grasp comes by 9 months and mature one by 12 months. So, the child is able to do milestones of at or before 7 months of age, but not attained milestones related to 9 months and beyond. Hence the probable age of the child is 7 months. References: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Tables 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.4; Nelson textbook of pediatrics, 20 th edition, pg-69
