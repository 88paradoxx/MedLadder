Sample MCQ #1

You are teaching a group of medical students the differential diagnosis of edema in children. You take them to the bedside of a patient who has edema associated with hypoproteinemia secondary to proteinuria. Of the following, the feature you are MOST likely to observe in this patient is?

ACola-coloured urine, oliguria, and hypertension
BEdema that improves throughout the day
CGenital edema
DPitting pedal edema
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Edema that improves throughout the day

Nephrotic syndrome usually presents with edema that is periorbital and more prominent early in the morning and with minimal systemic symptoms. Edema is the most easily identified clinical feature associated with hypoproteinemia. Impaired cardiac function leads to worsening peripheral edema as the day progresses and is associated with exertion and increased activity. Pitting edema is not pathognomonic for any specific underlying etiology. Reference: Nelson textbook of pediatrics, 1 st SA ed., pg. 2521

Sample MCQ #2

A 9-yr old boy passes hard infrequent stools for the last 2 years. Also, sometimes he soils his under wear. On examination, you found an anal fissure and his rectum is full of hard stools. All other examination is normal. Your most likely diagnosis is functional constipation. What is the most effective initial treatment?

ACombination of Polyethylene glycol solution & enema
BHigh fiber diet and lactulose
CLactulose, High fiber diet & Senokot
DHigh fiber diet and follow up after 3 months
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Combination of Polyethylene glycol solution & enema

The child has now presented with severe constipation with rectal impaction. The 1st step in treatment of severe constipation is disimpaction which requires administration of Polyethylene glycol solution (movicol) (or mineral oil) &plusmn; rectal enema As per OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Page 301, any child with constipation who has hard stools felt on per rectal examination or has abdominal fecoliths or retentive encopresis should undergo immediate disimpaction as the first step in management. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Page 301; Nelson textbook of pediatrics, 1 st SA ed., pg. 1765

Sample MCQ #3

A child has been recently diagnosed as Celiac Disease. His parents ask you for foods which are safe to be consumed. You will suggest all of the following foods as safe to eat in this patient, except?

AQuinoa
BSorghum
CSemolina
DPoha
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Semolina

Since the child has been diagnosed with Celiac Disease, the child should avoid eating foods and grains which contain gluten in them. Among the options, Semolina is puffed wheat and thus should be avoided. Grains unsafe to eat in Celiac Disease- Contain gluten. Mnemonic is BROW Barley Rye Oats Wheat, including whole wheat & semolina (suji/rava) Grains safe to eat in Celiac Disease- Do not contain gluten. Includes Rice, including Puffed Rice like Poha Maize Quinoa Sorghum Chia Seeds Flax Seeds Tapioca Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Page 320; Nelson 21 st Edition, Chapter 364
