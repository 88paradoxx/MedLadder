Sample MCQ #3

An 8-month-old child has profuse diarrhea since last 3 days. The stool routine shows absence of blood and stool culture shows no growth. There is no fever and no dehydration. Child is growing fine otherwise. The perianal area shows skin excoriation. What is likely cause of this diarrheal illness?

ASalmonella infection
BLactose malabsorption
CE. Coli infection
DCeliac disease
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Lactose malabsorption

The history is very much suggestive of post viral lactose malabsorption. After the viral enteritis, the mucosal brush border cells are lost and these causes lactose malabsorption. Also , perianal excoriation is favorable to diagnosis of lactose intolerance. Other bacterial enteritis might show growth in culture or may have changes in stool routine like pus cells, occult blood. Though, they cannot be ruled out completely, likelihood of lactose malabsorption is more than bacterial. Celiac disease is a cause of chronic diarrhea and failure to thrive.
