Sample Chromosomal Disorders MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Wolf Hirschhorn syndrome involves which among the following genetic abnormalities?

ADel 3p
BDel 4p
CDel 6p
DDel 18q
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Del 4p

Wolf Hirschhorn syndrome. Also known as 4p- (deletion of4pl6), this syndrome is characterized by microcephaly and unusual development of the nose and orbits that produces an appearance suggesting an ancient Greek warrior's helmet. Other anomalies commonly seen include cleft lip and palate and cardiac and renal defects. Seizure disorders are common, and the majority of patients have severe intellectual disability. Reference: CDT Pediatrics 2020/Page 1097

Sample MCQ #2

Which among the following disorders is an example of polygenic inheritance?

AFragile X syndrome
BG6PD deficiency
CCystic fibrosis
DCleft palate
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Cleft palate

Cleft lip/palate shows polygenic/multifactorial inheritance. Fragile X syndrome and G6PD deficiency show X linked recessive, whereas Cystic fibrosis shows Autosomal recessive inheritance. Reference: Nelson 21 st Ed/Chapter 97

Sample MCQ #3

A male boy has a large head, large ears, large jaw and large testes. He also has features of autism and delayed development. Likely diagnosis is?

AWilliams Syndrome
BRett Syndrome
CFragile X Syndrome
DDown Syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Fragile X Syndrome

The findings of large head, large ears, large jaw and large testes (macro-orchidism) are suggestive of Fragile X Syndrome. More commonly seen in boys, it is considered to be a trinucleotide repeat disorder Involves >200 CGG repeats in FMR-1 gene on Chromosome Xq27.3 Other features include hyperextensible joints, increased risks of ADHD, Autism, Anxiety Disorders Most patients have low IQ, indicating intellectual disability and delayed development milestones during childhood. Reference: Nelson 21 st Ed, Chapter 98.5

Sample MCQ #4

What type of chromosomal abnormality is associated with acute promyelocytic leukemia?

AChromosomal translocation (12;21)
BChromosomal translocation (15;17)
CChromosomal translocation (4;11)
DChromosomal translocation (9;22)
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Chromosomal translocation (15;17)

Childhood acute leukemias can be divided into myelocytic and lymphocytic. Acute myelocytic leukemias (AML) involve greater than 20% of myeloid blasts in either of these. Myeloid lines include erythrocytes, granulocytes, monocytes, and megakaryocytes. Acute lymphocytic leukemia (ALL) involves the B and T cell lymphocyte lineage. There are different chromosomal rearrangements that can occur, including translocations, deletions, and inversions. Chromosomal translocation (15;17) has a specific association with acute promyelocytic leukemia (APL). This translocation involves the alpha retinoic acid receptor gene on chromosome 17, which is why the specific treatment for APL is all-trans retinoic acid. Reference: Nelson textbook of Pediatrics, 20 th edition, pg. 2442

Sample MCQ #5

A child with Trisomy 21 developed Transient Leukemia. Such patients are likely to show which additional genetic abnormalities?

AMosaicism
BAPP gene deletion
CGATA-1 mutations
DGenomic Imprinting
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. GATA-1 mutations

Upto 10% of Down Syndrome patients can develop Transient leukemia, previously called as Transient Myeloproliferative Disorder. Those developing Transient Leukemia in such patients are often found to have GATA-1 mutations in the transcription factors, producing proliferation of blast cells. 20-30% of these patients tend to develop AML-M7 leukemia by 3 years of age. Reference: Nelson 20 th Ed, Page 609, OP Ghai 10 th Ed, Page 667.

Sample MCQ #8

Down syndrome patients are found to have a reduced life expectancy compared to the general population. Which among the following is considered the chief cause of mortality especially in the first decade of life?

ACongenital Heart Disease
BAspiration pneumonia
CAcute lymphoblastic leukemia
DCervical spine injury
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Congenital Heart Disease

As per OP Ghai 10th Edition, the chief cause of early mortality in Down Syndrome patients is Congenital Heart Disease. Furthermore, Endocardial Cushion Defects (ECDs) are the most common congenital heart disease in patients with Down Syndrome. Extra-Edge Point: Nelson 21 st Ed says that the current average life expectancy in Down Syndrome is 50-55 years. Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Page 668, Nelson 21 st Edition, Chapter 98.2
