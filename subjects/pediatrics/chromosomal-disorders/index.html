Sample Chromosomal Disorders MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Wolf Hirschhorn syndrome involves which among the following genetic abnormalities?

ADel 3p
BDel 4p
CDel 6p
DDel 18q
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Del 4p

Wolf Hirschhorn syndrome. Also known as 4p- (deletion of4pl6), this syndrome is characterized by microcephaly and unusual development of the nose and orbits that produces an appearance suggesting an ancient Greek warrior's helmet. Other anomalies commonly seen include cleft lip and palate and cardiac and renal defects. Seizure disorders are common, and the majority of patients have severe intellectual disability. Reference: CDT Pediatrics 2020/Page 1097

Sample MCQ #2

Which among the following disorders is an example of polygenic inheritance?

AFragile X syndrome
BG6PD deficiency
CCystic fibrosis
DCleft palate
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Cleft palate

Cleft lip/palate shows polygenic/multifactorial inheritance. Fragile X syndrome and G6PD deficiency show X linked recessive, whereas Cystic fibrosis shows Autosomal recessive inheritance. Reference: Nelson 21 st Ed/Chapter 97

Sample MCQ #3

A male boy has a large head, large ears, large jaw and large testes. He also has features of autism and delayed development. Likely diagnosis is?

AWilliams Syndrome
BRett Syndrome
CFragile X Syndrome
DDown Syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Fragile X Syndrome

The findings of large head, large ears, large jaw and large testes (macro-orchidism) are suggestive of Fragile X Syndrome. More commonly seen in boys, it is considered to be a trinucleotide repeat disorder Involves >200 CGG repeats in FMR-1 gene on Chromosome Xq27.3 Other features include hyperextensible joints, increased risks of ADHD, Autism, Anxiety Disorders Most patients have low IQ, indicating intellectual disability and delayed development milestones during childhood. Reference: Nelson 21 st Ed, Chapter 98.5
