Sample MCQ #4

Dinutuximab has been recently approved for which among the following pediatric malignancy?

AALL
BNeuroblastoma
CWilms tumour
DRhabdomyosarcoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Neuroblastoma

Dinutuximab is a monoclonal antibody that targets a diasialoganglioside, GD2, which has ubiquitous expression on neuroblastoma cells Incorporation of the mAb into consolidative therapy after autologous stem cell transplant improves the 2-yr event-free survival from 46.5% to 66.5%. Dinutuximab is approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) as a standard of care for this subset of patients. Reference: Nelson 21 st Edition, Chapter 525

Sample MCQ #5

Which among the following is contraindicated in childhood dysentery?

ALoperamide
BOndansetron
CProbiotics
DCiprofloxacin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Loperamide

Antimotility agents (loperamide) are contraindicated in children with dysentery and probably have no role in the management of acute watery diarrhea in otherwise healthy children. Similarly, antiemetic agents, such as the phenothiazines, are of little value and are associated with potentially serious side effects (lethargy, dystonia, malignant hyperpyrexia). Incorrect Options: Option B- Ondansetron is safe and a useful adjunct in troublesome vomiting. Option C- Probiotics can be given but are not routinely indicated. Option D- Remember: Ciprofloxacin is the drug of choice in childhood dysentery. Reference: Nelson Textbook of Pediatrics, 20th Edition, Page Number-1873; OP Ghai Essential Pediatrics, 10th Edition, Page Number-314.

Sample MCQ #6

A 4-year-old child is presented with abdominal lump. Bone scan is being done to rule out which of the following condition?

ANeuroblastoma
BRetinoblastoma
CNHL
DPrimitive neuroectodermal tumours
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Neuroblastoma

Among the given options, Neuroblastoma frequently produces bony metastasis. Bony metastases in Neuroblastoma often occurs to orbital bones, producing proptosis and/or Raccoon Eyes/Panda Eyes. Vertebral spread is also reported. As per Nelson 21 st Edition, Metastatic spread in Neuroblastoma, which is more common in children >1 yr old at diagnosis, occurs through local invasion or distant hematogenous or lymphatic routes. The most common sites of metastasis overall in Neuroblastoma are the regional or distant lymph nodes, long bones and skull, bone marrow, liver, and skin. Kindly note that bone scan is needed in Neuroblastoma as well as Clear cell sarcoma variant of Wilms tumour in children. Reference: Nelson 21 st Edition, Chapter 525; OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Page 637-639

Sample MCQ #7

"I am a pediatric malignancy. I often afflict young children & can produce lung, lymph node and liver metastases. FNAC & Blind Biopsy is not recommended in me. I respond well to chemotherapy with Vincristine, Actinomycin-D and Doxorubicin. Who am I?"

ANeuroblastoma
BLymphoblastic Leukemia
CWilms Tumour
DHodgkin's Lymphoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Wilms Tumour

Clues to the diagnosis of Wilms Tumour here. Clue 1- Pediatric malignancy in young children. Wilms tumour occurs in children aged 2-3 years. Clue 2- Metastases to lung, lymph nodes and liver is seen in Wilms tumour Clue 3- Blind biopsy is avoided since it can upstage the disease, as per Nelson 21 st Imaging studies include plain abdominal radiography, abdominal ultrasonography (US), and CT of the abdomen to define the intrarenal origin of the mass and differentiate it from adrenal masses (e.g., neuroblastoma) and other masses in the abdomen Clue 4- Responds to chemotherapy with Vincristine, Actinomycin-D and Doxorubicin- Seen in Wilms Tumour. After radical nephrectomy, most patients receive chemotherapy using the said agents. Reference: Nelson 21 st Edition, Chapter 526; OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Pages 636-638

Sample MCQ #8

Which statement about childhood brain tumors is true?

AMost are located in the midline and/or below the tentorium cerebri.
BBrain tumors are a rare type of cancer in childhood.
CSigns of increased intracranial pressure are rare in midline tumours
DSeizures are the presenting complaint in all cases.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Most are located in the midline and/or below the tentorium cerebri.

Most brain tumours in children are infratentorial, hence option A is correct. About other options Brain tumours are relatively frequent in childhood among malignancies and comprise most common solid malignancy in childhood. Signs of raised ICP can develop commonly with midline brain tumours like medulloblastoma Seizures may or may not be present in all cases Reference: OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Pages 642-643
