Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is the most common cause of shock in paediatric patient?

AHemorrhagic
BDehydration
CSeptic
DCardiogenic
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Dehydration

Dehydration (hypovolemic) shock is the most common type of shock in paediatric population. Common Causes in Paediatrics Gastrointestinal Losses: Diarrhea (e.g., rotavirus, cholera). Vomiting. Hemorrhage: Trauma (e.g., accidents). Post-surgical bleeding. Burns: Fluid loss through damaged skin. Other Causes: Inadequate fluid intake (e.g., neglect, illness). Third-spacing (e.g., sepsis, nephrotic syndrome). Septic shock is most common in neonates. Tags: Topic: SHOCK Difficulty level: MUST KNOW Reference: Manual of paediatric emergencies & critical care, 2 nd ed., pg. 158

Sample MCQ #2

Two weeks after a viral syndrome, a 2-year-old develops bruising and generalized petechiae that is more prominent over the legs. He has neither hepatosplenomegaly nor lymph node enlargement. Laboratory testing reveals a normal hemoglobin, hematocrit, and white blood cell count and differential. The platelet count is 15,000/mm3. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

AAcute lymphoblastic leukemia
BAplastic anemia
CImmune thrombocytopenic purpura
DThrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Immune thrombocytopenic purpura

Immune (or idiopathic) thrombocytopenic purpura (ITP) is common in children. In most cases, a preceding viral infection can be documented. The platelet count frequently is less than 20,000/mm3, but other laboratory test results are normal, including the bone marrow aspiration (which may show an increase in megakaryocytes). These patients have no hepatosplenomegaly or generalized lymphadenopathy. Treatment consists of observation or possibly intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIG), intravenous anti-D (in Rh-positive patients), immunosuppressives, or steroids. The history must be reviewed for other possible causes of thrombocytopenia, including recent MMR vaccination, drug ingestion, and human immunodeficiency virus. Reference: Nelson textbook of Pediatrics, 1 st SA edition, pg. 2402

Sample MCQ #3

Desmopressin is relatively contraindicated in which type of Von Willebrand disease?

AType 1
BType 2A
CType 2B
DType 2M
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Type 2B

Type 2B vWD results from gain-of-function mutations that increase the ability of vWF to bind platelets. This leads to increased clearance of both vWF and platelets from circulation and results in the loss of high-molecular-weight multimers and decreased vWF activity, similar to that seen in type 2A vWD. Desmopressin is relatively contraindicated in type 2B vWD as it may accelerate VWF-platelet binding and clearance. Please note that Desmopressin is highly effective in Type 1 vWD Reference: Nelson textbook of pediatrics, 1 st SE edition, pg. 2390
