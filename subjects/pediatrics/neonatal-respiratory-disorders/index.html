Sample Neonatal Respiratory Disorders MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following cells are deficient is a premature infant with RDS?

AAlveolar capillary endothelial cells
BType I alveolar cells
CType II alveolar cells
DAlveolar macrophages
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Type II alveolar cells

Hyaline membrane disease Also called as respiratory distress syndrome (RDS) Major cause of this disease is Deficiency of pulmonary surfactant produced by type II alveolar cells Commonly seen in. Premature infants Maternal diabetes (Gestational diabetes mellitus) delivery by cesarean section Reference: Ganong 25th edition page no.631

Sample MCQ #2

In children with respiratory distress, there is deficiency of which of the following lipids:

ADipalmitoyl phosphatidylcholine
BSphingomyelin
CCardiolipin
DLeukotrienes
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Dipalmitoyl phosphatidylcholine

Surfactant lipids Account for approximately 90% of surfactant Major surfactant lipids is: Dipalmotoyl phosphatidylcholine (DPPC) - Lecithin- most abundant component of surfactant Reference: Ganong 25th edition page no.631

Sample MCQ #3

In preterm baby with respiratory distress syndrome which of the following lipid would be deficient?

ASphingomyelin
BCardiolipin
CDipalmitoyl phosphatidyl choline
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Dipalmitoyl phosphatidyl choline

Phospholipid Dipalmitoyl Phosphatidyl Choline acts as surfactant and Acts to reduce surface tension, which helps in proper expansion of Lungs. In preterm babies, it is deficient. So preterm babies are prone for respiratory distress syndrome
