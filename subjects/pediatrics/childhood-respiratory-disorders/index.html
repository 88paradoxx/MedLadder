Sample MCQ #3

A 10-year-old boy has a history of recurrent sinusitis and multiple episodes of pneumonia. You suspect CF and order a sweat chloride test. The sweat electrolyte test result is within the normal range. What is your next step in management?

APerform DNA testing for CFTR gene mutations.
BPerform a pH probe test for gastroesophageal reflux.
CReassure parents that he does not have CF.
DPlace him on a high-calorie, high-protein diet.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Perform DNA testing for CFTR gene mutations.

The child has recurrent upper and lower respiratory tract infections suggesting CF. The sweat chloride test can yield falsely low values, so the next step would be to perform DNA testing to identify any of the common CFTR mutations. Negative sweat chloride test results do not exclude CF. Reference: Nelson textbook of pediatrics, 1 st SA ed., pg. 2104
