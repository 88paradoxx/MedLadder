Sample MCQ #1

While performing subjective verification of refraction, the cross cylinder is used to check:

AAxis of the cylinder to be prescribed
BPower of the cylinder to be prescribed
CBoth of the above
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Both of the above

Cross cylinder examination (otherwise known as Jackson's cross cylinder, JCC) is an examination used to refine the astigmatic refraction by fine-tuning the axis and strength of astigmatism. While a rough estimate can be obtained via retinoscopy or automated refractometry, cross-cylinder examination remains invaluable in fine-tuning the refraction for best vision. It confirm the axis and power of cylinder both Eg of types of JCC 0.25 JCC = +0.25DS/-0.50DC X 90 0.50 JCC = +0.50DS/-1.00DC X 90 1.00 JCC = +1.00DS/-2.00DC X 90 Reference: Khurana Optics 2/e

Sample MCQ #3

If the youth is in poor health or does much near work the perpetual overaction of the ciliary muscle is likely to produce symptoms; the condition is often called

AAccommodative asthenopia
BPathological myopia
CAstigmatism
DSpasm of accommodation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Accommodative asthenopia

In the young, the condition may cause no symptoms. When symptoms are present or arise, they are chiefly referable to the abnormal amount of accommodation to which these eyes are subjected, and to the lack of balance between accommodation and convergence. A healthy youth has an ample reserve of accommodation, and if hypermetropic, can accommodate for distant and near objects without being conscious of the act. If the youth is in poor health or does much near work the perpetual overaction of the ciliary muscle is likely to produce symptoms; the condition is often called accommodative asthenopia or 'eye strain' Reference: Parson ch 8, P. 79
