Sample MCQ #1

While performing subjective verification of refraction, the cross cylinder is used to check:

AAxis of the cylinder to be prescribed
BPower of the cylinder to be prescribed
CBoth of the above
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Both of the above

Cross cylinder examination (otherwise known as Jackson's cross cylinder, JCC) is an examination used to refine the astigmatic refraction by fine-tuning the axis and strength of astigmatism. While a rough estimate can be obtained via retinoscopy or automated refractometry, cross-cylinder examination remains invaluable in fine-tuning the refraction for best vision. It confirm the axis and power of cylinder both Eg of types of JCC 0.25 JCC = +0.25DS/-0.50DC X 90 0.50 JCC = +0.50DS/-1.00DC X 90 1.00 JCC = +1.00DS/-2.00DC X 90 Reference: Khurana Optics 2/e

Causes of premature presbyopia include all of the following except:

AGeneral debility
BPrimary open-angle glaucoma
CUncorrected myopia
DPremature sclerosis of the lens

Rather in uncorrected myopia presbyopia occurs late, as they already have a good near vision without glasses Reference: Khurana Optics 2/e

Sample MCQ #3

If the youth is in poor health or does much near work the perpetual overaction of the ciliary muscle is likely to produce symptoms; the condition is often called

AAccommodative asthenopia
BPathological myopia
CAstigmatism
DSpasm of accommodation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Accommodative asthenopia

In the young, the condition may cause no symptoms. When symptoms are present or arise, they are chiefly referable to the abnormal amount of accommodation to which these eyes are subjected, and to the lack of balance between accommodation and convergence. A healthy youth has an ample reserve of accommodation, and if hypermetropic, can accommodate for distant and near objects without being conscious of the act. If the youth is in poor health or does much near work the perpetual overaction of the ciliary muscle is likely to produce symptoms; the condition is often called accommodative asthenopia or 'eye strain' Reference: Parson ch 8, P. 79

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following is an example of compound myopic, against the rule astigmatism:

A-2D Sph -2Dcyl at 180
B-2D Sph - 1Dcyl at 90
C+2D Sph -2Dcyl at 90
D-2 Dcyl at 90
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. -2D Sph - 1Dcyl at 90

When eyewear prescriptions are written, they can be classified into different areas depending on the power or refractive error. There are seven categories to which prescriptions can fall: Simple Hyperopia, the Rx will be (+) sphere Simple Myopia, the Rx will be (-) sphere Simple Myopic Astigmatism, the Rx will be plano (-) Simple Hyperopic Astigmatism, the Rx will be (+) Compound Hyperopic Astigmatism, major meridian power will be (+)(+) on both 90 and 180 degree axis Compound Myopic Astigmatism, major meridian power will be (-)(-) on both 90 and 180 degree axis Mixed Astigmatism, major meridian powers will be opposites (-)(+) (+)(-) on both 90 and 180 degree axis

Sample MCQ #5

In Against-the-rule astigmatism:

AVertical meridian is more curved than the horizontal
BHorizontal meridian is more curved than the vertical
CBoth meridia are equally curved
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Horizontal meridian is more curved than the vertical

Astigmatism WTR is produced when the corneal curvature is steepest in the vertical meridian; conversely astigmatism ATR is produced when the steepest corneal meridian is horizontal. Reference: Khurana Optics 2/e

Sample MCQ #6

Which type of astigmatism is most likely associated with keratoconus?

ARegular myopic astigmatism
BIrregular myopic astigmatism
CRegular hypermetropic astigmatism
DIrregular hypermetropic astigmatism
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Irregular myopic astigmatism

The cornea is at first transparent and the vision is impaired due to myopic astigmatism. The patient becomes myopic, but the error of refraction cannot be satisfactorily corrected with ordinary glasses owing to the parabolic nature of the curvature which leads to irregular astigmatism in the later stages. Reference: Parson&rsquo;s diseases of the Eye-Chap 12 &ndash; Diseases of Cornea

Sample MCQ #7

Cases in which astigmatism causes asthenopic symptoms

AGive full correction
BUndercorrect
COvercorrect
DNot given
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Give full correction

If the astigmatic error is small (less than 0.5 D) and not associated with symptoms, spectacles are unnecessary unless the highest visual acuity is desired. However, in all cases in which astigmatism causes asthenopic symptoms, full optical correction should be ordered for constant use, that is, both for distant and near vision. Other measures are rigid contact lenses, Toric contact lenses, astigmatic LASIK (1.5-5 D), arcuate keratotomy and Toric phakic IOLs. Reference: Parson ch 8, P. 82

Sample MCQ #8

Because of circle of least diffusion, the distant vision is comparatively good in:

ASimple myopic astigmatism
BCompound myopic astigmatism
CMixed astigmatism
DCompound hypermetropic astigmatism
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Mixed astigmatism

Circle of least confusion/diffusion: The smallest cross-section of a blur circle between the two focal lines formed by the astigmatic lens. Also defined as &ldquo;At the dioptric midpoint of focal lines the cross-section of sturm's Conoid appears as a circular patch of light rays with best overall focus. If the retina is placed at the &rdquo; Circle of least diffusion&rdquo; of Strum's conoid, the distant vision is often found to be surprisingly good with a relatively high degree of mixed astigmatism. Reference: Khurana Optics 2/e
