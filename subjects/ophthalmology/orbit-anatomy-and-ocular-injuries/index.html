Sample MCQ #1

True about chemical injury to eye all except

AAcid injury are more severe in eye
BWash is to be done by ringer lactate
CSteroids are given to decrease inflammation
DIOP can be high in initial period
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Acid injury are more severe in eye

Alkali (penetrate inside) burns are more severe as compared to acid(coagulate on surface) Corneal epithelial and stromal damage by coagulase enzyme released by damaged epithelium and cause a lot of inflammation. Limbal ischaemia results from loss of stem cells. Vascularisation and conjuctivalisation of cornea occurs. Severe cases can have corneal ulceration and perforation. Reference: 9th/e kanski chapter 22 trauma pg. 912 chemical injury

Sample MCQ #2

Most dangerous intraocular foreign body is

AGlass
BGold
CCopper
DIron
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Copper

Chalcosis, also called chalcosis bulbi, is a chronic reaction to a copper-containing intraocular foreign body (IOFB) When copper enters the eye, it is oxidized from its uncharged elemental form, Cu, to Cu 2+ ions. Though the ions are free to diffuse throughout the vitreous, they have a propensity to deposit in intraocular basement membranes including Descemet's membrane of the cornea (KF ring) and the internal limiting membrane (ILM) Foreign bodies containing less copper ( 85%) show a generalized pattern of intraocular copper deposition and are associated with more devastating outcomes such as suppurative endophthalmitis and phthisis bulbi. Reference: 9th/e kanski chapter 22 trauma pg. 911 IOFB

Sample MCQ #3

Cherry Red spot in children after trauma is seen in

ACRAO
BCRVO
CBerlin's edema
DNiemann Pick Disease
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Berlin's edema

Commotio retinae Commotio retinae is caused by concussion of the sensory retina resulting in cloudy swelling that gives the involved area a grey appearance. It most frequently affects the temporal fundus. If the macula is involved, a 'cherry-red spot' may be seen at the fovea. Severe involvement may be associated with intraretinal haemorrhage that can involve the macula. The prognosis in mild cases is good, with spontaneous resolution in around 6 weeks. Sequelae to more severe commotio may include progressive pigmentary degeneration and macular hole formation Reference: Kanski 8th edition/873

Sample MCQ #5

Which one of the following statements describing the adult orbit is false?

AThe volume of the orbit is 30 ml.
BThe maximum width is 1 cm behind the anterior orbital margin.
CThe depth of the orbit averages 50 mm from the orbital entrance to the apex.
DThe medial walls are parallel and border the nasal cavity, ethmoid air cells, and sphenoid sinus posteriorly.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. The depth of the orbit averages 50 mm from the orbital entrance to the apex.

The depth of the orbit varies from 40 to 45 mm from the orbital entrance to the orbital apex. The distance to the apex becomes important in the dissection or excision of a tumor in the posterior orbit or in the repair of an extensive blowout fracture Reference : kurana 4/e, p. 48

Sample MCQ #6

In which of the following quadrants are retinal dialyses most often found following blunt trauma?

AInferotemporal, superonasal
BSuperonasal, inferonasal
CSuperotemporal, superonasal
DInferotemporal, superotemporal
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Inferotemporal, superonasal

Blunt trauma may result in a variety of retinal breaks including retinal dialyses, macular holes, giant retinal tears, horseshoe retinal tears, operculated retinal tears, and large, necrotic, sometimes posterior retinal breaks often associated with chorioretinitis sclopetaria. Retinal dialyses, however, are the most common retinal breaks found following blunt ocular trauma. Retinal dialyses resulting from blunt trauma are most often found in the inferotemporal and superonasal quadrants. All patients with a history of blunt trauma need to be given a careful retinal examination with scleral depression to look for retinal breaks after their pain, iritis, or hyphema, if any, has resolved. Reference: Kanski 8th edition/873

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following is the most common indication for repair of medial orbital wall fractures?

AEpiphora
BEntrapped inferior rectus muscle
CSinusitis
DEntrapped medial rectus muscles
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Entrapped medial rectus muscles

Medial orbital wall fractures often cause enophthalmos and/or medial rectus entrapment with restrictive strabismus, both of which are indications for surgical repair. Reference: Kanski 8th edition/866

Sample MCQ #8

What is the MC site of intra ocular FB

ACornea
BLens
CSclera
DVitreous
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Cornea

The most common site of entry of an IOFB is the cornea (82.9%) followed by the sclera (11.4%), and the limbus (5.7%). The most common location of an intraocular foreign body lodged in the posterior segment is intravitreal (61%), followed by intra-retinal (14%) and sub-retinal(5%). Reference: 9th/e kanski chapter 22 trauma pg. 911 IOFB
