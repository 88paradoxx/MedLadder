Sample MCQ #2

A patient is suffering from gyrate atrophy of choroid which is due to the deficiency of enzyme Ornithine aminotransferase. Which of the following is beneficial for the patient?

AArginine free diet
BLysine
CVitamin B6 supplementation
DAll of above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of above

Ornithine aminotransferase deficiency (also known as gyrate atrophy of the choroid and retina) is an inborn error of ornithine metabolism, caused by decreased activity of the enzyme ornithine aminotransferase. Biochemically, it can be detected by elevated levels of ornithine in the blood. Clinically, it presents initially with poor night vision, which slowly progresses to total blindness . It is believed to be inherited in an autosomal recessive manner. Research suggests there can be some adverse effect on muscles and also the brain. The cause of this is somewhat unclear but may relate to very low levels of creatine often found in this population. Treatment may include vitamin B6, lysine or dramatic dietary change to minimize arginine from patient's diet . Reference: Kanski, 7th Edition, Pg no. 473.

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is not a common ocular association in retinitis pigmentosa?

AMyopia
BPrimary open angle glaucoma
CHypermetropia
DKeratoconus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Hypermetropia

Ocular associations of retinitis pigmentosa include myopia, primary open-angle glaucoma, microphthalmos, conical cornea (keratoconus) and posterior subcapsular cataract. Reference: Comprehensive Ophthalmology 7 th edition &ndash; A.K.Khurana, Chapter-12 Diseases of retina.
