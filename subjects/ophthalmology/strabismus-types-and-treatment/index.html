Sample MCQ #1

A regular contact lens user presents with complaints of severe pain, redness, photophobia and blurring of vision for more than two weeks not responding to standard treatment. On examination, a paracentral ring shaped lesion with stromal infiltrates and overlying epithelial defects is observed in the affected eye. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis

ABacterial keratitis
BAcanthomoeba keratitis
CViral keratitis
DFungal keratitis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Acanthomoeba keratitis

A history of contact lens usage and a corneal ulcer not responding to standard treatment ( antibiotics and cylcoplegics ) suggest acanthamoeba ulcer. Reference: Parson&rsquo;s diseases of the Eye-Chap 12 &ndash; Diseases of Cornea.

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following patient scenarios is/are not suitable for keratoprosthesis implantation?

AChemical burns in one eye
BAdvanced Keratoconus
CFungal corneal ulcer with endophthalmitis
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

A keratoprosthesis is a device designed to replace the cornea. Unfortunately, an ideal keratoprosthesis has not yet been devised. Implantation of a keratoprosthesis is considered only as a last resort where penetrating keratoplasty is not possible or has repeatedly failed. Implantation of a keratoprosthesis is reserved for bilaterally blind patients suffering from severe ocular surface disorders such as following chemical burns, Stevens&ndash;Johnson syndrome, severe dry eyes and recurrent graft rejections. Preoperative prerequisites include a minimum vision of light perception and absence of gross posterior segment disorders as evaluated by ultrasonography. Advanced keratoconus: first line of treatment : keratoplasty Fungal endophthalmitis: requires vitrectomy, not keratoprosthesis Reference: Parson&rsquo;s diseases of the Eye-Chap 12 &ndash; Diseases of Cornea

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is the treatment of Candida endophthalmitis?

AVoriconazole
BIntravenous Amphotericin B
CPars plana vitrectomy
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Treatment of Candida endophthalmitis includes: Antifungal treatment : Agent is decided based on culture sensitivity. Intravenous amphotericin-B in combination with oral flucytosine is given, but resistance is a concern. Voriconazole orally or intravenously has a broad spectrum of antifungal action with low reported resistance and high ocular penetration; adjunctive intravitreal treatment may be given(100 &micro;g in 0.1 ml), with serial injections probably needed. Pars plana vitrectomy should be considered at an early stage, especially for severe or unresponsive disease. Along with providing a substantial culture specimen, it reduces fungal and antigen load, facilitates therapeutic agent penetration and clears the ocular media. Reference: Kanski, 7th Edition, Pg no. 454.
