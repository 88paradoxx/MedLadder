Sample MCQ #1

Intraocular lens (IOL) which can correct astigmatism is

AToric IOL
BMultifocal IOL
CMonofocal IOL
DAccomodative IOL
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Toric IOL

Toric IOLs have an integral cylindrical refractive component to compensate for pre-existing corneal astigmatism . The main potential problem is rotation within the capsular bag (rare), and may be corrected by early surgical repositioning. Reference: Yanoff and Duker 4th edition 368

Sample MCQ #2

Endophthalmitis involve all the layers of the eyeball except

ACornea
BSclera
CChoroid
DRetina
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Sclera

Endophthalmitis is the most dreaded and worst possible complication of cataract surgery. It is a suppurative inflammation starting from the vitreous which extends to all the parts of the eye except the sclera . If the sclera also becomes involved , the condition is then called as panophthalmitis . Reference: Yanoff and Duker 4th edition 724/725

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is/are not complications noted in early post-operative period of keratoplasty?

AWound leak
BAstigmatic refractive error
CRecurrence of primary disease
DBoth B & C
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Both B & C

Complications in the early postoperative period include wound leak, shallow anterior chamber, persistent or nonhealing epithelial defect, secondary glaucoma, graft oedema, uveitis, graft infection, inflammatory &lsquo;sterile&rsquo; suture infiltrates and primary graft failure. In the late postoperative period (after 3 weeks), complications include graft rejection, graft infection, secondary glaucoma, loose sutures and high astigmatism. Very late complications (after 1 year) include graft rejection and wound dehiscence from minor trauma and recurrence of the primary disease. Reference: Parson&rsquo;s diseases of the Eye-Chap 12 &ndash; Diseases of Cornea
