Sample MCQ #1

Intraocular lens (IOL) which can correct astigmatism is

AToric IOL
BMultifocal IOL
CMonofocal IOL
DAccomodative IOL
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Toric IOL

Toric IOLs have an integral cylindrical refractive component to compensate for pre-existing corneal astigmatism . The main potential problem is rotation within the capsular bag (rare), and may be corrected by early surgical repositioning. Reference: Yanoff and Duker 4th edition 368

Sample MCQ #2

Endophthalmitis involve all the layers of the eyeball except

ACornea
BSclera
CChoroid
DRetina
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Sclera

Endophthalmitis is the most dreaded and worst possible complication of cataract surgery. It is a suppurative inflammation starting from the vitreous which extends to all the parts of the eye except the sclera . If the sclera also becomes involved , the condition is then called as panophthalmitis . Reference: Yanoff and Duker 4th edition 724/725

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is/are not complications noted in early post-operative period of keratoplasty?

AWound leak
BAstigmatic refractive error
CRecurrence of primary disease
DBoth B & C
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Both B & C

Complications in the early postoperative period include wound leak, shallow anterior chamber, persistent or nonhealing epithelial defect, secondary glaucoma, graft oedema, uveitis, graft infection, inflammatory &lsquo;sterile&rsquo; suture infiltrates and primary graft failure. In the late postoperative period (after 3 weeks), complications include graft rejection, graft infection, secondary glaucoma, loose sutures and high astigmatism. Very late complications (after 1 year) include graft rejection and wound dehiscence from minor trauma and recurrence of the primary disease. Reference: Parson&rsquo;s diseases of the Eye-Chap 12 &ndash; Diseases of Cornea

Sample MCQ #4

Treatment of traumatic cataract in children ?

AECCE + IOL
BLensectomy
CContact lens
DGlasses
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. ECCE + IOL

Ans. A. ECCE + IOL Traumatic cataract in children is a common cause of unilateral loss of vision. Penetrating injuries are usually more common than blunt injuries. At the time of presentation after trauma to eye, primary repair of the corneal or scleral wound is usually preferred, Cataract surgery GCCE) with IOL implantations is performed later following complete evaluation of damage to the intraocular structures by ancillary methods such as B-scan ultrasonography.

Sample MCQ #5

Expulsive hemorrhage in cataract surgery is from?

AVortex vein
BCiliary artery
CChoroidal vein
DNone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Ciliary artery

Ans, B. Ciliary artery Expulsive hemorrhage after cataract extraction or glaucoma surgery = Rupture of ciliary artery. Expulsive hemorrhage in Retinal/Vitrous operation = Direct trauma to choroidal or vortex vein.

Sample MCQ #6

Best option to correct aphakia is

ASpectacle
BContact lens
CIntraocular lens
DBandage contact lens
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Intraocular lens

IOL implantation has the following advantages: Minimal after-care of patient Minimum aniseikonia (difference in image size seen by the eyes) Rapid return of binocularity and Normal peripheral vision Reference: Parson ch 8, P. 85

Sample MCQ #7

Which one of the following single changes results in the selection of a lens more powerful than intended?

AUsing an A-constant of 116.8 instead of 117.3
BAxial length measured as 20.5 mm rather than 20.2 mm
CAverage keratometry of 44.50 D in place of 44.00 D
DPlacing the lens in the ciliary sulcus instead of in the capsular bag
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Placing the lens in the ciliary sulcus instead of in the capsular bag

The lens power formula is dependent on axial length (in millimetres) and keratometry (in dioptres) as follows: Lens power = A &minus; 2.5 (axial length) &minus; 0.9 (average keratometry) A higher 'A-constant', 'decreased axial length', or 'decreased keratometry' readings would result in the calculation of a stronger lens. Moving a convex (plus) lens more anteriorly in the eye and away from the nodal point also effectively increases its power. Reference: Khurana Optics 2/e, P. 45

Sample MCQ #8

A patient had a -10 D myopic LASIK treatment years ago. Now, she presents for cataract surgery. All of the following measurements would be helpful in the calculation of the lens power for cataract surgery in this patient except:

APreoperative refraction
BAmount of LASIK treatment
CPostoperative topography measurements
DCentral corneal thickness
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Central corneal thickness

A number of formulas have been developed to calculate the IOL power after LASIK and refractive surgery, including the Aramberri Double K method, clinical history method, Masket method, and others. These rely upon pre- and postoperative refractions and keratometry, the LASIK treatment, and adjustments to these measurements. Although corneal thickness may influence the curvature of the cornea, the actual corneal curvature measurements and refractions are the most useful in the calculations for lens power post-LASIK. Reference: Khurana Optics 2/e, P. 51
