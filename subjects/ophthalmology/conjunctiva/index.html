Which of the following are indications for Pterygium surgery?

AEncroachment in pupillary area
BSignificant corneal astigmatism
CCosmetic disfigurement
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

A pterygium is best left alone unless it is progressing towards the pupillary area, causes excessive astigmatism, a restriction of ocular motility or is disfiguring. Reference: Parson&rsquo;s Diseases of the Eye-Edition 23-Chap 11-Diseases of the Conjunctiva

Sample MCQ #5

Where does the limbal part of conjunctiva derive its blood supply from?

AAnterior conjunctival artery
BPosterior conjunctival artery
CBoth
DNone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Both

Explanation: The limbal part of the conjunctiva derives its blood supply from Both the anterior and posterior conjunctival arteries. Blood Supply of the Conjunctiva: Marginal Conjunctiva: Supplied by the marginal arcade of the eyelid. Forniceal Conjunctiva: Supplied by the peripheral arcade of the eyelid. Limbal (Bulbar) Conjunctiva: Supplied by both: Posterior conjunctival arteries: Branches of the peripheral arterial arcade of the eyelids. Anterior conjunctival arteries: Branches of the anterior ciliary arteries. These two systems anastomose near the limbus (corneal margin) to form the pericorneal plexus.

Sample MCQ #6

Sub-mandibular nodes receive lymphatics from which part of conjunctiva?

AMedial 1/3 of superior conjunctiva
BMedial 2/3 of inferior conjunctiva
CLateral 2/3 of superior conjunctiva
DBoth A & B
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Both A & B

The lymphatic drainage is to the submandibular nodes, which drain the medial one-third of the superior conjunctiva and the medial two-thirds of the inferior conjunctiva, and the preauricular nodes, which drain the lateral two-thirds of the superior conjunctiva and lateral one-third of the inferior conjunctiva. Reference: Parson&rsquo;s Diseases of the Eye-Edition 23-Chap 11-Diseases of the Conjunctiva

Sample MCQ #8

Which of the following statements regarding management of bacterial conjunctivitis is incorrect?

ATopical antibiotics are mainstay in treatment.
BTopical steroid use is not indicated.
CSystemic antibiotics will be required in pre-septal cellulitis.
DUse of bandage is recommended for associated photophobia.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Use of bandage is recommended for associated photophobia.

Management of Bacterial conjunctivitis: Topical anti-biotics: Broad spectrum drugs ( Ofloxacin/ chloramphenicol) 4-6 times a day Topical steroids: Not indicated Systemic anti-biotics: in case of pyrexia/ pre-septal cellulitis Use of bandage: contra-indicated- prevents drainage of secretions/ increases local temperature and promotes bacterial growth For photophobia, sun glasses can be used. Pad and bandage can promote growth of more bacteria Reference: Parson&rsquo;s Diseases of the Eye-Edition 23-Chap 11-Diseases of the Conjunctiva
