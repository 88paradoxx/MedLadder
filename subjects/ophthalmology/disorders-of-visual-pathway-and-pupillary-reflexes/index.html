Sample MCQ #1

A 40-year old woman develops blurred vision at near in the left eye. She has a mid-dilated pupil that reacts only to near stimulus. What is the most likely diagnosis?

AThird cranial nerve palsy
BAccommodative insufficiency
CAdie's pupil
DAccommodative spasm
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Adie's pupil

An isolated dilated pupil that reacts to near better than light is typically from a tonic(Adie) pupil. Loss of accommodation frequently occurs with pupillary dilation. Accommodative insufficiency and accommodative spasm are not associated with pupillary dilation. Third nerve palsy would be expected to have other features, including ptosis and an ocular misalignment. In Adie's Tonic Pupil, the affected pupil is larger(anisocoria). Its exact cause is not known. It is usually unilateral, associated with absent knee jerk and occurs more often in young women. Adie's pupil constricts with weak pilocarpine(0.125%) drops, while normal pupil does not. Reference: A.K.Khurana, 5th Edition, Pg: 293.

Sample MCQ #2

Swinging light test is positive in ?

AConjunctivitis
BGlaucoma
CRetrobulbar neuritis
DKeratoconus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Retrobulbar neuritis

Ans. C. Retrobulbar neuritis Swinging flash light is used for relative efferent pathway defect (RAPD) which is most characteristic of Iesions in the optic nerve. Thus it is positive in retrobulbar neuritis.

Sample MCQ #3

A 42-year-old woman has anisocoria that is greater in light. The right pupil is sluggishly reactive to light and she has normal ocular motility, alignment and normal eyelid position. What would be the best next step?

A10% cocaine eye drops
BSlit lamp examination
CMRA of the brain
DCarotid ultrasonography
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Slit lamp examination

The findings suggest a problem with the pupillary sphincter(greater anisocoria in light). Normal lid position, alignment and eyelid position make third nerve palsy unlikely. Cocaine is useful in the diagnosis of Horner syndrome. Slit-lamp biomicroscopy would be the most helpful to distinguish abnormalities in the iris that might suggest a tonic pupil or trauma as the cause of anisocoria. Reference: A.K.Khurana, 5th Edition, Pg: 293, Parsons, 22nd, Edition, Pg: 33.

Sample MCQ #5

Which of the following pupillary sign is seen during concussion injury to brain?

AMarcus gun pupil
BHutchinson pupil
CWernicke Hemianopic pupil
DArgyll Robertson Pupil
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Hutchinson pupil

Concussion injuries to the brain These are usually associated with subdural hemorrhage and unconsciousness which may produce the following ocular signs. Hutchinson's pupil: It is characterised by initial ipsilateral miosis followed by dilatation with no light reflex due to raised intracranial pressure. If the pressure rises still further, similar changes occur in the contralateral pupil. Therefore, presence of bilateral fixed and dilated pupils is an indication of immediate cerebral decompression. Papilledema: When it appears within 48 hours of the trauma, it indicates extra or intracerebral hemorrhage and is an indication for immediate surgical measures. While the papilledema appearing after a week of head injury is usually due to cerebral oedema. Reference: AK Khurana, 5th Edition, Pg: 311.

Sample MCQ #6

Major arterial circle is present at

ARoot of iris
BPupillary border of iris
CNear optic disc
DNear Ora Serrata
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Root of iris

It is an anastomoses of long PCA and ACA at the root of the iris From it several branches arise to reach ciliary process. One goes to pupillary margin and forms minor arterial circle Reference: Khurana 8 ed, page 141

Sample MCQ #8

The primary visual cortex is located in

ACentral sulcus
BCalcarine sulcus
CSuperior Temporal Sulcus
DParieto occipital sulcus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Calcarine sulcus

The primary visual area(Area no 17) is located in the Calcarine sulcus in the Occipital lobe of the brain. The accessory visual areas are Area 18 and Area 19. Reference: Yanoff and Duker, 4th edition, Pg: 910-911.
