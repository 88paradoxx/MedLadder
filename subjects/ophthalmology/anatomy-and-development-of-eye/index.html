Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following is true about Cloquet's canal?

AThe hyaloid artery in a foetus runs through it
BContains the primary vitreous at birth
CRuns from lens to optic disc
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Cloquet&rsquo;s canal, which contains the primary vitreous at birth, runs straight from the lens to the optic disc. The secondary vitreous eventually becomes liquefied and shrinks. Between 40 and 70 years of age in most individuals and earlier in myopes, vitreous liquefaction or syneresis occurs; the vitreous mass gradually shrinks and collapses, causing its separation from the retina, a condition known as PVD. Condensations of the vitreous fibrils are present within this liquefied vitreous and are visible as floaters. Reference: Parson&rsquo;s diseases of the eye, 23 rd ed. Ch 17-Diseases of retina and vitreous

Sample MCQ #5

Which muscle originates in the orbital apex above the annulus of Zinn?

ASuperior oblique
BSuperior rectus
CLateral rectus
DInferior oblique
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Superior oblique

Explanation: The superior oblique and levator palpebrae muscles originate in the orbital apex superior to the annulus. All the recti muscles arise in a fibrous ring around the optic foramen to the nasal side of the axis of the eye, the annulus of Zinn, and are inserted in the sclera by flat tendinous insertions about 10 mm broad. The medial rectus is inserted into the sclera about 5.5 mm to the nasal side of the corneoscleral margin, the inferior rectus 6.6 mm below, the lateral rectus 7 mm to the temporal side and the superior rectus 7.75 mm above The inferior oblique originates behind the lacrimal fossa.

Sample MCQ #6

The ophthalmic artery provides blood supply to which of the following structures of the eye?

ACornea
BRod and cone cells
CThe nucleus of the crystalline lens
DBoth b and C
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Rod and cone cells

The cornea and the crystalline lens are the avascular structures of the eye. The remaining structures are supplied by the branches of ophthalmic artery which is a branch of internal carotid artery. Reference: Parson, 22nd Edition, Pg no. 238-244.

Sample MCQ #7

Each rectus muscle is supplied by two to three anterior ciliary arteries, except:

AMedial rectus
BLateral rectus
CSuperior rectus
DInferior rectus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Lateral rectus

The lateral rectus is supplied by one anterior ciliary artery. Reference : kurana 2/e, p. 47

Sample MCQ #8

The only extraocular muscle taking origin from anterior part of orbit is

AIO
BSO
CLPS
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. IO

The inferior oblique muscle has its origin on the maxillary bone just posterior to the inferior medial orbital rim and lateral to the nasolacrimal canal. The inferior oblique is the only extraocular muscle to have its anatomic origin in the anterior orbit. Reference: Kanski, 5 th ed, Page no. 529.
