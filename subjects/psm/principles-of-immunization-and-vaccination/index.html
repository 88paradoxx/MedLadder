Sample MCQ #2

Which vaccine follows open vial policy

ABCG
BPENTAVALENT
CJE
DMMR
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. PENTAVALENT

Explanation: Pentavalent vaccine (which protects against Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus, Hepatitis B, and Hib) is a liquid vaccine that comes in multi-dose vials and does follow the open vial policy . If stored correctly (2-8&deg;C), not expired, unopened vials are clean, and aseptic technique is followed, it can be used up to 28 days after opening. Explanation for other options: BCG (option A): Does not follow open vial policy. It is a lyophilized (freeze-dried) vaccine that must be reconstituted and used within 4-6 hours. After that, it must be discarded. JE (option C): incorrect it is also a lyophilized vaccine, and once reconstituted, it must be used within a short time (usually 4 hours). Does not follow open vial policy. MMR (option D): incorrect it is also lyophilized, and must be used within 4 hours of reconstitution. Does not follow open vial policy.
