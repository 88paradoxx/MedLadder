Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following statements best describes health propaganda?

AIt is actively acquired
BIt appeals to emotions
CIt is behaviour centred
DIt aids in developing a reflective behaviour
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. It appeals to emotions

Explanation: Health propaganda is designed to influence attitudes, beliefs, and behaviors related to health. Its main strategy is to appeal to emotions- evoking fear, empathy, or excitement-to persuade individuals to act or change perceptions. Logic and critical reasoning are generally secondary to emotional impact. Explanation of Other Options: It is actively acquired (Option A): Health propaganda is often passively received via media campaigns, advertisements, or public messaging, rather than actively sought out. It is behavior centered (Option C): While it may indirectly affect behavior, the primary focus is on shaping attitudes and perceptions through emotional appeal. It aids in developing a reflective behavior (Option D): Health propaganda emphasizes immediate emotional response , not deep cognitive reflection or long-term critical thinking.

Sample MCQ #4

In which of the following situations is confidentiality maintained?

AGroup discussion
BCounselling
CPanel discussion
DSeminar
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Counselling

Explanation: Confidentiality is a core principle of counselling . Personal details, problems, and sensitive information shared during counselling sessions must remain private between the counsellor and the client. This confidentiality fosters trust, openness, and therapeutic effectiveness . Exceptions apply in situations where: There is risk of harm to self or others Mandatory reporting is required by law Explanation of Other Options: Group discussion (Option A) : Not confidential since information is exchanged openly among participants. Panel discussion (Option C) : Conducted in front of an audience; no confidentiality is possible. Seminar (Option D) : Educational forum, where information is shared publicly and not private.

Sample MCQ #7

Teaching villagers about wound care after a dog bite by enacting the procedure represents which mode of health communication?

ALecture
BGroup discussion
CFlashcards
DDemonstration
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Demonstration

Explanation: Demonstration is a teaching method where a skill, process, or procedure is shown and enacted in front of an audience . It is particularly effective in community health settings, as it enhances visual learning and practical understanding . In this case, enacting wound care after a dog bite is a clear example of demonstration-based health education . Explanation of Other Options: Lecture (Option A): Involves one-way verbal communication where information is spoken to the audience without visual or hands-on enactment. Group discussion (Option B): Relies on two-way interactive exchange of ideas among participants; no enactment is involved. Flash cards (Option C): Function as visual aids with short cues or points; useful for memory recall, but they don't show an actual process like wound care.
