Sample MCQ #1

Burden of disease in a society is best indicated by

AIncidence
BPrevalence
CCase fatality rate
DDALY
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. DALY

Disability-adjusted life years (DALY) : DALY is a measure of overall disease burden, expressed as a number of years lost due to ill-health, disability or early death. The DALY is becoming increasingly common in the field of public health and health impact assessment. The Global Burden of Disease project combines the impact of premature mortality with that of disability. It captures the population impact of important fatal and nonfatal disabling conditions through a single measure. It is calculated by formula : DALY = YLL + YLD The DALY relies on an acceptance that the most appropriate measure of the effects of the chronic illness is time. One DALY, therefore, is equal to one year of healthy life lost.

Sample MCQ #6

Human living standards can be compared in different countries by

AHDI
BPQLI
CHPI
DDALY
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. HDI

The HDI is a summary composite measure of a country's average achievements in three basic aspects of human development: health, knowledge and standard of living. A component in HDI includes GNI percapita/Income which can be considered as a proxy of Human living standards. Reference: Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th Edition, Chapter Concept Of Health & Disease.

Sample MCQ #8

Sexual dysfunction is associated with a deficiency of which of the following minerals?

AZinc
BCopper
CMagnesium
DMolybdenum
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Zinc

Explanation: Zinc deficiency is closely associated with sexual dysfunction. Zinc is crucial for hormone synthesis, especially testosterone, and supports normal reproductive function in males. A deficiency can result in decreased testosterone levels, erectile dysfunction, reduced libido, and impaired spermatogenesis. Explanation of other options: Copper (Option B): While important for enzymatic activity and iron metabolism, copper is not directly implicated in sexual dysfunction. Magnesium (Option C): Magnesium plays a role in over 300 biochemical reactions but is not directly linked to sexual health or dysfunction. Molybdenum (Option D): A trace element involved in enzymatic processes, but no clear evidence supports a role in sexual function. Reference: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9778609/
