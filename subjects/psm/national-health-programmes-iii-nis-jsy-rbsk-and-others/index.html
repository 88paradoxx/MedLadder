Sample MCQ #1

Recently, the Government of India added a vaccine to the national immunisation schedule against a certain agent which is the leading cause of viral gastroenteritis in children. What is the dosing schedule of this vaccine?

A1 dose, Intradermal at birth
B3 doses,orally at 6,10,14 weeks
C2 doses orally at 16 and 24 months
D3 doses Intramuscular at 6,10,14 weeks
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 3 doses,orally at 6,10,14 weeks

The rotavac vaccine has been added to the national immunisation schedule in 2020 in selected states. It is given at 5drops/dose, orally at 6,10,14 weeks, along with Pentavalent vaccine and OPV. Sample MCQ #2

For a population of 70,00,000 with 30% of it belonging to the slum population, how many Urban PHCs are required for the slum population according to NUHM?

A22
B42
C32
D52
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 42

Explanation: According to NUHM, 42 U-PHCs are required for this scenario. According to NUHM guidelines, an Urban Primary Health Centre (U-PHC) is typically established for every 50,000-60,000 population. When targeting slum areas specifically, one U-PHC is recommended for approximately 25,000-30,000 slum population. In exceptional cases, such as highly dense or isolated slum clusters, one U-PHC may be set up for populations as low as 5,000-10,000. In the given question, the total population is 70,00,000. Out of this, 30% is the slum population, which means the slum population is 21,00,000 (30% of 70,00,000). Using the standard norm of one U-PHC per 50,000 people, we divide the slum population by 50,000. 21,00,000 &divide; 50,000 = 42 Therefore, to serve the slum population of this city, 42 Urban PHCs would be required.

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following is correct about fiPV dosing schedule in NIS?

A6wk 10wk 9m
B6wk 14wk 9m
C6wk, 10wk, 14wk
D6wk, 10wk, 12wk
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 6wk 14wk 9m

Explanation: Fractional Inactivated Polio Vaccine (fIPV) is included in the National Immunization Schedule (NIS) of India. As per the latest guidelines, fIPV is given at: 6 weeks 14 weeks 9 months (along with measles-containing vaccine) This schedule is designed to boost immunity against poliovirus while complementing the doses of Oral Polio Vaccine (OPV). Explanation of other options : 6wk 10wk 9m (Option A): The second dose is given at 14 weeks , not 10 weeks . 6wk, 10wk, 14wk (Option C): The third dose is given at 9 months , not 14 weeks . 6wk, 10wk, 12wk (Option D): This is not in line with the recommended schedule, as the second and third doses are at 14 weeks and 9 months , respectively. Key Takeaways: The correct fIPV dosing schedule in India is 6 weeks, 14 weeks, and 9 months , aligning with the National Immunization Schedule to ensure robust protection against polio.

Sample MCQ #5

Rukmini is attending the village health nutrition and sanitation program day. How frequently is this conducted?

AEvery month
BEvery week
CEvery 14 days
DEvery 3 months
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Every month

Explanation: The Village Health, Nutrition and Sanitation Day (VHND) is conducted once every month. (Option B,C and D ruled out) It is typically held at the Anganwadi Center (AWC) and serves as a crucial platform for providing integrated health, nutrition, and sanitation services to the community, particularly targeting women and children. Location: Primarily held at the Anganwadi Center (AWC). Day: Preferably on Wednesdays, but can be scheduled on any other day in the same month if a village is missed. Key Activities: Immunization, antenatal care, health education, nutrition services, and sanitation promotion. Key Takeaways: VHND is a monthly outreach activity. It is a cornerstone of primary healthcare delivery at the village level under the National Health Mission (NHM).

Sample MCQ #6

Check-ups conducted for children aged 9-18 years under Rashtriya Bal Swasthya karyakram is done for every?

AOnce a year
BOnce in every three months
COnce a month
DSemi-annually
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Semi-annually

Explanation: Under the Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram (RBSK) , school-going children in the age group of 6-18 years are screened by mobile health teams twice a year , which is semi-annually.(Option A,B and C ruled out) This frequent screening schedule is designed for the early detection and management of the 4 Ds: Defects at birth Diseases Deficiencies Developmental delays. Target Group: Children aged 6-18 years in government and government-aided schools. Screening Team: Comprises Mobile Health Teams with a Medical Officer, Pharmacist, and Lab Technician. Purpose: Early identification and intervention for health conditions to reduce child mortality and morbidity. Key Takeaways: RBSK mandates semi-annual (twice-a-year) health screening for school children aged 6-18 years. This ensures regular monitoring and timely intervention for common childhood health conditions.

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following is a service provided under Pradhan Mantri Ayushman Bharat - Health and Wellness Centres ?

ALaundry
BDietary
CSecurity
DPotable water
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Dietary

Explanation: Under the Pradhan Mantri Ayushman Bharat Health and Wellness Centres (PM-ABHWC, also called Arogya Mandir) , essential services include: Dietary services (nutritional support, therapeutic diet where needed) Outpatient and preventive care Maternal and child health care Non-communicable disease screening and management Essential drugs and diagnostics Referral services Dietary services are specifically included to ensure nutritional support during illness and recovery . Explanation of Incorrect Options: Laundry (Option A): Supportive/hospital maintenance service, not part of Arogya Mandir package . Security (Option C): A general facility requirement, not a defined service under PM-ABHWC. Potable water (Option D): Availability of safe water is a basic infrastructure requirement , but not classified as a health service under Arogya Mandir.

Sample MCQ #8

Which of the following is correct regarding the Ayushman Bharat scheme?

ARs 5 lakh/per family per year is given
BRs 10 lakh/per family per year is given
CRs 6 lakh/per family per year is given
DRs 2 lakh/per family per year is given
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Rs 5 lakh/per family per year is given

Explanation: The Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) is the world's largest government-funded healthcare program. It provides: Health insurance coverage up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year (Options B, C & D ruled out) Coverage applies for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization Targeted towards poor and vulnerable families (over 10.74 crore families) Benefits are cashless and paperless , available across empaneled public and private hospitals in India This makes it a critical scheme in reducing out-of-pocket expenditure and improving access to healthcare.
