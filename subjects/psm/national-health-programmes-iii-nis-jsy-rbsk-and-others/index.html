Sample MCQ #1

Recently, the Government of India added a vaccine to the national immunisation schedule against a certain agent which is the leading cause of viral gastroenteritis in children. What is the dosing schedule of this vaccine?

A1 dose, Intradermal at birth
B3 doses,orally at 6,10,14 weeks
C2 doses orally at 16 and 24 months
D3 doses Intramuscular at 6,10,14 weeks
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 3 doses,orally at 6,10,14 weeks

The rotavac vaccine has been added to the national immunisation schedule in 2020 in selected states. It is given at 5drops/dose, orally at 6,10,14 weeks, along with Pentavalent vaccine and OPV. Sample MCQ #2

For a population of 70,00,000 with 30% of it belonging to the slum population, how many Urban PHCs are required for the slum population according to NUHM?

A22
B42
C32
D52
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 42

Explanation: According to NUHM, 42 U-PHCs are required for this scenario. According to NUHM guidelines, an Urban Primary Health Centre (U-PHC) is typically established for every 50,000-60,000 population. When targeting slum areas specifically, one U-PHC is recommended for approximately 25,000-30,000 slum population. In exceptional cases, such as highly dense or isolated slum clusters, one U-PHC may be set up for populations as low as 5,000-10,000. In the given question, the total population is 70,00,000. Out of this, 30% is the slum population, which means the slum population is 21,00,000 (30% of 70,00,000). Using the standard norm of one U-PHC per 50,000 people, we divide the slum population by 50,000. 21,00,000 &divide; 50,000 = 42 Therefore, to serve the slum population of this city, 42 Urban PHCs would be required.
