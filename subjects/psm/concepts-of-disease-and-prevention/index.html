Sample MCQ #1

Secondary prevention is applicable to

ACausal factors
BEarly stage of disease
CLate stage of disease
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Early stage of disease

Ans. is 'b' i.e., Early stage of disease Primordial prevention → Before risk factor present. Primary prevention → Before onset of disease (risk factor present). Secondary prevention → In early stage of disease. Tertiary prevention → Late stage of disease.

Sample MCQ #2

Highly sensitive test, when ___________, rules out a disease.

Apositive
Bnegative
Czero
Dnone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. negative

Highly sensitive test, when negative, rules out a disease. Reference: Park&rsquo;s Textbook of Preventive and Social Medicine, 25 th Edition, pg- 153

Sample MCQ #3

Information especially of a biased or misleading nature used to promote a political cause or point of view is?

APropaganda
BHealth promotion
CHealth education
DAdvocacy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Propaganda

Correct answer is Option A Propaganda is communication that is primarily used to influence or persuade an audience to further an agenda, which may not be objective and may be selectively presenting facts to encourage a particular synthesis.
