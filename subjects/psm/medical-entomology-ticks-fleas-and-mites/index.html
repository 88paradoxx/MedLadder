Sample MCQ #1

A patient presents with fever, an eschar, and regional lymphadenopathy after a trip to a forested area. The causative organism is Orientia tsutsugamushi. Which of the following vectors is responsible for its transmission?

AMite
BTick
CLouse
DFlea
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Mite

Explanation: The correct vector is the Mite . Scrub typhus, caused by Orientia tsutsugamushi, is transmitted to humans through the bite of infected larval stages (chiggers) of trombiculid mites. Explanation of other options: Tick (Option B): Ticks are vectors for diseases like Kyasanur Forest Disease, Lyme disease, and Rickettsial diseases like Rocky Mountain spotted fever, but not for scrub typhus. Louse (Option C): Lice are vectors for diseases like epidemic typhus (Rickettsia prowazekii) and relapsing fever. Flea (Option D): Fleas are vectors for diseases like plague (Yersinia pestis) and endemic/murine typhus (Rickettsia typhi). Key Takeaways: Scrub Typhus = Orientia tsutsugamushi = Vector is Mite (chigger).
