Sample MCQ #1

How many individuals are allocated to one ASHA worker for the population in rural areas?

A500
B1200
C2500
D1000
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 1000

Explanation: The standard population norm for an Accredited Social Health Activist (ASHA) worker in rural areas is one ASHA per 1000 population. (Option A,B and C ruled out) This ratio is designed to ensure that the ASHA, as a community-level link worker, can effectively cover and provide health services to a manageable number of households. ASHA workers are female community health activists selected from and accountable to the villages they serve. They must be local residents, women aged 25-45 years who are married, divorced, or widowed, and possess formal education up to the 10th standard. A proposed revision recommends increasing the norm to two ASHAs per 1,000 population to improve coverage and reduce workload. Key Takeaways: The official allocation for an ASHA worker is 1 per 1000 population in rural areas.

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is not a part of the urban health care delivery system?

APHC
BCHC
CSwasthya Chowki
DSubcenter
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Subcenter

Explanation: The health care delivery system in India differs for rural and urban populations. Rural areas: Health Sub-centers (HSC), Primary Health Centres (PHC), Community Health Centres (CHC). Urban areas: Urban Primary Health Centres (UPHC), Urban Community Health Centres (UCHC), Swasthya Chowkis, dispensaries, and urban hospitals. (Options A, B & C ruled out) A Subcenter is designed exclusively for rural settings and does not exist as a formal component of the urban health infrastructure.

Sample MCQ #4

What is the typical number of deliveries performed by a Type-A Primary Health Center (PHC) in a month?

ALess than 20
B20 or above
CLess than 30
D30 or above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Less than 20

Explanation: Type-A PHCs are small, rural healthcare facilities serving a limited population. They provide essential maternal and child health services but handle fewer deliveries compared to larger facilities . On average, the number of deliveries performed in a Type-A PHC is less than 20 per month .
