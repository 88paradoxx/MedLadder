Jowar is Pellogerogenic due to excess of -

ALeucine
BLysine
CTryptophan
DMethioninc

✓ Correct Answer: A. Leucine

Ans. is 'a' i.e., Leucine o Similar to maize, Jowar also Contains excess of leucine. o Leucine interferes with conversion of tryptophan to niacin. o Pellagra has been reported in India in Telangana area of Andhra Pradesh because of Sower (Sorghum vulgare) consumption.

Sample MCQ #2

Maximum linolenic acid is present in ?

ACoconut oil
BSoyabean oil
CGroundnut oil
DS afflower oil
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. S afflower oil

Ans. is 'd' i.e., Safflower oil [Ref Park 22"d /e p. 566] The richest source of linoleic acid is safflower oil. Sources of linoleic acid in decreasing order are safflower oil > cornoil > Sunflower oil > Soyabean oil > Sesame oil > ground nut oil.

Sample MCQ #3

Cervical cancer screening using the co-test includes:

AVisual Inspection with Acetic Acid (VIA) and Pap smear together
BPap smear and HPV DNA test together
CBlood test for antibodies and Pap smear together
DVIA alone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Pap smear and HPV DNA test together

Explanation: Co-testing for cervical cancer involves performing both a Pap smear (cytology) and HPV DNA testing Purpose: Pap smear: Detects cellular abnormalities (pre-cancerous changes). HPV DNA test: Detects high-risk HPV strains associated with cervical cancer. Recommended for women &ge;30 years for improved sensitivity compared to either test alone. Explanation of Incorrect Options: VIA and Pap smear together (Option A): VIA is a visual screening method ; co-testing specifically refers to cytology + HPV DNA. Blood test for antibodies and Pap smear (Option C): No role of blood antibodies in cervical cancer screening; not part of co-testing. VIA alone (Option D): VIA is a stand-alone screening method , not a co-test.
