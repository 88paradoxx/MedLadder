Sample MCQ #1

When should a primigravida initiate breastfeeding after a normal delivery of a healthy newborn?

AWithin 1 hour
BAfter 1 hour
CAfter 4-6 hours
DAfter 24 hours
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Within 1 hour

Explanation: Early initiation of breastfeeding, ideally within the first hour after birth , is recommended for all healthy newborns, especially for primigravida mothers. Early initiation is important because: It stimulates milk production in the mother. The newborn receives colostrum , which is rich in nutrients and antibodies, providing protection against infections . Promotes mother-baby bonding and helps establish successful breastfeeding.

Sample MCQ #2

The following statements about breast milk are true except:

AThe maximum milk output is seen at 12 months
BThe coefficient of uptake of iron in breast milk is 70%
CCalcium absorption of human milk is better than that of cow's milk
DIt provides about 70 K cals per 100 ml
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. The maximum milk output is seen at 12 months

Maximum milk output occurs around 3 to 6 months postpartum , not 12 months, as infant demand peaks and the mother&rsquo;s milk supply is well-regulated. Iron uptake from breast milk is 70% , due to high bioavailability, allowing infants to absorb it efficiently. Calcium in human milk is better absorbed than in cow&rsquo;s milk, facilitating better bone development. Caloric content of breast milk is around 70 Kcals per 100 ml , meeting the energy needs of infants. Key Takeaways: Peak milk production is around 3-6 months postpartum . Breast milk provides highly bioavailable iron and calcium and approximately 70 Kcals per 100 ml . Sample MCQ #3

The most common presentation for IgA nephropathy is:

ANephritic syndrome
BAsymptomatic proteinuria
CMicroscopic hematuria
DRepeated gross hematuria
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Repeated gross hematuria

Answer is D (Repeated Gross Hematuria): The most common presentation of IgA Nephropathy is with recurrent episodes of Gross (Macroscopic) Hematuria during or immediately following an upper respiratory tract infection 'Recurrent attacks of Painless Gross Hematuria represent the classic clinical presentation of IgA Nephropathy' — Rudolph's Paediatrics
