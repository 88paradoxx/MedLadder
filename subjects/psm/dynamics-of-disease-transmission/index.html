Sample MCQ #1

Which statement accurately describes the single exposure point source epidemic?

AHas more than one incubation period
BHas multiple peaks
CExplosive in nature.
DSlow rise and fall
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Explosive in nature.

Explanation: A single exposure point source epidemic occurs when a group of people are exposed to an infectious agent simultaneously (e.g., contaminated food, water). The outbreak shows a sudden, explosive rise in cases, followed by a rapid decline once the exposure ends. (Option D ruled out) The epidemic curve typically displays a sharp single peak , corresponding to a single incubation period. (Options A & B ruled out)

Sample MCQ #2

The area is declared free of epidemic

ATill last secondary case occurs
BNo new case reported for the incubation period of disease since last case
CNo new case reported for twice the incubation period of disease since last case
DNo new case reported for six months since last case
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. No new case reported for twice the incubation period of disease since last case

An area is declared free of epidemic till no new cases are reported for twice the Incubation period of disease from last case Reference: Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th edition, Chapter Principles of Epidemiology & Epidemiology methods.

Sample MCQ #3

Migration study is used to study

ASocio-demographic reasons for migration of a population
BPrevalence of disease in a population
CEnvironmental and genetic factors in a disease in population
DDisease with long incubation period
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Environmental and genetic factors in a disease in population

Migration studies is the academic study of human migration. Its a method of understanding the relative importance of inherited genetics and environmental factor in medical conditions whose incidence varies around the world Reference: Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th edition, Chapter Principles of Epidemiology & Epidemiology methods.
