Sample MCQ #1

Sachin visited the Emergency Department after he had received a Category 3 wound by a cat at his Veterinary centre. He had received pre-exposure prophylaxis. What should be the next line of management?

AWound management only
BWound management + ARV + HRIg
CARV + HRIg
DWound management + ARV
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Wound management + ARV

In case a person has received Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis, HRIg is not needed to be given. Hence, in this case, Wound management + ARV is only given. Post-Exposure prophylaxis in a person who has already received Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis: Cat 1: Wound Management Cat 2: Wound Management Cat 3: Wound Management + ARV (only 2 doses are administered on Day 0&3) Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis: 3 doses on Day 0, 7 & 21/28 Reference: Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd Edition, Page Number 715

Sample MCQ #2

In a CHC area in a particular day, in which of the following diseases the prevalence may be zero though the incidence may be quite high in the area

AIschemic Heart Disease
BTB
CRabies
DLeprosy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Rabies

All new cases of rabies (Incidence) are dying fast, so Prevalence will be zero at one point Reference: Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th edition, Chapter Principles of Epidemiology & Epidemiology methods.

Sample MCQ #3

A patient presents with a history of category 3 dog bites. He received rabies prophylaxis for a monkey bite 6 months ago. What is the next step in management?

AWound cleaning only
BWound cleaning + rabies vaccine on day 0 & 3
CWound cleaning+rabies vaccine on day 0,3 & 7
DWound cleaning+IM vaccine +RIG
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Wound cleaning + rabies vaccine on day 0 & 3

Explanation: For patients who have previously completed rabies vaccination (within the last 3-6 months), management after a new category 3 exposure includes: Immediate wound cleaning Booster rabies vaccination on days 0 and 3 This approach triggers a rapid anamnestic immune response , providing sufficient protection without additional interventions like rabies immunoglobulin (RIG) . Explanation of Other Options: Wound cleaning only (Option A) : Category 3 bites require vaccination even in previously vaccinated individuals. Wound cleaning + rabies vaccine on day 0, 3 & 7 (Option C) : Only 2 booster doses are needed for previously vaccinated patients. Wound cleaning + IM vaccine + RIG (Option D) : RIG is reserved for rabies-naive patients or those without prior vaccination.
