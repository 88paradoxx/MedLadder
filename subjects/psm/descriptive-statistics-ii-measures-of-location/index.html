Sample MCQ #1

Quartiles and tertiles are measures of:

ADispersion
BCentral tendency
CLocations
DVariation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Locations

Explanation: Quartiles (dividing data into four equal parts: Q1, Q2, Q3) and tertiles (dividing data into three equal parts) are positional measures or measures of location . They describe the position of a value within a dataset but are not measures of central tendency (which identify the center) or dispersion (which describe spread). (Options A,D ,B ruled out) Q2 (the second quartile) is the same as the median , a measure of central tendency, but quartiles/tertiles as a group are classified as locational measures. Key Takeaways: Quartiles and tertiles are measures of location, describing specific points in a data set.
