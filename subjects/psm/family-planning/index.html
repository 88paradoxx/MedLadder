Sample MCQ #1

Absolute contraindication of IUD

AUnexplained uterine bleeding
BPrevious ectopic pregnancy history
CHistory of PID
DSmall size fibroid
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Unexplained uterine bleeding

CONTRAINDICATIONS ABSOLUTE : Suspected pregnancy Pelvic inflammatory disease Vaginal bleeding of undiagnosed etiology Cancer of the cervix, uterus or adnexa and other pelvic tumours Previous ectopic pregnancy. Malignant trophoblastic disease or known pelvic tuberculosis RELATIVE : Anemia Menorrhagia History of PID since last pregnancy Purulent cervical discharge Distortions of the uterine cavity due to congenital malformations, fibroids etc Unmotivated person. KEY TAKE AWAY The ideal IUD candidate : The Planned Parenthood Federation of America (PPFA) has described the ideal IUD candidate as a woman : Who has borne at least one child Has no history of pelvic disease Has normal menstrual periods Is willing to check the IUD tail Has access to follow-up and treatment of potential problems, and Is in a monogamous relationship.

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following is not a component of the RCH program?

ASafe motherhood
BWomen education and empowerment
CChildren and new-born care
DScreening and treatment of STD/RTI
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Women education and empowerment

Explanation: The Reproductive and Child Health (RCH) program focuses on improving maternal and child health. Its core components include: Family Planning: Services for contraception and fertility regulation. Maternal Health (Safe Motherhood): Essential and emergency obstetric care to reduce maternal mortality. ( Option A ruled out ) Child Health: Immunization, management of childhood illnesses, and newborn care (Child Survival component). ( Option C ruled out ) Prevention and Management of RTI/STD: Screening and treatment of Reproductive Tract Infections (RTIs) and Sexually Transmitted Diseases (STDs) . ( Option D ruled out ) While women's education and empowerment is an important aspect of broader public health and gender programs, it is not a direct component of the RCH program , which focuses specifically on health services related to reproduction and child health .

Sample MCQ #3

Mr. X married Mrs. Y, and the couple had two children, born in 2002 and 2005. In 2020, Mr. X's father, who was employed with the State Bank of India (SBI), retired and moved in with Mr. X and his family, accompanied by his wife. Which of the following best describes the family transition?

AFormation in 2002
BRegression in 2020
CExtension in 2002
DDissolution in 2020
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Extension in 2002

Explanation: Family extension occurs when a new member (such as a child) is added to the family. In this case, Mr. X and Mrs. Y had their first child in 2002 , which extended their family. Explanation of other options: Formation in 2002 (Option A): Incorrect, because the family was formed when Mr. X and Mrs. Y got married (before 2002) . Regression in 2020 (Option B): Regression occurs when family members leave or die, which is not the case here. Dissolution in 2020(Option D): Dissolution happens when a family breaks apart (e.g., through divorce or death), but here, the family remained intact. Key Takeaways: Extension of a family occurs when new members, such as children or dependent relatives, are added. The birth of the first child in 2002 marks the extension of the nuclear family. Family formation relates to marriage; regression and dissolution involve loss or breakdown.
