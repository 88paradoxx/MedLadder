Sample MCQ #2

ANot premalignant
BCO2 laser microsurgery is the treatment of choice
CRecurrence is common after surgery
DIntralesional cidofovir is helpful
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Not premalignant

Juvenile papillomas are premalignant. All papillomas should be examined histopathologically to exclude any dysplastic features or malignant transformation. They are caused by HPV-6,11. The infection is transmitted from mother to child at the time of birth. CO2 laser is the treatment of choice. Recurrences are common after surgery. BSteroids only
CSpeech therapy

Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Stripping of cords

Leukoplakia or keratosis larynx Localized form of epithelial hyperplasia involving upper surface of one or both vocal cords. White plaque or warty growth on the cord without affecting its mobility. Precancerous condition Treatment is stripping of vocal cords followed by histology of tissue for any malignant change. Chronic laryngeal irritants should be eliminated. Sample MCQ #5

Which is the narrowest part of Infant larynx

ASupraglottis
BSubglottis
CGlottis
DVentricle
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Subglottis

Infant's larynx is small and conical. The diameter of cricoid cartilage is smaller than the size of glottis, making subglottis the narrowest part . Paediatric endotracheal tube selection depends on this. In adults, glottis is narrowest part . Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, pg. no 335 Scott Brown's Otolaryngology, Head and Neck Surgery - 8 th edition - Volume 3 - Page number - 1081

Sample MCQ #6

The ideal management of Juvenile papilloma of larynx is

AWait and watch
BAntiviral drugs
CCO2 laser surgery
Dendotracheal intubation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. CO2 laser surgery

Management of Juvenile papilloma of larynx - Microlaryngoscopy and CO2laser excision avoiding injury to vocal ligament. Recurrence is common and procedure needs to be repeated several times. In the absence of CO2 laser - tumour removed under microscope with cup forceps or a debrider. Medical therapies - can be used as adjuvants. Sample MCQ #7

Which is the narrowest part of the adult larynx airway?

ASupraglottis
BGlottis
CSubglottis
DTrachea
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Glottis

Explanation: The adult laryngeal airway is divided into three regions: supraglottis, glottis, and subglottis . The glottis , which includes the true vocal cords and the space between them, is the narrowest part of the adult airway. In contrast, the pediatric airway has its narrowest point at the cricoid cartilage (subglottis). Knowledge of the narrowest airway region is crucial for airway management, endotracheal intubation, and recognizing potential sites of obstruction. Explanation of Other Options: Supraglottis (Option A): Includes epiglottis, arytenoids, false cords, and ventricles; wider than the glottis. Subglottis (Option C): Area below the vocal cords to the cricoid; narrowest in children. Trachea (Option D): Continues below the subglottis; larger than glottis in adults. Reference: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33232069/

CTurban epiglottis

✓ Correct Answer: B. painless disease

Lupus larynx Involves the anterior part of the larynx Epiglottis is the most common site. Painless condition. Associated with lupus of nose and larynx. The remaining points are true for tuberculosis of larynx. (OPTIONS a,c,d) TB larynx is a painful disease associated with pulmonary TB.
