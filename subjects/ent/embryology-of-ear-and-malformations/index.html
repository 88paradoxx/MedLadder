Sample MCQ #1

Which structure of the ear develops from all three germ layers?

ATympanic membrane
BExternal auditory canal
CStapes
DVestibular labyrinth
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Tympanic membrane

The tympanic membrane develops from all three germinal layers. The outer epithelial layer is formed by the ectoderm The inner mucosal layer of the endoderm The middle fibrous layer is formed by the mesoderm. Explanation of other options: External auditory canal (Option B): The External auditory canal develops from the ectodermal layer. Stapes (Option C): The Stapes develop from the endodermal layer. Vestibular labyrinth (Option D): Forms from the otic placode (ectoderm) and surrounding mesoderm, but does not involve endoderm. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Page Number: 15

Sample MCQ #2

Feature of Scheibe dysplasia is

ASuperior semicircular canal dehiscence
BAbnormality in bony labyrinth
CDysplasia of cochlea and Saccule
D1.5 turns in cochlea
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Dysplasia of cochlea and Saccule

Scheibe dysplasia Most common inner ear anomaly. Dysplasia of cochlea and saccule; hence also called cochleosaccular dysplasia.(Pars Inferior) Bony labyrinth is normal. Superior part of membranous labyrinth(utricle and semicircular ducts) is normal.(Pars Superior) Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 135.

Sample MCQ #3

False about Mondini dysplasia:

AInner ear malformation
BAlways occur as an isolated anomaly
CCochlear implants are used in treatment
DMay be associated with Pendred syndrome & DiGeorge syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Always occur as an isolated anomaly

Mondini dysplasia may be seen in Pendred, Waardenburg, branchio-oto-renal, Treacher-Collins and Wildervanck syndromes along with other anamolies. Mondini dysplasia: Inner ear malformation Only basal coil is present or cochlea is 1.5 turns. There is incomplete partition between the scalae due to absence of osseous spiral lamina. Condition is unilateral or bilateral Can be treated by a cochlear implant. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 135.

Sample MCQ #4

Saccule of inner ear develop from

APars anterior
BSacculus Posterior
CPars superior
DPars inferior
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Pars inferior

During embryonic development, the saccule, one of the two otolith organs in the inner ear, develops from the pars inferior to the otocyst . The saccule is responsible for detecting linear acceleration and head position. Other options: Sacculus anterior This is a small sac that is located in the utricle of the inner ear. It is not involved in the development of the saccule. Pars superior: This is the superior portion of the otic vesicle. It gives rise to the utricle and the semicircular canals.

Sample MCQ #5

Cochlear implant is contraindicated in all except:

AMichel aplasia
BAbsent cochlear nerve
CMild intracochlear ossification
DPersistent otitis media and mastoiditis without eradication of the disease
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Mild intracochlear ossification

Mild intracochlear ossification is not a contraindication for cochlear implant. Option a - Michel aplasia - Complete absence of bony and membranous labyrinth. No hearing aids or cochlear implantation can be used. Option b - Cochlear implant works by stimulating the auditory nerve. So absence of cochlear nerve is a contraindication for its use. Option d - Persistent otitis media and mastoiditis without eradication of the disease - Bone Anchored Hearing Aids should be considered. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 135, 146.

Sample MCQ #6

A congenitally deaf child on radiological evaluation of cochlea and brain with MRI/CT scan has been found to having normal cochlea with absence of eighth nerve on both sides. What is the best method of hearing rehabilitation in this case?

AUnilateral cochlear implant surgery
BBilateral cochlear implant surgery
CAuditory brain stem implant surgery
DBone anchored hearing aids
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Auditory brain stem implant surgery

Indications of ABI include: Neurofibroma type 2 - Bilateral vestibular schwannoma Congenital aplasia of VIII nerve Cochlear aplasia(Michel Aplasia) The prerequisite for cochlear implant surgery is presence of normal VIII nerve. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 147.

Sample MCQ #7

True About cochlear implant is:

AUsed for mild - to - moderate hearing loss
BIt is implanted through the oval window
CMondini's Cochlear dysplasia is not a contraindication
DContraindicated in Children < 5yrs
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Mondini's Cochlear dysplasia is not a contraindication

Mondini dysplasia of cochlea refers to the condition when there are one and half turns of cochlea rather than two and half. There is absence of the apical modiolus and interscalar septum, resulting in an incomplete partitioning of the cochlea together with an enlarged vestibular aqueduct(EVA) and dilated vestibule. This is not a contraindication of cochlear implantation. Reference : Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Chapter name: Rehabilitation of the Hearing Impaired, Page number: 145
