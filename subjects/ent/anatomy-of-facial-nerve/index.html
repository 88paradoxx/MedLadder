Sample Anatomy of Facial Nerve MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is the surgical landmark for first genu of facial nerve?

APyramid
BPromontory
CProcessus cochleariformis
DPonticulus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Processus cochleariformis

Processuscochleariformis Hook like projection on medial wall of middle ear. Surgical landmark for first genu of facial nerve. It demarcates the geniculate ganglion which lies just anterior to it. Tympanic segment of the facial nerve starts at this level. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Pg. no. - 7, 108.

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following is not a boundary of facial recess?

AFossa incudis
BTympanic segment of the facial nerve
CMastoid segment of the facial nerve
DChorda tympani nerve
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Tympanic segment of the facial nerve

Facial recess is the surgical landmark for posterior tympanotomy. Boundaries: Medial boundary - Vertical segment of the facial nerve (mastoid segment) Lateral boundary - Chorda tympani Superior boundary - Fossa incudis (short process of incus) Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Page Number-6.

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is surgical landmark of first genu of facial nerve?

APyramid
BProcessus Cochleariformis
CLateral SCC Bulge
DPromontory
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Processus Cochleariformis

SURGICAL LANDMARKS OF FACIAL NERVE For middle ear and mastoid surgery Processus It demarcates the geniculate ganglion which lies just anterior to it. Tympanic segment of the nerve starts at this level. Oval window and horizontal The facial nerve runs above the oval window (stapes) and below the horizontal canal. Short process of Facial nerve lies medial to the short process of incus at the level of aditus. Nerve runs behind the pyramid and the posterior tympanic sulcus. Tympanomastoid In vertical or mastoid segment, nerve runs behind this suture. Digastric ridge. The nerve leaves the mastoid at the anterior end of digastric ridge. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose, AND Throat by PL Dhingra, Pg.108,109
