Sample Disorders of External Ear MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

All are true about malignant otitis externa except

ASkull base osteomyelitis
BTechnetium scan is more useful in the prognostic evaluation of this entity
CESR can be used to monitor the progression of disease
DFacial nerve is the most commonly involved nerve
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Technetium scan is more useful in the prognostic evaluation of this entity

Malignant otitis externa = Skull base osteomyelitis is locally destructive infection of bony EAC which is part of skull base. It is mostly seen in old diabetics and caused by Pseudomonas. Radionucleide gallium(good for prognostic evaluation) and technetium scan(good for diagnosis) are helpful in the management. Also ESR is a good tool to monitor disease activity. \ Reference : Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Chapter name: Some Memorable Nuggets for Rapid Review, Page number: 548

Sample MCQ #2

A 62 year old diabetic male with severe pain in ear and granulation with discharge in the external ear. On examination, the patient has facial nerve palsy. What is the most likely diagnosis?

AAcute suppurative otitis media
BMalignant otitis externa
CChronic suppurative otitis media
DExternal ear malignancy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Malignant otitis externa

Malignant Otitis Externa is an inflammatory condition caused by pseudomonas infection usually in the elderly diabetics, or in those on immunosuppressive drugs. Its early manifestations resemble diffuse otitis externa but there is excruciating pain and appearance of granulations in the ear canal.

Sample MCQ #3

Malignant otitis externa is characterised by all of the follwing except

ASevere otalgia
BFacial nerve palsy
CPale granulations in middle ear
DPresence of Sequestrum
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Pale granulations in middle ear

Necrotizing otitis externa, also called malignant otitis externa, is caused by pseudomonas infection in an elderly patient with diabetes. Symptoms include severe otalgia, facial nerve palsy can occur. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose, AND Throat by PL Dhingra, Pg.548

Sample MCQ #4

A 50 year old female patient has presented with the complaint of long standing left ear discharge, foul smelling, blood stained. She has left sided facial palsy. She also gives history of ear ache which is worse at night. On otoscopy, friable granulations are seen in the middle ear. What is the most probable diagnosis?

AMalignant otitis externa
BMalignancy of middle ear
CGlomus jugualare
DKeratosis Obturans
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Malignancy of middle ear

This patient is middle aged with long standing blood tinged ear discharge and ear ache worse at night. She also has granulations in the middle ear. All these point to a diagnosis of Malignancy of the middle ear. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 130, 131.

Sample MCQ #5

While syringing for the ear wax, the cold water should not be used. What is the appropriate reason for this?

AIt may lead to perforation of tympanic membrane
BIt may lead to vertigo
CIt may lead to infection of external auditory canal.
DIt may make the wax hard and difficult to remove.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. It may lead to vertigo

EAC OCCLUSION BY WAX COLLECTION : Some people produce more wax which leads to EAC occlusion by wax collection. This will lead to conductive hearing loss (30 dB CHL approx.) Treatment is PARADICHLOROBENZENE (wax dissolving drops), use these drops for a few days and the suction clearance or syringing is done. SYRINGING-direction of water is Posterosuperior. Use water at body temperature. In syringing avoid cold water because it can lead to vertigo because of inner ear stimulation. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 49, 63.

Sample MCQ #6

Keratosis obturans is caused due to

AAbnormal collection of cerumen in the outer part of external auditory canal
BAbnormal collection of cerumen in the deeper part of external auditory canal
CAbnormal epithelial migration of skin of tympanic membrane
DChronic foul smelling ear discharge present in external auditory canal
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Abnormal epithelial migration of skin of tympanic membrane

Keratosis Obturans : EAC skin sheds off to produce keratin inside EAC. EAC skin migrates outwards, bringing keratin and wax out of EAC. Rarely this skin migration pattern is absent, that will lead to collection of keratin+ wax as an expansile mass in ear, this disease is called KERATOSIS OBTURANS. Treatment is surgery called CANALPLASTY. \ Reference : Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head \and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Chapter name: Diseases of External Ear, Page number: 64

Sample MCQ #7

A 5 year old patient presents with the history of hearing loss. On examination he is found to have a pearly white mass behind the intact tympanic membrane. What is the most possible diagnosis?

ACongenital cholesteatoma
BOtomycosis
COtosclerosis
DExosteosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Congenital cholesteatoma

Cholesteatoma : It is the ectopic presence of skin in the middle ear cleft. It is pearly white in colour. It is of 4 types : Primary Acquired Cholesteatoma (mc) Secondary Acquired Cholesteatoma Tertiary Acquired Cholesteatoma Congenital Cholesteatoma Congenital Cholesteatoma: We use LEVENSON&rsquo;S CRITERIA to diagnose congenital cholesteatoma . It presents as PEARLY WHITE MASS behind INTACT TYMPANIC MEMBRANE It is UNSAFE due to bone erosion . Bone Erosion leads to Complications, so it is called UNSAFE CSOM. Treatment : Surgery &rarr; MRM ( Modified Radical Mastoidectomy) also called as Tympanomastoid Exploration. Reference : Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Chapter name: Cholesteatoma and Chronic Otitis Media, Page number: 80

Sample MCQ #8

Best treatment for traumatic hematoma of the pinna is which of the following measures?

AIce packs and prophylactic antibiotics
BConservative management
CNeedle aspiration
DIncision, drainage and pressure bandage
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Incision, drainage and pressure bandage

PINNA HAEMATOMA: It is due to trauma. It is a sub-perichondrial hemorrhage. Treatment is aspiration or drainage + pressure bandage. Otherwise it will lead to Necosis of cartilage which will lead to PINNA DEFORMITY known as BOXER EAR/CAULIFLOWER EAR. Reference : Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Chapter name: Diseases of External Ear, Page number: 58
