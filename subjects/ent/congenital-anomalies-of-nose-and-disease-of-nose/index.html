Sample MCQ #1

Prussak's space is bounded by all except-

AFibers of lateral malleolar fold
BShrapnell's membrane
CLateral process of Malleus
DShort process of Incus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Short process of Incus

Boundaries of Prussak's space: Superior - Fibers of lateral malleolar fold Inferior - Lateral process of Malleus Lateral - Shrapnell's membrane Medial - Neck of malleus Trautmann's triangle - This is a triangular space bounded by: Bony labyrinth anteriorly. Sigmoid sinus posteriorly. Dura containing superior petrosal sinus superiorly. Boundaries of Macewen's triangle are as follows: Superiorly: Suprameatal crest Anterior-inferiorly: Posterosuperior wall of external auditory canal. Posteriorly: A tangential line to the posterior canal wall. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Page Number-549.

Sample MCQ #2

The pathway of spread of infection to cochlea in meningitis is

ACochlear aqueduct
BEndolymphatic sac
CVestibular aqueduct
DDuctus reuniens
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Cochlear aqueduct

Venous drainage of labyrinth is through three veins, namely internal auditory vein, vein of cochlear aqueduct and vein of vestibular aqueduct, which ultimately drain into inferior petrosal sinus and lateral venous sinus. Bacterial infections reach labyrinth through the middle ear (tympanogenic) or through CSF (meningogenic). Sensorineural hearing loss following meningitis is a well known clinical entity. Bacteria can invade the labyrinth along nerves, vessels, cochlear aqueduct or the endolymphatic sac. Membranous labyrinth is totally destroyed. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose & Head and Neck surgery by P L Dhingra, 9 th edition, Pg.no.38

Sample MCQ #3

Which ossicle has the least blood supply?

AHandle of Malleus
BFoot plate of Stapes
CLong process of Incus
DHead of Malleus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Long process of Incus

Incus has no muscle attached to it. Hence it has the least blood supply. Therefore, it is more vulnerable to be eroded in CSOM. Lenticular process followed by Long process of Incus are most commonly involved. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, pg. no - 77
