Sample Meniere's Disease MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A middle age female patient is complaining of transient sensation of rotational movements of surroundings with nausea while lying down on the bed. She has been experiencing this problem for last 3 days. Which part of the inner ear is the site of origin of pathology that is likely to be responsible for the given complaint?

ACrista
BMacula
CCupula
DAmpulla
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Macula

This is a case of BPPV(Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo). Otoliths or otoconia are calcium carbonate crystals lying embedded in the gelatinous layer of macula. Macula is the sensory end organ of utricle and saccule whose function is linear acceleration. In certain head positions, the displaced otoconia come to rest against the cupula of SCC causing vertigo. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 52.

Sample MCQ #2

One of the following is not the treatment modality for Meniere's Disease

ATranstympanic Gentamycin Injection
BIntermittent Inner ear pulse pressure therapy
CEndolymphatic sac decompression therapy
DSchwartz operation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Schwartz operation

Primary treatment is medical management. Refractory patients are offered further treatments. like transtympanic injection of gentamycin using microwicks or microcatheters. Meniett device is external device used for pulse pressure therapy to labyrinth. Donaldson's line is surgical landmark for localisation of endolymphatic sac to conduct endolymphatic sac decompression therapy. Schwartz operation is other name for cortical mastoidectomy which is done in cases of acute mastoiditis. Reference : Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Chapter name: Meniere's disease, Page number: 124

Sample MCQ #3

Third window effect is seen in:

AMeniere's disease
BPerilymphatic leak
CRuptured tympanic membrane
DSuperior semicircular canal dehiscence
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Superior semicircular canal dehiscence

In superior semicircular canal dehiscence, the patient will have conductive hearing loss due to leakage of sound waves from the dehiscence which acts like a third window. This is called third window phenomenon. Reference: Scott Brown's Otolaryngology, Head and Neck Surgery, 8 th edition, Volume 2, Page - 1192.

Sample MCQ #4

All are characteristic features of superior semicircular canal dehiscence except

AThird window phenomenon
BSensorineural hearing loss on audiometry
CTullio's phenomenon
DHRCT temporal bone is helpful in diagnosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Sensorineural hearing loss on audiometry

Patient shows conductive hearing loss on audiometry due to leakage of sound energy from third window which has been created in SCC(apart from oval and round window). This is called third window phenomenon. Reference : Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Chapter name: Meniere's Disease, Page number: 120

AIt's a Meniere's disease variant

CHearing loss is the first symptom followed by vertigo
DThe symptoms are in reverse order as compared to classical Meniere's disease.

Sample MCQ #7

All of the following are true about Meniere's disease Except

AEndolymph absorption abnormality
BIdiopathic cause
CInner ear ablation required in all cases
DLow frequency sensorineural hearing loss in early stages
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Inner ear ablation required in all cases

Inner ear ablation is only required in all those cases of Meniere's disease who have more frequent disabling episodes of vertigo with no significant residual hearing. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 123, 124.

Sample MCQ #8

A 36 year old male patient is suffering from Meniere's disease for last 3 years. He has developed very frequent disabling episodes of vertigo with normal cochlear function. What will be further appropriate treatment of this patient?

ATranstympanic injection of gentamicin
BVestibular neurectomy
CLabyrinthectomy
DOral Betahistine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Vestibular neurectomy

This patient has normal cochlear function and disabling vertigo. So a treatment method which preserves hearing is preferred. Among the options given, vestibular neurectomy is the correct answer. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 123, 124.
