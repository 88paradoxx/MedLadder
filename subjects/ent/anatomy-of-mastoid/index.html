Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following forms lateral wall of mastoid antrum?

AMastoid process
BSuprameatal triangle
CPetrous temporal bone
DTympanic cleft
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Suprameatal triangle

It is a large, air-containing space in the upper part of the mastoid and communicates with the attic through the aditus. Its roof is formed by tegmen antri, a continuation of the tegmen tympani and separates it from the middle cranial fossa. The lateral wall of antrum is formed by a plate of bone which is on an average 1.5 cm thick in the adult. It is marked externally on the surface of mastoid by suprameatal (MacEwen's) triangle Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose & Head and Neck surgery by P L Dhingra, 9 th edition, Pg.no.7,8

APolish the area with diamond burr
BApply cutting burr to the site of bleeding
CUse electrocautery to control the bleeding
DUse bone wax to control the bleeding

✓ Correct Answer: B. Apply cutting burr to the site of bleeding

Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 566. Scott Brown's Otolaryngology, Head and Neck Surgery, 8 th edition, Volume 2, Page - 1249.

Sample MCQ #3

Mohan, 22 years old, male gives a history of right sided ear discharge, foul smelling and occasionally blood stained for the last 6 years.For last 3 days patient has developed fever with chills, headache and pitting oedema over mastoid region. The most probable diagnosis is

AAtticoantral CSOM with Meningitis
BAtticoantral CSOM with Brain abscess
CAtticoantral CSOM with sigmoid sinus thrombosis
DAtticoantral CSOM with mastoiditis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Atticoantral CSOM with sigmoid sinus thrombosis

Foul smelling and occasionally blood stain discharge, suggest unsafe CSOM also called as Atticoantral CSOM Pitting edema over mastoid is Greisinger sign Mastoiditis do not presents with pitting edema over mastoid or headache Meningitis present with neck, rigidity, vomiting Brain abscess, presence with fever with chills.
