Sample Nasopharyngeal Carcinoma MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A 52 year adult patient presenting with right sided hearing loss has been subjected to audiological investigations and was found to have a type B tympanogram. What will be the priority in further management of this patient?

ANasopharyngoscopy and biopsy
BConservative management
CMyringotomy and grommet insertion
DAntibiotic and decongestant therapy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Nasopharyngoscopy and biopsy

This is a suspected case of Nasopharyngeal carcinoma(NPC). Type B tympanogram is suggestive of glue ear(serous otitis media). The unilateral serous otitis media(presenting as conductive hearing loss) in an adult patient should always arise the suspicion of nasopharyngeal carcinoma. Hence, nasopharyngoscopy should be done and biopsy of any suspicious area should be taken. Nasopharyngeal cancer is the occult hidden primary. Cervical lymphadenopathy is the most common presentation of NPC. Myringotomy and grommet insertion will be done for glue ear at a later date when NPC is ruled out. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Pg. no. 297

Sample MCQ #3

In Nasopharyngeal carcinoma, which of the following is true about the hearing loss in the patient?

AUnilateral Sensorineural hearing loss due to 8 th nerve involvement
BBilateral Sensorineural hearing loss due to 8 th nerve involvement
CUnilateral Conductive hearing loss due to serous otitis media
DBilateral conductive hearing loss due to secretory otitis media
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Unilateral Conductive hearing loss due to serous otitis media

Nasopharyngeal carcinoma will cause unilateral eustachian tube blockage and hence it will lead to unilateral serous otitis media= glue ear. This causes unilateral conductive hearing loss. VIII th nerve is not involved in NPC. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Pg. no. 297
