Sample MCQ #1

A child presented with ear infection with foul-smelling discharge. On further exploration, a small perforation is found in the pars flaccida of the tympanic membrane. What would be the most appropriate next step in management?

ATopical antibiotics and decongestants for 4 weeks
BIV antibiotics and follow-up after a month
CTympanoplasty
DTympanomastoid exploration
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Tympanomastoid exploration

Foul smelling discharge with perforation of pars flaccida of the tympanic membrane is suggestive of atticoantral CSOM. It is always managed surgically. So tympanomastoid exploration(MRM) is the correct answer. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 85, 87.

Sample MCQ #2

The ideal treatment for T1N0M0 glottic cancer larynx is

ARadiotherapy
BLaser cordectomy
CTotal laryngectomy
DDecortication of vocal cord
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Laser cordectomy

T1N0M0 glottic malignancy - Tumour limited to vocal cord(s) with normal mobility. Carcinoma of the mobile membranous vocal cord is traditionally treated with radiotherapy. Now such lesions are precisely excised with CO2 laser under microscope with the same good results . Laser excision has the advantages of lower cost, lower duration of treatment and morbidity. REF:- Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra ,8 th Edition, pg. no - 363

Sample MCQ #3

Which is the preferred treatment for verrucous glottic malignancy of larynx stage T1N0M0?

AChemoradiation
BEndoscopic CO2 laser excision
CTotal laryngectomy
DRadiotherapy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Endoscopic CO2 laser excision

Carcinoma of the mobile membranous vocal cord is traditionally treated with radiotherapy. Now such lesions can be precisely excised with CO2 laser under microscope with the same good results. Laser excision has the advantages of lower cost, lower duration of treatment and morbidity. Similar T1 lesions of the supra- or infrahyoid epiglottis with or without neck nodes have been treated with CO2 laser. Cervical nodes in such cases are managed surgically with appropriate neck dissection. Endoscopic CO2 laser is getting popular at some centers where facilities of CO2 laser and expertise are available. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page Number-363.
