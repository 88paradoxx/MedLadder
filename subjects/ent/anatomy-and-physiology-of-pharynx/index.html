Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is not true about ethmoid bone anatomy?

ALamina papyracea is a part of the ethmoid bone.
BInferior Concha is a part of the ethmoid Bone
CCribriform plate is a part of the ethmoid bone
DMiddle concha is a part of the ethmoid bone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Inferior Concha is a part of the ethmoid Bone

Inferior concha is an independent bone . Superior and middle concha are parts of ethmoid bone. Uncinate process, lamina papyracea and cribriform plate are also parts of ethmoid bone. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page Number-164.

ATensor tympani tendon arises from anterior wall of middle ear

CEndolymphatic sac lies in epidural space and reabsorbs endolymph
DEpitympanum is the narrowest part of middle ear

✓ Correct Answer: D. Epitympanum is the narrowest part of middle ear

Middle ear has three parts- epitympanum, mesotympanum and hypotympanum. Mesotympanum is the narrowest part - Epitympanum (Attic) is the deepest part of middle ear Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, pg. no - 6, 13

Sample MCQ #4

'Ludwig's angina' is characterized by all the following EXCEPT

ACellulitis of floor of the mouth
BCaused by anaerobic organisms
CAphthous ulcers in the floor of Mouth
DInfection spreads from teeth
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Aphthous ulcers in the floor of Mouth

Aphthous ulcers are recurrent and superficial, usually involving movable mucosa, i.e., inner surfaces of lips, buccal mucosa, tongue, floor of mouth and soft palate, while sparing mucosa of the hard palate and gingivae. Ludwig's Angina : Infection of submandibular space(sublingual space + submaxillary spaces) or floor of mouth. It is mainly Cellulitis. Dental Infections account for 80% of the cases. Roots of premolars cause sublingual space infection while roots of the molar teeth cause submaxillary space infection Mixed infections involving aerobes and anaerobes is seen. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingraz, 8 th Edition, pg. no. 255, 31

Sample MCQ #5

In Plummer-Vinson syndrome, the region involved is:

APosterior pharyngeal wall
BValleculae
CPostcricoid region
DPyriform sinus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Postcricoid region

Plummer vinson syndrome(Paterson-Brown-Kelly syndrome) - Barium swallow shows a web in the postcricoid region and the same can be seen on oesophagoscopy. It is due to subepithelial fibrosis in this region. Predominantly affects females past 40 years. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, pg. no. 401

APost Cricoid area
BPyriform Fossa
CVallecula
DPosterior Pharyngeal wall

Sample MCQ #7

Nucleus ambiguus is not associated with which cranial nerve:

AX
BXI
CIX
DXII
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. XII

Ans. D: XII Nucleus Ambiguus Function: * Motor innervation of ipsilateral muscles of the soft palate, pharynx, larynx and upper esophagus. Pathway: * Axons of motor neurons in the nucleus ambiguus course with three cranial nerves: C.N. IX (glossopharyngeal), C.N. X (vagus), C.N. XI (the rostral or cranial portion of spinal accessory) to innervate striated muscles of the soft palate, pharynx, larynx and upper esophagus. Deficits: * Lesion of nucleus ambiguus results in atrophy (lower motor neuron) and paralysis of innervated muscles, producing nasal speech, dysphagia, dysphonia, and deviation of the uvula toward the opposite side (strong side). * No affection of the Sternocleidomastoid or Trapezius. These muscles are innervated by cells in the rostral spinal cord (caudal portion C.N. XI).

Sample MCQ #8

Treatment of choice in a case of Retropharyngeal abscess in a child 2 years of age presenting with stridor and respiratory distress is:

AAntibiotics for 48 hours and then drainage
BAirway Management
CAntibiotics only
DSteroids
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Airway Management

Retropharyngeal abscess Can present as a pediatric emergency . Airway management is the priority. Immediate incision and drainage of abscess should be done. This is usually done without anaesthesia as there is risk of rupture of abscess during intubation. A vertical incision is given in the most fluctuant area of the abscess. Suction should always be available to prevent aspiration of pus. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Pg. no. 313
