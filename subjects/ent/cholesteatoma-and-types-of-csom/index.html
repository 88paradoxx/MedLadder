Sample MCQ #1

Most common cause of conductive deafness in children less than 12 yrs:

ATubotympanic CSOM
BAtticoantral CSOM
CSerous otitis media
DAcute otitis media
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Serous otitis media

In above all the condition we see Hearing loss except in acute otitis media .In Acute otitis media patient present with Ear ache . The most common cause of hearing loss in children less than 12 years is Serous otitis media. Reference : Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Chapter name: Disorders of Middle Ear, Page number: 75

Sample MCQ #2

The most common site of cholesteatoma in the middle ear cleft is

AEpitympanum
BMesotympanum
CPrussack's space
DHypotympanum
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Prussack's space

Prussak's space is the most common site of cholesteatoma formation in middle ear. It lies between the pars flaccida and the neck of malleus. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 80, 549. Reference: Scott Brown's Otolaryngology, Head and Neck Surgery, 8 th edition, Volume 2, Page - 1191.

Sample MCQ #3

CT scan of a patient suffering from atticoantral CSOM shows an abscess at petrous apex. Which of the following is not a likely symptom in this case?

ARetroorbital pain
BDiplopia
CEar discharge
DSensorineural hearing loss
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Sensorineural hearing loss

Spread of infection from the middle ear to petrous bone is called Petrositis or Gradinego syndrome. This is a triad of Ear discharge Deep seated ear or Retro-orbital pain(CN V involvement) Diplopia(CN VI involvement - Lateral rectus palsy) It is a complication of CSOM. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 95.

Sample MCQ #5

What is the most common site of residual cholesteatoma after mastoid exploration in a case of unsafe CSOM?

AFacial Recess
BSinus tympani
CHypotympanum
DEpitympanum
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Sinus tympani

Sinus tympani Sinus tympani is a deep recess on medial wall of middle ear. It is a hidden area and most common site of residual cholesteatoma after mastoid surgery. Boundaries of facial Recess Superior - Ponticulus Inferior - Subiculum Lateral - Limited by vertical segment of facial nerve. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 87.

Sample MCQ #6

Teritary acquired cholesteatoma occurs in which of the following entities

APerforation
BBomb blast injury
CRetraction pocket
DEustachian tube dysfunction
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Bomb blast injury

After a blast injury, a cholesteatoma may arise behind a spontaneously healed tympanic membrane. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose, AND Throat by PL Dhingra

Sample MCQ #7

The marginal perforation of tympanic membrane if allowed to progress, will lead to:

APrimary acquired cholesteatoma
BSecondary acquired cholesteatoma
CTympanosclerosis
DCongenital Cholesteatoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Secondary acquired cholesteatoma

A posterosuperior marginal perforation if allowed to progress will lead to secondary acquired cholesteatoma(In these cases, there is already a pre-existing perforation in pars tensa). Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 80.

Sample MCQ #8

Tubercular otitis media is characterized by all except

APainful otorrhea
BMultiple perforations
CPale granulations
DFoul smelling ear discharge
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Painful otorrhea

Tubercular otitis media is characterized by multiple perforations in the tympanic membrane, pale granulations , and foul-smelling ear discharge . Painful otorrhea is not a typical feature of tubercular otitis media. In laryngeal Tb there will be painful phonation. Other options and why they are wrong: B. Multiple perforations: Multiple perforations in the tympanic membrane are a characteristic feature of tubercular otitis media due to the destructive nature of the disease. C. Pale granulations: Tubercular otitis media is associated with pale or edematous granulations due to the chronic inflammatory process. D. Foul-smelling ear discharge: Foul-smelling or purulent ear discharge is commonly observed in tubercular otitis media due to the chronic infection.
