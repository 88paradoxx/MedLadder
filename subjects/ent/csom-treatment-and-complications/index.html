Sample MCQ #1

A patient of right unsafe CSOM has presented with double vision and pain behind the right eye. What is the name of this entity?

AGriessenger sign
BSampter's triad
CGradinego Syndrome
DTrotter's Triad
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Gradinego Syndrome

PETROSITIS- GRADINEGO SYNDROME It is an infection of Petrous apex air cells. It's a complication of ASOM or CSOM. Petrous apex has relation with 5th and 6th cranial nerves. It has 3 features- PNEMONIC GERD. G-Gradinego syndrome E-Ear discharge R-Retro Orbital pain due to 5 th nerve D-Diplopia-due to 6 th nerve. CT scan of temporal bone will show abscess at Petrous apex. Treatment :&ndash; Surgical exploration. Reference : Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Chapter name: Complications of Suppurative Otitis Media, Page number: 95

Sample MCQ #2

A patient of right unsafe CSOM has presented with history of diplopia and pain behind the right eye. What is the further ideal investigation in this clinical scenario?

AAudiometry
BHRCT temporal bone
CAural swab for culture sensitivity
DVestibular evoked myogenic potentials
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. HRCT temporal bone

Retro orbital pain and diplopia are suggestive of petrositis. Along with otorrhoea, it forms the triad of Gradinego syndrome. Diagnosis of petrous apicitis requires HRCT tempral bone which shows abscess at Petrous apex. Treatment - Cortical, modified radical or radical mastoidectomy + IV antibiotics Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 95.

Sample MCQ #3

Mohan, 22 year old, male gives history of right sided ear discharge, foul smelling and occasionally blood stained for last 6 years. He also complains of hearing loss for last 2 years. For last 3 days patient has developed fever with chills, headache and oedema over mastoid region. The most probable diagnosis is

ATubotympanic CSOM with Meningitis
BAtticoantral CSOM with Brain abscess
CAtticoantral CSOM with Lateral sinus thrombosis
DTubotympanic CSOM with subdural empyema
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Atticoantral CSOM with Lateral sinus thrombosis

Scanty, foul smelling ear discharge is seen in atticoantral CSOM. The patient recently developed fever with chills, headache and oedema over the mastoid(Griesinger sign). All these point to a diagnosis of Sigmoid sinus thrombosis(Lateral sinus thrombosis). TREATMENT Modified radical mastoidectomy with opening the sinus wall and removal of thrombus + i/v antibiotics. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 101.

Sample MCQ #6

A young lady presents to OPD with a history of sudden onset unilateral hearing loss, tinnitus, and dizziness following an episode of acute otitis media two weeks back. On examination, Rinne's test and fistula test were found positive. However, she initially refused treatment and again presented with complaints of worsened hearing loss in the affected ear. Further examination, revealed a negative fistula test. What would be the most likely finding on the Rinne test in this scenario?

ATrue positive
BTrue negative
CFalse positive
DFalse negative
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. True positive

Explanation: The patient with otitis media , has developed labyrinthine fistula as a complication, which caused sensorineural hearing loss (SNHL) , tinnitus, and vertigo. This will result in a: Positive Rinne's test Positive fistula test: Due to perilymph leak or fistula Since treatment was refused, the condition progressed, resulting in complete labyrinthine destruction or the dead ear. This caused a negative fistula sign. However, the ear now has profound unilateral SNHL, where Rinne's test remains positive (a true positive result). Rinne's Test: The Rinne test compares bone conduction (BC) to air conduction (AC) of sound. In conductive hearing loss , BC > AC (i.e., the sound is heard better through bone conduction). In sensorineural hearing loss , AC > BC (i.e., the sound is heard better through air conduction).

Sample MCQ #7

A 38-year-old male presents with suspected diagnosis of suppurative labyrinthitis. A positive Rinne test and a positive fistula sign were recorded on initial examination. The patient refused treatment and returned to the emergency department after 2 weeks complaining of deafness on the same ear. On examination, now the fistula sign was found to be negative. What would be the expected finding on repeating the Rinne test?

ATrue-positive Rinne
BTrue-negative Rinne
CFalse-positive Rinne
DFalse-negative Rinne
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. False-negative Rinne

The patient was earlier having labyrinthine fistula. Because of no treatment, he developed dead labyrinth. A negative fistula sign in this case indicates a dead labyrinth which implies severe sensorineural hearing loss. In such cases a false negative Rinne's test is most likely. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 26, 48.

Sample MCQ #8

A 20yrs old Male present with unilateral Ear discharge, pallor, fever and positive Crowe Beck Test. The clinical diagnosis is:

ACavernous Sinus Thrombosis
BLateral Sinus Thrombosis
CSagittal Sinus thrombosis
DBrain Abscess
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Lateral Sinus Thrombosis

Crowe Beck sign is seen in Sigmoid sinus thrombosis(Lateral sinus thrombosis). It is the engorgement of retinal veins due to pressure on the jugular vein of healthy side seen on fundoscopy. This is absent on the affected side. This is due to blockage of venous drainage of brain. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page - 100, 101.
