Sample MCQ #1

Turbinate that articulates with maxilla is

ASuperior
BMiddle
CInferior
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Inferior

Inferior turbinate articulates with the maxilla. Superior and middle turbinates are parts of ethmoid bone. Reference &ndash; Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra ,8 th Edition, pg. no 159

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following is the treatment for atrophic Rhinitis?

ASteroid nasal spray
BModified Young's operation
CImmunotherapy
DAntihistamine.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Modified Young's operation

ATROPHIC RHINITIS (OZAENA) is a chronic inflammation of nose characterized by atrophy of nasal mucosa and turbinate bones. The nasal cavities are roomy and full of foul-smelling crusts. Surgical management includes: (a) Young's operation . Both the nostrils are closed completely just within the nasal vestibule by raising flaps. They are opened after 6 months or later. In these cases, mucosa may revert to normal and crusting reduced. (b) Modified Young's operation. To avoid the discomfort of bilateral nasal obstruction, modified Young's operation aims to partially close the nostrils. It is also claimed to give the same benefit as Young's.

Sample MCQ #3

Which structure is most commonly involved in the compression of the anterior ethmoid nerve in Sluder's neuralgia?

ASuperior turbinate
BMiddle turbinate
CInferior turbinate
DNasal septum
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Middle turbinate

Explanation: The anterior ethmoidal nerve is most commonly compressed at the middle turbinate followed by the inferior turbinate. Compression of the anterior ethmoidal nerve leads to anterior ethmoid neuralgia, also referred to as Sluder's neuralgia. Most common cause is a spur impinging on the lateral wall of nose. It is characterised by neuralgic pain in the lower half of the face with nasal congestion, rhinorrhea and increased lacrimation.
