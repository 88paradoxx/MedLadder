Sample MCQ #1

What is capula in the inner ear?

ASensory end organ of utricle and saccule
BSensory end organ of macula
CGelatinous layer around macula
DGelatinous layer around cristae
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Gelatinous layer around cristae

The sensory end organ of utricle and saccule are called as macula . They are responsible for linear Balance. The sensory end organ of semicircular canals are called cristae . They are responsible for angular Balance. Each semicircular canal have one narrow end and one dilated end. The dilated end is called ampulla. Cristae are present inside ampulla Cristae contains Gelatinous layer which contains cupula.

Sample MCQ #2

Mark the correct statement(s) regarding Inner and Outer hair cells: Inner hair cells are in a single row and transmit auditory impulses.II. Outer hair cells are in 3-4 rows and modulate the function of inner hair cells.III. Outer hair cells are more responsible for movement of the tectorial membrane. IV. Outer and inner hair cells are present in the ratio of 3:1.

AI, II and IV are correct
BI, II and III are correct
CAll are correct
DII, IV are correct
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. I, II and IV are correct

Explanation: Inner hair cells (IHCs): IHCs are single row cells responsible for transmitting auditory impulses to the brain via afferent fibers . Outer hair cells (OHCs): OHCs are arranged in 3-4 rows and modulate the function of inner hair cells via efferent pathways, amplifying cochlear sensitivity. Outer hair cells and tectorial membrane movement: OHCs do not primarily move the tectorial membrane . Their role is mainly cochlear amplification ; tectorial membrane motion is a passive mechanical response. (Statement 3 is incorrect) Ratio of Outer: Inner hair cells: OHCs (~12,000) outnumber IHCs (~3,500), approximately a 3:1 ratio.

Sample MCQ #3

As per the travelling wave theory of hearing given by Von Bekesy, sound energy creates a wave-like movement in which structure of the inner ear?

AReissner's membrane
BBasilar membrane
CTectorial membrane
DSecondary tympanic membrane
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Basilar membrane

Travelling wave theory of hearing has been given by Von Bekesy which states that - A sound wave depending on its frequency, reaches maximum amplitude on a particular place in the basilar membrane and stimulates that segment. Therefore, higher frequencies are sensed in the basal turn and low frequencies are sensed in the apex of cochlea. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Page Number- 20.
