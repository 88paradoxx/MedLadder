Sample MCQ #2

Ideal treatment for Reinke's Oedema

AAntibiotics and steroids
BSteroids only
CSpeech therapy
DDecortication of cords
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Decortication of cords

Reinke's oedema is due to oedema of the subepithelial space of the vocal cord (Reinke's space). TREATMENT- Decortication of the vocal cord (removal of strip of epithelium is done on one side and 3 - 4weeks on the other). Voice rest, speech therapy, cessation of smoking Reference - Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, pg. no - 342

Sample MCQ #3

All are true about Reinke's odema except

AKnown as Smoker's larynx
BPresents with hoarseness
CSpeech therapy and Proton Pump Inhibitor are the mainstay of treatment
DSub-epithelial cordectomy is the treatment of choice
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Speech therapy and Proton Pump Inhibitor are the mainstay of treatment

Under the epithelium of vocal cords is a potential space with scanty subepithelial connective tissues. It is bounded above and below by the arcuate lines, in front by anterior commissure, and behind by vocal process of arytenoid. Oedema of this space causes fusiform swelling of the membranous cords (Reinke&rsquo;s oedema). Stopping smoking and Subepithelial cordectomy is the treatment of choice. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose, AND Throat by PL Dhingra, Pg.342
