Sample MCQ #3

The first part of PCT has which of the following transport proteins?

ASodium potassium pump
BNa glucose co transporter
CSodium hydrogen antiport
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

PCT reabsorbs 67% of Nacl, water, K+ & filtered urea. 70% of calcium, 100% of glucose & amino acids, 80% of phosphate & bicarbonate. Sodium reabsorption is mainly due to Na+ - H+ antiport Another major transport protein for sodium is SGLT2. Other important sodium transporters in PCT are Na/Pi cotransporter, Na amino acid cotransporter & Na/lactate CT cotransporter. Na+-H+ antiporter is the main determinant of Na & H20 reabsorption in PCT. Na & H+ exchange is linked directly to reabsorption of HCO3- through Na+ Hco3- cotransporter at basal membrane Key Takeaway: The first part of the PCT contains Na+/K+ ATPase, Na+-glucose co-transporter (SGLT2), and Na+-H+ antiporter (NHE3), all playing crucial roles in sodium and solute reabsorption.
