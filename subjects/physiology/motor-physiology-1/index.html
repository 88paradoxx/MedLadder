Sample MCQ #1

During hip replacement, loss of joint and ligament receptor leads to

ANo loss of sensation
BLoss of all sensations
CJoint sensation lost in stable position
DDecrease in sensation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. No loss of sensation

After hip replacement - loss of joint receptors doesn't cause loss of joint position sense After knee replacement - there is decline in joint position sense Reference: Nazan Tugay, Proprioception after Arthroplasty, page no. 4

Sample MCQ #2

Maximum area in homunculus signifies?

ALow control and skilled movements
BMore muscle fibers are present
CIncreased preciseness of movements
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Increased preciseness of movements

Size of the cortical receiving area for the impulse from a particular body part is proportional to the use of the part concerned with precision of movements Reference: Ganong 25th edition page no.169

Sample MCQ #3

Cutting of pyramidal tract after decussation causes all expect

APositive Babinski sign
BLoss of superficial reflex
CLoss of proprioception of opposite limb
DParalysis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Loss of proprioception of opposite limb

Loss of proprioception of opposite limb usually seen in damage to spinal cord Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.241
