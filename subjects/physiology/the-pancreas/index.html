Sample MCQ #1

Adrenaline effects insulin by?

AStimulation of alpha cells
BStimulation of beta cells
CStimulation of delta cells
DStimulation of g cells
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Stimulation of beta cells

Ans. B. Stimulation of beta cells Adrenaline decreases insulin secretion by acting on alpha-2 receptors on beta-cells ofpancreas.

Sample MCQ #2

Sympathetic stimulation has following effect on insulin release

AStimulation
BInhibition
CInhibition followed by stimulation
DNo effect
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Inhibition

&alpha;-Adrenergic stimulators like norepinephrine, epinephrine inhibit insulin secretion &beta;-Adrenergic stimulation increase insulin secretion &alpha;-Adrenergic action predominates so, there is inhibition of insulin secretion Reference: Ganong, 25th edition/page no.433

Sample MCQ #3

Insulin secretion is normally stimulated by

AGLP-1
BGLP-2
CVIP
D&alpha; - adrenergic receptors
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. GLP-1

Oral glucose stimulates the release of certain hormones from GIT that acts on beta cells of pancreas to increase insulin secretion. Such GI hormones are termed as Incretins Incretin hormones are: Glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP1) - most potent incretin Gastric inhibitory peptide (GIP) or Glucose dependent insulinotropic peptide Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.434
