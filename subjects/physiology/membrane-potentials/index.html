Sample MCQ #1

X, Y, Z are the three ions permeable. X = -50 and Y = -30. If at RMP, when there is no net electro genic transfer, what is the value of Z?

A+20
B-20
C+80
D-80
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. +80

No net electro genic transfer at RMP means X+Y+Z=0 Since X= -50 and Y= -30 then Z must be +80 since (-80+80=0) Reference: Guyton textbook of Medical physiology, 11 th edition page no.58

Sample MCQ #2

Resting membrane potential is mainly due to:

ANa+
BK+
CCl+
DMg+
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. K+

At rest, the inside of the cell is negative compared to the exterior. This inside negativity is called as Resting Membrane Potential (RMP) At rest, cell is permeable to potassium ions (positively charged) through leaky potassium ion channels. This passive diffusion of potassium ions from inside to outside of the cell generates RMP. It is also called as diffusion potential Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.9

Sample MCQ #3

Electric potential of resting membrane for a given electrolyte is given by which equation:

ANernst equation
BGoldman
CDonnan equation
DNone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Nernst equation

Equilibrium potential for single ion can be calculated from Nerst equation Describes relation of diffusion potential to on conc difference across a membrane In reality, multiple ions more across cell membrane &rarr; Goldman equation is used According to Nernst equation, EMF (mV) = + 61/z x log (conc inside/ conc outside) where EMF is electromotive force and z is electrical charge of ion Reference: Guyton & Hall, 14th edition pg no. 61

Sample MCQ #4

Resting membrane potential of nerve is equal to equilibrium potential of

ANa+
BCl-
CK+
DHCO3-
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Cl-

Resting membrane potential of nerve is equal to equilibrium potential of chloride ion Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.10

Sample MCQ #5

Which of the following equation is correct regarding equilibrium potential for diffusion?

AEMF = 25 log Ca/Cb
BEMF = 41 log Ca/Cb
CEMF = 61 log Ca/Cb
DEMF = 80 log Ca/Cb
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. EMF = 61 log Ca/Cb

The equilibrium potential for any ion X can be calculated from an equation called as Nernst equation which is: EX = &plusmn;61 log [X o]/[Xi] where EX = equilibrium potential, [Xo] = concentration outside the cell, [Xi] = concentration inside the cell Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no .9

Sample MCQ #7

After application of one stimulus, the time period in which the second stronger stimuli can from an impulse is called

AAbsolute refractory period
BRelative refraction period
CLatent refractory period
DLocal response
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Relative refraction period

Absolute refractory period - No stimulus, no matter how strong, will not excite the nerve during absolute refractory period Relative refractory period - stronger than normal stimulus (suprathreshold stimulus) produces action potential in relative refractory period Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no. 90
