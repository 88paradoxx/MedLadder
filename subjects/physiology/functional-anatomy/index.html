Sample Functional Anatomy MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Measurement of anatomical dead space is done by

AHelium dilution method
BSpirometer
CSingle breath N2 washout method
DXenon-Scan
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Single breath N2 washout method

Anatomic dead space Is the volume of air present in the conducting zone (first 16 generations) which are not used for gas exchange Volume of anatomic dead space (ml) is approximately equal to the body weight in pounds. For a 70 kg man (154 pounds) it is 154 ml It is usually measured by Single breath N 2 washout method Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.632

Sample MCQ #4

Which among the following has the highest airway resistance?

ASmall sized bronchioles
BRespiratory bronchioles
CAlveolar duct
DBronchus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Bronchus

Bronchus has the highest airway resistance. The greatest amount of resistance to airflow occurs not in the tiny air passages of the terminal bronchioles but in some of the larger bronchioles and bronchi near the trachea. The reason for this high resistance is that there are relatively few of these larger bronchi in comparison with the approximately 65,000 parallel terminal bronchioles, through each of which only a minute amount of air must pass.

Sample MCQ #5

Maximum voluntary ventilation is

A25 L/min
B50 L/min
C100 L/min
D150 L/min
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 150 L/min

Maximum Voluntary Ventilation (MVV) MVV is the largest volume of air an individual breath in and out of the lungs in 1 minute It is usually measured for 10-15 seconds and extrapolated for 1 minute Normal MVV is 120-170 L/min Reference: Ganong 25th edition/p
