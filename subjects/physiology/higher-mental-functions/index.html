Sample Higher Mental Functions MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following play most important role in memory

ASynaptic network
BElectric conduction network
CConductivity circuit
DConductivity network
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Synaptic network

Memory information is stored across diffuse network of neurons in brain Synapses across those neuronal networks is strengthened by repetition of what is learned Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.284

Sample MCQ #2

Mitral and periglomerular cells are seen in

AMedulla.
BOlfactory bulb
CPrimary visual cortex
DGeniculate body
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Olfactory bulb

Olfactory bulb has got special synaptic units called olfactory glomeruli where four types of cells are seen namely, Mitral cells Tufted cells Granule cells Periglomerular cells Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no. 217

Sample MCQ #3

All are true about reticular activating system except

AInvolved in arousal
BPolysynaptic pathway
CReceives collateral from all sensory pathway
DProduce &alpha; - block on ECG
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Produce &alpha; - block on ECG

Reticular activating system (RAS) Is a polysynaptic pathway It plays an important role in promoting wakefulness (arousal) It receives collaterals from sensory pathways It is also involved in regulation of heart rate, blood pressure, and respiration Alpha block When attention is focused on something, the alpha rhythm is replaced by fast, irregular low-voltage activity RAS have no role in mediating alpha block Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.273

Sample MCQ #4

A short duration high frequency stimulation with 5Hz of perforating pathway in hippocampus leads to

ALong term potentiation
BPost tetanic potentiation
CLong term depression
DHabituation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Long term potentiation

Brief high-frequency train of stimuli (tetanus) &rarr; long-lasting increase in amplitude of EPSP &rarr; long term potentiation secretion of excitatory neurotransmitter &rarr; acts of membrane excitatory receptor &rarr; increased membrane's permeability to Na+ &rarr; Na entry into axon RMP increases from -65 to -45mV &rarr; EPSP is +20mV Reference: Guyton & Hall, 14th edition pg no. 740

Sample MCQ #5

The representation hemisphere (cerebral) is better than the categorical hemisphere at?

ALanguage functions
BRecognition of objects by their form
CUnderstanding of printed words
DMathematical calculation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Recognition of objects by their form

Left hemisphere Previously called Dominant hemisphere New name - categorical hemisphere Mainly concerned with language and speech Also involved in mathematical and scientific skills Right hemisphere Previously called nondominant hemisphere New name - Representational hemisphere Mainly concerned with visuospatial relations Also involved in Identification of objects form and music Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.291

Sample MCQ #6

Slow wave sleep associated with

ADreams
BCardiac arrhythmia
CPenile intumescence
DDelta activity
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Delta activity

Delta wave Frequency is Seen during deep sleep Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.275

Sample MCQ #7

K-complex and sleep spindles are seen in which stage of sleep

AREM
BStage 1 NREM
CStage 2 NREM
DStage 3 NREM
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Stage 2 NREM

Stage 2 NREM Waves with frequency 12-14 Hz called sleep spindles seen high voltage biphasic waves called K complexes also seen Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.273

Sample MCQ #8

The processing of short-term memory to long term memory is done in

APrefrontal cortex
BHippocampus
CNeocortex
DAmygdala
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Hippocampus

Major Function of Hippocampus - conversion of short-term memory to long term memory (consolidation of memory) Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.283
