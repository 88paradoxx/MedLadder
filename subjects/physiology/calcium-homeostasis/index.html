Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following organs is not involved in calcium homeostasis?

AKidneys
BSkin
CIntestines
DLungs
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Lungs

Three major organs in calcium homeostasis are: Skin Liver Kidney Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.377

Sample MCQ #2

Tetany will result from which of the following disorder?

AMetabolic acidosis
BRespiratory alkalosis
CHyperoxemia
DHypercalcemia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Respiratory alkalosis

TETANY is seen in: Alkalosis Hypocalcemia Hypomagnesemia During hyperventilation CO2 will be washed out. Greater amount of the calcium will bind with plasma proteins (albumin). Therefore patients with respiratory alkalosis are more susceptible for hypocalamic tetany. While in acidosis, less calcium bind with plasma proteins so more free calcium

Sample MCQ #3

Parathormone increases calcium absorption by acting at:

APCT
BDCT
CLoop of Henle
DCollecting duct
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. DCT

PTH acts directly in renal tubules to increase calcium resorption from distal tubules through TRPV5 Reference: Ganong, 25th edition/page no.379

Sample MCQ #4

1- &alpha; hydroxylation in vitamin-D metabolism takes place in:

ASkin
BLiver
CKidney
DBlood
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Kidney

25-hydroxycholecalciferol is converted in the cells of the proximal tubules of the kidneys to the active product called 1,25-dihydroxycholecalciferol or calcitriol The responsible for this hydroxylation is called 1 &alpha; -Hydroxylase Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.378

Sample MCQ #5

Parathormone has all the following effects, except:

AIncreased bone resorption
BIncreased ca +2 reabsorption in kidney
CIncreased phosphate reabsorption in kidney
DIncreased calcitriol synthesis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Increased phosphate reabsorption in kidney

Parathormone hormone increases calcium in the blood and decreases phosphate in the blood therefore. It cause resorption of the bone and also increases calcium absorption from the kidney while inhibits phosphate absorption from the kidney causing phosphate excretion Reference: Ganong's Review of Medical Physiology, 25th Edition, Page Number-379.

Sample MCQ #6

Increased calcium levels lead to:

AIncreased 1, 25 dihydroxy cholecalciferol
BIncreased 24, 25 dihydroxy cholecalciferol
CDecreased calcitonin
DIncreased parathormone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Increased 24, 25 dihydroxy cholecalciferol

Kidney also expresses another important enzyme called 24-Hydroxylase whose action forms the inactive metabolite 24, 25-dihydroxyvitamin D This inactive form conversion is facilitated during increased serum calcium levels Reference: Ganong, 25th edition/page no.378

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following cause low serum calcium

AVit D
BParathyroid
CGH
DGlucocorticoids
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Glucocorticoids

Glucocorticoids decreases plasma Ca 2+ levels by inhibiting osteoclasts Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.383

Sample MCQ #8

Site of-hydroxylation of cholecalciferol:

AKidney
BSkin
CLiver
DLungs
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Liver

25 hydroxylation occurs in liver In the liver, Vitamin D 3 is 25-hydroxylated by an enzyme called 25-Hydroxylase Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.377
