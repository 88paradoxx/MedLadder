AGuanylyl cyclase
BcAMP
CIP3
DDAG
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Guanylyl cyclase

The second messenger systems that are activated through coupling of hormone-receptor complexes by G-protein are: Adenylyl cyclase-cAMP system Inositol triphosphate (IP3) Diacylglycerol (DAG) Calcium (Ca&sup2; + ) system Guanylyl cyclase-cGMP system Guanylyl cyclase is the enzyme responsible for formation of nitric oxide. Nitric oxide is the second messenger involved in cell signaling Reference: Ganong 25th edition page No.56

Sample MCQ #8

Which of the following hormones have an intracellular receptor?

AThyroxine
BGlucagon
CEpinephrine
DParathormone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Thyroxine

Thyroxine hormones have intracellular receptors. Steroid and thyroid hormones bind to the intracellular receptors which regulate gene transcription. The hormone diffuses through the cell membrane into the cytoplasm and combines with the receptor which is present either in cytoplasm or in nucleus. The receptor for glucocorticoid is present in cytoplasm whereas the receptor for thyroxine and other steroid hormones are located in the nucleus. Other options: Option B,C and D: Glucagon, Epinephrine and Parathormone bind to receptors present on the cell membrane. The peptide hormones bind to the cell surface receptors and activate a series of intracellular signal transduction systems.
