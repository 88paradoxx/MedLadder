Sample MCQ #1

Counter current mechanism is present in which of the following parts of the body, except?

AEye
BTestes
CKidney
DGut contract
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Eye

In eyes there is no counter current mechanism. Other options: Option B: Testes are kept cool by the heat exchange in a countercurrent manner between spermatic arteries and veins as it is necessary for normal spermatogenesis to occur as spermatogenesis occurs at lower temperature than interior of the body. Option C: Hyperosmolarity of renal medullary interstitium is established by counter current multiplier and urea recycling is maintained by countercurrent exchange in the Vasa recta. Option D: Counter current mechanism in the villi increases the blood flow in the villi by decreasing oxygen availability for local metabolic reactions of the gut.

Sample MCQ #2

The following is the action of melatonin-

AFacilitates ACTH secretion
BPrevents sleep induction
CRegulates the circadian day night rhythm
DRelease of TSH
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Regulates the circadian day night rhythm

Melotonin receptors are MT 1 and MT 2 receptors MT 1 - Promotes sleep MT 2 - Synchronize circadian rhythm Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.278

Sample MCQ #3

Common precursor of mineralocorticoid, glucocorticoids and sex steroids:

APregnenolone
Ba-hydroxyprogesterone
CDehydrotesterone
DDeoxycorticoid
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Pregnenolone

Adrenal steroid hormones are derived from cholesterol In the mitochondria, cholesterol is converted to pregnenolone by cholesterol desmolase, also called as side- chain cleavage enzyme Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.362

Sample MCQ #4

Hormone exclusively secreted by adrenal gland of female?

AProgesterone
BTestosterone
CEstrogen
DDHEA-S
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. DHEA-S

DHEA-S is the major adrenal sex steroid Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.366
