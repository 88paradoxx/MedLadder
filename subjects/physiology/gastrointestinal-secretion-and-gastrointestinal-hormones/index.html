Sample MCQ #1

Secretion of bile into bile canaliculus is by

AOsmotic gradient
BFacilitated diffusion
CActive transport across the membrane
DSimple diffusion
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Active transport across the membrane

Transport of substances in bile depends on sodium (active transport) Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.461

Sample MCQ #2

Maximum gastric secretin occurs in

ACephalic phase
BIntestinal phase
CGastric phase
DNone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Gastric phase

Gastric phase accounts for 60% of gastric secretion Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.457

Sample MCQ #3

Most important stimulant for bile secretion is

ACholecystokinin
BSecretin
CBile acid
DBile salt
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Bile salt

Bile salts are the most potent stimulus to increase bile secretion. Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.461

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following is choleretic

ACCK
BSecretin
CFatty acid
DAmino acids
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Secretin

Such substances that enhance secretion of bile salts are termed as choleretics Examples are bile salts and secretin Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.461

Sample MCQ #5

All of the following GIT hormones belong to secretin family except-

ASecretin
BGlucagon
CCCK
DVIP
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. CCK

Gastrin and cholecystokinin belongs to gastrin family Reference: Ganong 25th edition page no.471

Sample MCQ #6

Stimulator of secretin secretion-

AEpinephrine
BAcid
CAcetylcholine
DFat
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Acid

Stimulator of secretin is acidic chyme In response to acidic chyme in duodenum, secretin causes pancreatic juice rich in bicarbonate Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.455, 472

Sample MCQ #7

Which causes antral gastrin release-

AAntral distension
BAcid
CSecretion
DCalcitonin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Antral distension

Antral distension causes gastrin release Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.455.468

Sample MCQ #8

All of the following have inhibitory effect on secretion of gastric, except-

AAcid in duodenum
BVIP
CEpinephrine
DGlucagon
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Epinephrine

Stimuli that increase gastrin secretion: Peptides, amino acids (Phenylalanine, tryptophan), Distention, Gastrin releasing peptide, Calcium, Epinephrine Stimuli that decrease gastrin secretion: Acid, somatostatin, Secretin, GIP, VIP, glucagon, calcitonin Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.468
