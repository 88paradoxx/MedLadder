Sample MCQ #1

Capacitance vessels have in their wall

AMore elastic tissue and less muscle
BLess elastic tissue and more muscle
CMore elastic tissue and more muscle
DLess elastic tissue and less muscle
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Less elastic tissue and less muscle

Veins have the capacity to store large amount of blood- "Capacitance vessels" They have less elastic tissue and less smooth muscle in their walls Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page.567

Sample MCQ #3

Pulmonary vascular resistance decreases if which of the following occurs?

AThe lungs are inflated to total lung capacity
BSympathetic stimulation to the pulmonary vessels is increased
CAlveolar oxygen tension is decreased
DCardiac output is increased
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Cardiac output is increased

Increasing cardiac output or pulmonary artery pressure causes pulmonary vascular resistance (PVR) to passively decrease due to two mechanisms-distention of the perfused vessels, and recruitment of more parallel vascular beds. At high lung volumes, the pulmonary capillaries ("alveolar" vessels) are stretched and compressed causing an increased PVR. At low lung volumes, the "extraalveolar" blood vessels are compressed, increasing PVR. The lowest PVR occurs near FRC. Decreasing PO2 or increasing PCO2 or H+ concentration causes PVR to rise. The sympathetic nervous system has little effect on PVR under physiologic conditions, but experimentally, stimulation of sympathetic nerves will constrict the pulmonary vessels, causing increased PVR. Reference: Ganong, pp 661-664. Levitzky, pp 92-107.
