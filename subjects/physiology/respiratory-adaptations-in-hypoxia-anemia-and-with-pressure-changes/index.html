Sample MCQ #1

Blood stored in citrate phosphate dextrose is better for hypoxia patients, than acid citrate dextrose because:

AIt has less P50
BIt is less acidic
CThe fall in 2,3 BPG is less
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. The fall in 2,3 BPG is less

2-3 bisphosphoglycerate binds to beta chain of the hemoglobin, this is required in Rbcs to release oxygen from hemoglobin at the tissue leaving Right Shift therefore CPD is preferred over ACD In hypoxic patient so that 2-3BPG fall less and Oxygen delivery to tissues is better.

Sample MCQ #2

Formula to calculate minute ventilation volume:

ATV X RR
BFiO 2 /PEEP
CTV X (RR - dead space)
DTV + (RR - dead space)
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. TV X RR

Minute ventilation Also called as pulmonary ventilation It is the volume of air inspired or expired per minute It is equal to the tidal volume (TV) multiplied by respiratory rate (RR) Minute ventilation = TV X RR Normal minute ventilation is 6-7.5L/min Reference: Ganong 25th edition page no.628

Sample MCQ #3

CO 2 diffuses more easily through the respiratory membrane than O 2 because it is

ALess dense
BMore soluble in plasma
CLess molecular weight
DLes PCO 2 in the alveoli
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. More soluble in plasma

The diffusion coefficient of CO 2 is about 20 times that of O 2 So, CO 2 diffuses much faster and easily than O 2 Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.639
