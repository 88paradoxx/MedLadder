Sample MCQ #1

Most diffusible ion in excitable tissue is:

ANa+
BK+
CPO4-
DCl-
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. K+

At rest, cell is permeable to potassium ions (positively charged) through leaky potassium ion channels. This passive diffusion of potassium ions from inside to outside of the cell generates RMP Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.9

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following is used in exocytosis?

ACa
BMg
CNa
DK
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Ca

Exocytosis - cell vomiting Large molecules are released to the exterior of cell following fusion of vesicle with cell membrane Fusion of vesicles with cell membrane requires Ca Eg- Hormone, neurotransmitter release Reference: Guyton & Hall, 14th edition pg no. 46

Sample MCQ #3

Eukaryotic plasma membrane is made up of all except

ACarbohydrates
BTriglycerides
CLecithin
DCholesterol
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Triglycerides

Three type of lipids are seen in the cell membrane: Lipids with phosphate group attached-Phospholipids Lipids with carbohydrate group attached-Glycolipids Sterols-cholesterol Triglycerides are not seen in cell membrane Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no. 35
