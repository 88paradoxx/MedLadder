Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following is not mediated through negative feedback mechanism

ABP regulation
BGrowth hormone release
CThrombus formation
DACTH release
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Thrombus formation

Thrombus formation is an example of positive feedback Negative feedback- an INCREASE level of that particular parameter is immediately followed by a DECREASE Examples of negative feedback are: Blood pressure regulation by Baroreflex Endocrine Hormone Regulation. Reference: Guyton, 13 th edition page no.7

Sample MCQ #3

Intercellular communication in eukaryotes is by

ALysosome
BPeroxisome
CGenome
DExosome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Exosome

Intercellular communication in eukaryotes is by exosome. Exosomes are derived from Multivesicular bodies (MVB). These extracellular vesicles are mediators of cell to cell communication in eukaryotes. Other options: Option A: Lysosomes are membrane-bound spherical organelles which contain a variety of hydrolytic enzymes meant for intracytoplasmic digestion. Lysosomes remove intracellular products of metabolism from the cell. Option B: Peroxisomes are small spherical organelles that contain oxidases which promote oxidation of lipids and catalases that liberate oxygen from H2O2. They protect the tissue from oxidative stress. Option C: The genetic information stored in chromosomes of a cell is known as the genome. The human genome contains about 3 billion nucleotide pairs and in diploid cells they are organized into 23 pairs of chromosome

Which of the following is a microfilament?

ATubulin
BActin
CDesmin
DVimentin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Actin

Microfilaments Are mainly actin and myosin concerned with muscle contraction and cell motility Some types of cell motility occur solely as the result of actin polymerization and do not involve the activity of myosin Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.38

Sample MCQ #5

Basal metabolic rate closely associated with?

ALean body mass
BBody surface area
CBody mass index
DBody height
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Body surface area

Body surface area is the most important factor that affects basal metabolic rate "Rubner's law" states that BMR should be related to surface area rather than body mass. This law is also called as "surface law of metabolism" Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.488

Sample MCQ #8

Relation between current, electromotive forces and resistance in a circuit by Ohm's law is stated as

Ai = E/R
Bi-R/E
Ci = RE
DAll the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. i = E/R

Ohm's Law which deals with relationship between potential difference, current and resistance V =I * R In vascular physiology, current flow is equated with the blood flow (F) and the voltage difference is equated with the pressure difference (P) across the vessel wall. So, the modified Ohm's Law is, P =F * R Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.572
