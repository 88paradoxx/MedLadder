Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following hormone is responsible for galactokinesis?

AProlactin
BOxytocin
CEstrogen
DFSH
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Oxytocin

Oxytocin plays an especially important role in lactation-a role that is far better understood than its role in delivery. In lactation, oxytocin causes milk to be expressed from the alveoli into the ducts of the breast so that the baby can obtain it by suckling. Actions of oxytocin Contraction of myoepithelial cells in the breast Milk is forced from the mammary alveoli into the ducts and ejected. Contraction of the uterus During pregnancy, oxytocin receptors in the uterus are up-regulated as parturition approaches, although the role of oxytocin in normal labor is uncertain. Oxytocin can be used to induce labor and reduce postpartum bleeding. Option a) prolactin: responsible for galactopoiesis Option c) ductular proliferation of breast
