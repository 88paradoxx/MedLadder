The oxygen carrying capacity of an 18- year-old boy with hemoglobin of 14g/dl is

A14
B16
C18
D22
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 18

Oxygen Carrying Capacity of Hemoglobin Each gram of hemoglobin transport 1.34 ml. of oxygen So, here in the question, its 14 x 1.34 =18 Reference: Ganong, 25th edition/page no.640

Sample MCQ #2

True about O2 Binding to myoglobin

ASigmoid shaped curve
BMore affinity than hemoglobin
CBinds to 4 molecules of O2 to Myoglobin
DP50 is 26 mmHg
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. More affinity than hemoglobin

Myoglobin is the iron - containing pigment that stores oxygen mainly present in skeletal muscle Each molecule of myoglobin can bind with only one molecule of oxygen myoglobin binds with oxygen with increased affinity leading to loading of oxygen (left shift) Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.641

Sample MCQ #3

Citrate phosphate dextrose stored blood is better for hypoxic patients than and citrate dextrose stored blood because it is:

ALess acidic
BThere is less decrease in 2,3 DPG
CIt has low P50
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. There is less decrease in 2,3 DPG

DPG mutase enzyme is inhibited by acid leading to decrease in 2,3 DPG formation. This leads to left shift of OHDC and less oxygen delivery in tissue So, Acid Citrate Dextrose is not preferred in hypoxic patient. Reference: Ganong 25th edition page no.641

Sample MCQ #4

Increase oxygen delivery to tissues in response to increased CO 2 is-

ABohr effect
BHaldane Effect
CHamburger effect
DChloride shift
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Bohr effect

The decrease in O 2 affinity of hemoglobin when pH of blood falls (PCO 2 rises) is called the Bohr effect. This leads to release of oxygen at tissues Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.641

Sample MCQ #5

Venous blood with high hematocrit is seen in

ARBC high chloride
BPlasma high Na
CPlasma high HCO3
DRBC high K
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. RBC high chloride

Chloride Shift Also called as Hamburger phenomenon The channel responsible for chloride shift is called as anion exchanger 1 (AE 1). It is also called as Band 3 protein present in RBC membrane HCO3 - leaves the RBCs in exchange for Cl - chloride shift) and is transported to the lungs in the plasma Cl -ion is an osmotically active particle that drags water along with it into the RBCs Since RBCs take up water, it increases in size. This is the reason why hematocrit of venous blood is normally 3% greater than that of arterial blood In lungs, chloride shift happen in opposite direction leading to shrinkage of RBC's Reference: Ganong 25th edition/page no.641

Sample MCQ #6

Increase in P50 in oxygenation curve is due to decrease in

ApH
BOxygen
CTemperature
DCO2
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. pH

P50-is the partial pressure of oxygen at which hemoglobin saturation with oxygen is 50% The value of P50 is around 25-27 mm Hg Hemoglobin affinity for O2 is inversely related to the P 50 If the oxyhemoglobin dissociation curve is shifted to the right, the P 50 increases Decrease in pH (acidosis) causes right shift of ODC. So, the answer is pH Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.641

Sample MCQ #7

2,3 BPG binds with which of the following?

AGlobin part of myoglobin
BGlobin part of hemoglobin
CHeme part of myoglobin
DHeme part of hemoglobin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Globin part of hemoglobin

2,3-Bisphosphoglycerate(2,3-BPG) is found in large amounts in red cells. It is formed from 3-phosphoglyceraldehyde, which is a product of glycolysis via the Embden-Meyerhof pathway. It is a highly charged anion that binds to the &beta; chains of deoxyhemoglobin leading to unloading of oxygen to tissues . One mole of deoxyhemoglobin(Taut form or T form of hemoglobin) binds 1 mol of 2,3-BPG. Other options: 2,3-BPG does not bind to alpha and gamma chains.
