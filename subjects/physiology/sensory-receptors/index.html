Sample MCQ #1

The distance by which two touch stimuli must be separated to be perceived as two separate stimuli is greatest at

AThe lips
BThe palm of the hand
CThe back of scapula
DThe dorsum of the hand
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. The back of scapula

Since touch receptors are distributed sparsely on some body areas, such as backs, knees and elbows, the distance needed to discriminate between two tiny point sources of touch may be large Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.161

Sample MCQ #3

Receptor of joint capsule and ligaments is

ASlow adapting
BFast adapting
CNon adapting
DDifferent to slow and fast adapting
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Slow adapting

Slowly adapting receptors These types of receptors fire in response to a stimulus initially but if the stimulus is maintained, they continue firing but their frequency is decreased Examples are Merkel cells, Ruffini corpuscles, Pain receptors, Muscle spindles, joint capsule receptors Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.161

Sample MCQ #4

Single neuron receive 500 inputs from 150 different sensory cells

AFeedback inhibition
BFeedforward excitation
CConvergence
DDivergence
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Convergence

Single neuron receives 500 inputs from 150 different sensory cells and refers to convergence. Convergence means signals from multiple inputs uniting to excite a single neuron. Convergence can result from multiple input fibers from a single source. Convergence can also result from input signals (excitatory or inhibitory) from multiple sources. Convergence is one of the important means whereby the central nervous system correlates, summates, and sorts different types of information.
