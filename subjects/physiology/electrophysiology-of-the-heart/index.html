B-70 mV
C+70 mV
D-90 mV
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. -90 mV

RMP of ventricular myocyte is - 90 mv Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.521

Sample MCQ #2

Repolarization is isolated muscle piece fiber proceeds from

AEpicardium to Endocardium
BEndocardium to Epicardium
CLeft to right
DRight to left
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Endocardium to Epicardium

In isolated cardiac ventricular muscle cell, the direction of depolarization and repolarization are the same from endocardium to epicardium In whole heart, repolarization proceeds from epicardium to endocardium (opposite in direction to depolarization) Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no. 523

Sample MCQ #3

Depolarization of human ventricular muscle starts from

APosterobasal part of ventricle
BLeft side of interventricular septum
CUppermost portion of interventricular septum
DBasal portion of ventricular
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Left side of interventricular septum

Ventricular depolarization begins from left side of interventricular septum Last parts of the heart to be depolarized are posterobasal portion of the left ventricle, the pulmonary conus, and the uppermost portion of the septum. These are the areas which repolarize first Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no. 525

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following is the function of Hyperpolarizing Cyclic nucleotide (HCN) channel

AConduction of impulse
BGeneration of sinus rhythm
CTo maintain the cytoplasmic hyperpolarization
DTo maintain mitochondrial membrane potential
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Generation of sinus rhythm

HCN channels are also called funny current channels They are involved in generation of sinus rhythm Reference: Ganong, 25th edition page no.521

Sample MCQ #5

Inward flow of Na + in Heart leads to

APlateau phase
BAction potential
CRepolarization
DNo change
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Action potential

To be more precise, influx of sodium ions causes depolarization of ventricular action potential. It is usually seen in the form of a rapid upstroke (spike) Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no. 521

Sample MCQ #6

Plateau phase of ventricular muscle is d/t opening of

ANa + Channels
BK + Channels
CCa - Na channels
DClosure of K + channels
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Ca - Na channels

Plateau phase Mainly due to the efflux of potassium ions balanced by influx of calcium ions through calcium channels called as Ca - Na channels Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no 521

Sample MCQ #7

Which part shows prepotential followed by action potential?

AVentricles
BPapillary muscle
CPurkinje fibers
DChordae tendini
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Purkinje fibers

Prepotentials are characteristics of pacemaker tissues in heart Purkinje fibers is one of the latent pacemakers of heart Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no. 521

Sample MCQ #8

In cardiac action potential, phase 0 is due to

ANa+ K+ ATPase
BSodium influx
CPotassium influx
DCalcium influx
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Sodium influx

Phase 0 is the early depolarization characterized by opening of voltage-gated sodium channels leading to sodium influx Reference: Ganong 25 th edition page no. 522
