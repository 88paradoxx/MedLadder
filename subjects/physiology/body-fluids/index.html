Sample MCQ #1

True about body fluid osmolarity:

AMajor contribution is proteins
BMajor contribution is Na+
CECT osmolarity is 250 mOsm/L
DMeasured by dilution method
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Major contribution is Na+

Major contributory of body fluid osmolarity is Na+ Osmolarity: no of osmoles per litre of solution Total Osmolarity (mOsm/L H2O) plasma = 299.8 interstitial = 300.8 intracellular = 301.2 Reference: Guyton & Hall,14th edition pg no. 54

Sample MCQ #2

Normal range of serum osmolality is (mOsm/L):

A280-300
B250-270
C300-320
D210-230
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 280-300

Normal osmolarity = 280-300 mOsm/L H2O Osmolarity = 2 [Na+] + 0.055 [Glucose] + 0.36 [BUN] where BUN - Blood urea nitrogen Osmolarity is osmolar conc expressed as osmoles per litre of solution Osmolarity is more commonly used than osmolality as it is more practical to measure Reference: Guyton & Hall, 14th edition pg no. 43,44

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is NOT TRUE about CSF?

ARemoval of CSF during dural tap causes intense intracranial headache
BNormally contain no neutrophils
CFormed by arachnoid villi within the ventricles
DpH is less than that of plasma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Formed by arachnoid villi within the ventricles

The CSF is formed in the choroid plexuses and the remainder is formed around blood vessels and along ventricular walls. CSF fills the ventricles and subarachnoid space. In humans, the volume of CSF is about 150 mL and the rate of CSF production is about 550 mL/d. Thus the CSF turns over about 3.7 times a day. The composition of CSF is essentially the same as that of brain extracellular fluid (ECF), which in living humans makes up 15% of the brain volume. pH (7.31 - 7.34) slightly less than plasma (7.35 - 7.45). CSF contain no neutrophils. Its differential count is: Lymphocyte - 60-70%, Monocytes - 30-50%, Neutrophils - None. Ref: Barrett K.E., Barman S.M., Boitano S., Brooks H.L. (2012). Chapter 33. Circulation through Special Regions. In K.E. Barrett, S.M. Barman, S. Boitano, H.L. Brooks (Eds), Ganong's Review of Medical Physiology, 24e.

Sample MCQ #4

Effect of infusion of hypotonic saline

AIncreased ICF only
BIncreased ECF only
CIncreased in both ICF and ECF
DIncreased ICF and decreased ECF
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Increased in both ICF and ECF

If hypotonic solution is added to extracellular fluid, ECF osmolarity decreases To obtain same osmolarity, water from ECF moves into ICF by osmosis So both ICF and ECF volume increased Reference: Guyton & Hall, 14th edition pg no. 42,43

ASucrose
BMannitol
CInulin
DAminopyrine

✓ Correct Answer: C. Inulin

Inulin-Gold standard substance for ECF measurement Other indicators used in ECF measurement are: Sucrose Mannitol Sodium Thiosulphate Reference: Guyton, 13 th edition page no.309

Sample MCQ #6

You are an intern and want to determine the quantity of IV fluids needed for a patient. How much amount of transient loss/insensible loss per day would you account for in your calculations?

A150-200 ml/day
B200-350 ml/day
C350-400 ml/day
D500-600 ml/day
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 500-600 ml/day

Insensible water loss is the water loss that occurs through respiratory system, skin and feces It usually amounts to 500-600 ml/day Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.4

Sample MCQ #7

ECF is how much part of total body water

AOne-third
BHalf
CTwo-third
DNone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. One-third

ECF is 1/3 rd total body water (20%) ICF is 2/3 rd total body water (40%) Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.4

Sample MCQ #8

False about total body water (TBW) (Recent Question 2015)

AICF is 2/3 of TBW
BIn new born TBW is 60% body weight
CPremature newborns have more TBW
DIn adults, TBW is 60% of body weight
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. In new born TBW is 60% body weight

It is more in children (75%) when compared to adults (60%) This is because of more body surface area in children and that makes them more prone for dehydration It reaches the adult value of 60% when the child attains 1 years of age Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.4
