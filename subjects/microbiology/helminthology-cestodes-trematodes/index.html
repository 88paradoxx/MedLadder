Sample MCQ #1

An example of type I hypersensitivity is:

ALepromin test
BTuberculin test
CCasoni's test
DArthus reaction

✓ Correct Answer: C. Casoni's test

a) Lepromin test: Type 4 Hypersensitivity b) Tuberculin test: Type 4 Hypersensitivity c) Casoni's test: Type 1 Hypersensitivity d) Arthus reaction: Type 3 Hypersensitivity Examples of Type I Hypersensitivity: Experiments: P-K reaction Schultz Dale phenomenon Theobald smith phenomenon Systemic anaphylaxis Localized anaphylaxis (atopy) such as: Allergic rhinitis (or hay fever) Asthma Food allergy Atopic urticarial Atopic dermatitis (allergic eczema) Drug allergy Wheal and flare reaction. Sample MCQ #2

INCORRECT about Enterotest (String test) is :

AMethod for obtaining duodenal specimen
BPatient is allowed to swallow a capsule containing coiled thread
CUsed for demonstration of giardia lamblia
DNot used for obtaining eggs of Fasciola hepatica
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Not used for obtaining eggs of Fasciola hepatica

Enterotest is a simple method of obtaining duodenal contents. Patient is allowed to swallow a gelatin capsule in which a thread of nylon yarn is coiled. The end of the yarn is affixed to the patient's face. Upon reaching the stomach, gelatin dissolves and a weighted string is carried to duodenum. Secretions are retrieved after 3-4 hours and content adhered to yarn is scraped off and examined under microscope. Traditionally used for Giardia lamblia, it is now used to collect eggs of F.hepatica, larva of S.stercoralis and oocyst of Isospora. Reference : Paniker&rsquo;s textbook of Parasitology - 8th Edition, Pg. No. -35

Sample MCQ #3

Intestinal bilharziasis (Schistosomal dysentery) is commonly seen in infection with -

AS.haematobium
BS.mansoni
CS.japonicum
DS.intercalatum
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. S.mansoni

Option B - S.mansoni resides in venules of inferior mesenteric vein in sigmoido-rectal region. Because of the presence of characteristic eggs having lateral spine, eggs deposited in the intestinal wall leads to erosion, microabscesses, granuloma and dysentry. Reference: Paniker's textbook of Parasitology - 8th Edition, Pg. No. 148
