Sample MCQ #1

Most potent stimulator of Naive T-cells -

AMature dentritic cells
BFollicular dentritic cells
CMacrophages
DB-cell
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Mature dentritic cells

Mature Dendritic cells Mature B-cells and T-cells before antigenic exposure are called naive-B and T cells respectively. Sequence of events in activation of naive T cells. Immature dendritic cells in the epidermis are called langerhans cell. These immature dendritic cells (langerhans cells) capture the antigen in the epidermis. After capturing the antigen these cells secrete cytokines. These cytokines cause loss of adhesiveness of langerhans cells. Langerhans cells separate from each other and migrate into lymphatic vessels. In lymphatic vessel, maturation of langerhans cells takes place. Then these mature langerhans dendritic cells reach to naive T cells in the lymph nodes and present antigen to these cells and activate them.

Sample MCQ #2

A 20-year-old female presents with history of recurrent episodes of swelling of the submucosal tissue of the gastrointestinal and respiratory tracts from childhood. Laboratory evaluation demonstrates that her C1 inhibitor level is less than 6% of the reference value. The most likely diagnosis is:

AHereditary angioedema.
BNutrition-based immune deficiency.
CParoxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria.
DWiskott-Aldrich syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Hereditary angioedema.

Hereditary angioedema is caused by deficient levels of C1 inhibitor. This reduces the ability to control activation of the classical pathway. Autosomal-dominant inheritance Gene locus: SERPING1(C1 inhibitor) on chromosome 11 Excessive spontaneous activation of classical complement pathway (especially C2) causing local inflammation of gastrointestinal and respiratory tracts. Swelling of tracheal and bronchial passages can be life-threatening. Nutrition-based immunodeficiencies are not does not presents with severely reduced levels of specific cell types or related molecules. Paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria is caused by a deficiency of CD59. Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome is caused by a deficiency of the Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome protein. Reference: Ananthanarayan and Paniker's Textbook of Microbiology 10th edition Chapter 19, Pg 187-190

Sample MCQ #3

Lysozyme is found in significant quantities in all of the following bodily secretions, EXCEPT:

ALacrimal secretions
BCSF
CSaliva
DRespiratory tract secretions
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. CSF

Lysozyme is present in tears, saliva, human breast milk and respiratory mucus secretions. It is also present in cytoplasmic granules of the macrophages and the polymorphonuclear neutrophils (PMNs). Reduced lysozyme levels have been associated with bronchopulmonary dysplasia in newborns. In certain cancers (especially myelomonocytic leukemia) excessive production of lysozyme by cancer cells can lead to toxic levels of lysozyme in the blood. High lysozyme blood levels can lead to kidney failure and low blood potassium. It is used as a marker of sarcoidosis disease activity and is suitable for disease monitoring. (Sensitive but less specific) Reference: Ananthanarayan and Paniker's Textbook of Microbiology 12th edition Chapter 14, Page Number: 175
