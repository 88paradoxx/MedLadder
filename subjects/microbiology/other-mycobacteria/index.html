Sample MCQ #5

Which of the following human pathogenic bacteria cannot be cultivated on artificial cell-free culture media?

AMycobacterium leprae
BStaphylococcus aureus
CMycobacterium tuberculosis
DStreptococcus pneumoniae
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Mycobacterium leprae

Explanation: Mycobacterium leprae , the causative agent of leprosy (Hansen's disease) , is an obligate intracellular bacterium that cannot be grown on artificial culture media . It has never been consistently cultured in vitro. It is propagated in the footpads of mice or nine-banded armadillos for research purposes. Staphylococcus aureus, M. tuberculosis, S. pneumoniae a ll of these bacteria can be readily cultured on standard or enriched artificial laboratory media (e.g., blood agar, LJ medium, chocolate agar). (Options B, C and D ruled out) Key Takeaways: leprae is uncultivable on artificial media. Other notable uncultivable pathogens include Treponema pallidum and Rickettsia spp.

Sample MCQ #7

How are live bacilli of Mycobacterium leprae typically characterized under the microscope?

AIrregularly shaped with fragmented ends
BUniformly stained with parallel sides and round ends
CStained in clusters with irregular shapes
DInvisible in the smear
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Uniformly stained with parallel sides and round ends

Uniformly stained with parallel sides and round ends : Correct ; live bacilli display a uniform stain with distinct parallel sides and round ends, appearing five times longer than wide. Why Not Others : Irregularly shaped with fragmented ends : Incorrect; this description is typical of dead bacilli rather than live ones. Stained in clusters with irregular shapes : Incorrect; live bacilli should be uniform and not clustered or irregularly shaped. Invisible in the smear : Incorrect; live bacilli are visible and identifiable using the Ziehl-Neelsen stain. Key Takeaway : Live M. leprae bacilli are characterized by being uniformly stained with parallel sides and round ends , facilitating their identification under the microscope. Tags : inicet 2020 Reference: Shastri, A. (2021). Essentials of Medical Microbiology (3rd ed.). Publisher. Chapter-54-Leprosy , pp-536.

Sample MCQ #8

Which of the following Non-Tuberculous Mycobacteria species is commonly associated with pulmonary infections in immunocompetent individuals?

AMycobacterium leprae
BMycobacterium tuberculosis
CMycobacterium avium complex
DMycobacterium bovis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Mycobacterium avium complex

Mycobacterium avium complex : Correct ; this includes Mycobacterium avium and Mycobacterium intracellulare, which are associated with pulmonary infections in immunocompetent individuals. Why Not Others : Mycobacterium leprae : Misleading as it pertains only to leprosy, not respiratory issues. Mycobacterium tuberculosis : A known pathogen for tuberculosis, distinctly separate from NTM. Mycobacterium bovis : Primarily zoonotic and not typically linked to pulmonary disease in healthy individuals. Key Takeaway : The Mycobacterium avium complex is commonly linked to pulmonary infections in immunocompetent individuals, distinguishing it from other mycobacterial species. Tags : fmge 2021 Reference: Shastri, A. (2021). Essentials of Medical Microbiology (3rd ed.). Publisher. Chapter-63, pp-623.
