Sample MCQ #1

Intestinal re-infection (Hyper infection syndrome) is seen in -

AT.trichiura
BS.stercoralis
CE.vermicularis
DA.duodenale
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. S.stercoralis

Option B - in S.stercoralis infection, if patient has defective cellular response - AIDS patient, lepromatous leprosy, corticosteroid therapy or severe malnutrition, rhabditiform larva which are released in intestinal lumen instead of passing into feces develop into filariform larva in intestine which penetrate intestinal mucosa and lodge into lungs and various tissue and organs - condition called as intestinal re-infection or Hyper infection syndrome. Option A & D - intestinal re-infection usually does not occur in these parasites. Option C - auto-infection and retro-infection is a feature of E. vermicularis in which eggs deposited in perianal skin, autoinfects person due to itching and unhygienic habits or larva hatched in perianal skin moves retrograde through anus causing retrograde autoinfection. Reference: Paniker's textbook of Parasitology - 8th Edition, Pg. No. -184

Sample MCQ #2

Following statement is INCORRECT about Visceral larva migrans

ACommonly caused by Toxocara canis
BCommon mode of infection is ingestion
CLarva develops into adults in ectopic sites
DCan also be caused by larva of S.stercoralis and A.lumbricoides in ectopic sites
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Larva develops into adults in ectopic sites

Option C - Visceral larva migrans (VLM) or toxocariasis is a zoonotic infection usually caused by dog or cat ascarids of the Toxocara genus. The eggs are ingested and then hatch into larvae, which penetrate the intestine and start migrating. These nematodes can infect but not mature in humans so larva is not developed into adults. It is also seen in human nematodes like S.stercoralis and A.lumbricoides, when larvae of these parasites are not able to complete their normal development and enter ectopic sites. Reference : Paniker&rsquo;s textbook of Parasitology - 8th Edition, Pg. No. -167
