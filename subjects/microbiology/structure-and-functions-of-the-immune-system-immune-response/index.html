Sample MCQ #1

T independent antigen acts through:

AMacrophages
BB cells
CCD8 T cells
DCD4 T cells
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. B cells

T-independent antigens can stimulate B cells directly without the need for T-cell help. These antigens are typically polysaccharides or repetitive structures that can cross-link B cell receptors, leading to B cell activation and antibody production. Key Takeaway: T-independent antigens activate B cells directly, bypassing the need for T helper cells. Tag: FMGE 2023 Tag Line: Moderate Reference: Apurva Shastri, Review of Microbiology and Immunology , chapter 12 on antigens - antibody reaction.PP-152.

Sample MCQ #2

All of the following interactions occur between antigen-antibody reactions except:

AIonic bond
BCovalent bond
CHydrogen bond
DVander waal force
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Covalent bond

Antigen-antibody interactions primarily involve ionic bonds, hydrogen bonds, and Van der Waals forces. Covalent bonds are not typically involved in these interactions. Key Takeaway: Antigen-antibody interactions are primarily mediated by non-covalent forces. Tag: May know Tag Line: Moderate Reference Apurva Shastri, Review of Microbiology and Immunology , chapter 12 on antigens &ndash; antibody reaction.PP-160.

✓ Correct Answer: A. Polysaccharide

Following antigens have poor immunogenicity (difficult induction of antibody): Smaller size/molecular weight antigens Nucleic acid > Lipids > Carbohydrates (in decreasing order) Antigens not degradable by tissue enzyme self-antigen Reference : Ananthanarayan and Paniker's Textbook of Microbiology 10th edition Chapter 15, Pg 148-150

✓ Correct Answer: A. Tears and saliva

IgA is concentrated in secretions and on mucus surfaces and Resistant to digestive enzymes. IgM is present in trace amounts in all secretions and does not vary considerably.
