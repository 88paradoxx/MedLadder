Sample MCQ #1

Acellular pertussis vaccine is composed of: a) Pertussis toxoid b) FHA c) Pertactin d) IgA e) Adhesins f) cAMP

Aa,b,c
Ba,b,c,d
Ca,e,d,f
Da,b,c,d,e,f
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. a,b,c

Acellular Pertussis Vaccine: It is composed of pertussis toxoid and 2 or more other bacterial components such as FHA, pertactin or fimbriae. Though the efficacy is same as the WC vaccine, it is associated with fewer side effects as compared with the latter and can be safely given after 5&ndash;6 years. It is available as DaPT (along with diphtheria and tetanus toxoid). It is recommended for older children who have missed their primary doses of DPT. Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 641

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following special factors are required for Haemophilus? A. Hemin B. Christmas factor C. Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide D. Fibrinogen E. Thrombin F. Ca2+ G. Fe2+

Aa, b, c, f
Ba, c
Cb, c
De, f, g
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. a, c

Haemophilus requires two accessory growth factors (factor X and V) in blood for their growth. Factor X is a hemin, present freely in blood. Factor V is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD), which is present inside RBCs. It is also produced by some bacteria, such as Staphylococcus aureus. Growth surrounding X and XV disks only: Produced by species that require X factor, but not V factor; examples include H. ducreyi and A. aphrophilus. Growth surrounding V and XV disks only: Produced by species that require X factor, but not V factor; examples include H. parainfluenzae, H. parahaemolyticus and A. paraphrophilus. Growth surrounding combined XV disk only: Produced by species that require both X and V factors; examples include: H. influenzae, H. aegyptius and H. haemolyticus. Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 612

Sample MCQ #4

Which growth factor is essential for the growth of Haemophilus influenzae in culture media?

AVitamin B12
BHemin (Factor X)
CFolic acid
DSodium chloride
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Hemin (Factor X)

Hemin (Factor X): Correct - Haemophilus influenzae specifically requires hemin (Factor X) for growth, which must be supplied in enriched culture media, as it cannot synthesize it. Why Not Others : Vitamin B12: Incorrect - While important for many bacteria, it is not necessary for the growth of Haemophilus influenzae . Folic acid: Incorrect - Although it may be involved in metabolic processes, it is not the primary growth factor required by Haemophilus influenza . Sodium chloride: Incorrect - Sodium chloride is used for osmotic balance but is not a specific growth factor for Haemophilus influenzae . Key Takeaway: Hemin (Factor X) is crucial for the growth of Haemophilus influenzae in culture media, as this organism cannot synthesize it. Tags: good to know Tag Line: EASY Reference: Shastri, A. (2021). Essentials of Medical Microbiology (3rd ed.). Publisher, Chapter 61, pp 610.

✓ Correct Answer: C. ii, iv

Following scenario most likely suggests Bordetella Pertussis infection. B. pertussis causes Whooping Cough/100 days fever. Clinical course is divided into 3 stages. Catarrhal phase: 1-2 weeks // cough, coryza, lacrimation, low-grade fever // highly infectious stage // smear and culture +ve Paroxysmal phase: smear and culture -ve // whooping cough and post-tussive vomiting Convalescent phase: recovery phase Vaccination status has increased.
