Sample MCQ #1

Cholera is known to cause diarrhea through its interaction with specific receptors in the human body. Which of the following is known to make it easier for cholera toxin to enter the body through the intestinal mucosa?

AGLUT 1
BGM2 ganglioside
CCephalin
DGM1 ganglioside
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. GM1 ganglioside

Explanation: Cholera toxin is produced by Vibrio cholerae and is the main factor responsible for watery diarrhea in cholera. The toxin attaches to receptors on the intestinal epithelial surface and enters the cells. The specific receptor is GM1 ganglioside , a glycosphingolipid located in the intestinal cell membrane. Binding to GM1 initiates endocytosis of the toxin. Inside the cell, the toxin disrupts ion transport, causing massive fluid loss and diarrhea. Explanation of other options: GLUT1 (Option A): Facilitates glucose transport across membranes; not a receptor for cholera toxin. GM2 ganglioside (Option B): A glycosphingolipid, but not the binding site for cholera toxin. Cephalin (Option C): A membrane phospholipid, unrelated to cholera toxin entry. Reference: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3089049/

Sample MCQ #2

A 25 yr old man presents with history of diarrhea and fever since 5 days.There is a history of consumption of uncooked crabs and oysters on the trip to Bangkok. On culture ,it showed swarming on blood agar and the organism was found to be motile. Identify the culture media which is blood agar with high salt content which shows beta hemolysis

AWagatsuma agar
BChocolate agar
CXLD agar
DRCM medium
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Wagatsuma agar

Halophilic vibrios can withstand higher salt concentration (>6%) in contrast to V. cholerae, which can tolerate upto 6%. They are widespread in marine environment. Kanagawa phenomenon: It causes hemolysis on Wagatsuma agar (a special type of high salt blood agar). Reference : Ananthnarayan and Paniker's textbook of Microbiology 12 th edition pg no.487

Sample MCQ #5

An 18-year girl was playing in a slowly moving stream when she cut her foot on a sharp object. Three days later, she was brought to the emergency room because of pain and swelling at the site of the wound and drainage of pus from it. The most likely cause of the infection is

AVibrio vulnificus
BEscherichia coli
CAeromonas hydrophila
DProteus mirabilis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Vibrio vulnificus

Vibrio vulnificus: Cutaneous Infection Apart from sepsis, V. vulnificus can also cause primary wound infection (Vulnificus is Latin word for "wound maker" ). Characterized by painful erythematous swelling or cellulitis or even vesicular, bullous or necrotic lesions. It generally affects people without underlying disease, in contrast to sepsis, which is usually observed in patients with underlying disease such as liver disease, iron overload or immunosuppression. Wound infections can also be produced by Vibrio parahaemolyticus. Other options are less likely in comparison to vibrio vulnificus to cause wound infection. Reference: Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no. 417

AVibrio Cholerae
BVibrio Parahaemolyticus

✓ Correct Answer: B. Vibrio Parahaemolyticus

Parahemolyticus shows kanagawa phenomenon on Wagatsuma agar. It grows in upto 8% salt solution, shows swarming growth on blood agar. It is known to cause invasive diarrhea. Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page 417

Sample MCQ #7

You are studying biochemical reactions of bacteria. You perform the string test where a suspension of the organism in 0.5% sodium deoxycholate produces a viscous mucoid string when lifted with an inoculating loop. Which of the following reactions and cultural characteristics are shown by this bacterium (Vibrio cholerae)? i. Cholera red reaction ii. Kanagawa phenomenon iii. Black colonies on Tinsdale media iv. Yellow colonies on TCBS agar v. School of fish appearance on wet mount

Aii, iii
Bi, ii, iv, v
Ci, iv
Di, iii, v
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. i, iv

The above image depicts string test, shown by Vibrio Cholerae. A suspension of the bacteria, along with bile salts gives a positive test. Along with this, Vibrio also shows a positive nitrate reduction test. On a wet mount, it gives a FISH IN STREAM appearance. It is a non- fastidious bacteria. Transport media include autoclaved sea water and VR media. Enrichment media includes Alkaline peptone water and Monsur's taurocholate tellurite agar. Selective media are TCBS, where V.cholerae show yellow colonies and Monsur's GTTA. they are late lactose fermenters. Other options- Kanagawa phenomena is shown by V. parahaemolyticus Black colonies on Tinsdale media are shown by C. diphtheria School of fish appearance is shown by H.ducreyi, while V.cholera shows Fish in stream Appearance Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page 414

Sample MCQ #8

Which of the following conditions is required for growing microaerophilic bacteria like Campylobacter?

A5% O2, 85% N2, and 10% CO2
B5-10% CO2
CAnaerobic conditions
D37&deg;C without CO2
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 5% O2, 85% N2, and 10% CO2

Campylobacter Growth Conditions Microaerophilic Environment: Campylobacter species thrive in a specific atmospheric condition of 5% O2, 85% N2, and 10% CO2 . Unsuitable Conditions: Other oxygen levels or gas compositions do not support the growth of microaerophilic bacteria like Campylobacter. KEY TAKE AWAY: Campylobacter, Helicobacter and Actinomyces Israeli - microaerophilic, require- 5% O2 , 85% N2 and 10% CO2 . Tags: inicet 2022 Reference: Shashtri A.
