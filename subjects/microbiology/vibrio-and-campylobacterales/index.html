Sample MCQ #1

Cholera is known to cause diarrhea through its interaction with specific receptors in the human body. Which of the following is known to make it easier for cholera toxin to enter the body through the intestinal mucosa?

AGLUT 1
BGM2 ganglioside
CCephalin
DGM1 ganglioside
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. GM1 ganglioside

Explanation: Cholera toxin is produced by Vibrio cholerae and is the main factor responsible for watery diarrhea in cholera. The toxin attaches to receptors on the intestinal epithelial surface and enters the cells. The specific receptor is GM1 ganglioside , a glycosphingolipid located in the intestinal cell membrane. Binding to GM1 initiates endocytosis of the toxin. Inside the cell, the toxin disrupts ion transport, causing massive fluid loss and diarrhea. Explanation of other options: GLUT1 (Option A): Facilitates glucose transport across membranes; not a receptor for cholera toxin. GM2 ganglioside (Option B): A glycosphingolipid, but not the binding site for cholera toxin. Cephalin (Option C): A membrane phospholipid, unrelated to cholera toxin entry. Reference: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3089049/

Sample MCQ #2

A 25 yr old man presents with history of diarrhea and fever since 5 days.There is a history of consumption of uncooked crabs and oysters on the trip to Bangkok. On culture ,it showed swarming on blood agar and the organism was found to be motile. Identify the culture media which is blood agar with high salt content which shows beta hemolysis

AWagatsuma agar
BChocolate agar
CXLD agar
DRCM medium
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Wagatsuma agar

Halophilic vibrios can withstand higher salt concentration (>6%) in contrast to V. cholerae, which can tolerate upto 6%. They are widespread in marine environment. Kanagawa phenomenon: It causes hemolysis on Wagatsuma agar (a special type of high salt blood agar). Reference : Ananthnarayan and Paniker's textbook of Microbiology 12 th edition pg no.487
