Sample MCQ #1

Regarding T.cruzi, which of the following is most accurate :

ATransmitted by Tsetse fly
BHumans are major reservoir
CExists as both amastigote and trypomastigote in humans
DComplications of chronic diseases are common in children
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Exists as both amastigote and trypomastigote in humans

Option C - Metacyclic trypomastigotes are infective forms of parasite which after entry through bug bite, enters R-E cells and convert into amastigote forms. Some of them, after converting through epimastigote form, convert to trypomastigote forms inside blood. Reduviid bug is a vector which when feeds on human blood, acquires infection. Option A - Tsetse fly is a vector which transmits T.brucei Option B - Armadillo, cat ,dog and pigs are major reservoir Option D - complications of chronic diseases are common in adults, while in children acute disease is common. Reference : Paniker&rsquo;s textbook of Parasitology - 8th Edition, Pg. No. -48

Sample MCQ #2

Best way to differentiate amoebic dysentery stool from bacillary dysentery stool in microscopy is -

APresence of macrophage
BPresence of Charcot-Leyden crystals
CPresence of RBCs in clumps
DPresence of trophozoite with ingested RBC
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Presence of trophozoite with ingested RBC

Option D &ndash; the only confirmatory way to diagnose E.histolytica infection (amoebic dysentery) is presence of trophozoites with ingested RBCs. Macrophage can be present in amoebic as well as bacillary dysentery. Though characteristically present in clumps, RBC&rsquo;s can be present discrete and it is very difficult to identify type of dysentery based on arrangement of RBCs as its not very specific. Charcot-Leyden crystals are present in amoebic dysentery but it is also present in other parasitic infection and allergic conditions. Reference : Paniker&rsquo;s textbook of Parasitology - 8th Edition, Pg. No. -20

Sample MCQ #3

Conversion of uninucleate cyst into mature quadrinucleate cyst in E.histolytica is accompanied by all EXCEPT -

AProduction of refractile cyst wall
BReduction in glycogen mass
CAppearance of chromatoid body
DSuccessive nuclear division
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Appearance of chromatoid body

Option C &ndash; Uninucleate cyst is spherical about 10-20 &micro; in size. It has a single nucleus, large glycogen mass and 1-4 chromatoid bodies. With successive nuclear division, glycogen mass is reduced in size and the chromatoid body disappears and the cyst is surrounded by refractile cyst wall. Reference : Paniker&rsquo;s textbook of Parasitology - 8th Edition, Pg. No. -16
