Sample MCQ #1

Two girls in the same class are diagnosed with meningococcal meningitis. Their 12-year-old close friend is in fear of contracting disease. What advice should be given to her-

ATwo doses of polysaccharide vaccine
BTwo doses of conjugate vaccine
CSingle dose of meningococcal vaccine
DNo vaccine needed as exposure has already occurred.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Two doses of conjugate vaccine

Meningococcal meningitis can spread from one person to other. Because it stays in nasopharynx of person. Now in the given case 2 girls in the same class are already diagnosed with meningococcal meningitis. Their friend must get two dose of conjugate vaccine to prevent getting infection. Incorrect options : Option A is wrong two dose of polysaccharide vaccine. Option C i s wrong Single dose of meningococcal vaccine. Because meningococcal vaccine has two dose not one. Option D is wrong, vaccine can help in spreading of infection. No matter exposure has occurred or not, vaccine can always help. Key Takeaways: Conjugate vaccines (MCV4) are preferred over polysaccharide vaccines (MPSV4). Close contacts of meningococcal meningitis cases require vaccination and antibiotic prophylaxis. Meningococcal conjugate vaccine induces stronger, long-lasting immunity and reduces carriage.

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following is a component uniquely found in the outer membrane of gram-negative bacteria?

ALipoproteins
BPeptidoglycan
CLipopolysaccharide (LPS)
DTeichoic acid
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Lipopolysaccharide (LPS)

Explanation: Lipopolysaccharide (LPS) , also known as endotoxin , is a unique and defining component of the outer membrane of gram-negative bacteria . It consists of lipid A (the toxic component), core polysaccharide, and O antigen. It is a major virulence factor and potent activator of the host innate immune system. Explanation of other options: Lipoproteins (Option A): Found in both gram-positive (e.g., Braun's lipoprotein) and gram-negative bacteria. Peptidoglycan (Option B): A core component of the cell wall in both gram-positive (thick layer) and gram-negative (thin layer) bacteria. Teichoic acid (Option D): A component found embedded in the thick peptidoglycan layer of gram-positive bacteria; it is absent in gram-negative bacteria. Key Takeaways: LPS (endotoxin) is specific to the outer membrane of gram-negative bacteria. Teichoic acid is specific to gram-positive bacteria.

Sample MCQ #5

A 25-year-old woman has a history of repeated infections with Neisseria species, including Neisseria meningitidis. She reports several episodes of meningitis and septicemia. Complement testing reveals a deficiency in C5, C6, C7, C8, and C9. Which complement deficiency is most likely contributing to the patient’s recurrent Neisseria infections?

ADeficiency of C1 esterase inhibitor
BDeficiency of C3
CDeficiency of C5-9 (membrane attack complex)
DDeficiency of Properdin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Deficiency of C5-9 (membrane attack complex)

The deficiency of C5 through C9 components affects the formation of the membrane attack complex (MAC), which is essential for the lysis of certain bacteria, including Neisseria species. This deficiency makes individuals highly susceptible to infections caused by Neisseria, as the MAC is crucial for killing these Gram-negative bacteria. Key Takeaway: Deficiency in C5 through C9 components results in an increased risk of Neisseria infections due to the impaired ability to form the membrane attack complex. Tags: INICET | must know Reference: Shastri, A. (2023). Immunology: Chapter 13on Complement System, pp. 163-167.

Sample MCQ #8

The difference between Gram positive and gram negative bacterium is that the latter contains:

ATeichoic acid
BMuramic acid
CN-acetyl neuraminic acid
DAromatic amino acids
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Aromatic amino acids

Each peptidoglycan layer is mucopeptide (murein) chain composed of alternate subunits of N-acetyl muramic acid (NAM) and N-acetyl glucosamine (NAG) molecules. It is present in both Gram positive and negative bacteria. Teichoic acid are polymers of ribitol or glycerol joined by phosphate groups. They are present in Gram positive bacteria. Cell wall teichoic acid: Covalently linked to NAM of cell wall. Lipoteichoic acid: attached to lipids of cell membrane Aromatic and sulphur containing aminoacids are present in Gram negative bacteria.
