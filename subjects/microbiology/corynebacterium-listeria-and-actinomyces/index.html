Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is not a step in Pathogenesis of listeriosis?

AIncrease in cAMP
BIntracellular survival
CHost cell actin polymerization
DDirect cell to cell spread
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Increase in cAMP

Pathogenesis of listeriosis: Intracellular survival : After entry into the intestinal epithelium, it survives inside the host cell, mainly due to lysis of phagosome by forming pores (mediated by listeriolysin O) Then it causes host cell actin polymerization , which helps the bacterium to reach near the cell membrane. Finally, it migrates to the adjacent epithelial cells/ macrophages by direct cell-to-cell spread, mediated by listeriopods . Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 698.

Sample MCQ #2

Periodontal disease form of actinomycosis is caused mainly by:

AA. israelii
BA. naeslundii
CA. odontolyticus
DBoth B and C
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Both B and C

Dental caries and periodontal diseases: Mainly caused by naeslundii and A. odontolyticus. Oral-cervicofacial actinomycosis: This is the most common form, usually presented as a painless, slow growing, hard mass with cutaneous fistulas, a condition commonly known as lumpy jaw. Thoracic actinomycosis may present as cavitary lesions and pleural effusion. Abdominal form: It occurs due to spillage of intestinal flora secondary to bowel surgery or other conditions of bowel such as appendicitis. Pelvic form: It occurs following intrauterine contraceptive devices (IUCDs) insertion. Brain abscesses. Bone destruction and soft tissue infections. Disseminated form: It may occur due to hematogenous spread. Lungs and liver are the common sites; where multiple nodules are formed. Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 543.

Less than 48 hrs duration Pharyngeal/laryngeal - 20000 to 40000 units. Nasopharyngeal - 40000 to 60000 units More than 72 hrs duration 80000 to 120000 units. So all the options given are not true. Key Takeaways: Diphtheria Antitoxin (DAT) neutralizes circulating toxin Given along with antibiotics (penicillin/erythromycin) Dose depends on severity and duration of symptoms
