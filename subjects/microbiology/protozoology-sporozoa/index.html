Sample MCQ #3

You are performing the ZN staining for your microbiology practicals. You notice that the decolourizer is 5% sulphuric acid. Which of the following will stain acid fast positive with this concentration of sulphuric acid? i. M.leprae ii. MAC iii. Legionella iv. M.tuberculosis v. Oocyst of cryptosporidium vi. Oocyst of tenia vii. Actinomyces

Ai, v
Bi, v, vi
Ci, v, vii
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. i, v

The above question describes the staining method of modified ZN staining , wherein 5% sulphuric acid is used instead of 20%. The former concentration shows positive with M. Leprae, whereas the latter shows acid fastness for M.TB and Atypical mycobacteria. Oocysts of Cyclospora, Isospora and Cryptosporidium are also acid fast with 5% H2SO4 Nocardia, Legionella and Tenia oocysts are acid fast with 0.5%-1% H2SO4. Actinomyces is not acid fast. Reference: Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page 29

Sample MCQ #5

Malaria is not seen in patients with :

AG6PD deficiency
BSickle cell trait
CThalassemia
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Option D - These are inherent, natural, non-immune mechanisms of host resistance against malarial parasites which have evolved as survival advantage under environmental stress induced by Plasmodia. Duffy negative RBC lacks glycoprotein receptors required by merozoite to attach and penetrate. Thus duffy negative blood group people are naturally protected from P.vivax. Reference: Paniker's textbook of Parasitology - 8th Edition, Pg. No. -80

Sample MCQ #6

Schizont of P.falciparum are not found in peripheral blood because of :

ASchizonts are absent in life cycle
BKilled by antibodies
CDevelop only in capillaries of internal organs
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Develop only in capillaries of internal organs

Option C - Merozoites released from liver cells infects RBS&rsquo;s. Inside RBC, they convert into early trophozoite- late trophozoite and Schizont. Late trophozoite, early and mature schizonts are not found in peripheral blood as this stage occurs inside capillaries of internal organs like spleen, liver and kidney. Thus, only trophozoite (ring forms ) and gametocytes are diagnostic forms present in peripheral blood smear. Reference: Paniker's textbook of Parasitology - 8th Edition, Page no : 73

Sample MCQ #7

Young RBCs are preferentially infected by :

AP.falciparum
BP.vivax
CP.malariae
DP.ovale
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. P.vivax

Option B - P.vivax merozoites after entering into RBC convert into early trophozoite(ring form) about 2.5 -3 in diameter which remains in middle of RBC. This early ring form further convert to late trophozoite form having size of 6-7 . In order to accommodate such a large ring, RBC has to enlarge. RBCs infected with P.vivax became pale, enlarged, irregular and became mottled having Schuffner's dots. Reference: Paniker's textbook of Parasitology - 8th Edition, Pg. No. -72

Sample MCQ #8

Which form of malarial parasite is present in the saliva of infective mosquitoes?

ARing form
BSporozoite
CSchizont
DGametocyte
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Sporozoite

Option B - Sporozoite is an infective form developed in the female anopheles mosquitoes after external incubation period. It is formed when introduced inside the human body through mosquito bite. It undergoes asexual development inside liver cells (pre-erythrocytic schizogony). Merozoites released from pre-erythrocytic schizogony infects RBCs and undergoes a developmental cycle of trophozoite-schizont-merozoite. Reference : Paniker's Textbook of Parasitology, 8th Edition, Page Number-67.
