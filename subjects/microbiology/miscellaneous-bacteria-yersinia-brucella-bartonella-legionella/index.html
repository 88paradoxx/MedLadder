Sample MCQ #1

B.quintana most commonly causes:

A100 days fever
B5th day disease
C5 day fever
D6th disease
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 5 day fever

Quintana: It is transmitted by louse. It is the agent of - Trench fever: It occurred as epidemics in the trenches during World War I. It is a mild febrile illness which lasts for five days, subsequent episodes occurring every 5 days (hence also called as 5 days fever). Thereafter, it was found to be silent for decades. However, the disease has re-emerged in USA recently. Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 315

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following amino acids is required for isolation of F.tularensis?

AGlycine
BCysteine
CLysine
DGlutamic acid
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Cysteine

Isolation of F.tularensis is very difficult as it is highly fastidious. It needs Special media, such as: BCG agar (blood cysteine blood agar) CHAB agar (cysteine heart agar supplemented with 9% heated sheep blood). Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 808

Sample MCQ #3

Drug of choice for rabbit fever is:

AGentamicin
BAmpicillin
CDoxycycline
DCiprofloxacin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Gentamicin

Tularemia, also known as &ldquo;rabbit fever,&rdquo; is a disease caused by the bacterium Francisella tularensis. Tularemia is typically found in animals, especially rodents, rabbits, and hares. Gentamicin is considered as the drug of choice,given 5mg/kg for 7-10 days. Doxycycline or ciprofloxacin can be given as alternatives. Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 810

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following subspecies of Francisella tularensis is most virulent for humans?

ATularensis
BHolarctica
CMediasiatica
DNovicida
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Tularensis

Tularensis has four subspecies: tularensis, holarctica, novicida, and mediasiatica. The first three subspecies are found in North America, whereas subspecies mediasiatica is found in central Asia. Subspecies tularensis is the most common and the most virulent among all. Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 808

Sample MCQ #6

A 29-year-old woman who worked as a research assistant in a veterinary microbiology laboratory complained of intermittent fever, anorexia, profuse sweating, malaise, headache, normo-tensive and muscle pain especially of the neck and shoulder, and arthralgia for 3 d. She was one of the 7 laboratory workers who were involved in a research on isolation of B. melitensis from goats for the past 6 months. Which of the following is not the mode of transmission of this organism?

ADirect contact
BFood borne
CVertical transmission
DAir borne
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Vertical transmission

B.melitensis is the most pathogenic species, followed by B. abortus and B. suis. Human infection with other species is extremely rare. Transmission: Brucellosis is zoonotic, transmitted from infected animals to man by various modes: Direct contact: The most common route is direct contact of abraded skin or mucosa with the infected animal tissue, blood, urine, vaginal discharge or placenta Food-borne: By ingestion of unpasteurized milk or dairy products or undercooked meat from infected animals or rarely vegetables or water contaminated with animal excreta Air-borne: By inhalation of dust or aerosols in the infected cowshed or slaughterhouses Person-to-person spread is extremely rare; through breast milk or by tissue transplantation or blood transfusions. Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 316

AMilk ring test
BRose Bengal card test

DWhey agglutination test

Brucellosis is diagnosed in animals by various methods such as: Milk ring test and Rose Bengal card test; detecting antibodies in infected milk. Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 318

Sample MCQ #8

What is the WHO regimen for Brucellosis?

ARifampicin + doxycycline
BGentamicin + doxycycline
CCeftriaxone + doxycycline
DRifampicin + gentamicin + doxycycline
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Rifampicin + doxycycline

Various regimens are recommended for the treatment of brucellosis. Standard regimen in adults: Gentamicin for 7 days plus doxycycline for 6 weeks. Streptomycin can be given alternative to gentamicin. WHO regimen in adults: Rifampicin for 6 weeks plus doxycycline for 6 weeks Relapse or treatment failure occurs in 5&ndash;10% of cases. For CNS involvement: Ceftriaxone is added to the regimen and treatment is prolonged for 3&ndash;6 months. Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 319
