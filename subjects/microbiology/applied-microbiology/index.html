Sample MCQ #1

What is one example of a situation where routine environmental microbiological sampling is indicated?

AOutbreak investigation of a new infection
BAssessment of a new method for equipment sterilization
CMonthly water surveillance in hemodialysis units
DEvaluation of a new hospital construction
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Monthly water surveillance in hemodialysis units

Monthly water surveillance in hemodialysis units: Correct , routine monitoring is critical to ensure safe water quality for dialysis patients. Why Not Others: A. Outbreak investigation: Focuses on targeted sampling, not routine checks. B. Equipment sterilization: Involves evaluative sampling after changes, not regular monitoring. D. New hospital construction: Involves specific sampling rather than ongoing routine checks. Key Takeaways: Routine environmental sampling is indicated for monthly water surveillance in hemodialysis units to ensure safe water quality . Other situations often require targeted or specific sampling rather than routine. Tags : good to know Reference: Guidelines on routine and targeted environmental microbiological sampling.

Sample MCQ #2

Hospital associated infection can be defined as all of the following except:

AThe infection should be incubating at the time of admission
BThe symptoms should appear at least after 48 hours or after admission
CThe patient should be admitted for a reason other than the infection in context
DInfections acquired in hospital, but symptoms present after discharge
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. The infection should be incubating at the time of admission

Healthcare-associated infections (HAIs) can be defined as- The infections acquired in the hospital by a patient admitted for a reason other than the infection in context , The infection should not be present or incubating at the time of admission , and The symptoms should appear at least after 48 hours of admission. This also include: Infections that are acquired in the hospital but symptoms appear after discharge. Occupational infections among staff of the healthcare facility (e.g. needle stick injury transmitted infections). Infection in a neonate that results while passage through the birth canal (in contrast to congenital infections due to transplacental transmission, which are not HAIs). Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 229

Sample MCQ #3

Patients with similar infections requiring contact precautions can be placed together if single isolation rooms aren't available. This is called Cohorting. What is the minimum distance for a spatial separation of two patients in a room?

A2 feet
B3 feet
C4 feet
D5 feet
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 3 feet

For infection control: Single isolation room with a bathroom facility is preferred. If not available, then cohorting is recommended. Cohorting may be carried out in various ways Patients with similar infections requiring contact precautions can be placed together either in the same isolation room, or in the same cubicle or corner of a ward or Spatial separation of minimum of 3 feet distance between the beds with privacy curtains. Other measures for infection control are: Hand Hygiene should be maintained. Health care workers should wear PPE. Single-use patient-dedicated equipment. Patient movement should be limited only to medically-necessary purposes. Disinfection of rooms should be carried out frequently and adequately. Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 236
