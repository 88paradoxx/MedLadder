Sample MCQ #1

Which cleaning agent is recommended for effectively eliminating Clostridium difficile spores from environmental surfaces?

AAlcohol-based disinfectants
BHydrogen peroxide
CSodium hypochlorite (bleach)
DQuaternary ammonium compounds
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Sodium hypochlorite (bleach)

C. Sodium hypochlorite (bleach): This agent is highly effective at killing C. difficile spores, making it the recommended choice for disinfection in environments where C. difficile is present. Why Not Others: A. Alcohol-based disinfectants: These are ineffective against C. difficile spores, as they do not have sporicidal properties. B. Hydrogen peroxide: While it has some antimicrobial activity, it is not as effective as sodium hypochlorite for specifically eliminating C. difficile spores. D. Quaternary ammonium compounds: These compounds lack efficacy against C. difficile spores, making them unsuitable for this purpose. Key Takeaway: Sodium hypochlorite (bleach) is the recommended disinfectant for eliminating C. difficile spores from surfaces, ensuring effective infection control. Tags : good to know Reference: CDC guidelines on disinfection for C. difficile.

Sample MCQ #2

Which serotype of botulinum toxin is known for being the most severe in human disease?

ASerotype A
BSerotype B
CSerotype E
DSerotype G
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Serotype A

Serotype A : This serotype is known for being the most severe and is the one most commonly associated with human botulism. Why Not Others : Serotype B does cause disease but lacks the severity associated with serotype A . Serotype E is less severe and not the primary concern in human botulism cases. Serotype G is not recognized for its severity in human infections, unlike serotype A . Key Takeaway : Serotype A of botulinum toxin is the most severe and is commonly linked to human disease . Tags : neet 2020 Reference: Shastri, A. (2021). Essentials of Medical Microbiology (3rd ed.). Publisher. Chapter-40 Food poisoning pp-397.

Sample MCQ #5

What type of toxins does Clostridium difficile produce?

AEndotoxins
BEnterotoxins and cytotoxins
CNeurotoxins
DHemotoxins
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Enterotoxins and cytotoxins

Pathogenic strains of clostridium difficile produces 2 distinct toxins.Toxin A is an enterotoxin and Toxin B is a cytotoxin.Both are high molecular weight capable of binding to specific receptors on the intestinal mucosal cells. Sample MCQ #6

Which clostridial species is most commonly associated with gas gangrene?

AClostridium novyi
BClostridium histolyticum
CClostridium perfringens
DClostridium sordellii
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Clostridium perfringens

Clostridium novyi : While this species can cause gas gangrene, it is less common compared to C. perfringens. Clostridium histolyticum : This species is rarely implicated in gas gangrene cases. Clostridium perfringens : This is the most common etiological agent of gas gangrene, accounting for about 60% of cases . Clostridium sordellii : Although associated with other infections, it is not the primary cause of gas gangrene. Why Not Others : Clostridium novyi and Clostridium histolyticum are less prevalent causes of gas gangrene. Clostridium sordellii is linked to different types of infections, not gas gangrene. Key Takeaway : Clostridium perfringens is the most common pathogen responsible for gas gangrene . Tags : neet 2020 Reference: Etiological agents of gas gangrene.

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following is the primary mode of transmission for Clostridium difficile?

AAirborne droplets
BContaminated water
CFecal-oral route
DVector-borne transmission
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Fecal-oral route

C. Fecal-oral route: This is the primary mode of transmission, as C. difficile spores can be ingested through contaminated surfaces, hands, or food, leading to infection. Why Not Others: A. Airborne droplets: This is not a primary transmission route for C. difficile; the spores are not spread through the air. B. Contaminated water: While water can harbor pathogens, it is not the main transmission pathway for C. difficile. D. Vector-borne transmission: This mode is not relevant to C. difficile, as it does not rely on vectors like insects for transmission. Key Takeaway: C. difficile is primarily transmitted via the fecal-oral route, underscoring the need for stringent hand hygiene and effective surface disinfection to prevent infection. Tags : fmge 2021 Reference: CDC guidelines on Clostridium difficile infection control.

AClostridium tetani
BBotulism
CCampylobacter Jejuni

✓ Correct Answer: D. Clostridium perfringens
