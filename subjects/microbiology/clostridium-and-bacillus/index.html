Sample MCQ #1

Which cleaning agent is recommended for effectively eliminating Clostridium difficile spores from environmental surfaces?

AAlcohol-based disinfectants
BHydrogen peroxide
CSodium hypochlorite (bleach)
DQuaternary ammonium compounds
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Sodium hypochlorite (bleach)

C. Sodium hypochlorite (bleach): This agent is highly effective at killing C. difficile spores, making it the recommended choice for disinfection in environments where C. difficile is present. Why Not Others: A. Alcohol-based disinfectants: These are ineffective against C. difficile spores, as they do not have sporicidal properties. B. Hydrogen peroxide: While it has some antimicrobial activity, it is not as effective as sodium hypochlorite for specifically eliminating C. difficile spores. D. Quaternary ammonium compounds: These compounds lack efficacy against C. difficile spores, making them unsuitable for this purpose. Key Takeaway: Sodium hypochlorite (bleach) is the recommended disinfectant for eliminating C. difficile spores from surfaces, ensuring effective infection control. Tags : good to know Reference: CDC guidelines on disinfection for C. difficile.

Sample MCQ #2

Which serotype of botulinum toxin is known for being the most severe in human disease?

ASerotype A
BSerotype B
CSerotype E
DSerotype G
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Serotype A

Serotype A : This serotype is known for being the most severe and is the one most commonly associated with human botulism. Why Not Others : Serotype B does cause disease but lacks the severity associated with serotype A . Serotype E is less severe and not the primary concern in human botulism cases. Serotype G is not recognized for its severity in human infections, unlike serotype A . Key Takeaway : Serotype A of botulinum toxin is the most severe and is commonly linked to human disease . Tags : neet 2020 Reference: Shastri, A. (2021). Essentials of Medical Microbiology (3rd ed.). Publisher. Chapter-40 Food poisoning pp-397.

✓ Correct Answer: C. Increased hospital stay
