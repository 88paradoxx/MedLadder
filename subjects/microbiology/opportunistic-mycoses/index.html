Sample MCQ #1

Latex agglutination study of the antigen in CSF helps in the diagnosis of

ACryptococcus
BCandidiasis
CAspergillosis
DHistoplasmosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Cryptococcus

Cryptococcal antigens can be detected in CSF, serum and urine by latex agglutination test. Antigen titre in CSF stays for longer duration and is directly proportional to mortality of the patients. Reference : Jagdish Chander 3/e p306, Harrison 18/e p1651,17/e p1252 Reference : Ananthnarayan and Paniker's textbook of Microbiology 12 th edition pg no. 473

Sample MCQ #5

A 34-year-old HIV positive patient presents with complaints of high-grade fever, frothy expectoration and cough for 2 days. Upon testing for different etiologies with a sample of lung fluid obtained from Broncho-Alveolar Lavage (BAL), the Gomori Methanamine Silver stain showed a CRUSHED PING PONG ball appearance, suggesting Pneumocystis Jirovecii infection. Which of the following morphological form is observed in the sample?

AEgg
BCyst
CTrophozoite
DIt is intracellular
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Cyst

Like protozoa, Pneumocystis exists in cyst and trophozoite forms. The cysts are found in the environment, whereas in human tissues both cysts and trophozoites (containing 4-8 sporozoites) are found. Once inhaled, the cysts are carried to the lungs where they transform into the trophozoite stage. The trophozoites induce an inflammatory response, that leads to recruitment of plasma cells resulting in formation of frothy exudate filling the alveoli. Hence, this condition is also called plasma cell pneumonia. Gomori's methenamine silver (GMS) staining is the method of choice to demonstrate the cysts of P. jirovecii. The cysts resemble black colored crushed ping-pong balls, against the green background. Treatment is with Cotrimoxazole. Reference: Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page 679

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following is positive by India-ink staining on a CSF sample for demonstration of capsule of yeast?

APneumococcus
BMeningococcus
CKlebsiella pneumoniae
DCryptococcus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Cryptococcus

Negative staining: The background stains black whereas unstained bacteria stand out in contrast. It is used for the demonstration of capsules which do not take up simple stains. Example: India ink or nigrosine Capsulated bacteria: Pneumococcus (Streptococcus pneumoniae), Meningococcus (Neisseriae meningitidis), Klebsiella pneumoniae Capsulated fungus yeast: Cryptococcus neoformans Reference: Ananthanarayan and Paniker's Textbook of Microbiology 12th edition, Pg 12

Sample MCQ #8

A 1.5-month-old male infant presents with persistent diarrhea, Pneumocystis carinii pneumonia, and an oral thrush with Candida albicans. HIV PCR is negative. The most likely cause of these findings is:

AReduced B cells.
BX-linked inheritance of HLA genes.
CDefective isotype switching.
DDefective T cell function.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Defective T cell function.

The fungal infection is highly suggestive of a T cell defect. Choices A and C do not of themselves imply a deficiency in T cell function. HLA genes are autosomal, not X-linked. Reference: Ananthanarayan and Paniker's Textbook of Microbiology 10th edition Chapter 19 , Pg 187-190
