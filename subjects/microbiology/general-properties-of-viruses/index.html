Sample MCQ #2

Choose the correct statement with respect to nucleic acid replication in viruses

AAll DNA viruses replicate in Cytoplasm except Poxvirus
BAll DNA viruses replicate in nucleus except Poxvirus
CAll RNA viruses replicate in nucleus except Retrovirus
DAll RNA viruses replicate in cytoplasm except Papillomavirus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. All DNA viruses replicate in nucleus except Poxvirus

In DNA viruses, the DNA replication occurs in the nucleus except in poxviruses, which synthesize DNA in the cytoplasm. In RNA viruses, the RNA replication occurs in the cytoplasm except in retroviruses and orthomyxoviruses, which synthesize RNA in the nucleus. Option D is incorrect because Papilloma virus is not an RNA virus Reference: Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd Edition, Page Number 83

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following families includes non-enveloped RNA viruses that cause gastroenteritis?

AParvoviridae
BReoviridae
CAdenoviridae
DPicornaviridae
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Reoviridae

Reoviridae is a family of non-enveloped RNA viruses that includes rotavirus, a major cause of gastroenteritis. The other options either include DNA viruses or non-gastroenteritis related RNA viruses. Key Takeaway: Identifying Reoviridae as a family of non-enveloped RNA viruses associated with gastroenteritis is essential for understanding and managing outbreaks of this illness, especially in children.
