Sample MCQ #1

Organism most likely responsible for ventilation associated pneumonia is:

AAcinetobacter baumannii
BAcinetobacter calcoaceticus
CAcinetobacter lwoffii
DB. Cepacia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Acinetobacter baumannii

Baumannii causes widespread hospital infections such as: Ventilator associated pneumonia Central line associated bloodstream infection Post-neurosurgical meningitis Catheter- associated UTI Wound and soft tissue infections Infections in burn patients Acinetobacter can be grouped into several genomospecies. A. baumannii is the most pathogenic species. Most other species such as A.calcoaceticus and A. lwoffii are rarely pathogenic to man. Reference: Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page no: 646

ACatalase
BIndole reduction
CUrease
DOxidase

✓ Correct Answer: D. Oxidase

Mallei differs from B. pseudomallei in being non-motile and oxidase negative.
