Sample Hypersensitivity MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A 30-year-old male presents with headache, fatigue, light-headedness, dyspnea, and tachycardia. Laboratory investigations reveal decreased hemoglobin and a positive direct Coomb's test. The patient is presently on an antibiotic for symptoms of upper respiratory infection. These findings suggest which of the following?

AType I, mediated by IgG antibodies
BType II, mediated by IgG antibodies
CType III, mediated by IgG antibodies
DType III, mediated by IgG or IgM antibodies
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Type II, mediated by IgG antibodies

Type II reactions involve antibodies directed against self-cells (erythrocytes) or membranes. Certain drugs react with erythrocytes to form neoantigens. Type I responses are against foreign antigens (e.g., allergens), cause IgE responses, and do not invoke a Coomb's reaction. Type III reactions involve soluble antigen-antibody complexes, and type IV reactions do not involve antibody. Reference: Ananthanarayan and Paniker's Textbook of Microbiology 10th edition Chapter 16, Pg 164-168

Sample MCQ #2

True regarding Histamine release from mast cell granules:

AIt requires the cells to have IgE of only one antigenic specificity per cell
BIt is Ca2+ independent phenomenon
CIt cannot occur in the absence of antigen-specific IgE
DIt occurs after IgE Fc receptor crosslinking
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. It occurs after IgE Fc receptor crosslinking

Basophils and mast cells bind IgE via high affinity Fc receptors, so each cell has IgE of many different specificities on its surface. Ca2+is required Many non-lgE triggers (C3a and C5a) can produce granule release. IgE Fc receptor crosslinking triggers release of granule content from mast cell. Referene: Ananthanarayan and Paniker's Textbook of Microbiology 10th edition Chapter 16, Pg 164-168

Sample MCQ #3

A young student immediately felt difficulty in breathing and tightness in his chest. Hestarted sweating and fainted. His friends found out that a bee had just stung him. The emergency school medical team attended him and within minutes he recovered. Which one of the following drugs was administered by the medical team?

AAntihistamine
BTheophylline
CEpinephrine
DIgE Blocking antibody
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Epinephrine

This patient had a major anaphylactic reaction and his tracheal-bronchial smooth muscle has contracted. Epinephrine is the only treatment in this group to reverse this reaction. It will relax the smooth muscle of the respiratory tree and stimulate the heart to beat stronger to raise the blood pressure. Reference: Ananthanarayan and Paniker's Textbook of Microbiology 10th edition Chapter 16, Pg 164-168
