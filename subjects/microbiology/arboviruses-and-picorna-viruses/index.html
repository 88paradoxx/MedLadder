Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is true for IPV (Salk)?

AProvides herd immunity
BProvides local gut immunity
CCan be given in immunocompromised patient
DLive vaccine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Can be given in immunocompromised patient

Salk (Injectable Polio Vaccine) is prepared by Formalin killed strain. No local & herd immunity Zero chance of VAPP & VDPV Can be given to immunocompromised patient, pregnant and old aged patients Short duration of protection Relatively safer than OPV Sabin (Oral Polio Vaccine) is prepared from live attenuated strain. Provides both local & herd immunity More chances of VAPP & VDPV Cannot be given to immunocompromised patient, pregnant and old aged patients Longer duration of protection Reference: Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, Page Number 711

Sample MCQ #3

Which antigenic type of polio is responsible for Vaccine-associated paralytic poliomyelitis?

AType 1 (Brunhilde and Mahoney strain)
BType 2 (Lansing and MEF-1 strain)
CType 3 (Leon and Saukett strain)
DNone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Type 3 (Leon and Saukett strain)

Vaccine-associated paralytic poliomyelitis (VAPP) is due to an OPV-like isolated strain, which is slightly genetically divergent from parental OPV strain. VAPP is spread among recipient and its close contacts, and it cannot cause an outbreak. Vaccine-derived polioviruses (VDPVs) show high level of genetic divergence from parental OPV strain at VP1 sequences. Most VDPV cases occur due to IPV type 2 strain. VDPVs can be classified into 3 types: Circulating VDPVs Immunodeficiency-associated VDPVs Ambiguous VDPVs Reference: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0264410X98004095
