Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following findings on physical examination is most likely to indicate a tear of the Achilles tendon?

ASqueezing the calf with the person lying face down causes the ankle to flex.
BThe area around the ankle is swollen and painful when touched.
CWhen the person bends the knees while lying face down, the ankle is not flexed.
DWhen the person is standing, there is no gap in the Achilles tendon.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. When the person bends the knees while lying face down, the ankle is not flexed.

When the person bends the knees while lying face down, the ankle is not flexed. This finding on physical examination usually indicates a tear of the Achilles tendon. Choice A: If squeezing the calf with the person lying face down does not cause the ankle the flex, then the person has a complete Achilles tendon tear. Choice B: Swelling and pain in the ankle could indicate any number of diagnoses, not just an Achilles tendon rupture. Choice D: If a gap is felt in the Achilles tendon with the person standing, then the Achilles tendon is torn. Reference: Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Lower Limb, Page number: 623

Sample MCQ #2

Compression of the lateral plantar nerve as it passes between the flexor digitorum brevis and quadratus plantae could result in weakness of any of the following actions EXCEPT:

AAbduction of the great toe
BAdduction of the middle toe
CAbduction of the little toe
DAdduction of the great toe
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Abduction of the great toe

The medial plantar nerve innervates abductor hallucis, so this muscle would be saved if the lateral plantar nerve was disrupted. The muscles responsible for all the rest of the actions listed are innervated by the lateral plantar nerve, or its branch, the deep lateral plantar nerve: Adduction of the middle toe: plantar interosseus muscles (deep branch); Abduction of the little toe: abductor digiti minimi (lateral plantar); Adduction of the great toe: adductor hallucis (deep branch); Abduction of the middle toe: third dorsal interosseus muscle (deep branch). Reference: Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Lower Limb, Page number: 638

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following nerves originates in the lumbosacral plexus?

AAxillary
BSciatic
CPhrenic
DIlioinguinal
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Sciatic

The sciatic nerve originates from both the lumbar and sacral plexus. Specifically, it originates from the fourth and fifth lumbar nerve plexuses and first to the third sacral nerve plexuses. It is the largest nerve in the body formed by nerve roots from the lower spine or lumbosacral plexus. It passes deep in the gluteal region and down the posterior part of the thigh all the way to the heel and sole of the foot. It primarily serves an essential role in the sensory and motor functions of the skin and muscles of the thigh, leg, and foot. Reference: Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Lower Limb, Page number: 557
