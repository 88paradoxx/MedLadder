Sample MCQ #1

When does hyaline cartilages ossify approximately ?

A20 years in males and 22 years in females
B14 years in males and 11 years in females
C13 years in males and 8 years in females
D18 years in males and 16 years in females
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 20 years in males and 22 years in females

The laryngeal framework is composed of cartilages connected by ligaments and membranes. Ossification of laryngeal cartilages usually begins at the age of 20 years in males and in females at the age of 22 years when skeletal growth is complete , and is a degenerative process in hyaline cartilages with increasing age. Ossification may occur in one or more laryngeal cartilages, although it varies in age of onset and extent. Usually starting first in the thyroid cartilage at about 30 years, it is complete by 65 years of age. Ossification in the cricoid cartilage proceeds from behind forward. Reference: Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Head and neck, Page number - 1053

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following stains are used to visualize the elastic fibers present in the elastic cartilage?

AHaematoxylin and eosin stain
BVerhoeff's stain
CGMS Silver stain
DAlcian Blue stain
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Verhoeff's stain

The elastic fibers present in the elastic cartilage can be visualised by Verhoeff's stain. The fibers stain dark purple/black with Verhoeff's stain. The Verhoeff's stain binds to the elastin protein, which is the main constituent of elastic fibers. Reference: Textbook of Netter's Essential Histology, 2nd Edition, Integumentary System, Page number - 243

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following cartilage is referred to as the yellow cartilage?

AHyaline catilage
BElastic cartilage
CFibrous cartilage
DFibroelastic cartilage
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Elastic cartilage

The elastic cartilage is also known as yellow fibrocartilage. The elastic cartilage resembles the hyaline cartilage in that it contains individual or small groups of chondrocytes that are surrounded by type Il collagen fibrils. However, it differs from hyaline cartilage in that, much of the interterritorial matrix consists of fine yellow elastic fibres containing elastin protein. Reference: Textbook of Netter's Essential Histology, 2nd Edition, Integumentary System, Page number - 243

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following statements regarding cartilage is false?

AHyaline cartilage has perichondrium
BFibrocartilage lacks perichondrium
CArticular cartilage has perichondrium
DElastic cartilage has perichondrium
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Articular cartilage has perichondrium

Articular cartilage despite being a type of hyaline cartilage lacks perichondrium. Cartilage is a connective tissue composed of cells (chondrocytes) and fibers (collagen or yellow elastic fibers). These are embedded in a firm, gel-like matrix that is rich in mucopolysaccharides. It is much more elastic than bone. Cartilage does not have blood vessels or lymphatics. Chondrocytes get their nutrition by diffusion. Cartilage has no nerves and is insensitive. Reference: Textbook of B d Chaurasia's handbook of General Anatomy, 4th Edition, Page number - 57

Sample MCQ #8

Which of the following structures do the Sharpey's fibers connect?

APeriosteum to synovial membrane
BJoint capsule to periosteum
CPeriosteum to bone
DEnamel to dentin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Periosteum to bone

Sharpey's fibers are thick collagen fibers that connect the periosteum to the underlying bone. Sharpey's fibers are present in the bone and the teeth. Bone - tether the periosteum to the underlying bone. Teeth - connect the cementum to the periosteum of the alveolar bone (tooth socket). Reference: Textbook of B d Chaurasia's handbook of General Anatomy, 4th Edition, Page number - 57
