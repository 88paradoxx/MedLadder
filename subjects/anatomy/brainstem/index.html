Sample Brainstem MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

The arrangement of the functional columns of cranial nerve nuclei from ventral to dorsal aspect in the brainstem is as follows:

AVisceral Efferent > Visceral Afferent >Somatic Afferent > Somatic Efferent
BVisceral Efferent > Somatic Efferent >Visceral Afferent > Somatic Afferent
CSomatic Afferent > Visceral Afferent >Visceral Efferent > Somatic Efferent
DSomatic Efferent > Visceral Afferent >Visceral Efferent > Somatic Afferent
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Somatic Afferent > Visceral Afferent >Visceral Efferent > Somatic Efferent

The pattern of the arrangement of cranial nerve nuclei from the ventral to dorsal aspect is: Somatic Afferent > Visceral Afferent > Visceral Efferent > Somatic Efferent Reference: Langman's Medical Embryology 14th edition, Page number - 326

Sample MCQ #2

A 60-year-old patient who suffered a stroke is unable to swallow food. On examination, the gag reflex is absent. The motor nucleus of the vagus nerve involved here emerges from which part of the brainstem.

AAnterolateral sulcus
BPosterolateral sulcus
CPontomedullary junction
DLateral to olive
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Posterolateral sulcus

Each nucleus ambiguus innervates the ipsilateral side of the palate and each cerebral hemisphere projects corticonuclear fibers to each nucleus ambiguus, but the predominance of fibers go to the contralateral nucleus. The fibers from the nucleus ambiguus emerge through the postero-lateral sulcus of the brainstem Reference: Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Head and neck, Page number - 311

Sample MCQ #3

Of the following, the item most closely associated with the pons is:

ATelencephalon
BDiencephalon
CMesencephalon
DMetencephalon
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Metencephalon

The metencephalon differentiates into the cerebellum, the coordination center for posture and movement and the pons, which serves as a neural relay center. Reference - Textbook of Langman's, 12th Edition, Central Nervous System Embryology Page number - 287
