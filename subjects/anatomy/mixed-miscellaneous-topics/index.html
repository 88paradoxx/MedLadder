Sample Mixed / Miscellaneous Topics MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Which artery lies in the floor of the anatomical snuffbox?

AUlnar artery
BRadial artery
CBrachial artery
DAnterior interosseous artery
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Radial artery

The radial artery passes through the floor of the anatomical snuffbox over the scaphoid and trapezium. The snuffbox is bounded laterally by abductor pollicis longus and extensor pollicis brevis, and medially by extensor pollicis longus.

Sample MCQ #2

The deep inguinal ring is an opening in which fascial layer?

AExternal oblique aponeurosis
BInternal oblique muscle
CTransversalis fascia
DScarpa fascia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Transversalis fascia

The deep inguinal ring is a defect in the transversalis fascia, located lateral to the inferior epigastric vessels. It transmits the spermatic cord in males and the round ligament in females into the inguinal canal.

Sample MCQ #3

Within the femoral triangle, the arrangement of structures from lateral to medial is?

AArtery, vein, nerve, lymphatics
BNerve, artery, vein, lymphatics
CVein, artery, nerve, lymphatics
DNerve, vein, artery, lymphatics
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Nerve, artery, vein, lymphatics

The mnemonic NAVEL gives the lateral-to-medial order in the femoral triangle: Nerve, Artery, Vein, Empty space (lymphatics). The triangle is bounded by the sartorius laterally, adductor longus medially, and the inguinal ligament above.

Sample MCQ #4

Which muscle is NOT part of the rotator cuff?

ASupraspinatus
BTeres major
CInfraspinatus
DSubscapularis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Teres major

The rotator cuff is formed by SITS: supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor and subscapularis. Teres major is not part of the cuff; it assists latissimus dorsi in arm extension and medial rotation.

Sample MCQ #5

How many flexor tendons pass through the carpal tunnel along with the median nerve?

A6
B8
C9
D12
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 9

Nine flexor tendons pass under the flexor retinaculum with the median nerve: four flexor digitorum superficialis, four flexor digitorum profundus, and one flexor pollicis longus. Compression here causes carpal tunnel syndrome.

Sample MCQ #6

From superficial to deep, the neurovascular order in the popliteal fossa is?

AArtery, vein, nerve
BNerve, vein, artery
CVein, nerve, artery
DNerve, artery, vein
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Nerve, vein, artery

In the popliteal fossa the tibial nerve is most superficial, the popliteal vein lies in the middle, and the popliteal artery is deepest, directly on the popliteus muscle and joint capsule.

Sample MCQ #7

The inferior vena cava passes through the diaphragm at which vertebral level?

AT6
BT8
CT10
DT12
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. T8

The inferior vena cava pierces the central tendon of the diaphragm at T8, accompanied by the right phrenic nerve. The esophagus passes at T10 and the aorta at T12.

Sample MCQ #8

Which nerve passes through the foramen ovale?

AOphthalmic nerve (V1)
BMaxillary nerve (V2)
CMandibular nerve (V3)
DFacial nerve (VII)
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Mandibular nerve (V3)

The foramen ovale transmits the mandibular nerve (V3), the accessory meningeal artery, the lesser petrosal nerve and an emissary vein. The maxillary nerve passes through the foramen rotundum instead.
