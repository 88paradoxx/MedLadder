Sample MCQ #2

What is the correct sequence of stem cell potency based on their ability to differentiate?

APluripotent > Totipotent > Multipotent> Oligopotent > Unipotent
BTotipotent > Pluripotent > Multipotent> Oligopotent > Unipotent
CTotipotent > Pluripotent > Oligopotent > Multipotent > Unipotent
DPluripotent > Totipotent > Oligopotent > Multipotent > Unipotent
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Totipotent > Pluripotent > Multipotent> Oligopotent > Unipotent

The correct order of arrangement of stem cells, based on their potency to differentiate into different cells (highest to lowest) is totipotent > pluripotent > multipotent > oligopotent > unipotent. Reference - Textbook of Langman's, 12th Edition, General Embryology Page number - 32
