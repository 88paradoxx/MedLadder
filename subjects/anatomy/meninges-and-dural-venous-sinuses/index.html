What helps differentiate the source of ACTH-dependent hypercortisolism in patients with inconclusive biochemical testing and imaging, and is considered the gold standard for distinguishing Cushing disease from ectopic ACTH syndrome ?

AOral dexamethasone test
BInferior petrosal sinus sampling
CCavernous sinus sampling
DDural venous sinus sampling

✓ Correct Answer: B. Inferior petrosal sinus sampling

Bilateral inferior petrosal sinus sampling (BIPSS) is a useful test for differential diagnosis of central vs. ectopic adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH)-dependent Cushing's syndrome (CS). Sample MCQ #3

Both the dural sac and the subarachnoid space end at which vertebral level?

AL 4
BL 5
CS 2
DS 1
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. S 2

Although the dural sac and subarachnoid space end at S2, the filum terminale externum (AKA coccygeal ligament) is a continuation of the dura mater that extends below the end of the dural sac to attach to the coccyx. Reference: Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Back, Page number - 104

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following vein is formed by union of the two internal cerebral veins, basal vein of rosenthal and supplies straight sinus as its tributary ?

AMiddle cerebral vein
BGreat cerebral vein of galen
CAnterior cerebral vein
DPosterior communicating artery
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Great cerebral vein of galen

The great cerebral vein (vein of Galen) is a deep cerebral vein formed by the basal veins of Rosenthal, the internal cerebral and some superior cerebellar veins. It is situated in the quadrigeminal cistern, posterior to the brainstem and third ventricle. And it drains into straight sinus. Reference: Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Head and neck, Page number - 281

Sample MCQ #5

Spinal dura ends at the level of:

A2nd sacral vertebra
B5th sacral vertebra
C2nd lumbar vertebra
D5th lumbar vertebra
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 2nd sacral vertebra

Explanation: The spinal dura extends from foramen magnum to the lower border of second sacral vertebra (S2). Similarly arachnoid mater also terminates in S2, whereas pia mater continues further as filum terminale to the coccyx.
