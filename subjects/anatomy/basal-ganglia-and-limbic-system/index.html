Sample MCQ #1

Amygdala body present in which of the following lobe?

AParietal lobe
BInsula lobe
COccipital lobe
DTemporal lobe
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Temporal lobe

Explanation: The amygdaloid body is an almond-shaped mass of grey matter in the temporal lobe, located deep to the uncus and functionally part of the limbic system, consisting of lateral, central, and basal nuclei near the hippocampus.

Sample MCQ #2

Fornix is made of which type of fibre?

AAssociation and commissural
BAssociation and projection
CAssociation
DAssociation, Projection and commissural
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Association, Projection and commissural

Fornix: it is a large bundle of mainly projection fibers. It connects the hippocampus with the mammillary body. It consists of the sole efferent system of hippocampus. Hippocampal commissure of fornix is commissure fibers connecting two hippocampi. Some fibres of fornix pass above the splenium of corpus callosum to end in cingulate gyrus of the same side and constitute the dorsal fornix is made up of association fibres. Thus fornix consists of all three types of white fibres.

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following structures together known as neostriatum?

APutamen and globus pallidus
BPutamen and caudate nucleus
CCaudate nucleus and globus pallidus
DCaudate nucleus, putamen and globus pallidus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Putamen and caudate nucleus

Explanation: The neostriatum (striatum) is formed by the caudate nucleus and putamen, which share similar architecture and receive most input to the basal ganglia. The globus pallidus is not part of the neostriatum, it mainly serves as the output structure.
