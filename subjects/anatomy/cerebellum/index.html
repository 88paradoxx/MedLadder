Sample MCQ #1

All of the following symptoms appears in case of involvement of neocerebellum, EXCEPT:

ATremors
BDysarthria
CDysdiadochokinesis
DAsteriognosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Asteriognosis

Explanation: Neocerebellar lesions cause incoordination of voluntary limb movements, leading to intention tremors, dysmetria, dysdiadochokinesis, asynergia and dysarthria. However, astereognosis is not a cerebellar function, it is a parietal lobe lesion finding (somatosensory cortex), where the patient can not recognise objects by touch. Hence asteriognosis is the only symptom that does not belong to neocerebellar involvement . Astereognosis is associated with lesion in parietal lobe.

Sample MCQ #3

All of the followings are forming longitudinal fibres in pons, EXCEPT:

AThe corticospinal tracts
BThe pyramidal tracts
CThe corticopontine fibres
DThe pontocerebellar fibres
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. The pontocerebellar fibres

Explanation: White matter of the pons consists of longitudinal and transverse fibres. The longitudinal fibres include: The corticospinal tracts The corticonuclear (pyramidal) tracts The corticopontine fibres ending in the pontine nuclei. The transverse fibres are pontocerebellar fibres beginning from the pontine nuclei and going to the opposite half of the cerebellum, through the middle cerebellar peduncle.

Sample MCQ #4

As a researcher studying the circuitry of the cerebellum, which of the following nuclei do you wish to lesion if you are focusing mainly on the flocculo-nodular lobe circuitry of the cerebellum?

AFastigial nucleus
BDentate nucleus
CClarkes nucleus
DSpinal border cells
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Fastigial nucleus

The correct answer is Fastigial nucleus. The flocculo-nodular lobe circuitry of the cerebellum is primarily involved in coordinating eye movements and balance. The Fastigial nucleus is responsible for receiving input from the flocculo-nodular lobe and sending output to the vestibular nuclei, which are important for balance control. Therefore, lesioning the Fastigial nucleus would specifically target the circuitry related to the flocculo-nodular lobe. Reference : Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Head and Neck, Page number - 841

Sample MCQ #5

As a researcher studying the circuitry of the cerebellum, which of the following nuclei do you know to be the major output neuron of the cerebellar cortex?

APurkinje cells
BDentate nucleus
CFastigial nucleus
DClarke's nucleus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Purkinje cells

Purkinje cells are the major output neurons of the cerebellar cortex. They receive inputs from various sources within the cerebellum and send their axons to different targets outside the cerebellum. These cells play a crucial role in motor coordination and learning. They are characterized by their distinctive morphology, with a large, flat dendritic tree that receives inputs from parallel fibers and a single axon that projects to the deep cerebellar nuclei. Purkinje cells are critical for the integration and modulation of motor signals, making them the major output neurons of the cerebellar cortex. Reference : Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Head and Neck, Page number - 841

Sample MCQ #6

A 70-year old man with a history of hypertension went to work and had sudden onset of nausea and vomiting. He was taken to ER, where his exam was notable for slurred speech, dysmetria on finger-to-nose testing on the left side. His gait was normal. Where is the lesion?

ACerebellar vermis
BRight cerebellar hemisphere
CLeft cerebellar hemisphere
DFastigial nucleus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Left cerebellar hemisphere

The patient's symptoms of slurred speech and dysmetria on the left side indicate a lesion in the left cerebellar hemisphere. The cerebellum is responsible for coordinating voluntary movements, including speech and limb movements. Lesions in the cerebellar hemisphere can result in ipsilateral (same side) symptoms, such as the ones seen in this patient. Sample MCQ #7

A 45-year-old man was admitted to the hospital with a cerebellar hemorrhage. The patient exhibited an unsteady "drunk like" gait and the physician noted that he swayed while entering the examination room. Which region of the cerebellum was most likely affected by the bleed?

AHemispheres of the anterior lobe
BHemisphere of the posterior lobe
CInterposed nuclei
DFlocculonodular lobe
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Flocculonodular lobe

The patient's symptoms of an unsteady "drunk like" gait and swaying suggest that the cerebellar hemorrhage affected the flocculonodular lobe. The flocculonodular lobe is responsible for coordinating balance and posture, and damage to this region can result in ataxia and unsteady movements. Reference : Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Head and Neck, Page number - 841
