Sample Face, Nose & Palate, Eye, Ear MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Many facial malformations are believed to be due to:

AA failure of the oral membrane to rupture
BA failure of neural crest cells to migrate into the facial processes
CA failure in growth of the head fold
DAn abnormal persistence of the pharyngeal clefts
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. A failure of neural crest cells to migrate into the facial processes

Proper migration of neural crest cells into the face area is vitally important to facial development. Neural crest tissue will form much of the facial skeleton. Reference - Textbook of Langman's, 12th Edition, Head and Neck Embryology Page number - 275

Sample MCQ #2

Identify the correct associations:

APrimary palate - palatine shelves of maxillary processes
BNeural crest cells - facial processes
CNasolacrimal groove - lateral nasal process and mandibular process
DCleft lip - medial & lateral nasal processes
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Neural crest cells - facial processes

The primary palate arises from the intermaxillary segment. Neural crest cells are vital to the development of the facial swellings. The nasolacrimal groove separates maxillary and lateral nasal swellings. Cleft lip can result from failure of fusion between the maxillary and medial nasal prominences. Reference - Textbook of Langman's, 12th Edition, Head and Neck Embryology Page number - 273

Sample MCQ #3

The membranous labyrinth develops from which structure?

AFirst pharyngeal pouch
BFirst pharyngeal cleft
COtic vesicle
DMeckel's cartilage
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Otic vesicle

The membranous labyrinth of the internal ear is derived from the otic vesicle. The surface ectoderm on either side of the hindbrain thickens to form otic placodes. The otic placodes invaginate to form otic vesicles (auditory vesicles). Reference - Textbook of Langman's, 12th Edition, Head and Neck Embryology Page number - 268
