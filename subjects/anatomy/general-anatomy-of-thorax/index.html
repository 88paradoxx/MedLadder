Sample General Anatomy of Thorax MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

While marking the oblique fissure of the lung on a cadaver. Which of the following landmarks will not touch the line while drawing surface markings for this fissure?

A6th intercostal space
B5th rib
C7th rib
D4th costal cartilage
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 4th costal cartilage

The surface marking of the oblique tissue of the lung will not touch 4th costal cartilage. On either side, the upper and lower lobes of the lung are separated by the oblique fissure. This can be marked by a line that runs anteroinferiorly from the posterior end of the 4th rib (the spinous process of the T3 vertebra), crosses the 5th rib in the midaxillary line, continues inferiorly, crossing either the 6th intercostal space/ 7th rib on the right or the 5th rib/ 5th intercostal space on the left, on both sides at the midclavicular line. The left oblique fissure is located at a higher level and courses more vertically than the right oblique fissure. Reference : Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Thorax, Page number - 124

Sample MCQ #2

The relationship of the components of the neurovascular bundle from below upwards in intercostal space is

ANerve - vein - artery
BNerve - artery - vein
CVein - artery - Nerve
DArtery - nerve - vein
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Nerve - artery - vein

The arrangement of the components of the neurovascular bundle in the intercostal space from below upwards is nerve-artery-vein. The arrangement can be remembered from above downwards with the mnemonic VAN: vein-artery-nerve. Reference: Textbook of Gray's Anatomy for students, 3rd Edition, Thorax, Page number - 162

Sample MCQ #3

Retinitis pigmentosa is due to defect in which gene-

AScotopsin
BRhodopsin
CPigmented epithelium
DNone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Rhodopsin

Ans. B. Rhodopsin Several different rhodopsin gene mutations have been identified in the pedigrees with autosomal dominant retinitis pigmentosa.
