Sample Diencephalon MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Of the following, the item most closely associated with the regulation of visceral and endocrine functions is:

ATelencephalon
BDiencephalon
CMesencephalon
DMetencephalon
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Diencephalon

The hypothalamus of the diencephalon is the control center for visceral and endocrine functions. Reference - Textbook of Langman's, 12th Edition, Central Nervous System Embryology Page number - 287

Sample MCQ #2

In the list below, which embryonic structures are correctly matched with the adult structure and function?

AMesencephalon - colliculi - visual and auditory reflexes
BMetencephalon - medulla - reflex center
CTelencephalon - thalamus - sensory relay and integration
DTelencephalon - cerebellum - motor coordination
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Mesencephalon - colliculi - visual and auditory reflexes

The colliculi serve as relays for auditory and visual reflexes and arise from the mesencephalon. The medulla is derived from myelencephalon. The diencephalon contains the thalamus, a sensory relay station. The cerebellum arises from metencephalon. Reference - Textbook of Langman's, 12th Edition, Central Nervous System Embryology Page number - 287

Sample MCQ #3

A 55 year old male patient underwent an MRI scan for the evaluation of stroke. Incidentally, it was found that the pineal gland was absent. Defect in the development of which of the following structures will likely lead to pineal agenesis?

ATelencephalon
BDiencephalon
CMetencephalon
DMyelencephalon
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Diencephalon

The pineal gland develops from the diencephalon. Hence, defect in its development can cause pineal agenesis. The most caudal part of the roof plate of the diencephalon develops into the pineal gland. It is also known as the pineal body or epiphysis cerebri. The pineal body initially appears as an epithelial thickening in the midline. It begins to evaginate by the 7th week and becomes a solid organ. Reference - Textbook of Langman's, 12th Edition, Central Nervous System Embryology Page number - 297
