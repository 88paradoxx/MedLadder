Sample NSAIDs MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Non steroidal anti inflammatory drugs, such as aspirin act by inhibiting the activity of the enzyme:

ALipoxygenase
BCyclooxygenase
CPhospholipase A2
DLipoprotein lipase
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Cyclooxygenase

Drugs acting as inhibitors COX NSAID Aspirin: Inhibits COX-1 and COX-2. Indomethacin and ibuprofen-inhibit cyclooxyge- nases by competing with arachidonate. Coxibs: Selectively inhibit COX-2 Some coxibs have been withdrawn or suspended from the market due to undesirable side effects and safety issues. Reference: Harper's 32 nd edition, page 241

Sample MCQ #2

A patient on aspirin presented with occult blood in his stool. Inhibition of which of the following by aspirin would best explain this adverse event?

APhospholipase A2
BThromboxane A2
CPLC
DPGF2&alpha;
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Thromboxane A2

Aspirin, even in small doses, irreversibly inhibits TXA2 synthesis by platelets. Thus, it interferes with platelet aggregation and bleeding time is prolonged to nearly twice the normal value. Long-term intake of large doses decreases synthesis of clotting factors in the liver and predisposes to bleeding. This can be prevented by prophylactic vit K therapy. Aspirin particles coming in contact with gastric mucosa promotes local back diffusion of acid. This causes focal necrosis of mucosal cells and capillaries&rarr;acute ulcers, erosive gastritis, congestion and microscopic haemorrhages. The occult blood loss in stools is increased by even a single tablet of aspirin.

Sample MCQ #3

A pregnant woman was prescribed indomethacin. What effect could be expected in the fetus?

APremature closure of ductus arteriosus
BDuctus arteriosus is kept patent
CClosure of fossa ovalis
DHepatotoxicity
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Premature closure of ductus arteriosus

Ductus arteriosus closure During foetal circulation ductus arteriosus is kept patent by local elaboration of PGE2 by COX-2. Unknown mechanisms switch off this synthesis at birth and the ductus closes. When this fails to occur, small doses of indomethacin or aspirin bring about closure in the majority of cases within a few hours by inhibiting PG production. Administration of NSAIDs in late pregnancy has been found to promote premature closure of ductus in some cases. Risk of postpartum haemorrhage is increased. Prescribing of NSAIDs near term should be avoided

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following patient characteristics is the most compelling reason for avoiding celecoxib in the treatment of rheumatoid arthritis?

AAlcohol abuse
BGout
CMyocardial infarction
DPeptic ulcer disease
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Myocardial infarction

Celecoxib is a COX-2-selective inhibitor. Although the COX-2 inhibitors have the advantage over nonselective NSAIDs of reduced gastrointestinal toxicity, clinical data suggest that they are more likely to cause arterial thrombotic events. A history of myocardial infarction would be a compelling reason to avoid a COX-2 inhibitor. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 650

Sample MCQ #5

A 6-year-old girl suffering from the effects of an aspirin overdose. Which of the following syndromes is this patient most likely to exhibit as a result of this drug overdose?

ABone marrow suppression and possibly aplastic anemia
BFever, hepatic dysfunction, and encephalopathy
CHyperthermia, metabolic acidosis, and coma
DRapid, fulminant hepatic failure
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Hyperthermia, metabolic acidosis, and coma

Salicylate intoxication is associated with metabolic acidosis, dehydration, and hyperthermia. If these problems are not corrected, coma and death ensue. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 647

Sample MCQ #6

Read the following statements regarding the use of given drugs and choose the correct option. 1. Morphine is used for the treatment of pulmonary oedema 2. Indomethacin can be used in the treatment of headache 3. Aspirin is used in the treatment of dyspepsia 4. Pethidine can be used in treatment of biliary colic

AStatements 1 and 2 are correct
BStatements 1,2 and 3 are correct
CStatements 1,3 and 4 are correct
DStatements 1,2 and 4 are correct
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Statements 1,2 and 4 are correct

Morphine helps in pulmonary oedema by reducing the preload by redistributing the blood and therefore reducing the pulmonary capillary pressure. Indomethacin like many other NSAIDS inhibit the synthesis of prostaglandins and relieve symptom of headache. Aspirin is a non competitive inhibitor of COX and causes gastric ulcers, does not relieve dyspepsia. Opioids produce a general increase in resting tone of the gut wall and sphincters. These effects arise from stimulation of both &micro;- and &kappa;-receptors on neuronal plexuses in the gut wall. An increase in biliary pressure caused by opioid-induced spasm at the sphincter of Oddi can exacerbate biliary colic. Hence, Pethidine can be used in the treatment of biliary colic.

Sample MCQ #7

The main advantage of ketorolac over aspirin is that ketorolac

ACan be combined more safely with an opioid such as codeine
BDoes not prolong the bleeding time
CAvailable in a parenteral formulation that can be injected intramuscularly or intravenously
DLess likely to cause acute renal failure in patients with some preexisting degree of renal impairment
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Available in a parenteral formulation that can be injected intramuscularly or intravenously

Ketorolac exerts typical NSAID effects. It prolongs the bleeding time and can impair renal function, especially in a patient with preexisting renal disease. Its primary use is as a parenteral agent for pain management, especially for treatment of postoperative patients. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 648

Sample MCQ #8

A patient with chronic kidney disease underwent nephrectomy. Which of the following can be used for post-operative pain for this patient?

ANaproxen
BIndomethacin
CDiclofenac
DParacetamol
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Paracetamol

Paracetamol (acetaminophen) Actions: The central analgesic action of paracetamol is like aspirin, i.e. it raises pain threshold, but has weak peripheral anti-inflammatory components. Analgesic action of aspirin and paracetamol is additive. Paracetamol is a good and promptly acting antipyretic. Paracetamol has negligible anti-inflammatory action. In contrast to aspirin and other NSAIDs, paracetamol does not stimulate respiration or affect acid-base balance; it does not increase cellular metabolism. It has no effect on CVS. Gastric irritation is insignificant-mucosal erosion and bleeding occur rarely only in overdose. It does not affect platelet function or clotting factors and is not uricosuric.
