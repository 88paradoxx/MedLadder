✓ Correct Answer: D. 300 mg OD/week

Ans. is `d' i.e., 300 mg OD/week Drug Dose for chemoprophylaxis Chloroquine 300 mg (3 tablets of 100 mg or 2 tablets of 150 mg) once a week or 100 mg/day for 6 day every week Proguanil 400 mg per day (2 tablets of 200 mg) Mefloquine 250 mg once a week Doxycycline 100 mg per day.

A 13 years old female patient came to OPD with the complaints of fever with chills since 3-4 days. ATissue Schizonticides
BBlood Schizonticides
CGametocides
DNone of the above

✓ Correct Answer: B. Blood Schizonticides

Several classes of antimalarial drugs are available. Drugs that eliminate developing or dormant liver forms are called tissue schizonticides; those that act on erythrocytic parasites are blood schizonticides; and those that kill sexual stages and prevent transmission to mosquitoes are Reference: LANG' Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Sample MCQ #3

Chloroquine is highly effective ______________.

ATissue Schizonticides
BBlood Schizonticides
CGametocides
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Blood Schizonticides

When not limited by resistance, chloroquine is a highly effective blood schizonticide. It is also moderately effective against gametocytes of P vivax, P ovale, and P malariae but not against those of P falciparum. Reference: LANG' Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G.
