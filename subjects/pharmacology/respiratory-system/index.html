Sample MCQ #1

Mechanism of action of leukotriene pathway inhibitors?

AInhibition of 5- lipoxygenase
BLTD4 receptor antagonist
CA and B both
DLTB4 receptor agonist
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. A and B both

Two approaches to interrupting the leukotriene pathway have been pursued: inhibition of 5-lipoxygenase, thereby preventing leukotriene synthesis; and inhibition of the binding of LTD 4 to its receptor on target tissues, thereby preventing its action. Sample MCQ #2

A 10-year-old boy with asthma uses an albuterol and low-dose inhaled mometasone. Which of the following recommendations is most appropriate to prevent complications from this treatment?

ATrimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole
BPantoprazole use prior to meals
CMinimizing use of a spacer
DOral rinsing after medication administration
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Oral rinsing after medication administration

Oral rinsing after inhaled steroid use is the most appropriate recommendation. In bronchial asthma, the use of inhaled glucocorticoids such as mometasone results in more targeted steroid delivery to the lungs with much less systemic absorption. However, some of the drug is deposited in the oral cavity, which predisposes patients to local opportunistic fungal infections, especially candidiasis. Gargling or oral rinsing immediately after inhalation is recommended to reduce the local steroid burden and decrease the risk of infection. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 713

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is FALSE for deriphyline?

ACauses tachycardia
BCauses bradycardia
CCauses cardiac arrhythmias
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Causes bradycardia

The methylxanthines have positive chronotropic and inotropic effects. At low concentrations, these effects appear to result from inhibition of presynaptic adenosine receptors in sympathetic nerves increasing catecholamine release at nerve endings. The higher concentrations (> 10 &mu;mol/L, 2 mg/L) associated with inhibition of phosphodiesterase and increases in cAMP may result in increased influx of calcium. At much higher concentrations (> 100 &mu;mol/L), sequestration of calcium by the sarcoplasmic reticulum is impaired. Reference: LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 20- Drugs used in asthma by Dr. Homer A. Boushy, MD. Page no. 345

Sample MCQ #4

1. Tiotropium does not inhibit M2- receptor- mediated- auto-down-regulation of Acetylcholine release. 2. Tiotropium dissociates most rapidly from M2 Receptors.

ASentence 1 and 2 both are True and 2 is correct reason for 1
BSentence 1 and 2 both are True but 2 is not the correct reason for 1
CSentence 1 is True but Sentence 2 is False
DSentence 1 and 2 both are False.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Sentence 1 and 2 both are True and 2 is correct reason for 1

Ipratropium appears to be at least as effective in patients with COPD that includes a partially reversible component. A longer acting, selective antimuscarinic agent, tiotropium, is approved as a treatment for COPD. It binds to M1, M2, and M3 receptors with equal affinity, but dissociates most rapidly from M 2 receptors, expressed on the efferent nerve ending. This means that tiotropium does not inhibit the M2 -receptor&ndash;mediated auto&ndash;down-regulation of acetylcholine release, and thus confers a degree of receptor selectivity. Reference: LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 20- Drugs used in asthma by Dr. Homer A. Boushy, MD. Page no. 347

Sample MCQ #6

A 15-year-old girl is diagnosed with asthma and treatment is begun with an albuterol + LD inhaled Beclomethasone inhaler as needed. The patient is at greatest risk for which of the following adverse effects?

AAcneiform eruptions
BOropharyngeal candidiasis
CEasy bruisability
DHypoglycemia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Oropharyngeal candidiasis

Oropharyngeal candidiasis (thrush) is the most common adverse effect of treatment with inhaled corticosteroids. It can be prevented by gargling with water after use of the inhaler and/or by adding a spacer to metered dose inhalers. This patient is experiencing symptoms at night and > 2 times per week on her current regimen of PRN albuterol (SABA). The addition of a daily, low-dose inhaled steroid to her regimen is the preferred management for mild asthma. Because inhaled corticosteroids do not take full effect until they have been used for approx. 1 week, a follow-up visit should be scheduled after this time to assess symptom control. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 709

Pediatric dose of Monteleukast is

A10 mg once daily
B10 mg twice daily
C4 mg once daily
D4 mg twice daily
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 4 mg once daily

Some patients appear to have particularly favourable responses, but no clinical features allow identification of &ldquo;responders&rdquo; before a trial of therapy. In the USA, zileuton is approved for use in an oral dosage of 1200 mg of the sustained-release form twice daily; zafirlukast, 20 mg twice daily; and montelukast, 10 mg (for adults) or 4 mg (for children) once daily.
