Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following drug cannot be used in biliary colic ?

ADiclofenac
BDicyclomine
CTramadol
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Tramadol

The opioids contract biliary smooth muscle, which can result in biliary colic. The sphincter of Oddi may constrict, resulting in reflux of biliary and pancreatic secretions and elevated plasma amylase and lipase levels. Reference : LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 31- Opioid Analgesics & Antagonists by Dr. Mark A. Schumacher,PhD,MD, Dr. Alan I. Basbaum,PhD,& Dr. Walter L. Way,MD. Page no. 552

Sample MCQ #2

Opioid antitussive agents are _________. 1.Codeine 2.Levopropoxyphen 3.Dextromethorphan 4.Pentazocine 5.Noscapine 6.Tramadol

A1,2,3,4
B1,2,3,6
C1,2,3,5
D2,3,5,6
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 1,2,3,5

The opioid derivatives most commonly used as antitussives are dextromethorphan, codeine, levopropoxyphene, and noscapine (levopropoxyphene and noscapine are not available in the USA). They should be used with caution in patients taking monoamine oxidase inhibitors (see Table 31&ndash;5 ). Antitussive preparations usually also contain expectorants to thin and liquefy respiratory secretions. Reference: LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 31- Opioid Analgesics & Antagonists by Dr. Mark A. Schumacher,PhD,MD, Dr. Alan I. Basbaum,PhD,& Dr. Walter L. Way,MD. Page no. 559

Sample MCQ #3

Opioid antitussive agents are banned by FDA for children of age under __.

A6 months
B5 years
C6 years
D12 years
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 6 years

Importantly, due to increasing reports of death in young children taking dextromethorphan in formulations of over-the-counter &ldquo;cold/cough&rdquo; medications, its use in children less than 6 years of age has been banned by the FDA. Moreover, because of variations in the metabolism of codeine, its use for any purpose in young children is being reconsidered. Reference: LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 31- Opioid Analgesics & Antagonists by Dr. Mark A. Schumacher,PhD,MD, Dr. Alan I. Basbaum,PhD, PhD,& Dr. Walter L. Way,MD. Page no. 559
