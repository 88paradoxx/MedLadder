Sample Anti-diuretics MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A 45-year-old woman with lung cancer has abnormally low serum osmolality and hyponatremia. A drug that increases the formation of dilute urine and is used to treat SIADH is

AAmiloride
BDesmopressin
CMannitol
DTolvaptan
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Tolvaptan

Retention of water with hyponatremia and inability to form dilute urine in the fully hydrated condition is characteristic of SIADH. Antagonists of ADH (Tolpvaptan) are needed to treat this condition. Reference : Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 268

Sample MCQ #4

Nocturnal enuresis best t/t is

APositive reinforcement
BPunishment
CBed alarm
DDesmopressin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Positive reinforcement

Answer- A. Positive reinforcement Consistent dry bed training with positive reinforcement has a success rate of 85% and bed and pad alarm systems have a success rate of approximately 75% with relapse rate that are lower than those with pharmacotherapy.

Sample MCQ #5

5 year old child bed wetting Rx of choice ?

ANo treatment
BImipramin
CDesmopressin
DMotivational therapy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. No treatment

Ans. is 'a' i.e., No treatment o No treatment is given to children below 6 years of age because of high spontaneous cure rate. o After 6 years treatment include. t) Behavioral therapy : This is the treatment of choice. it) Pharmacological treatment : It is used when non-pharmacological (behavioral) therapy fails. Desmopressin is the drug of choice. Other drugs used are impramine and oxybutinin.
