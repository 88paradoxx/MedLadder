Sample MCQ #1

Tocilizumab is a newer monoclonal antibody for treatment of rheumatoid arthritis. It acts against:

AIL-3
BIL-6
CIL-12
DIL-2
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. IL-6

Tocilizumab is a monoclonal antibody that selectively binds to the interleukin-6 receptor and prevents IL-6 from binding to its receptor (IL-6R) on the liver, lung, and synovial fibroblasts Reference: Tocilizumab - StatPearls - NCBI Bookshelf (nih.gov)

Sample MCQ #2

A 50 years old male patient came to emergency department with complaints of difficulty in breathing, bilateral lower limb oedema, chest pain. Sweating. Patient was on treatment for asthma but he did not take medications for 3,4 months. His SpO2 was 97% on air. On Auscultation, Third heart sound was heard and bilateral coarse crepitus was heard over lung fields. ECG was s/o CCF and Chest X Ray was suggestive of pulmonary oedema. Intravenous morphine is started for pain. Mechanism of action of morphine in pulmonary oedema is ________________.

AReduced Anxiety
BReduced cardiac preload
CReduced cardiac afterload
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

The relief produced by intravenous morphine in dyspnea from pulmonary edema associated with left ventricular heart failure is remarkable. Proposed mechanisms include reduced anxiety ( perception of shortness of breath) and reduced cardiac preload (reduced venous tone) and afterload (decreased peripheral resistance). However, if respiratory depression is a problem, furosemide may be preferred for the treatment of pulmonary edema. On the other hand, morphine can be particularly useful when treating painful myocardial ischemia with pulmonary edema. Reference: LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 31- Opioid Analgesics & Antagonists by Dr. Mark A. Schumacher,PhD,MD, Dr. Alan I. Basbaum,PhD,& Dr. Walter L. Way,MD. Page no. 553

Sample MCQ #3

A 45 years old patient came to emergency department with the complaints of severe chest pain, sweating, palpitation and pain radiating to back. His pulse rate was 130/minute and BP was 140/90 mm Hg. His ECG was s/o Anterolateral wall MI. All opioid analgesic can be used in this patient except ___________.

AMorphine
BTramadol
CMeperidine
DFentanyl
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Meperidine

Cardiovascular system- Most opioids have no significant direct effects on the heart and, other than bradycardia, no major effects on cardiac rhythm. Meperidine is an exception to this generalization because its antimuscarinic action can result in tachycardia. Anterolateral wall MI leads to Tachycardia and Hypertension. Most of the opioids causes but Meperidine causes tachycardia so it cannot be used. Reference : LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J.
