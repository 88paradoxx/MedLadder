Sample MCQ #1

All of the following drugs bind to microtubules and thus interfere with their assembly or disassembly, except:

ACiprofloxacin
BColchicine
CVinblastine
DGriseofulvin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Ciprofloxacin

Option A: Ciprofloxacin is fluoroquinolone class of antibiotic, which inhibits DNA replication by inhibiting bacterial DNA topoisomerase and DNA gyrase. Certain drugs bind to microtubules and thus interfere with their assembly or disassembly. These include colchicine, Vinblastine, Griseofulvin & paclitaxel. Option B: colchicine which is used for treatment of acute gouty arthritis Option C: Vinblastine is a vinca alkaloid used for treating certain types of cancer Option D: Griseofulvin is an antifungal agent Paclitaxel (Taxol) is effective against ovarian cancer. Reference: Harper's illustrated biochemistry 32nd edition CH 51 PN 625

Sample MCQ #2

Which drug is commonly used to treat cancers by inhibiting DNA replication through interference with topoisomerase II?

AEtoposide
BVincristine
CDactinomycin
DGemcitabine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Etoposide

Explanation: Etoposide is a chemotherapy drug that targets topoisomerase II in eukaryotic cells. Etoposide stabilizes the topoisomerase II-DNA complex after it has introduced a double-strand break, preventing the enzyme from re-ligating the DNA. This results in DNA breaks, which interfere with DNA replication and lead to cell death. Etoposide is widely used in the treatment of various cancers, such as lung cancer, testicular cancer, and lymphomas, making this the correct answer.
