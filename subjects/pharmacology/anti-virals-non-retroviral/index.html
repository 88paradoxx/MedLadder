Sample MCQ #1

A patient came to OPD with the complaints of generalized body oedema, difficulty in breathing and anuria since 3-4 days. Laboratory investigation and USG was performed. USG was s/o Chronic Renal Failure. What will be the half-life acyclovir in this pt?

A2.5-3 hrs
B10 hrs
C20 hrs
D25 hrs
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 20 hrs

Acyclovir is cleared primarily by glomerular filtration and tubular secretion. The half-life is 2.5&ndash;3 hours in patients with normal renal function and 20 hours in patients with anuria. Reference : LANG' Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 49- Antiviral Agents by Dr. Sharon Safrin,MD. Page no. 862

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following agent is useful against HBV, HIV and Herpesvirus?

ATenofovir
BAdefovir
CA&B both
DLamivudine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Adefovir

Although initially and abortively developed for treatment of HIV infection, adefovir dipivoxil gained approval, at lower and less toxic doses, for treatment of HBV infection. Adefovir dipivoxil is the diester prodrug of adefovir, an acyclic phosphonated adenine nucleotide analog . It is phosphorylated by cellular kinases to the active diphosphate metabolite and then competitively inhibits HBV DNA polymerase and causes chain termination after incorporation into viral DNA. Adefovir is active in vitro against a wide range of DNA and RNA viruses, including HBV, HIV and herpesviruses. Reference : LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 49- Antiviral Agents by Dr. Sharon Safrin,MD. Page no. 884

Sample MCQ #3

Drugs least effective in severe covid-19 infection?

ALMWH
BFavipiravir
CRemdesivir
DDexamethasone

✓ Correct Answer: B. Favipiravir

Favipiravir inhibits SARS-CoV-2 viral replication in vitro with evidence of clinical benefit in open label trials.

Sample MCQ #4

A 34 years old male patient came to OPD with the complaint of bilateral genital vesicles, fever and itching. He is a known case of HIV and on ART since last 4 years. So, he is diagnosed as genital HSV infection in HIV. There are three nucleoside analogues are licensed for treatment of HSV and VZV. Which of the following are three agents?

AAcyclovir, penciclovir, famciclovir
BAcyclovir, valacyclovir, famciclovir
CValacyclovir, penciclovir, famciclovir
DValacyclovir, famciclovir, lamivudine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Acyclovir, valacyclovir, famciclovir

Three oral nucleoside analogues are licensed for the treatment of HSV and VZV infections: acyclovir, valacyclovir, and famciclovir. They have similar mechanisms of action and comparable indications for clinical use; all are well tolerated. Acyclovir has been the most extensively studied It was licensed first and is the only one of the three that is available for intravenous use in the United States. Comparative trials have demonstrated similar efficacies of these three agents for the treatment of HSV but modest superiority of famciclovir and valacyclovir for the treatment of herpes zoster infections. Reference: LANG' Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 49- Antiviral Agents by Dr. Sharon Safrin, MD. Page no. 862

Sample MCQ #5

A 35 years old female patient of recurrent herpes labialis came to OPD for 5th episode of herpes labialis. She was prescribed acyclovir topical ointment 5 times daily for 4 days. Acyclovir requires ________ phosphorylation steps for activation.

AOne
BTwo
CThree
DFour
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Three

Acyclovir ( Figure 49&ndash;2 ) is an acyclic guanosine derivative with clinical activity against HSV-1, HSV-2, and VZV, but it is approximately 10 times more potent against HSV-1 and HSV-2 than against VZV. In vitro activity against Epstein-Barr virus (EBV), cytomegalovirus (CMV), and human herpesvirus-6 (HHV-6) is present but weaker. Acyclovir requires three phosphorylation steps for activation. Reference: LANG' Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 49- Antiviral Agents by Dr. Sharon Safrin, MD. Page no. 862

Sample MCQ #6

1. Acyclovir is selectively activated in infected cells 2. It requires viral kinase for initial phosphorylation

ASentence 1 and 2 both are True and 2 is correct reason for 1
BSentence 1 and 2 both are True but 2 is not the correct reason for 1
CSentence 1 is True but Sentence 2 is False
DSentence 1 and 2 both are False.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Sentence 1 and 2 both are True and 2 is correct reason for 1

Acyclovir requires three phosphorylation steps for activation. It is converted first to the monophosphate derivative by the virus specified thymidine kinase and then to the di- and triphosphate compounds by host cell enzymes (Figure 49-3). Because it requires the viral kinase for initial phosphorylation, acyclovir is selectively activated and the active metabolite accumulates only in infected cells. Acyclovir triphosphate inhibits viral DNA synthesis by two mechanisms: competition with deoxyGTP for the viral DNA polymerase, resulting in binding to the DNA template as an irreversible complex. Reference : LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 49- Antiviral Agents by Dr. Sharon Safrin,MD. Page no. 862

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following drug achieves serum levels orally that is approximately those achieved by intravenous acyclovir?

AValacyclovir
BFamciclovir
CFoscarnet
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Valacyclovir

Valacyclovir is the L-valyl ester of acyclovir.It is rapidly converted to acyclovir after oral administration via first pass enzymatic hydrolysis in the liver and intestine, resulting in serum levels that are three to five times greater than those achieved with oral acyclovir and approximate those achieved with intravenous acyclovir. Oral bioavailability is 54&ndash;70%, and cerebrospinal fluid levels are about 50% of those in serum. Elimination half-life is 2.5&ndash;3.3 hours. Reference: LANG' Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 49- Antiviral Agents by Dr. Sharon Safrin, MD. Page no. 864

Sample MCQ #8

AIDS patients who are treated with valacyclovir(8g/d) chronically, had an increased incidence of _____________.

AIdiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura
BThrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura
CColon carcinoma
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura

Valacyclovir is generally well tolerated, although nausea, headache, vomiting, or rash occasionally occur. At high doses, confusion, hallucinations, and seizures have been reported. AIDS patients who received high-dosage valacyclovir chronically (ie, 8 g/d) had an increased incidence of gastrointestinal intolerance as well as thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura and hemolytic-uremic syndrome; this dose has also been associated with confusion and hallucinations in transplant patients. In a recent study, there was no evidence of increased birth defects in 181 infants who were exposed to valacyclovir during the first trimester. Reference: LANG' Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 49- Antiviral Agents by Dr. Sharon Safrin, MD. Page no. 864
