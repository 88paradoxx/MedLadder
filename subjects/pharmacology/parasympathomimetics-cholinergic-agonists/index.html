Sample MCQ #1

A 28-year-old woman presents with recent onset of weakness of her hands, diplopia, and difficulty swallowing. She has been treated for myasthenia gravis for several years. Which of the following is the best drug for distinguishing between myasthenic crisis and cholinergic crisis ?

AAtropine
BEdrophonium
CPhysostigmine
DPyridostigmine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Edrophonium

Any of the cholinesterase inhibitors (choices B, C, or D) would effectively correct myasthenic crisis. However, because cholinergic crisis (if that is what is causing the symptoms) would be worsened by a cholinomimetic, we choose the shortest-acting cholinesterase inhibitor, edrophonium. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 113

Sample MCQ #2

A 45-year-old farmer is admitted for Parathion poisoning. Which one of the following is its characteristics?

AIf treated early, its toxicity may be partly reversed by pralidoxime
BIt is less toxic to humans than malathion
CIt is more persistent in the environment than DDT
DIt is poorly absorbed through skin and lungs
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. If treated early, its toxicity may be partly reversed by pralidoxime

The "-thion" organophosphates (those containing the P=S bond) are activated, not inactivated, by conversion to "-oxon" (P=O) derivatives. They are less stable than halogenated hydrocarbon insecticides of the DDT type; therefore, they are less persistent in the environment. Parathion is more toxic than malathion. It is very lipid-soluble and rapidly absorbed through the lungs and skin. Pralidoxime has very high affinity for the phosphorus atom and is a chemical antagonist of organophosphates. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 115

Sample MCQ #3

A 30-year-old woman complains of severe bloating after undergoing abdominal surgery. She also finds it difficult to urinate. Mild cholinomimetic stimulation without vasodilation is preferred for treatment. Which agent should be preferred ?

ABethanechol
BDistigmine
CCevimeline
DCarbachol
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Distigmine

Bethanechol or distigmine is often effective in relieving post-op ileus. Neostigmine and bethanechol in moderate doses have significantly different effects on vascular endothelium. Bethanechol directly acts on muscarinic receptor of vascular endothelium and cause vasodilation, while Distigmine, Indirectly acting AchE inhibitor, does not cause vasodilation. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 132
