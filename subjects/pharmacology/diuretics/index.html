Sample MCQ #1

A mild hypertensive patient presented with chronic renal insufficiency and pedal edema. On investigations, serum creatinine was found to be 1.9 mg/dL with GFR <40 ml/min. Which of the following drugs should be used for management of hypertension in this patient?

APrazosin
BAliskiren
CMetolazone
DMetoprolol
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Metolazone

Metolazone In common with loop diuretics it is able to evoke a clinically useful response even in severe renal failure (g.f.r. ~15 ml/min), and has marked additive action when combined with furosemide. An additional proximal tubular action has been demonstrated; PO4 reabsorption that occurs in PT, is inhibited. It is excretedunchanged in urine. Metolazone has been usedmainly for edema and occasionally for hypertension

Sample MCQ #2

A 75-year-old man with COPD and chronic heart failure presents to you for follow-up. The patient takes multiple medications for these problems. Which of the following strategies may prove to have additional benefit in the treatment of this patient?

AExercise program involving running
BUse of calcium channel blockers at high dose
CStopping use of NSAIDs
DUse of beta-blockers at high doses
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Stopping use of NSAIDs

Chronic HF is typically managed by A reduction in physical activity Low dietary intake of sodium ( Treatment of comorbid conditions Judicious use of diuretics, inhibitors of the renin-angiotensin system, and inotropic agents. Drugs that may precipitate or exacerbate HF, such as NSAIDs drugs, alcohol, calcium channel blockers, high-dose beta-blockers, and some antiarrhythmic drugs, should be avoided if possible. Patients with HF complain of dyspnea on exertion, orthopnea, paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea, fatigue, and dependent edema. Vigorous exercise in this deconditioned patient may worsen heart function. Use of calcium channel blockers at high doses can worsen heart failure. Use of beta-blockers at high doses can worsen heart failure. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 221

Sample MCQ #3

A 60-year-old patient complains of paresthesias and occasional nausea associated with one of the drugs she is taking. She is found to have hyperchloremic metabolic acidosis. She is probably taking

AAcetazolamide for glaucoma
BAmiloride for edema associated with aldosteronism
CFurosemide for severe hypertension and heart failure
DHydrochlorothiazide for hypertension
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Acetazolamide for glaucoma

Paresthesias and gastrointestinal distress are common adverse effects of acetazolamide, especially when it is taken chronically, as in glaucoma. The observation that the patient has metabolic acidosis also suggests the use of a carbonic anhydrase inhibitor like acetazolamide. Reference : Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 262
