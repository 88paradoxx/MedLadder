Sample MCQ #1

A patient who is taking verapamil for hypertension and angina has become constipated. Which of the following drugs is an osmotic laxative that could be used to treat the patient's constipation?

AAluminum hydroxide
BDiphenoxylate
CMagnesium hydroxide
DMetoclopramide
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Magnesium hydroxide

A laxative that mildly stimulates the gut would be most suitable in a patient taking a smooth muscle relaxant drug such as verapamil. By holding water in the intestine, magnesium hydroxide provides additional bulk and stimulates increased contractions . A helpful mnemonic is magnesium "magnifies" stool, aluminum halts the stool. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 1091

Sample MCQ #2

A 38-year-old woman comes with motion sickness. She is planning a vacation cruise and has previously experienced severe nausea and vomiting while sailing. After the appropriate drug therapy is recommended, this patient should be counselled regarding which of the following side effects?

ACough
BDiarrhoea
CDry mouth
DNasal congestion
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Dry mouth

Perception of motion and orientation is determined by input from the vestibular, visual, and somatosensory systems. These 3 systems are usually congruent, but conflicting input can lead to motion sickness, characterized by nausea, dizziness, and autonomic symptoms (eg, diaphoresis). Integration of these sensory pathways occurs in the vestibular nuclei via muscarinic M1 and histaminic H1 neurotransmission. As such, antimuscarinic agents (eg, scopolamine) and first-generation antihistamines (eg, meclizine, dimenhydrinate) are most effective for management of motion sickness. Anticholinergic side effects are common with these medications and may include blurry vision, dry mouth, urinary retention, and constipation. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 1108

Sample MCQ #3

Which drug stimulates chloride secretion into the gut lumen and is used for irritable bowel syndrome?

ABalsalazide
BOmeprazole
CLinaclotide
DPancrelipase
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Linaclotide

Linaclotide is approved for the treatment of chronic constipation and IBS with predominant constipation. Linaclotide activates guanylyl cyclase-C on the luminal intestinal epithelial surface, which leads to activation of the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator (CFTR) leading to increased chloride-rich secretion and acceleration of intestinal transit. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 1103

Sample MCQ #4

A patient presented to hospital with three bouts of vomiting and treated with an antiemetic drug. Vomiting subsided but after some time, he developed abnormal movements. Which of the following drugs is used for treatment of these motor symptoms?

ABenzhexol
BCyproheptadine
CL-dopa
DHyoscine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Benzhexol

Central anticholinergics These are drugs having a higher central: peripheral anticholinergic action ratio than atropine, but the pharmacological profile is similar. The central anticholinergics act by reducing the unbalanced cholinergic activity in the striatum of parkinsonian patients. These drugs produce 10-25% improvement in parkinsonian symptoms lasting 4-8 hours after a single dose. Generally, tremor is benefited more than rigidity; hypokinesia is improved the least. Sialorrhoea is controlled by their peripheral action. Anticholinergics are the only drugs effective in drug (phenothiazines, other D2 antagonists) induced parkinsonism. The side effect profile is similar to atropine. Impairment of memory and cognition, organic confusional states and blurred vision are more common in the elderly. Urinary retention is possible in elderly males.

Sample MCQ #5

A 32-year-old woman has irritable bowel syndrome with diarrhoea that is not responsive to conventional therapies. Despite the small risk of severe constipation and ischemic colitis, the patient decides to begin therapy with alosetron. Alosetron has which of the following receptor actions?

A5-HT3 receptor antagonist
B5-HT4 receptor agonist
CD2 receptor antagonist
DNK1 receptor antagonist
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 5-HT3 receptor antagonist

Serotonin plays a major regulatory role in the enteric nervous system, and the potent 5-HT3 receptor antagonist alosetron has shown efficacy in treating women with IBS that is accompanied by diarrhoea. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 1093

Sample MCQ #6

A 55-year-old woman with Type 1 Diabetes complains of severe bloating and abdominal distress, especially after meals. Evaluation is consistent with diabetic gastroparesis. Which of the following is a prokinetic drug that could be used in this situation?

AAlosetron
BCimetidine
CLoperamide
DMetoclopramide
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Metoclopramide

Of the drugs listed, only metoclopramide is considered a prokinetic agent (i.e., one that increases propulsive motility in the gut) . Alosetron is a 5HT3 receptor blocker, cimetidine is a H2 receptor blocker, loperamide is an opioid, and sucralfate forms a protective layer over an ulcer. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, 14th Edition, Page number- 1099.

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following is an antagonist at NK1 receptors and is used to prevent or reduce chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting?

AAprepitant
BBosentan
CBradykinin
DOndansetron
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Aprepitant

Aprepitant and ondansetron, both are used to treat chemotherapy induced nausea and vomiting. Aprepitant acts by inhibition of NK1 receptors in the solitary nucleus. Central anti-emetic effect B : Bosentan, competitive antagonist of endothelin-1 at the endothelin receptor that decreases pulmonary vascular resistance. Commonly used for treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension. C: Bradykinin, not a drug but mediator, which releases from ACEi. D : Ondansetron is an antagonist at 5-HT3 receptors. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 290

Sample MCQ #8

A 55-year-old male patient, known case of carcinoma of the pyriform fossa with invasion of thyroid cartilage, was decided to be given chemotherapy. Cisplatin and 5-fluorouracil were started in this patient. What is the mechanism of action of cisplatin?

AForms DNA cross links
BStabilizes microtubules to block M phase
CInhibits DHFR during S phase
DInhibits during S phase.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Forms DNA cross links

Explanation: With better understanding of the impact of multimodality treatment, chemotherapy is used as both definitive and adjuvant regimens. The mechanism of action of the various drugs used in head and neck SCC are as follows. Platinum Compound cisplatin and Carboplatin - form DNA cross links (Option A) Taxanes - paclitaxel and docetaxel - stabilise microtubules to block M phase (Option B) Antifolates - Methotrexate - inhibit DHFR during S phase (Option C) Fluoropyrimidines - 5 Fluorouracil - inhibit DNA synthesis during S phase (Option D)
