Sample MCQ #1

Pyrantel pamoate is not effective against______________.

APinworm, Ascaris
BHookworm
CTrichuriasis, strongyloidiasis
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Trichuriasis, strongyloidiasis

Pyrantel pamoate is a broad-spectrum antihelminthic highly effective for the treatment of pinworm, ascaris, and Trichostrongylus orientalis It is moderately effective against both species of hookworm. It is not effective in trichuriasis or strongyloidiasis. Oxantel pamoate, an analog of pyrantel not available in the USA, has been used successfully in the treatment of trichuriasis; the two drugs have been combined for their broad-spectrum antihelminthic activity Reference: LANG' Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 53-Clinical Pharmacology of the Antihelminthic Drugs by Dr. Philip J. Rosenthal, MD. Page no. 945

Sample MCQ #2

What is the maximum dose of pyrantel that can be given in one day?

A0.5 gm
B1 gm
C2 gm
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 1 gm

The standard dose is 11 mg (base)/kg (maximum, 1 g), given orally once with or without food. For pinworm, the dose is repeated in 2 weeks, and cure rates are greater than 95%. The drug is available in the USA without prescription for this indication. For ascariasis, a single dose yields cure rates of 85&ndash;100%. Treatment should be repeated if eggs are found 2 weeks after treatment. For hookworm infections, a single dose is effective against light infections; but for heavy infections, especially with Necator americanus, a 3-day course is necessary to reach 90% cure rates. A course of treatment can be repeated in 2 weeks. Reference: LANG' Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 53-Clinical Pharmacology of the Antihelminthic Drugs by Dr. Philip J. Rosenthal, MD. Page no. 945

Sample MCQ #3

A patient presented with an episode of seizure and CT scan revealed presence of calcified cysts in brain with no evidence of Hydrocephalus. A diagnosis of Neurocysticercosis was made. Which of the following statements regarding treatment of this condition is correct?

AOnly anti parasitic drugs should be given
BAnti parasitic drugs should be given and after the completion of course, Steroids should be given
CSteroids should be started first followed by anti parasitic drugs
DOnly anti epileptic drugs should be given
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Steroids should be started first followed by anti parasitic drugs

Steroids to be given first to prevent any hyperinflammatory syndrome due to dying parasites from Albendazole treatment. Neurocysticercosis: When albendazole/praziquantel is used to kill cysticerci lodged in the brain, prednisolone 40 mg/day or equivalent is given for 2-4 weeks to suppress the reaction to the dying larvae. All other options are not the correct ways to treat the patient.
