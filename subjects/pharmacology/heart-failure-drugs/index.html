Sample MCQ #1

Hypertension and pulmonary edema associated with scorpion sting is managed by:-

ACarvedilol
BPrazosin
CSpironolactone
DPhentolamine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Prazosin

Scorpion sting is a dreadful medical emergency. Scorpion stings cause a wide range of symptoms, from severe local reactions to cardiovascular, respiratory and neurological manifestations. Alpha-receptors play a vital role in the pathogenesis of cardiac failure and pulmonary edema due to scorpion sting. Prazosin is a selective alpha-1 adrenergic receptor blocker. It dilates veins and arterioles, thereby reducing preload and left ventricular impedance without rise in heart rate and renin secretion. It also inhibits sympathetic outflow in the central nervous system. It enhances insulin secretion, which is inhibited by venom action. It has also been found useful even in cases with hypotension. Thus, its pharmacological properties can antagonize the hemodynamic, hormonal and metabolic effects of scorpion venom action

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following drugs does not reduce mortality in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF)?

ADigoxin
BBeta-blockers
CACE inhibitors
DHydralazine + isosorbide dinitrate
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Digoxin

Explanation: Digoxin is a positive inotrope used in HFrEF to improve symptoms and reduce hospitalizations due to heart failure. However, large clinical trials have shown it has no significant impact on overall mortality . Explanation of other options: B) Beta-blockers, C) ACE inhibitors, D) H-ISDN: All of these are pillars of HFrEF therapy with robust evidence from clinical trials demonstrating significant mortality benefit . Key Takeaways: Drugs that reduce mortality in HFrEF include ACEIs/ARBs/ARNIs, beta-blockers, MRAs, and SGLT2 inhibitors. Digoxin improves symptoms but not survival.

ACongestive cardiac failure
BAcute congestive glaucoma
CAcute renal failure
DPulmonary edema
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Acute congestive glaucoma

Mannitol is very helpful in lowering of intraocular pressure in cases of Acute congestive glaucoma. MOA: Increases the extracellular oncotic pressure hence extracting water from cells and tissues. Uses Its indications are: Increased intracranial or intraocular tension (acute congestive glaucoma, head injury, stroke, etc To maintain GFR and urine flow in impending acute renal failure To counteract low osmolality of plasma and ECF due to rapid haemodialysis or peritoneal dialysis (dialysis disequilibrium). Mannitol is contraindicated in renal insufficiency, acute tubular necrosis, anuria, pulmonary edema; acute left ventricular failure, CHF and cerebral haemorrhage.(Options A,C,D ruled out) The most common side effect is headache. Nausea and vomiting may occur; hypersensitivity reactions are rare.

Sample MCQ #6

A patient receiving digoxin develops side effects such as nausea and vomiting. His serum digoxin concentration is found to be 4 ng/mL, while the therapeutic range is up to 1 ng/mL. Given that digoxin has a half-life of 40 hours, how long will it take before the drug can be safely restarted?

A40 hours
B80 hours
C120 hours
D140-180 hours
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 80 hours

Explanation: It will take 80 hours before the drug can be safely restarted. The Plasma half-life (t&frac12;) of a drug is the time taken for its plasma concentration to be reduced to half of its original value. In the scenario, the digoxin serum concentration of 4 ng/ml is significantly above the therapeutic range. Starting at 4 ng/mL , it would take: 1 half-life (40 hrs) &rarr; 2 ng/mL 2 half-lives (80 hrs) &rarr; 1 ng/mL (within therapeutic range) Since the therapeutic range is &le;1 ng/mL , the drug can be safely restarted after 80 hours . Therefore, we should wait at least 80 hours before resuming treatment, considering digoxin's half-life of 40 hours.

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following have convincingly demonstrated mortality reduction for patients with HFpEF?

AACEIs
BARBs
CSildenafil
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. None of the above

While therapeutic targets in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFpEF) are relatively abundant and guided by the targets of disease modification, trials in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) have been largely disappointing. Overall, symptom management and blood pressure control are the goals in HFpEF treatment now. ACE inhibitors (option A) have been studies in many mechanistic studies and have shown no convincing mortality benefit. Similarly, angiotensin receptor blockers (option B) were studied in the CHARM-Preserved and I-PRESERVE studies and were found to have no mortality benefit. Sildenafil (a phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitor) was shown to improve filling pressures and RV function in patients with HFpEF, but did not show mortality benefit. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 248

On Auscultation, no abnormal heart sound or murmur was heard, bilateral rhonchi were heard over lung fields. His pulse rate is 88/min; BP is 116/78 mm Hg. His chest radiogram was s/o COPD. So, treatment started with oral theophylline. What is the effect on Cardiovascular system of theophylline?

APositive chronotropic, negative inotropic
BNegative chronotropic, positive inotropic
CPositive chronotropic and inotropic
DNegative chronotropic and inotropic
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Positive chronotropic and inotropic

The methylxanthines have positive chronotropic and inotropic effects. At low concentrations, these effects appear to result from inhibition of presynaptic adenosine receptors in sympathetic nerves increasing catecholamine release at nerve endings. The higher concentrations (> 10 &mu;mol/L, 2 mg/L) associated with inhibition of phosphodiesterase and increases in cAMP may result in increased influx of calcium.
