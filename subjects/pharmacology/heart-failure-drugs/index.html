Sample MCQ #1

Hypertension and pulmonary edema associated with scorpion sting is managed by:-

ACarvedilol
BPrazosin
CSpironolactone
DPhentolamine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Prazosin

Scorpion sting is a dreadful medical emergency. Scorpion stings cause a wide range of symptoms, from severe local reactions to cardiovascular, respiratory and neurological manifestations. Alpha-receptors play a vital role in the pathogenesis of cardiac failure and pulmonary edema due to scorpion sting. Prazosin is a selective alpha-1 adrenergic receptor blocker. It dilates veins and arterioles, thereby reducing preload and left ventricular impedance without rise in heart rate and renin secretion. It also inhibits sympathetic outflow in the central nervous system. It enhances insulin secretion, which is inhibited by venom action. It has also been found useful even in cases with hypotension. Thus, its pharmacological properties can antagonize the hemodynamic, hormonal and metabolic effects of scorpion venom action

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following drugs does not reduce mortality in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF)?

ADigoxin
BBeta-blockers
CACE inhibitors
DHydralazine + isosorbide dinitrate
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Digoxin

Explanation: Digoxin is a positive inotrope used in HFrEF to improve symptoms and reduce hospitalizations due to heart failure. However, large clinical trials have shown it has no significant impact on overall mortality . Explanation of other options: B) Beta-blockers, C) ACE inhibitors, D) H-ISDN: All of these are pillars of HFrEF therapy with robust evidence from clinical trials demonstrating significant mortality benefit . Key Takeaways: Drugs that reduce mortality in HFrEF include ACEIs/ARBs/ARNIs, beta-blockers, MRAs, and SGLT2 inhibitors. Digoxin improves symptoms but not survival.
