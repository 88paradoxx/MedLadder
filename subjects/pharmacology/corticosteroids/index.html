Sample Corticosteroids MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A 56-year-old woman with SLE had been maintained on a moderate daily dose of prednisone for 9 months. Her disease has finally gone into remission. Gradual tapering of a glucocorticoid is required for recovery of which of the following?

ADepressed release of insulin from pancreatic B cells
BHaematopoiesis in the bone marrow
CNormal osteoblast function
DThe hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal system
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. The hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal system

Exogenous glucocorticoids act at the hypothalamus and pituitary to suppress the production of CRH and ACTH. As a result, adrenal production of endogenous corticosteroids is suppressed. On discontinuance, the recovery of normal hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal function occurs slowly. Glucocorticoid doses must be tapered slowly, over several months, to prevent adrenal insufficiency. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 712

Sample MCQ #2

A new-born girl exhibited ambiguous genitalia, hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, and hypotension. Treatment consisted of fluid and salt replacement and hydrocortisone administration. Which of the following describes the primary therapeutic effect of glucocorticoid administration?

AIncreased adrenal estrogen synthesis
BInhibition of adrenal aldosterone synthesis
CPrevention of hypoglycemia
DSuppression of ACTH secretion
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Suppression of ACTH secretion

A 21&alpha;-hydroxylase deficiency prevents normal synthesis of cortisol and aldosterone, and causes accumulation of cortisol precursors. The hypothalamic-pituitary system responds to the abnormally low levels of cortisol by increasing ACTH release. High levels of ACTH induce adrenal hyperplasia and excess production of adrenal androgens, which can cause virilization of females and prepubertal males. Glucocorticoid is administered to replace the missing mineralocorticoid and glucocorticoid activity and to suppress ACTH release, which removes the stimulus for excess adrenal androgen production. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 715

Sample MCQ #3

A 75 years old male patient is known case of COPD and is on combination therapy of LABA + Corticosteroids. Most important action of corticosteroid is___.

ABeta receptor agonist
BAnti-fibrotic effect
CInhibition of infiltration of airway
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Inhibition of infiltration of airway

Corticosteroids have been used to treat asthma since 1950 and are presumed to act by their broad anti-inflammatory efficacy, mediated in part by inhibition of production of inflammatory cytokines. They do not relax airway smooth muscle directly but reduce bronchial reactivity and reduce the frequency of asthma exacerbations if taken regularly. Their effect on airway obstruction may be due in part to their contraction of engorged vessels in the bronchial mucosa and their potentiation of the effects of &beta;-receptor agonists, but their most important action is inhibition of the infiltration of asthmatic airways by lymphocytes, eosinophils, and mast cells. Reference: LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 20- Drugs used in asthma by Dr. Homer A. Boushy, MD.
