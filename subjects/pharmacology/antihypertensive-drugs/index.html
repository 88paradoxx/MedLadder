Sample MCQ #2

A 57-year-old man is found to have a blood pressure of 150/100 mm Hg on three successive occasions. He has begun on an antihypertensive agent. After taking the first dose of the medication, he goes out to play golf and collapses on the golf course. Which one of the following drugs may be responsible for his symptoms?

AAtenolol
BHydrochlorothiazide
CMetoprolol
DPrazosin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Prazosin

Prazosin produces first- dose hypotension, presumably by blocking alpha-1- receptors. This effect is minimized by initially giving the drug in small, divided doses. Other options do not cause first-dose hypotension. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg. no. 182

Sample MCQ #3

A 53-year-old man comes with shortness of breath and chest tightness. Blood pressure is 200/110 mm Hg and pulse is 90/min and regular. Lung examination reveals bibasilar crackles. Nitroglycerin infusion is started and results in significant symptomatic improvement. Which of the following intracellular events is most likely responsible for the beneficial effects of this patient's treatment?

ACalcium release from sarcoplasmic reticulum
BEnhanced cyclic mononucleotide degradation
CTyrosine kinase activation
DMyosin dephosphorylation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Myosin dephosphorylation

This patient's clinical presentation is consistent with acute pulmonary edema due to severely elevated blood pressure (hypertensive emergency). In such cases, intravenous vasodilators (nitroglycerine, sodium nitroprusside) are often used to improve the acute heart failure by reducing preload and afterload. Nitrates are metabolized within vascular smooth muscle cells to nitric oxide, which activates guanylate cyclase and promotes the conversion of guanosine triphosphate (GTP) to cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP). Increased levels of cGMP lead to decreased intracellular calcium (reduces the activity of myosin light-chain kinase) and activation of myosin light chain phosphatase. This promotes myosin light-chain dephosphorylation and vascular smooth muscle relaxation. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg. no. 221

Sample MCQ #4

57-year-old homeless man takes an antihypertensive agent but has not been able to obtain the medication. Patient's blood pressure is 190/110 mm Hg. Which one of the following antihypertensive drugs can precipitate a hypertensive crisis following abrupt cessation of therapy?

AClonidine
BDiltiazem
CEnalapril
DLosartan
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Clonidine

Increased sympathetic nervous system activity occurs if clonidine therapy is abruptly stopped after prolonged administration. Uncontrolled elevation in blood pressure can occur. Patients should be slowly weaned from clonidine while other antihypertensive medications are initiated. Other options do not cause increased sympathetic activity after abrupt discontinuation. Reference Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg. no. 185

Sample MCQ #5

An investigational study, a newly synthesized drug is found to have antihypertensive effects. Increasing drug concentrations can cause increase in arteriolar radius and decrease beta cell insulin secretion. The mechanism of action of which of the following drugs most closely resembles the mechanism of action of the experimental drug?

AAtenolol
BDiazoxide
CLosartan
DPhentolamine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Diazoxide

The experimental agent is able to do both vasodilation and to decrease the release of insulin in response to glucose. The question is which known drug has similar effects. The answer is diazoxide, a K+ channel opener with hyperglycemic effects; Diazoxide is a direct acting peripheral vasodilator that decreases both systolic and diastolic blood pressure. Specifically, diazoxide opens K channels on vascular smooth muscle, causing hyperpolarization and vasodilation, resulting in a decrease in peripheral vascular resistance. Opening K channels on pancreatic beta cells causes hyperpolarization and decreased calcium entry through voltage-gated calcium channels. As a result, diazoxide decreases insulin secretion in response to glucose. Reference : Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 183-84

Sample MCQ #6

A 56-year-old man with male pattern baldness and hypertension is placed on minoxidil. He has had a lack of response to antihypertensive agents with failure to respond to calcium channel blockers and ACE inhibitors. Prolonged use of this agent can be associated with which of the following effects?

ABaldness
BFluid Retention
CMuscular wasting
DUremic pericarditis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Fluid Retention

This drug causes dilation of resistance vessels (arterioles) but not of capacitance vessels (venules). Minoxidil is administered orally for treatment of severe to malignant hypertension that is refractory to other drugs. Reflex tachycardia and fluid retention may be severe and require the concomitant use of a loop diuretic and a beta-blocker. Minoxidil causes serious sodium and water retention, leading to volume overload, edema, and congestive heart failure. Minoxidil treatment also causes hypertrichosis (the growth of body hair). This drug is used topically to treat male pattern baldness. Option(A) This agent is used to treat male pattern baldness. Option(C) This agent is not associated with muscle wasting. Option(D) This agent does not cause uremic pericarditis. Reference Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg. no. 178

Sample MCQ #8

A 59-year-old man with hypertension presents with lower extremity edema. Physical examination reveals pitting edema from the ankles to the knees bilaterally. The patient has begun on furosemide. What could be side effect seen in this patient ?

BHypotension

Furosemide is a loop diuretic with important interactions that treating we must be aware of. This agent will potentiate the action of other antihypertensives, and the patient can become hypotensive.
