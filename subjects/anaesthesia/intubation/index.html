Sample Intubation MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Pre oxygenation replaces the nitrogen in the functional residual volume with oxygen. This can achieved by

ATidal respiration for 3-5 mins with 100 percent o2
B8 vital capacity breaths
CBoth a and b
Da or b
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. a or b

The optimal technique is uncertain; 3 min administration is thought to be as effective as 5 min administration, or even four vital capacity breaths. A tightly fitting face-mask (to prevent entrainment of room air) and adequate flow of O 2 are vital. Monitoring of end-expiratory O 2 concentration may be a useful guide during pre-oxygenation; washout of nitrogen from the lungs is indicated by an increase in expired O 2 concentration towards steady state (near 100% in ideal conditions with no gas leaks or mixing). Reference: FRCA A TO Z, page no 479

Sample MCQ #2

A patient suffering a stab wound to the abdomen arrives in the operating room for an emergency exploratory laparotomy. His BP is 70/40, and his HR is 118. Which of the following fluid regimens are appropriate for his fluid resuscitation?

ANormal saline, at a ratio of 3 mL per estimated 1 mL of blood lost
BBlood at a ratio of 3 mL blood administered to 1 mL of blood lost
CHetastarch (Hespan) in a ratio of 3 mL per estimated 1 mL of blood lost
DD5W in a ratio of 3 mL of D5W to 1 mL of blood lost
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Normal saline, at a ratio of 3 mL per estimated 1 mL of blood lost

Lactated Ringer's solution or Plasmalyte in approximately three times the volume of the blood lost, or colloid in a 1:1 ratio , until the transfusion trigger point is reached is used for fluid resuscitation of a patient. At that time, blood is replaced unit-for-unit as it is lost , with reconstituted packed red blood cells. Reference : Morgan and Mikhail's clinical anesthesiology 6th edition, pg. 1196.

Sample MCQ #3

A 54-year-old woman is undergoing a total thyroid ectomy under general anesthesia. The patient is awak ened in the OR, the mouth and pharynx are suctioned, and after intact laryngeal reflexes are demonstrated, the endotracheal tube is removed. Two days later, the anesthesiologist is consulted because the patient has severe stridor and upper airway obstruction. The most likely cause of airway obstruction in this patient is

ADamage to the recurrent laryngeal nerve
BHematoma
CTracheomalacia
DHypocalcemia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Hypocalcemia

The symptoms of hypocalcemia, which manifest as laryngospasm or laryngeal stridor, usually develop within the first 24 to 96 hours after total thyroidectomy. After the airway is established and secured, the patient should be treated with IV calcium in the form of either calcium gluconate or calcium chloride Reference: Barash: Clinical Anesthesia, ed 7, pp 352-353, 1330.

Sample MCQ #4

First year trainee in anaesthesia intubates a patient posted for an elective surgery. Which auscultator technique give the most reliable indication that the tracheal tube is in the trachea?

AAuscultation of the upper abdomen
BGurgling sounds with ventilation
CAirway auscultation
DAuscultation of high mid-axillary area
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Auscultation of high mid-axillary area

Auscultation should be performed high in each midaxillary area , not just the anterior chest region. The quality of the sounds is important. A gurgling sound (death rattle) suggests esophageal placement. This test is not always reliable. Auscultating the upper abdomen as well as the lungs may increase the reliability of auscultation. But these sounds may be confused with breath sounds that are often heard in the epigastric area in thin individuals and pediatric patients. Reference: Understanding Anaesthesia Equipment (Jerry A. Dorsch, Susan E. Dorsch) 5th edition pg 590. 5th edition pg 601.

Sample MCQ #5

Pre oxygenation replaces the nitrogen in the functional residual volume with oxygen. This increases the apnea time upto

A10 mins
B2 mins
C4 mins
D15 mins
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 10 mins

Pre-oxygenation. Administration of 100% O 2 before induction of anaesthesia. Increases the O 2 reserve in the lungs (by replacing N 2 O in the FRC) and thus the time to hypoxemia during subsequent apnea to 8-10 minutes, e.g. during tracheal intubation. Also increases arterial PO 2 , although O 2 content and saturation may not increase by much. Particularly useful when difficulties are anticipated, or in patients at risk from aspiration of gastric contents. Thus a vital part of 'rapid sequence induction'. Reference: FRCA A TO Z, page no 479

Sample MCQ #6

A junior trainee has been assigned the task to intubate a young female patient posted for a laparoscopic appendectomy. Which is the single best method among the following to rule out esophageal intubation in this patient?

ASeeing the tube pass through the vocal cords
BObserving chest wall movement during inspiration and exhalation.
CHearing breath sounds during controlled ventilation
DMonitoring CO2 in exhaled gases
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Monitoring CO2 in exhaled gases

The American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) Standards for Basic Anesthetic Monitoring and the American Association of Nurse Anesthetists (AANA) require that when a tracheal tube is inserted, its correct positioning must be verified by identification of CO 2 in the expired gas. Observation of passage of tube through the vocal cords, chest wall movement or chest auscultation are subjective methods and are inferior to capnography. Reference: Understanding Anaesthesia Equipment (Jerry A. Dorsch, Susan E. Dorsch) 5th edition pg 592

Sample MCQ #7

A 4-year-old child weighing 16 kg is scheduled for inguinal hernia repair. She is at the 55th percentile for height for her age. An appropriately sized uncuffed endotracheal tube for this patient will have an internal diameter of

A3.0 mm
B4.0 mm
C5.0 mm
D6.0 mm
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 5.0 mm

Appropriate uncuffed endotracheal tube size may be determined by the following formula (age in years): (Age/3) + 3.5 Ie., (4/3)+3.5 = 4.8 Ie, 5, as per the given options (Subtract 0.5 for appropriate sized cuffed ETT.) Reference: Airway management (Rashid Khan) 7th edition, pg 52

Sample MCQ #8

A 25 year old female has presented with achalasia cardia and planned for Heller myotomy. During the induction of anesthesia, the patient aspirates. The immediate management includes all, except

AHead end down
BIntubation and securing the airway
CSteroid and antibiotic prophylaxis
DSuction immediately
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Steroid and antibiotic prophylaxis

Patients with esophageal disease have the risk of pulmonary aspiration. In such patients, a rapid-sequence induction should be used. Aspiration leads to Mendelson's syndrome. Steroid and prophylactic antibiotic administration is not indicated as part of management. Reference : Morgan and Mikhail's clinical anesthesia, 6th edition, page no. 579
