Sample MCQ #1

When compared to a cylinder manifold, a vacuum-insulated evaporator (VIE) is the most economical way to store and supply oxygen in a large hospital. What is the single most important reason for this?

AThe critical temperature of liquid oxygen is ideal to store as a liquid
BLiquid oxygen is easier to store.
CVIE does not require rigorous maintenance.
DWhen evaporated, liquid oxygen occupies a large volume as gas.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. When evaporated, liquid oxygen occupies a large volume as gas.

Liquid oxygen is stored in a vacuum-insulated evaporator (VIE) at a temperature of -150&deg;C to -170&deg;C and at a pressure of 5-10 atmospheres. In simple terms this works like a giant thermos flask. At 15&deg;C, one volume of liquid oxygen occupies 842 times its volume as gas. Oxygen constantly evaporates from the top of the VIE. As this oxygen is very cold, it needs to be passed through a super heater. When the oxygen flows at a faster rate, the temperature of the liquid falls due to the latent heat of evaporation. To overcome this problem additional heat is provided by a pressure-raising vaporiser. Regular maintenance, monitoring of usage of oxygen and topping up the tank at regular intervals is necessary. Reference: Al-Shaikh B, Stacey S, Eds. Liquid oxygen. In: Essentials of anaesthetic equipment, 3rd ed. London, UK: Churchill Livingstone, Elsevier, 2007; Chapter 1: 9-10.

Sample MCQ #4

Which test is performed on gas cylinders to check the quality of manufacturing?

AThe tensile test
BFlattening test
CBend test
DAll the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All the above

The cylinders used in medical practice are either solid drawn or made from seamless steel tube, and never welded at the ends. After manufacturing, the cylinders are kept in a furnace at a temperature of 860-890 degree C and then carefully removed and cooled at atmospheric pressure . As the cylinders must withstand a high gas pressure and rough handling during transport, they are subjected to mechanical testing before marketing for use. The tensile test, flattening test, impact test and bend test are performed on one cylinder from a batch of every 100 finished cylinders. Reference: Anaesthesia Review: A Comprehensive Textbook by Atul, 5th Edition, Page Number 396-409.

Sample MCQ #5

1 kPa is approximately equivalent to:

A10 cm H2O
B14.7 psi
C0.76 mm Hg
D100 mbar
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 10 cm H2O

Explanation: Pressure Conversions: 1 kPa = 10 cm H2O is a valid approximation, which is useful in clinical settings for measuring pressures in respiratory physiology and anaesthesia. To understand this, atmospheric pressure is often measured in various units, including mmHg, mbar, and psi. Atmospheric Pressure Reference: Atmospheric pressure is defined as the pressure exerted by the weight of air in the atmosphere, commonly measured using a barometer. 760 mmHg (Torr) is equivalent to 1 bar = 1000 mbar = 101.3 kPa . Conversions for Context: 1 kPa &asymp; 7.5 mmHg (Option C) 1 bar = 14.5 psi , thus 1 kPa &asymp; 0.145 psi (Option B) 1 kPa is equivalent to 10 mbar , not 100 mbar (Option D) Key Takeaways: 1 kPa is approximately 10 cm H2O, a key conversion for understanding pressures in clinical practice. Familiarity with pressure units is essential in anaesthesia and critical care settings.

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following is NOT found in the low pressure circuit on an anesthesia machine?

AOxygen supply failure alarm
BFlowmeters
CVaporizers
DVaporizer check valve
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Oxygen supply failure alarm

The anesthesia machine, now more properly called the anesthesia workstation, has two main pressure circuits. The higher-pressure circuits consist of the gas supply from the pipelines and tanks, all piping, pressure gauges, pressure reduction regulators, check valves (which prevent backward gas flow), the oxygen pressure sensor shutoff valve (also called the oxygen failure cutoff or fail-safe valve), the oxygen supply failure alarm, and the oxygen flush valve-or, simplistically, everything up to the gas flow control valves and the machine common gas outlet. The low-pressure circuit starts with and includes the gas flow control valves, flowmeters, vaporizers, and vaporizer check valve and goes to the machine common gas outlet. Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following prevents delivery of hypoxic gas mixture once the oxygen pressure falls below 25 psi?

ADiameter index safety system
BPin index safety system
CInspiratory check valve
DFail-safe valve
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Fail-safe valve

The fail-safe valve automatically closes nitrous oxide (and other gases) to prevent delivery of hypoxic gas mixture to the patient. The fail-safe valve is designed to be activated when oxygen pressure falls below 25 psi. Reference: Lippincott's ANESTHESIA REVIEW: 1,001 QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS Paul Sikka, MD, PhD

Sample MCQ #8

The oxygen-flush valve provides which of the following oxygen flows (L/min) to the common gas outlet?

A10
B25
C50
D90
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 50

The oxygen-flush valve provides gas flow at pipeline pressures of about 45 to 55 psi at 35 to 75 L/min. The high flow of oxygen is provided directly to the common gas outlet, bypassing the flowmeters and vaporizers. One should be careful when using the oxygen-flush valve, as high gas flows at high pressures can cause lung barotrauma in the patient. Reference: Lippincott's ANESTHESIA REVIEW: 1,001 QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS Paul Sikka, MD, PhD
