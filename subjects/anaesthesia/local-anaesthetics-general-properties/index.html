Sample MCQ #1

Which is the reference local anaesthetic agent against which all agents are compared?

ALidocaine
BBupivacaine
CLevobupivacaine
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Lidocaine

Aside from the issue of a high incidence of TNS with spinal administration, lidocaine has had an excellent record as an intermediate duration anaesthetic, and remains the reference standard against which most anaesthetics are compared. Reference: LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 26- Local Anaesthetics by Dr. Kenneth Drasner,MD. Page no. 460

Sample MCQ #2

Local anaesthetic agents cause neural toxicity. Which of the following is TRUE? 1. Neural injury occurs from voltage gated sodium channels 2. Clinical effect and toxicity is tightly linked.

ASentence 1 and 2 both are True and 2 is correct reason for 1
BSentence 1 and 2 both are True but 2 is not the correct reason for 1
CSentence 1 is True but Sentence 2 is False
DSentence 1 and 2 both are False.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Sentence 1 and 2 both are False.

It is not clear what role these factors or others play in clinical injury. It is clear, however, that injury does not result from blockade of the voltage-gated sodium channel per se, and thus clinical effect and toxicity are not tightly linked. Reference LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 26- Local Anaesthetics by Dr. Kenneth Drasner,MD. Page no. 459

Sample MCQ #3

The manifestations of shellfish poisoning are due to the action of the poison on which channel?

AMg channel
BCa channel
CNa channel
DK channel
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Na channel

Saxitoxin and Tetrodotoxin are two potent poisons whose mechanism of action is like that of local anaesthetics - blockade of Na channels. Saxitoxin is produced by certain dinoflagellates which are consumed by shellfish &rarr; When such shell fish are consumed by humans, fatal shell fish poisoning occurs with hypotension and respiratory paralysis &rarr; This is due to blockade of Na channels by the poison in excitable cells preventing action potential generation Treatment consists of Gastric lavage Vasopressors Mechanical ventilation if respiratory paralysis sets in Prognosis is good when the patient survives after 24 hours Tetrodotoxin is a poison found in some fish of the order Tetraodontiformes (puffer fish) and newts Mechanism of action and manifestations are like shellfish poisoning Reference: Morgan and mikhail's clinical anaesthesiology 6th edition
