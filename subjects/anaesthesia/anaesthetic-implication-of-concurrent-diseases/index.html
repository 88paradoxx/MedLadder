Sample MCQ #1

A hyperthyroid patient presents to the casualty room following road traffic accident. He has sustained right leg crush injury that requires amputation. He is taken up for surgery under general anesthesia. Which of the following monitors is likely to indicate precipitation of a thyroid crisis intraoperatively

AHyperthermia
BElevated end tidal CO2 concentration
CTachycardia
DHyperglycaemia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Hyperglycaemia

Thyroid storm can present during and post surgery with fever (>40&deg;C), sweating, sinus tachycardia (>140bpm), coma, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea. Management involves rehydration with IV saline and glucose, administration of steroid, beta blockers, propylthiouracil and paracetamol. Reference : Oxford handbook of Anesthesia, edition 4, page no 577

Sample MCQ #2

A 37-year-old man with myasthenia gravis arrives in the emergency room confused and agitated after a 2-day history of weakness and increased difficulty breathing. ABGs on room air are Pao2 60 mm Hg, Paco2 51 mm Hg, HCO3 - 25 mEq/L, pH 7.3, Sao2 of 90%. His respiratory rate is 30 breaths/min and tidal volume (Vt) is 4 mL/kg. After administration of edrophonium 2 mg IV, his Vt declines to 2 mL/kg. What should be the most appropriate step in the management of this patient at this time?

ATracheal intubation and mechanical ventilation
BRepeat the test dose of edrophonium
CAdminister neostigmine
DAdminister atropine for cholinergic crisis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Tracheal intubation and mechanical ventilation

Cholinergic crisis can be differentiated from myasthenic crisis by administering small IV doses of anticholinesterases. With a cholinergic crisis, there are significant muscarinic effects (e.g., salivation, bradycardia, miosis) and an accentuated muscle weakness. Because this patient's Vt decreased with the administration of edrophonium, the diagnosis of cholinergic crisis is made. Although atropine may be needed to treat the cholinergic symptoms, muscle weakness will be worse and these patients need to be intubated until the muscle strength returns. Reference: Hines: Stoelting's Anesthesia and Co-Existing Disease, ed 6, p 450

Sample MCQ #3

An end stage renal disease patient has been scheduled for renal transplant. He is on thrice weekly hemodialysis. His blood investigations revealed high potassium levels more than 6.0 mEq/L and he was dialyzed preoperatively, repeat potassium was 5.2 mEq/L. The drugs to be used in caution include all except

ASuccinylcholine
BPethidine
CSevoflurane
DEtomidate
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Etomidate

Succinylcholine, depolarizing muscle relaxant can cause an increase in the potassium concentration up to 0.5 mEq/L can be used to facilitate endotracheal intubation only in the absence of hyperkalemia. Meperidine (pethidine), opioid should be avoided because of accumulation of its metabolite normeperidine. Sevoflurane usage for long hours can cause accumulation of compound A, that has been found nephrotoxic. Reference : Morgan and Mikhail's clinical anesthesia, 6th edition, page no. 689
