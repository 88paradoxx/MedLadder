EXPLANATION: Any clotting factor defect will present with deep tissue bleeding like joint bleeds, muscle bleeds etc. Any platelet defect will present with superficial bleeds like petechiae and mucosal bleeds. Hemophilia A is an X-linked recessive disorder characterized by defective synthesis of clotting factor VIII. It is a clotting factor defect, which typically presents with deep tissue bleeding like hemarthrosis (bleeding into the joints). As it is X linked recessive, it will affect mostly males. More common in males: As hemophilia A is an X-linked recessive disorder , it is more common in males because they have only one X chromosome. If that X carries the defective gene, they will manifest the disease. Females, with two X chromosomes, would need both X chromosomes to carry the defective gene to be affected, which is much rarer. Due to Factor VIII deficiency: Hemophilia A is caused by a deficiency of Factor VIII , which is essential for blood clotting.
