ID: 23371 | Answer: [D] Cardiac defect may be a cause of death.
QUESTION: Which of the following is true about DiGeorge syndrome?
OPTIONS: A) It results from a duplication of chromosome 22q11. | B) There is hypoplasia of the second pharyngeal pouch. | C) Apnea, jitteriness, and high-pitched cry may complicate the course of the disease | D) Cardiac defect may be a cause of death.
EXPLANATION: DiGeorge Syndrome DiGeorge syndrome is a congenital hypoplasia of organs derived from the third and fourth pharyngeal pouches. It results in hypoplasia or agenesis of the thymus and parathyroid glands, the auricle, and the external auditory canal; congenital cardiac anomalies; cleft palate; and short stature. It is associated with microdeletion of chromosome 22q11 . The main features are hypocalcemia, seizures, congenital heart disease, lymphocytopenia, and multiple minor anomalies. Hypocalcemia can manifest as apnea, jitteriness, crying episodes and tetany. Affected newborns may die from cardiac causes during the first month. Frequent infections due to a defect of cell-mediated immunity and failure to thrive may complicate the course of the surviving newborn. Reference: Nelson 20 th edition, pg-2650
