Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

MedLadder ("we", "our", "us") operates the MedLadder educational question bank and exam practice platform via our website (https://medladder.top) and the MedLadder Android mobile application (com.medladder.app). This Privacy Policy explains what personal and usage information we collect, how we store and process it, how it is shared, and the choices and deletion options available to you.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in using Google Sign-in or email credentials, we receive your account email address, display name, profile avatar URL (if provided by Google), and an internal unique account identifier (UUID). We do not collect or store your Google account password.
  • Quiz History & Study Progress: To provide personalized performance analytics, progress bars, and revision recommendations, we store your question attempts, chosen answers, correct/incorrect results, test scores, bookmark/flagged questions, mock test submissions, and module completion timestamps associated with your user account.
  • Payment & Subscription Details: If you purchase a MedLadder Pro subscription, the transaction is processed directly by our payment gateway, Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant). MedLadder only receives and stores transactional metadata necessary to activate and verify your access: your Razorpay Order ID, Payment ID, payment status, subscription tier, and validity/expiry dates. MedLadder does not collect, process, or store credit/debit card numbers, CVV codes, netbanking passwords, or UPI PINs.
  • Technical & Log Data: When accessing the service via our website or Android app, basic diagnostic and log data may be processed, including your device model, operating system version, browser/WebView type, and IP address (used for security monitoring, abuse prevention, and coarse geographic categorization).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, manage, and authenticate your MedLadder account.
  • To track, sync, and display your MCQ practice history, test scores, and study metrics across devices.
  • To process transactions, verify payment status, and activate your Pro subscription tier.
  • To detect, troubleshoot, and prevent security incidents, unauthorized access, and platform abuse.
  • To send essential transactional notifications (such as password reset links or payment confirmation notices).

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share information only with trusted third-party service providers essential to operating MedLadder:

  • Supabase: Provides our cloud backend, authentication, and database infrastructure. Data is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS/TLS and stored with Row-Level Security (RLS) protections.
  • Razorpay: Processes payments for Pro subscriptions in compliance with PCI-DSS standards.
  • Google Identity / OAuth: Authenticates user accounts securely without MedLadder ever seeing your Google password.
  • Google Analytics: Provides aggregated usage insights to improve user experience (see Section 4).
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation.

MedLadder does not include any third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, or data brokers. We do not track users across unrelated applications or websites for advertising purposes.

4. Analytics & Cookies

MedLadder uses Google Analytics to understand how users interact with our platform and to improve our educational content. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, session duration, device and browser specifications, operating system, and online or pseudonymous identifiers, depending on configuration. Google processes this information in accordance with its own privacy terms. You can learn more about Google's practices and opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Security

MedLadder uses Supabase for backend services and database storage. We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards—including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, encrypted storage at rest, and strict database row-level access controls—to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Personal data is retained for as long as your account remains active to maintain your study history and subscription benefits. In accordance with statutory legal obligations (such as taxation and accounting regulations under Indian law), payment transaction records (Order IDs and Payment IDs) are retained for the statutory retention period.

When an account deletion request is completed, we delete the associated personal data that we control, subject to any applicable legal, security, or legitimate record-retention requirements.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access your personal data, request corrections, or request complete deletion of your account and associated study data.

  • How to Request Account & Data Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your MedLadder account and all associated study progress by emailing us at 99medladder@gmail.com from your registered account email address. Please include "MedLadder Account Deletion Request" in the subject line.
  • Verification & Deletion Process: To protect your account security, we will verify that the request originates from the registered email address. Once verified, your user account, profile details, question attempt records, test scores, and bookmarks will be permanently deleted from our active databases within 14 business days. Any data retained for mandatory legal or statutory accounting reasons (e.g., payment invoice records) will be held solely for the required period and not used for any other purpose.
  • Revoking Google Access: If you signed in using your Google account, you can manage or revoke MedLadder's access at any time via your Google Account permissions page. Note that revoking Google access prevents future Google logins but does not automatically delete previously stored study progress—to delete your data completely, please submit an account deletion request as described above.

7. Children's Privacy

MedLadder is designed exclusively for medical students, medical graduates, and healthcare professionals preparing for postgraduate entrance and licensing examinations (FMGE, NEET PG, INI-CET, NEET SS). The platform is not directed to or intended for children under 13 years of age (or under 16 where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Medical & Educational Disclaimer

MedLadder is solely an educational and exam preparation resource. MedLadder does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical patient management. The application does not collect, transmit, or store patient health data, protected health information (PHI), or electronic medical records.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our legal obligations or application features. Any modifications will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
