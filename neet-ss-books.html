What is NEET SS?

NEET SS (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test – Super Speciality) is India's single national entrance exam for DM, MCh and DrNB super-speciality courses, conducted by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS). Only doctors holding an MD, MS or DNB in an eligible feeder broad specialty can appear.

NEET SS books — FAQs

Which book is best for NEET SS Pediatrics preparation?

MedLadder's NEET SS Pediatrics — Complete Review (free above) is built specifically for the NEET SS Pediatric group: 178 pages, 12 chapters, 24 labeled illustrations and 300 exam-style MCQs with explanations across every pediatric super-speciality.

What is the NEET SS exam pattern?

NEET SS is a 150-question computer-based test by NBEMS: 3 time-bound sections of 50 questions (50 minutes each), +4/−1 marking, 600 marks total, 2.5 hours.

Is there negative marking in NEET SS?

Yes — 1 mark is deducted for every wrong answer (25% negative marking); correct answers score +4 and unattempted questions score zero.

What is the syllabus for the NEET SS Pediatric group?

Paediatric neonatology, cardiology, neurology, nephrology, gastroenterology, hepatology and critical care — asked at PG-exit level. Our free Pediatrics book covers all of them.
